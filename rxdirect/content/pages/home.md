## Live-in or live-out: choosing the right arrangement

One of the first questions we ask is whether you want staff to live in your home or come in daily. Both work well, and the answer depends on your house, your routine and the role.

**Live-in staff** stay in your servant quarter. They are available early in the morning and late at night, which suits large families, homes with elderly parents and households with young children. You provide a clean room, a bathroom, meals and a weekly day off. Live-in cooks, maids, nannies, caretakers and domestic couples are the most common.

**Live-out staff** come at a fixed time and go home after their shift. They suit apartments, smaller homes and families who value privacy in the evening. Drivers, cleaners, office boys, gardeners and part-time cooks are usually live-out. Transport time matters here, so we try to match workers who live close to you.

If you are unsure, tell us your daily schedule and we will suggest what fits. Many families start with live-out staff and switch to live-in once trust is built.

## Questions to ask before you hire anyone

Whether you hire through us or on your own, these questions will save you trouble:

1. Can I see your original CNIC, and does the address on it match where you live now?
2. Who were your last two employers, and can I call them?
3. Why did you leave your last job?
4. What does a normal working day look like for you?
5. Are you comfortable with our routine, including weekends, guests and Ramadan timings?
6. What salary are you expecting, and what do you expect for leave and Eid bonus?
7. Do you have any health issues we should know about for this role?

Write the answers down. If you hire through RX Direct, we will already have asked most of these, and we will share what we found.
