## Cook hiring guide Pakistan

Our cook hiring guide for Pakistan covers everything from writing a brief to running a trial. Key ideas from our cook articles:

- A home cook and a chef are different roles. Most families need a cook.
- Taste is personal. A cook who is excellent for one family may not suit another, so always test with your normal meals.
- Kitchen hygiene matters as much as taste. Watch how a cook handles raw meat, dishes and leftovers.
- Diet needs, such as low oil meals for heart patients or sugar control for diabetics, should be part of the brief from day one.
- Ramadan routines change everything. Discuss sehri and iftar duties before the month starts.

## How to find domestic staff Pakistan: options compared

There are several ways to find domestic staff in Pakistan, and each has trade offs. Our articles compare them honestly:

| Method | Advantages | Risks |
|---|---|---|
| Family referral | Some trust built in | References often biased, no checks |
| Neighbour's staff network | Local, quick | Workers may leave together, no records |
| Facebook and OLX ads | Many options | Identity and references hard to verify |
| Individual agents | Quick, personal | Little accountability, no guarantee |
| Registered agency | Checks, records, replacement guarantee | Placement fee |

Whichever route you choose, the same basic checks apply: original CNIC, address confirmation, reference calls and a trial.

## Staff verification tips Pakistan

Many of our posts focus on verification, because it is where most families cut corners. Our staff verification tips for Pakistan include:

- Never accept a CNIC photo on WhatsApp as proof. See the original.
- Call references yourself, and ask open questions like "why did they leave?"
- Be careful when every reference is a relative or a friend of the worker.
- Confirm the address through someone other than the worker.
- For guards, ask for a police character certificate. For nurses, ask for qualifications.
- Keep a record of everything, with copies stored safely.
