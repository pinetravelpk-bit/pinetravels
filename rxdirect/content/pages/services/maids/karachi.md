## House maid Karachi: what the job covers

A house maid in Karachi usually handles sweeping and mopping, dusting, cleaning bathrooms and the kitchen, washing, drying, folding and ironing, changing bed linen, organising cupboards, and weekly cleaning of fans, windows, grills and balconies. In Karachi, windows and grills need more frequent cleaning because salt and dust build up quickly, and corners need watching for damp and mould in the humid months.

In apartments, she may also manage the balcony, coordinate with the building's sweeper and keep track of water supply. In bungalows, she often looks after more rooms, the drawing room for guests and the servant quarter area.

If cooking or childcare is needed, agree it at the start. A [cook](/services/cooks/karachi) or [nanny](/services/babysitters-nannies/karachi) alongside the maid often works better than adding duties gradually.

## Humidity, damp and laundry

Karachi's humidity is hard on clothes and homes. Laundry takes longer to dry, and clothes left damp develop a smell. Good maids hang clothes where air moves, use a dryer when available, iron promptly and never fold damp clothes into cupboards. They also watch for mould in bathroom corners, behind cupboards and on walls facing the sea breeze, and clean it early with the right products.

## Water and load-shedding

In many Karachi neighbourhoods, water is precious. A good maid sweeps before mopping, uses buckets rather than running taps, reuses water for balconies where suitable and reports leaks immediately. During load-shedding she plans laundry and ironing around power hours so work does not pile up. We ask candidates about these routines in interviews.

## Salt, grills and windows

Homes near the sea in Clifton and DHA face salt deposits on windows, grills and metal fittings. Regular wiping prevents rust and keeps windows clear. A maid who knows to clean grills and window channels weekly saves expensive repairs.

## How much one maid can handle

One full-time maid can usually keep a three to four bedroom home clean and manage laundry for four or five people. Larger bungalows, small children or frequent guests usually need extra help. Karachi's hospitality means many homes see relatives and friends several evenings a week, and the morning after a big gathering brings a mountain of dishes, floors and laundry. A part-time helper or [cleaner](/services/cleaners/karachi) on the busiest days keeps the workload fair and protects the maid you already have from burning out.
