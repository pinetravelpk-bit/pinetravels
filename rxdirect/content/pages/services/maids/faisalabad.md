## House maid Faisalabad: what the job covers

A house maid in Faisalabad usually handles sweeping, mopping and dusting, cleaning bathrooms and the kitchen, washing, drying, folding and ironing, changing bed linen, organising cupboards, and weekly cleaning of fans, windows, doors, the roof and courtyard. In joint family homes, she keeps the guest areas ready for visitors and helps after family gatherings.

Seasonal work includes extra dusting in the long, dusty summer, airing quilts and blankets in the sun before winter, washing curtains and deep cleaning before Ramadan and Eid, and preparing the house for weddings.

If cooking or childcare is needed, agree it at the start. A [cook](/services/cooks/faisalabad) or [helper](/services/helpers/faisalabad) alongside the maid is often better than adding duties gradually.

## Maids for joint family homes

A joint family home has more rooms, more people and more opinions. A maid may take instructions from several women in the house, which can be confusing. Agree who she reports to and how duties are divided. For very large homes, two maids or a maid and helper work much better than one person stretched thin. We shortlist maids with experience in large households and ask references how they managed the workload.

## Laundry for a big household

Laundry in a joint family can be enormous: school uniforms for several children, shalwar kameez for many adults, bed linen and towels. A maid needs to organise it by day and type, use machines efficiently and keep ironing under control. Formal and embroidered clothes for weddings may need special care. Show the maid how you want them handled. Faisalabad, as the textile capital, has many families who own fine lawn, cotton and silk clothing, and careless washing can ruin expensive fabric. A maid who separates colours, uses the right water temperature and irons carefully is worth her salary many times over.

## Dust and summer cleaning

Faisalabad's long summers bring dust storms and heat. Floors and furniture need dusting more often, windows stay closed during storms and water tanks need checking. A good maid adjusts the routine to the season. In winter, quilts and blankets come out and need airing in the sun; in monsoon, entrances and courtyards need extra attention to keep mud out of the house.

## Working alongside other household staff

Large Faisalabad homes often have a cook, a driver, a guard and sometimes a helper as well as the maid. Clear roles keep everyone working together. Decide who cleans the kitchen after meals, who handles laundry, who serves guests and who looks after the courtyard and roof. Without this, the maid often ends up picking up everyone else's leftover work, which leads to frustration and resignation. A short conversation with all staff in the first week, setting out who does what, prevents most of these problems.

Maids who have worked in sociable, busy homes understand this rhythm. ## How much one maid can handle

As a rough guide, one full-time maid can keep a three to four bedroom house clean and manage laundry for four or five people. A joint family of ten in a double storey house with a courtyard and roof is a different job entirely. In homes like these, two maids, or a maid and a helper, keep standards high without burning anyone out. Overloading one maid is the fastest way to lose her, and rehiring every few months costs far more than a second salary.
