## House maid Gujranwala: what the job covers

A house maid in Gujranwala usually handles sweeping, mopping and dusting, cleaning bathrooms and the kitchen, washing, drying, folding and ironing, changing bed linen, organising cupboards, and weekly cleaning of fans, windows, doors, the roof and courtyard. In busy homes, she keeps guest rooms ready and helps after family gatherings. In homes with elderly parents, she may also make tea, prepare simple meals if agreed, and keep the parents company.

Seasonal work includes dusting through the hot, humid summer, washing curtains, airing quilts before winter, and preparing the house when relatives arrive from abroad.

If cooking or caring for parents is a regular duty, agree it and the salary at the start. A [cook](/services/cooks/gujranwala) or [caretaker](/services/caretakers/gujranwala) alongside the maid is often the better arrangement.

## Maids for parents whose children live abroad

This is one of the most common reasons Gujranwala families contact us. A maid in this role does more than clean. She notices if a parent is unwell, makes sure meals are eaten, keeps the house safe and provides daily company. We look for maids whose references mention patience and kindness with elderly people. ## When relatives come home

When the family returns from abroad for Eid, weddings or summer, the house fills up. Guest rooms need preparing, laundry multiplies and floors need cleaning several times a day. A temporary helper or second maid for the visit keeps the regular maid from being overwhelmed. Tell us the dates in advance, since the weeks around Eid and the summer holidays are busy for temporary staff in Gujranwala.

## Busy business family homes

In homes with a busy family and frequent guests, the maid may take instructions from several family members. Agree who she reports to and how duties are divided. For large homes, a maid plus a helper keeps the workload fair.

## Looking after an empty house

Some Gujranwala homes stay closed for months while the whole family lives abroad. Dust settles, damp creeps into corners, pipes can leak unnoticed and the house quickly feels neglected. A maid on a regular schedule, perhaps two or three days a week, can keep the house aired, dusted and ready for the family's return. Usually this works best alongside a resident guard or as part of a [domestic couple](/services/couples/gujranwala), so someone is always on the property. Agree a simple checklist for each visit, and ask the maid or guard to send photos once a week. It gives families abroad confidence that their home is being looked after.

## Laundry and fine clothes

Gujranwala families, like most in Punjab, own plenty of formal and embroidered clothes for weddings and Eid. A good maid separates colours, washes delicate fabrics by hand, dries them in shade and irons carefully. Hot, humid summers make clothes slow to dry and prone to smell if left damp, so prompt hanging and ironing matter. Show the maid how you want expensive clothes treated, or keep them for the dry cleaner.

## Working alongside other staff

Many Gujranwala homes also have a cook, driver, guard or gardener. Agree who cleans the kitchen, who handles the courtyard and roof, and who serves guests, so the maid is not left with everyone else's work. A short conversation with all staff in the first week keeps things clear.

## Female staff and comfort

We ask families who lives in the house and whether other women are present, and share this honestly with maids. In homes where only an elderly father lives, families often prefer a male helper or a domestic couple instead.

## How much one maid can handle

One full-time maid can usually keep a three to four bedroom house clean and manage laundry for four or five people. Larger houses or busy families usually need extra help. A double storey house with a roof, courtyard and several guest rooms, or a home with small children and grandparents, is too much for one person to manage well every day. Adding a part-time helper on the busiest days is usually cheaper than losing a good maid to exhaustion.

## Protecting your home and your maid

Keep valuables locked, keep copies of the maid's CNIC and our verification summary, and register live-in staff with your society where required. For parents living alone, ask a relative to visit regularly. Regular visits reassure the parents, give the maid someone local to turn to if there is a problem, and keep the family abroad informed.
