## House maid Rawalpindi: what the job covers

A house maid in Rawalpindi usually handles sweeping, mopping and dusting, cleaning bathrooms and the kitchen, washing, drying, folding and ironing, changing bed linen, organising cupboards, and weekly cleaning of fans, windows, doors and the roof or courtyard. In joint family homes, the laundry alone can take hours, and tidying after a steady stream of relatives is part of the job.

Many families also want help before Ramadan and Eid, when the whole house is deep cleaned, curtains washed and the guest rooms prepared. Tell us about your household and workload.

If you also need cooking or childcare, say so at the start. Adding duties gradually without extra pay is the most common reason maids leave. A [cook](/services/cooks/rawalpindi) or [helper](/services/helpers/rawalpindi) alongside the maid often works better.

## Laundry and ironing

Laundry is a big part of the job in Rawalpindi, especially with school uniforms, office and military uniforms, and formal clothes for weddings. We ask maids about whites, colours, silk and embroidered clothes, and about automatic and semi-automatic machines. Show the maid how you want delicate clothes treated in the first week. Military uniforms in particular have their own standards for pressing, creases and starching, and some families prefer the batman or a laundry service to handle them. Agree who handles what, so expensive or official clothing is never ruined by guesswork.

## Monsoon, dust and seasonal cleaning

Rawalpindi's monsoon brings mud and damp, its summers bring dust storms, and winters bring heavy blankets that need washing and storing in spring. A good maid adjusts the cleaning routine to the season without constant reminders. In monsoon she keeps entrances dry and checks corners for damp. In summer she dusts more often and keeps windows closed during dust storms. Before winter she airs out quilts and blankets in the sun. These small habits keep a Rawalpindi home comfortable all year.

## How much one maid can handle

As a rough guide, one full-time maid can keep a three to four bedroom house clean and handle laundry for a family of four or five, with a weekly deep clean of one or two areas. A joint family of eight or ten, a double storey house with a roof and courtyard, small children or frequent guests usually need a second person. Overloading one maid leads to falling standards and an early resignation. Adding a part-time [cleaner](/services/cleaners/rawalpindi) or helper often costs less over a year than constantly rehiring.
