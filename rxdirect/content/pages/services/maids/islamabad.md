## House maid Islamabad: what the job covers

A house maid in Islamabad typically handles sweeping, mopping and dusting all rooms, cleaning bathrooms and the kitchen, washing, drying, folding and ironing clothes, changing bed sheets and towels, organising cupboards and wardrobes, and weekly cleaning of fans, windows, doors and balconies. In larger homes she also tidies after guests and helps keep reception rooms ready for visitors, which in Islamabad can mean several times a week.

Many families also want help with seasonal work: washing and storing winter blankets and woollens in spring, airing out quilts in autumn, and deep cleaning before Ramadan and Eid. Tell us about your household and we will shortlist maids comfortable with your workload.

If you need cooking or childcare as well, it is best to say so at the start. A maid who is gradually given extra jobs without extra pay usually leaves. A [cook](/services/cooks/islamabad) or [nanny](/services/babysitters-nannies/islamabad) alongside the maid is often the better arrangement.

## Seasonal work in Islamabad homes

Islamabad's seasons create their own housework. Spring means washing and storing heavy winter blankets. Monsoon brings mud, damp and mould in corners. Summer dust from the hills settles on everything. Before Eid, families want a deep clean. A good maid plans these jobs around the normal routine without needing constant reminders.

## How much one maid can handle

As a rough guide, one full-time maid can keep a three to four bedroom house clean and handle laundry for a family of four or five. Larger houses, small children or frequent guests usually need a second person, such as a helper or part-time cleaner.

## Laundry, ironing and formal wear

Laundry takes a large share of a maid's day in Islamabad homes: school uniforms, office shirts, bed linen, towels and, in winter, heavy woollens and blankets. Many families also own expensive formal wear for weddings and official events. During interviews we ask maids how they handle whites, colours, silk, chiffon and embroidered clothes, and whether they have used automatic and semi-automatic washing machines and dryers. If you own delicate or very expensive clothes, show the maid how you want them treated in the first week, or keep them for the dry cleaner. A short demonstration saves ruined outfits.

## Working while the family is out

In many Islamabad homes both parents work long hours in offices across the twin cities, and the maid is often alone in the house for much of the day. That needs trust. We shortlist maids whose references specifically say they worked unsupervised and could be trusted with keys and the home. In the first weeks, try to come home unexpectedly once or twice, keep valuables locked and agree a simple routine, such as a WhatsApp message when she arrives and leaves. Most families find that a verified maid settles into this arrangement quickly.

## Protecting your home and your maid

Keep valuables locked, keep copies of the maid's CNIC and our verification summary, and register live-in staff with your housing society or the Islamabad police where required. These steps also protect an honest maid from being wrongly suspected if something goes missing.

## Keeping a good maid

Pay on time, respect the weekly day off, speak politely and give clear duties. Maids stay longest where they feel respected. Families who treat maids well also find hiring easier, because good workers talk to each other. Small gestures count: proper meals, a fan or heater in the servant quarter depending on the season, new clothes at Eid, and understanding when a family emergency back home needs her attention. Maids who are treated this way often stay with Islamabad families for many years, sometimes until the children they once helped raise are grown.
