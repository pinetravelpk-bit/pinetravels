## House maid Lahore: what the job covers

A house maid in Lahore usually handles sweeping, mopping and dusting, cleaning bathrooms and the kitchen, washing, drying, folding and ironing, changing bed linen, organising cupboards and wardrobes, and weekly cleaning of fans, windows, doors and balconies. In larger homes in DHA, Gulberg and Model Town, she also keeps the drawing room ready for guests and tidies after the frequent gatherings that are part of Lahore life.

Seasonal work matters too: extra dusting in summer, keeping entrances dry in monsoon, wiping smog residue in winter, airing quilts before the cold months and deep cleaning before Ramadan and Eid.

If cooking or childcare is needed, agree it at the start. Adding duties gradually without extra pay is the most common reason maids leave. A [cook](/services/cooks/lahore) or [nanny](/services/babysitters-nannies/lahore) alongside the maid often works better.

## Maids for busy Lahore households

Lahore homes often host relatives and friends. After a weekend dawat, there are dishes, floors, bathrooms and guest rooms to clean, and sometimes laundry from overnight guests. Maids who have worked in sociable households understand this rhythm and plan for it. For very large or busy homes, a maid plus a helper or part-time [cleaner](/services/cleaners/lahore) keeps the workload fair.

## Laundry, ironing and formal wear

Lahore families own a lot of formal and embroidered clothing for weddings and events. We ask maids how they handle silk, chiffon, lawn and embroidered fabrics, and which machines they have used. Show the maid how you want expensive clothes cared for, or keep them for the dry cleaner. Lahore's hard water also leaves white marks on dark fabrics and scale in washing machines, so a maid who knows to use the right detergent, rinse properly and clean the machine filter regularly saves both clothes and appliances. In wedding season, when several outfits need pressing at once, a maid who plans ahead keeps the household calm.

## Working while the family is out

When both parents work, the maid is often alone in the house. We shortlist maids whose references say they could be trusted unsupervised. Keep valuables locked, come home unexpectedly once or twice early on, and agree a simple routine such as a message on arrival and departure. Most verified maids settle into this quickly, and after a few months many Lahore families trust them with keys and full responsibility for the house during the day.

## How much one maid can handle

One full-time maid can usually keep a three to four bedroom house clean and manage laundry for four or five people. Larger houses, small children or frequent guests usually need extra help. Overloading one maid leads to falling standards and resignations. In Lahore's sociable homes, where weekends often bring a houseful of relatives, it is worth thinking about the busiest days of your week rather than an average day. If Saturdays and Sundays are much heavier, a part-time helper on weekends may be all you need to keep the arrangement fair.

## Smog season and indoor air

From November, Lahore's smog settles on the city, and many families keep windows closed and run air purifiers, especially if children or elderly parents have asthma. This changes the cleaning routine. Dusting needs to be done with damp cloths so particles are trapped rather than spread, air purifier filters need regular cleaning, and floors near doors need extra mopping because grime is tracked in from outside. A maid who understands this helps keep indoor air cleaner during the worst weeks of the year.

## Protecting your home and your maid

Keep valuables locked, keep copies of the maid's CNIC and our verification summary, and register live-in staff with your society or local police where required. DHA, Bahria Town and most gated societies in Lahore require registration, and doing it in the first week saves trouble at the gate later.

## Keeping a good maid

Pay on time, respect the day off, speak politely and keep duties clear. Proper meals, a fan or cooler in summer and new clothes at Eid build loyalty. Lahore's summer heat is hard on live-in staff, so a ventilated servant quarter with a fan or air cooler is not a luxury but a basic need. Maids who feel respected stay for years.
