## House maid Multan: what the job covers

A house maid in Multan usually handles sweeping, mopping and dusting, cleaning bathrooms and the kitchen, washing, drying, folding and ironing, changing bed linen, organising cupboards, and weekly cleaning of fans, air coolers, windows, doors and the roof or courtyard. In summer, dusting often needs doing twice a day, and air cooler pads and fan blades collect dust quickly.

Many families also want help keeping guest rooms ready, because Multani hospitality means visitors are frequent. Seasonal work includes washing curtains after dust storms, airing quilts before winter and deep cleaning before Ramadan and Eid.

If cooking or caring for elderly parents is part of the job, agree it at the start. A [cook](/services/cooks/multan) or [caretaker](/services/caretakers/multan) alongside the maid often works better than adding duties gradually.

## Dust storms and summer cleaning

Dust is the defining challenge for Multan maids. After a storm, every surface needs wiping, curtains may need washing and floors need mopping more than once. A good maid closes windows when a storm is coming, uses damp cloths so dust is trapped rather than spread, and cleans air cooler pads and fan blades regularly. During interviews we ask maids how they handle dust and heat, and we prefer those with experience in South Punjab homes.

## Looking after the maid in summer

Multan's summer is hard on everyone, including household staff. Live-in maids need a room with a fan or air cooler, cold drinking water and rest during the hottest hours. Heavy tasks like mopping and laundry are best done early in the morning. A maid who is looked after in June will still be with you in December. Families who ignore this often find their maid leaving at the first chance.

## Joint family homes

In joint families, the maid may take instructions from several women. Agree who she reports to and how duties are divided. For very large homes, a maid plus a helper keeps the workload fair. Laundry, floors and guest rooms for ten or twelve people are simply too much for one person in Multan's heat, and a second pair of hands protects the maid you already have from burning out.

## Laundry in Multan's climate

Multan's heat dries laundry quickly in summer, which helps, but dust can settle on clothes hanging outside, and strong sun fades colours. A good maid hangs clothes in shade or brings them in as soon as they are dry, shakes out dust before folding and irons promptly. In winter, heavy shawls, blankets and quilts need careful washing and long drying. Formal and embroidered clothes for weddings deserve special care, so show the maid how you want them handled, or keep them for the dry cleaner.

## Guests and hospitality

Multani families are known for their hospitality. Relatives and visitors come often, and guest rooms must always be ready. After a gathering there are dishes, floors and bathrooms to clean, and sometimes bedding to change for overnight guests. A maid who has worked in sociable homes understands this rhythm and keeps the house presentable without being asked. If your home hosts often, mention it in your brief so we can match the right maid, and consider extra help on the busiest days.

## Working alongside other staff

Many Multan homes also have a cook, driver, guard or gardener. Agree who cleans the kitchen, who handles the courtyard and who serves guests, so the maid is not left with everyone else's leftover work. A short conversation with all staff in the first week prevents most misunderstandings.

## Female staff and comfort

## How much one maid can handle

One full-time maid can usually keep a three to four bedroom house clean and manage laundry for four or five people. Larger houses, joint families or frequent guests usually need extra help.

## Protecting your home and your maid

## Eid, harvest and leave
