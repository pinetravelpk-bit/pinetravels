## House maid Peshawar: what the job covers

A house maid in Peshawar usually handles sweeping, mopping and dusting, cleaning bathrooms and the kitchen, washing, drying, folding and ironing, changing bed linen, organising cupboards, and weekly cleaning of fans, windows, doors, the roof and courtyard. In many homes she also keeps the women's sitting areas and guest rooms ready, helps serve female guests and tidies after gatherings.

Winter work is significant in Peshawar: heavy quilts, blankets and woollens need washing and airing, rooms need extra care when gas heaters are in use, and floors get muddy in the rainy months. Before Ramadan, Eid and weddings, the whole house is deep cleaned.

If cooking or childcare is part of the job, agree it at the start. A [cook](/services/cooks/peshawar) or [nanny](/services/babysitters-nannies/peshawar) alongside the maid often works better than adding duties gradually.

## Purdah, privacy and household customs

Many Peshawar households have customs that a maid must respect: separate areas for women and male guests, rules about when men of the household are present, and expectations about modest dress and conduct. We discuss these openly with both the family and the maid before placement. Female maids, in turn, want to know who lives in the house, and we share that honestly so everyone is comfortable from the first day.

## Winter work and gas heaters

Peshawar winters are cold, and gas heaters are common. A maid should know to keep rooms ventilated, never leave heaters burning in closed rooms overnight, and report any smell of gas immediately. Winter also brings heavy laundry: quilts, shawls and woollens that need careful washing and long drying. On sunny winter days, a good maid takes quilts and mattresses out to the roof or courtyard to air them in the sun, an old Peshawar habit that keeps bedding fresh through the cold months.

## Guests and the hujra

Peshawar homes receive guests often. While male guests are usually served in the hujra by male staff or family members, the maid often helps prepare refreshments, keeps the women's guest areas ready and cleans up after gatherings. Maids experienced in Peshawar homes understand this rhythm. On busy days, such as Eid or when relatives arrive from the village, the work can double, with extra bedding to prepare, more dishes and floors that need cleaning several times. Mention in your brief how often your home hosts, so we can shortlist maids used to that pace, and consider a temporary helper for the busiest occasions.

## Laundry and fine fabrics

Peshawar families often own heavy embroidered shawls, waistcoats, fine shalwar kameez and wedding clothes that need careful handling. A good maid knows which fabrics can go in the machine and which need hand washing, how to dry woollens without stretching them, and how to iron embroidered pieces from the back. Show her how you want expensive clothes treated in the first week, or keep them for the dry cleaner. Children's school uniforms, which get dusty quickly in Peshawar, are a daily task in themselves.

## Working with other household staff

Many Peshawar homes also have a cook, a driver, a guard and sometimes a helper or chowkidar. Male and female staff often have separate duties and areas. Agree clearly who cleans the kitchen, who handles the courtyard and roof, and who serves which guests, so the maid is not left doing everyone else's work. A short conversation with all staff in the first week prevents most misunderstandings and keeps the household running smoothly.

## Maids for elderly parents

Many Peshawar families have parents at home while sons work in the Gulf or other cities. A maid who keeps the house clean, does the laundry, prepares simple meals if agreed and keeps an elderly mother company can make a real difference to daily life. Families abroad can arrange the placement remotely, with a relative involved in the interview and trial, and the maid can send short updates if the family wishes.

## How much one maid can handle

One full-time maid can usually keep a three to four bedroom house clean and manage laundry for four or five people. Joint families, large houses or frequent guests usually need extra help, such as a second maid or helper.

## Protecting your home and your maid

Keep valuables locked, keep copies of the maid's CNIC and our verification summary, and register live-in staff with your society or local police where required. KP Police operate a registration system for domestic workers and tenants.

## Eid and leave

Many maids from other districts go home for Eid. Plan leave and agree return dates in advance, and keep in touch by phone while she is away, since travel from Swat or Dir can take longer than expected. If a maid does not return, our guarantee means we start finding a replacement immediately.

## Keeping a good maid

Pay on time, respect the day off, speak politely, provide a warm room in winter and keep duties clear. Peshawar families value loyalty, and maids who feel respected often stay for many years. Help with travel home at Eid, support during a family emergency and fair treatment in front of guests build the kind of trust that makes a maid part of the household.
