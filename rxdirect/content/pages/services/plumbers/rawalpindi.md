## Pumps, motors and pressure

In most Rawalpindi homes, an electric pump lifts water from the underground tank to the overhead tank, and a pressure pump may boost flow to showers on upper floors. When a pump runs but no water arrives, the cause is usually an air lock, a worn foot valve, a leak on the suction side or a burnt capacitor. A plumber checks each in turn before replacing the motor. A float switch on the overhead tank stops overflow and saves electricity.

## Winter and geyser season

When the weather turns cold, geysers work hardest and faults show up: pilot lights that will not stay lit, thermostats that fail, rusted tanks that leak and burners clogged with soot. Book a geyser service in October or early November, before the rush, and have the gas connection checked for leaks at the same time.

## Geyser safety

Gas geysers are common in Rawalpindi homes and need care. A geyser should have a working thermostat, a pressure relief valve that is not blocked, a proper flue that takes fumes outside and a gas connection without leaks. Never install a gas geyser inside a closed bathroom. If you smell gas, notice soot around the burner or the pilot flame is yellow instead of blue, switch it off and call a plumber. Electric geysers need correct earthing and a breaker of the right size, so an electrician may need to check the connection too.

## Water tanks and hygiene

Overhead and underground tanks collect mud, rust and sometimes dead insects or birds. They should be cleaned and disinfected at least twice a year, and lids should close tightly. A plumber can clean the tank, fix float valves that let it overflow, replace leaking connections and fit a simple filter on the main line. Clean tanks mean fewer stomach problems for the family, especially children.

## What to do while you wait

For a burst pipe or major leak, close the main valve or switch off the pump, turn off the geyser, move valuables away from the water and mop up to prevent damage to floors and walls. For a blocked toilet, stop flushing. For a gas smell from a geyser, close the gas valve, open windows and do not switch on lights or appliances until a plumber has checked it.
