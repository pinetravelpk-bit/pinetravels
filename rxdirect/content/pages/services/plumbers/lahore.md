## Monsoon and flooding

Lahore's monsoon floods many streets within an hour, and sewers back up into homes, basements and ground floor bathrooms. Before July, clear roof drains, courtyard drains and the main sewer connection, and consider a non return valve on the main drain if your street floods often.

## Groundwater, sand and filters

Many Lahore homes rely on boreholes that bring up sand, silt and hard water. A sediment filter on the main line, regular cleaning of the underground tank and descaling geysers and showers once a year protect your fittings.

## Plumbing in renovations

When you renovate a bathroom or kitchen in Lahore, the pipework hidden behind the tiles matters more than the fittings you can see. Replace old GI pipes with good quality PPR while the walls are open, place valves where they can be reached, slope floor drains properly and pressure test every line before tiling. Fixing a leak after new tiles are laid means breaking them again. Our plumbers can work alongside your tile fixer and contractor so the job is done in the right order.

## Saving water at home

Water is precious across Pakistan, and Lahore is no exception. Fix dripping taps and running toilets quickly, because a single leaking flush can waste hundreds of litres a day. Fit aerators on taps, use a float switch so the overhead tank never overflows, wash cars with a bucket rather than a pipe and water gardens in the early morning. A plumber can check your whole system for silent leaks in one visit.

## Pumps, motors and pressure

In most Lahore homes, an electric pump lifts water from the underground tank to the overhead tank, and a pressure pump may boost flow to showers on upper floors. When a pump runs but no water arrives, the cause is usually an air lock, a worn foot valve, a leak on the suction side or a burnt capacitor. A plumber checks each in turn before replacing the motor. A float switch on the overhead tank stops overflow and saves electricity.

## Winter and geyser season

When the weather turns cold, geysers work hardest and faults show up: pilot lights that will not stay lit, thermostats that fail, rusted tanks that leak and burners clogged with soot. Book a geyser service in October or early November, before the rush, and have the gas connection checked for leaks at the same time.

## Geyser safety

Gas geysers are common in Lahore homes and need care. A geyser should have a working thermostat, a pressure relief valve that is not blocked, a proper flue that takes fumes outside and a gas connection without leaks. Never install a gas geyser inside a closed bathroom. If you smell gas, notice soot around the burner or the pilot flame is yellow instead of blue, switch it off and call a plumber. Electric geysers need correct earthing and a breaker of the right size, so an electrician may need to check the connection too.

## Water tanks and hygiene

Overhead and underground tanks collect mud, rust and sometimes dead insects or birds. They should be cleaned and disinfected at least twice a year, and lids should close tightly. A plumber can clean the tank, fix float valves that let it overflow, replace leaking connections and fit a simple filter on the main line. Clean tanks mean fewer stomach problems for the family, especially children.

## Warning signs of a bad plumbing job

Be careful if a plumber wants to cut walls before finding the leak, uses lots of tape and sealant instead of proper fittings, cannot explain what went wrong, insists on replacing parts that look fine, or leaves without testing. Good work is neat, tested under pressure and explained clearly. Our plumbers show you what they found and what they fixed.

## What to do while you wait

For a burst pipe or major leak, close the main valve or switch off the pump, turn off the geyser, move valuables away from the water and mop up to prevent damage to floors and walls. For a blocked toilet, stop flushing. For a gas smell from a geyser, close the gas valve, open windows and do not switch on lights or appliances until a plumber has checked it.
