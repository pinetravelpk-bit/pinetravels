## Plumbing service Pakistan: common problems and their causes

A plumbing service in Pakistan sees the same problems repeatedly:

**Low water pressure.** Often caused by a weak motor, air in the lines, a partly closed valve, rust inside old galvanised pipes or a tank placed too low. Sometimes the municipal supply itself is weak, and a pressure pump is the answer.

**Damp walls and peeling paint.** Usually a hidden leak in a pipe or joint inside the wall, or seepage from a bathroom above. Painting over it does not help. The leak must be found and fixed first.

**Blocked kitchen drains.** Grease, food scraps and tea leaves build up over time. Using a strainer and never pouring oil down the sink prevents most blockages.

**Running toilets.** A faulty flush valve can waste hundreds of litres a day. A simple repair saves water and money.

**Geyser problems.** Gas geysers need regular servicing of the burner and thermocouple. Electric geysers often fail because of hard water scale on the element.

## Geyser safety

Gas geysers cause several deaths every winter in Pakistan from carbon monoxide when installed in closed bathrooms or with blocked flues. A plumber should install gas geysers outside living spaces or in well ventilated areas, with a proper flue to the outside. Gas connections must be leak tested with soapy water, never with a flame. Have gas geysers serviced before each winter. Never sleep in a room with a gas heater running and no ventilation.

## Water tanks and hygiene

Overhead and underground tanks collect sediment, algae and sometimes insects over time. They should be cleaned every six months, more often if water comes from tankers. A plumber can check tank connections, float valves and overflow pipes during cleaning. Covered tanks with tight lids keep water cleaner and prevent mosquitoes breeding during monsoon.

## Saving water at home

Water is scarce in many Pakistani cities, and families in Karachi, Rawalpindi and parts of Lahore often depend on expensive tankers in summer. A plumber can help you waste less. A dripping tap can lose thousands of litres a year, and a running toilet even more. Fixing these is cheap. Other useful steps include fitting aerators on taps, using dual flush cisterns, insulating hot water pipes so you do not run taps waiting for warm water, and installing a float valve that stops tanks overflowing on the roof. A good plumber will point out waste they see during any visit.

## Hard water and filters

In many areas, especially parts of Lahore, Multan, Faisalabad and Karachi, the water is hard or has a high level of dissolved salts. Over time this leaves white scale on taps and showerheads, blocks geyser elements and shortens the life of washing machines. Plumbers can fit sediment filters on the main line, install softening systems and descale fittings. For drinking water, many families install a filter or RO system in the kitchen. Ask the plumber to fit it properly with a separate tap so drinking water and washing water do not mix.

## Winter plumbing in colder cities

In Islamabad, Peshawar and the hill areas near Murree, winter nights can drop below freezing. Exposed pipes on roofs and outer walls can freeze and crack, leading to leaks when they thaw. Insulating exposed pipes before winter, keeping roof tanks covered and draining unused outdoor lines prevents most problems. Geysers work hardest in these months, so servicing them in October or November is a good habit.

## Warning signs of a bad plumbing job

Some plumbing problems come from earlier repairs done badly. Watch for these signs: joints wrapped in plastic bags or rubber strips instead of proper fittings, pipes running across walls without clamps, drain pipes with no slope so water sits in them, new damp patches a few weeks after a repair, and a plumber who insists every pipe needs replacing without showing you why. If you notice these after any repair, ours or someone else's, tell us. Fixing a bad job early saves damage to walls, floors and ceilings.

## Monsoon and roof drainage

Heavy monsoon rain in Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Lahore exposes every weakness in roof drainage. Blocked roof drains cause water to pool, seep through cracks and drip into rooms below. Before monsoon, ask a plumber to clear roof outlets, check downpipes for blockages and cracks, and make sure water drains away from the foundation. In areas that flood, a non-return valve on the sewer line can stop dirty water backing up into the house.
