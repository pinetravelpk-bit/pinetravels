## Electrical technician Lahore: safety rules we follow

Every electrical technician in Lahore we send follows basic safety rules: switch off and isolate the circuit before working, test that it is dead, use the right wire gauge for the load, size breakers correctly, never bypass a breaker or RCD, earth metal appliances properly, keep wires inside conduit and boxes, and leave the work neat and labelled. ## Before summer: an electrical check

Before April, a quick check saves trouble: tighten connections in the DB box, check AC circuits and breakers, test the UPS and batteries, inspect old sockets and plugs, check the water pump wiring and earthing, and clean dust from the DB box. Many June breakdowns are caused by loose connections heating up under load.

## Electrical work in older houses

Older houses in Model Town, Garden Town, Gulberg and the Cantt may have wiring that cannot handle modern loads, too few sockets and no earthing. A partial or full rewire, done room by room, makes the house safer. We assess first and quote clearly before any walls are opened.

## Cutting electricity bills safely

With LESCO tariffs rising every year, many families ask how to bring the bill down. An electrician can help by checking for faulty wiring or earth leakage that wastes power, replacing old tube lights and bulbs with LEDs, fitting inverter AC connections properly, checking that the water pump and geyser are not running longer than needed and advising on timers. Never let anyone tamper with the meter or bypass it. Besides being illegal, it is dangerous and can lead to heavy fines and disconnection.

## Electrical safety for families with children

Homes with young children need a few simple precautions: socket covers on low sockets, no loose extension boards on the floor, cables tucked away from little hands, and an RCD on the main board so the supply cuts off quickly if someone touches a live wire. Teach older children never to touch switches with wet hands and to tell an adult if a plug feels hot. An electrician can check these points in a single visit.

## Water pumps and motors

Most Lahore homes rely on an electric pump to fill the overhead tank, and low voltage in summer burns out pump motors and capacitors. An electrician can check the pump wiring, fit the right capacitor and breaker, add a stabiliser where voltage is poor and install a float switch so the pump stops automatically when the tank is full, saving both the motor and the bill.

## New appliances and AC installation

When you buy a new air conditioner, geyser, oven or washing machine, the wiring behind it matters as much as the appliance. Each AC should have its own correctly sized wire and breaker, geysers need proper earthing, and kitchen appliances should not share one overloaded socket. Ask the electrician to check the circuit before the installer arrives, so the warranty is not lost to a wiring fault.

## Signs you should call an electrician now

Call immediately and switch off the main supply if you notice a burning smell, scorch marks on sockets, sparking, a shock from an appliance or tap, or a breaker that trips again as soon as you reset it.
