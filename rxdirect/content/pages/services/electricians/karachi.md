## Salt air, humidity and corrosion

Homes near the sea in DHA, Clifton and Defence View suffer from corrosion. Terminals in the DB box turn green, breakers stick, outdoor lights and sockets fail and AC outdoor units corrode. Regular checks, tightening and cleaning connections, sealed boxes and weatherproof outdoor fittings prevent many faults. Damp walls in humid months can also cause leakage currents, so good earthing is essential.

## Flats and shared wiring

In Karachi flats, the meter and main supply are often in a shared room, wiring runs through common shafts and old buildings may have tired wiring throughout. Our electricians work within the flat and coordinate with building management where shared parts are involved.

## Heat, ACs and load shedding

Karachi's long summer keeps ACs running for months. Each AC should have its own correctly sized breaker and wiring. UPS systems should run lights, fans and the router, not the AC. Batteries should be checked before summer.

## Cutting electricity bills safely

With K-Electric tariffs rising every year, many families ask how to bring the bill down. An electrician can help by checking for faulty wiring or earth leakage that wastes power, replacing old tube lights and bulbs with LEDs, fitting inverter AC connections properly, checking that the water pump and geyser are not running longer than needed and advising on timers. Never let anyone tamper with the meter or bypass it. Besides being illegal, it is dangerous and can lead to heavy fines and disconnection.

## Electrical safety for families with children

Homes with young children need a few simple precautions: socket covers on low sockets, no loose extension boards on the floor, cables tucked away from little hands, and an RCD on the main board so the supply cuts off quickly if someone touches a live wire. Teach older children never to touch switches with wet hands and to tell an adult if a plug feels hot. An electrician can check these points in a single visit.

## Water pumps and motors

Most Karachi homes rely on an electric pump to fill the overhead tank, and low voltage in summer burns out pump motors and capacitors. An electrician can check the pump wiring, fit the right capacitor and breaker, add a stabiliser where voltage is poor and install a float switch so the pump stops automatically when the tank is full, saving both the motor and the bill.

## New appliances and AC installation

When you buy a new air conditioner, geyser, oven or washing machine, the wiring behind it matters as much as the appliance. Each AC should have its own correctly sized wire and breaker, geysers need proper earthing, and kitchen appliances should not share one overloaded socket. Ask the electrician to check the circuit before the installer arrives, so the warranty is not lost to a wiring fault.

## Generators and changeover switches

Many Karachi bungalows and shops run a generator during long outages. The changeover must be wired correctly so the generator never feeds back into the K-Electric line, which can kill a lineman, and so the generator is not overloaded. An electrician can install a proper changeover switch and a separate backup circuit.

## Signs you should call an electrician now

Call immediately and switch off the main supply if you notice a burning smell, scorch marks on sockets, sparking, a shock from an appliance or wall, or a breaker that trips again as soon as you reset it.
