## Heat, coolers and the summer load

Multan's summer is the hardest test for any wiring. Wires and connections heat up, capacitors fail, and breakers trip under load. Before April, ask an electrician to tighten every connection in the DB box, check each AC and cooler circuit, replace weak capacitors in fans and pumps, test the UPS and batteries and check earthing. Air coolers combine water and electricity, so they must be properly earthed and never plugged into loose extension boards.

## Generators and changeover switches

Many Multan homes and shops keep a generator for long outages. The changeover must be wired correctly so the generator never feeds back into the MEPCO line, which can kill a lineman, and so it is not overloaded. An electrician can install a proper changeover switch and a separate backup circuit.

## Cutting electricity bills safely

With MEPCO tariffs rising every year, many families ask how to bring the bill down. An electrician can help by checking for faulty wiring or earth leakage that wastes power, replacing old tube lights and bulbs with LEDs, fitting inverter AC connections properly, checking that the water pump and geyser are not running longer than needed and advising on timers. Never let anyone tamper with the meter or bypass it. Besides being illegal, it is dangerous and can lead to heavy fines and disconnection.

## Electrical safety for families with children

Homes with young children need a few simple precautions: socket covers on low sockets, no loose extension boards on the floor, cables tucked away from little hands, and an RCD on the main board so the supply cuts off quickly if someone touches a live wire. Teach older children never to touch switches with wet hands and to tell an adult if a plug feels hot. An electrician can check these points in a single visit.

## Water pumps and motors

Most Multan homes rely on an electric pump to fill the overhead tank, and low voltage in summer burns out pump motors and capacitors. An electrician can check the pump wiring, fit the right capacitor and breaker, add a stabiliser where voltage is poor and install a float switch so the pump stops automatically when the tank is full, saving both the motor and the bill.

## New appliances and AC installation

When you buy a new air conditioner, geyser, oven or washing machine, the wiring behind it matters as much as the appliance. Each AC should have its own correctly sized wire and breaker, geysers need proper earthing, and kitchen appliances should not share one overloaded socket. Ask the electrician to check the circuit before the installer arrives, so the warranty is not lost to a wiring fault.

## Signs you should call an electrician now

Call immediately and switch off the main supply if you notice a burning smell, scorch marks on sockets, sparking, a shock from an appliance, cooler or tap, or a breaker that trips again as soon as you reset it.
