## Before summer: an electrical check

Islamabad's summer brings the heaviest load of the year. Before May, a quick check saves trouble: tighten connections in the DB box, check AC circuits and breakers, test the UPS and batteries, inspect old sockets and plugs, check earthing and clean dust from the DB box. ## Winter heaters and geysers

In winter, electric heaters, oil radiators and electric geysers add heavy load. Make sure heaters are plugged into sockets rated for them, not into extension boards, and that geyser circuits have the right breaker and earthing. A heater cable that feels hot or a plug that is discoloured is a warning sign.

## Monsoon storms and lightning

Islamabad's monsoon brings thunderstorms that cause power surges and sometimes lightning damage. Surge protection for sensitive appliances, good earthing and switching off expensive electronics during severe storms help. After a storm, if part of the house has no power or a breaker will not reset, call an electrician rather than forcing it.

Older houses in the F and G sectors may still have wiring that cannot handle modern loads, few sockets per room and no earthing. A full or partial rewire, done room by room, makes the house safer and more convenient. We assess first and quote clearly before any walls are opened.

## Cutting electricity bills safely

With IESCO tariffs rising every year, many families ask how to bring the bill down. An electrician can help by checking for faulty wiring or earth leakage that wastes power, replacing old tube lights and bulbs with LEDs, fitting inverter AC connections properly, checking that the water pump and geyser are not running longer than needed and advising on timers. Never let anyone tamper with the meter or bypass it. Besides being illegal, it is dangerous and can lead to heavy fines and disconnection.

## Electrical safety for families with children

Homes with young children need a few simple precautions: socket covers on low sockets, no loose extension boards on the floor, cables tucked away from little hands, and an RCD on the main board so the supply cuts off quickly if someone touches a live wire. Teach older children never to touch switches with wet hands and to tell an adult if a plug feels hot. An electrician can check these points in a single visit.

## Signs you should call an electrician now

Call immediately and switch off the main supply if you notice a burning smell, scorch marks on sockets, sparking, a shock from an appliance or tap, or a breaker that trips again as soon as you reset it.
