## Cutting electricity bills safely

With GEPCO tariffs rising every year, many families ask how to bring the bill down. An electrician can help by checking for faulty wiring or earth leakage that wastes power, replacing old tube lights and bulbs with LEDs, fitting inverter AC connections properly, checking that the water pump and geyser are not running longer than needed and advising on timers. Never let anyone tamper with the meter or bypass it. Besides being illegal, it is dangerous and can lead to heavy fines and disconnection.

## Electrical safety for families with children

Homes with young children need a few simple precautions: socket covers on low sockets, no loose extension boards on the floor, cables tucked away from little hands, and an RCD on the main board so the supply cuts off quickly if someone touches a live wire. Teach older children never to touch switches with wet hands and to tell an adult if a plug feels hot. An electrician can check these points in a single visit.

## Water pumps and motors

Most Gujranwala homes rely on an electric pump to fill the overhead tank, and low voltage in summer burns out pump motors and capacitors. An electrician can check the pump wiring, fit the right capacitor and breaker, add a stabiliser where voltage is poor and install a float switch so the pump stops automatically when the tank is full, saving both the motor and the bill.

## New appliances and AC installation

When you buy a new air conditioner, geyser, oven or washing machine, the wiring behind it matters as much as the appliance. Each AC should have its own correctly sized wire and breaker, geysers need proper earthing, and kitchen appliances should not share one overloaded socket. Ask the electrician to check the circuit before the installer arrives, so the warranty is not lost to a wiring fault.

## Signs you should call an electrician now

Call immediately and switch off the main supply if you notice a burning smell, scorch marks on sockets, sparking, a shock from an appliance or tap, or a breaker that trips again as soon as you reset it.

## What to prepare before the electrician arrives

Clear access to the DB box and the problem area, note which appliances were running when the fault happened, keep children and pets away and have any spare parts or fittings you have bought ready. ## Electrical work in older houses

Older houses may have wiring that cannot handle modern loads, too few sockets per room and no earthing. A partial or full rewire, done room by room, makes the house safer and more convenient. We assess first and quote clearly before any walls are opened.

## Monsoon storms

Monsoon storms bring surges and lightning. If water has entered the house, do not switch anything on until an electrician has checked the wiring, and keep outdoor sockets and lights in weatherproof fittings.
