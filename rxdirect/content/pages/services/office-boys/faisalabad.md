## Banking, LCs and courier errands

Errands are a big part of the job in Faisalabad's business offices. An office boy takes cheques and documents to banks, pays bills, drops samples and documents at courier offices and collects supplies. Good practice protects both the office and the office boy: a register of every errand, receipts for every payment, deposit slips for every bank visit and no large cash amounts carried without a reason. Start new staff with small errands and build trust over the first few months.

## Office boy, cleaner or receptionist?

A cleaner focuses on floors, washrooms and dusting, usually before or after working hours. A receptionist answers calls and manages visitors. An office boy covers the pantry, errands, photocopies, courier and general support, with light cleaning in smaller offices. Small offices often manage with one capable office boy, while larger offices and mill admin blocks do better with a separate cleaner.

## Pantry hygiene

Ask the office boy to wash cups with hot water and soap, wipe counters daily, keep milk and food in the fridge, throw out anything stale and empty bins every evening. Make sure the kettle and any gas stove are switched off before locking up.

## Heat, dust and fog

Faisalabad summers are long and hot, and motorcycle errands in the afternoon heat are exhausting. Provide water and avoid the hottest hours when you can. Dust storms can stop errands for an hour or two. In winter, fog on the roads around the city makes early morning and late evening trips risky, so plan errands for daylight hours.

## Confidential documents

Export houses, accountants and law firms handle confidential pricing, buyer and financial information. Tell your office boy clearly what must stay confidential, never leave sensitive documents at the photocopier and use sealed envelopes for anything sensitive.

## Working hours and overtime

Most Faisalabad offices work from 9am to 6pm, six days a week, while export houses often stay late around shipment deadlines. Agree hours, the weekly day off and overtime pay or time off in lieu at the start. Ramadan timings usually shorten the day.

## Training a new office boy

Spend the first week showing the new office boy how your office works: who takes tea how, where supplies are kept, which bank branch and courier office to use, how to log errands and who to ask when unsure. Correct calmly and praise good work. Many office boys grow into dispatch staff, store keepers or junior admin staff with the same company.

## Mill admin blocks and shift work

Mills and processing units in Faisalabad often run two or three shifts, and their admin blocks stay busy from early morning to late evening. An office boy in a mill office serves tea to managers and visiting buyers, carries files between the admin block, the production floor and the stores, and handles errands into the city. Agree clearly which shift the office boy covers, whether the company provides transport and meals, and who gives instructions, since several managers often want help at the same time. A simple daily task list avoids confusion and keeps the office boy from being pulled in every direction.
