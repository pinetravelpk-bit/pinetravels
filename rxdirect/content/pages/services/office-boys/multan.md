## Heat and summer errands

Multan's summer is one of the hottest in Pakistan, with temperatures above 45 degrees for weeks. An office boy riding a motorcycle to banks and suppliers in the afternoon heat is at real risk of heatstroke. Plan errands for the morning, provide cold water and a cool place to rest, and allow short breaks after long trips. Dust storms can stop errands suddenly, so keep documents in plastic folders.

## Banking and errands

An office boy in Multan deposits cheques at bank branches on Abdali Road and in the Cantt, pays bills, collects payments from dealers and drops documents at courier offices. Good practice protects both the office and the office boy: a register of every errand, receipts for every payment, deposit slips for every bank visit and no large cash amounts carried without a reason. ## Office boy, cleaner or receptionist?

A cleaner focuses on floors, washrooms and dusting, usually before or after working hours. A receptionist answers calls and manages visitors. An office boy covers the pantry, errands, photocopies, courier and general support, with light cleaning in smaller offices. Small offices often manage with one capable office boy, while larger offices do better with a separate cleaner.

## Pantry hygiene

Heat spoils milk and food quickly. Ask the office boy to keep milk in the fridge, wash cups with hot water and soap, wipe counters daily, throw out anything stale and empty bins every evening. Make sure the kettle and any gas stove are switched off before locking up.
