## Banking, bills and government offices

Errands are a big part of the job in Islamabad. An office boy deposits cheques at bank branches in Blue Area and Jinnah Super, pays bills, submits documents at government offices and courier counters, and collects letters and parcels. Good practice protects both the office and the office boy: a register of every errand, receipts for every payment, deposit slips for every bank visit and no large cash amounts carried without a reason. For new staff, start with small errands and build up trust over the first few months.

## Training a new office boy

Spend the first week showing the new office boy how your office works: who takes tea how, where supplies are kept, which bank branch and courier counter to use, how to log errands and who to ask when unsure. A simple written list of daily tasks helps. Correct calmly and praise good work. Many office boys grow into dispatch officers, store keepers or junior admin staff with the same company.

## Office boy, cleaner or receptionist?

Offices sometimes ask which role they really need. A **cleaner** focuses on floors, washrooms and dusting, usually before or after working hours. A **receptionist** answers calls, manages appointments and greets visitors, and needs good communication skills. An **office boy** sits between the two: pantry, tea, errands, photocopies, courier and general support, with light cleaning in smaller offices. Small offices with three to ten people often manage with one capable office boy. Larger offices usually do better with a separate cleaner, so the office boy is free for errands and meetings. Describe your office and we will suggest the right mix.

## Pantry hygiene and safety

The pantry is where most office illness starts. Ask the office boy to wash cups with hot water and soap rather than a quick rinse, wipe counters daily, keep milk and food in the fridge, throw out anything stale and empty the bins every evening. Check that the kettle, microwave and gas stove, if there is one, are in good condition, and make sure the office boy switches everything off before locking up.
