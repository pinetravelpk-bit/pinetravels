## Banking, pay orders and port errands

Errands are a big part of the job in Karachi. An office boy deposits cheques and collects pay orders on I.I. Chundrigar Road, pays bills, carries documents to clearing agents and shipping offices and drops parcels at courier counters. Good practice protects both the office and the office boy: a register of every errand, receipts for every payment, deposit slips for every bank visit and no large cash amounts carried without a reason. Mobile snatching is common in parts of Karachi, so ask the office boy to keep valuables out of sight and never resist a robbery.

## Meetings and visitors

Karachi offices host clients, auditors, buyers and suppliers all day. A good office boy offers water as soon as a guest sits down, serves tea or coffee promptly, keeps the board room ready and stays out of the room unless called. They never discuss what they overhear.

## Office boy, cleaner or receptionist?

A cleaner focuses on floors, washrooms and dusting, usually before or after working hours. A receptionist answers calls and manages visitors. An office boy covers the pantry, errands, photocopies, courier and general support, with light cleaning in smaller offices. Small teams often manage with one capable office boy, while larger offices do better with a separate cleaner.

## Pantry hygiene in the heat

Karachi's heat and humidity spoil milk and food quickly. Ask the office boy to keep milk in the fridge, wash cups with hot water and soap, wipe counters daily, throw out anything stale and empty bins every evening so they do not attract cockroaches. Switch off the kettle, microwave and any gas stove before locking up.

## Heat, monsoon and strike days

Karachi summers are hot and humid, and heatwaves can be dangerous for anyone riding a motorcycle around the city. Provide water, allow breaks and avoid sending the office boy out in the hottest hours. In monsoon, roads flood quickly, so plan errands around the forecast. On strike days, check whether travel is safe before sending anyone out.

Most Karachi offices run from 9am to 6pm, five or six days a week, with banks and trading firms often working late around month end. Agree hours, the weekly day off and overtime pay or time off in lieu at the start. Ramadan timings usually shorten the day.
