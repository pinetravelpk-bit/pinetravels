## Banking, bills and courier runs

Errands in Lahore take time. An office boy deposits cheques at bank branches on Main Boulevard and in DHA, pays bills, collects documents from suppliers and clients and drops parcels at courier offices. Good practice protects both the office and the office boy: a register of every errand, receipts for every payment, deposit slips for every bank visit and no large cash amounts carried without a reason. Start new staff with small errands and build trust over the first few months.

In Lahore, a visitor who is not offered tea notices. A good office boy offers water as soon as a guest sits down, serves tea or coffee the way your team likes it, keeps biscuits and snacks stocked and clears cups promptly. For client meetings, the meeting room should be ready before the client arrives. We brief office boys on these basics before placement.

## Office boy, cleaner or receptionist?

Offices sometimes ask which role they need. A cleaner focuses on floors, washrooms and dusting, usually before or after working hours. A receptionist answers calls and manages visitors. An office boy covers the pantry, errands, photocopies, courier and general support, with light cleaning in smaller offices. Small teams of three to ten people often manage with one capable office boy. Larger offices do better with a separate cleaner, so the office boy is free for errands and meetings.

## Pantry hygiene

Ask the office boy to wash cups with hot water and soap, wipe counters daily, keep milk and food in the fridge, throw out anything stale and empty bins every evening. Make sure the kettle, microwave and any gas stove are switched off before locking up.

## Smog, heat and monsoon

Lahore's seasons affect errands. In summer, temperatures above 45 degrees make long motorcycle runs exhausting, so provide water and avoid the hottest hours when you can. In monsoon, roads flood quickly, so keep a raincoat and plastic folders at the office. In smog season, provide a mask and avoid unnecessary trips on the worst days.

## Confidential documents

Law firms, accountants and HR teams handle confidential information. Tell your office boy clearly what must stay confidential, never leave sensitive documents at the photocopier and use sealed envelopes for anything sensitive.
