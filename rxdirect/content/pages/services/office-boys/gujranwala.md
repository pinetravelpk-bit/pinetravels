## Banking, payments and courier errands

Errands are a big part of the job. An office boy deposits cheques at bank branches on GT Road and in Civil Lines, pays bills, collects payments from local dealers and drops samples and documents at courier offices. Good practice protects both the business and the office boy: a register of every errand, receipts for every payment, deposit slips for every bank visit and no large cash amounts carried without a reason. Start new staff with small errands and build trust over the first few months.

## Stock and samples

In showrooms and factory offices, office boys handle stock, samples and small dispatches. Agree a simple record of what goes out and comes back, check it weekly and never leave a new office boy alone with the stock room keys in the first weeks. A clear system protects an honest office boy from suspicion.

## Office boy, cleaner or receptionist?

A cleaner focuses on floors, washrooms and dusting, usually before or after working hours. A receptionist answers calls and manages visitors. An office boy covers the pantry, errands, photocopies, courier and general support, with light cleaning in smaller offices. Small offices and showrooms often manage with one capable office boy, while larger factory offices do better with a separate cleaner.

## Pantry hygiene

Ask the office boy to wash cups with hot water and soap, wipe counters daily, keep milk and food in the fridge, throw out anything stale and empty bins every evening. Make sure the kettle and any gas stove are switched off before locking up.

## GT Road traffic, heat and fog

GT Road traffic through Gujranwala is heavy all day, and errands can take longer than expected. In summer, provide water and avoid the hottest hours. In winter, thick fog on GT Road makes early morning and evening trips dangerous, so plan errands for daylight.

## Working hours and overtime

Most Gujranwala offices and showrooms work six days a week, often from 9am or 10am to 6pm or later. Agree hours, the weekly day off and overtime pay or time off in lieu at the start. Ramadan timings usually change the day.

## Training a new office boy

Spend the first week showing the new office boy how your business works: who takes tea how, where supplies and samples are kept, which bank branch and courier office to use, how to log errands and who to ask when unsure. Correct calmly and praise good work. Many office boys grow into salesmen, store keepers or dispatch staff with the same company.

## Remittance, travel and family offices

Gujranwala has many travel agents, money exchangers and offices that serve families with relatives abroad. Office boys in these businesses handle documents, passports, photocopies and customer tea, and must be especially careful with identity papers and cash. Agree clear rules: documents never leave the office without being logged, passports are handed back only to the owner or an authorised person, and cash is counted in front of a second staff member. These simple steps protect customers, the business and the office boy.
