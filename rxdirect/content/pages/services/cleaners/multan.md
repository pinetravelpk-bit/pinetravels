## Dust storms and summer cleaning

Multan's summer can stretch from April to October, with temperatures above 45 degrees and dust storms that arrive without much warning. When the wind picks up, windows and doors need closing fast. Afterwards, every surface, windowsill and fan blade needs wiping, and courtyards, roofs, porches and cars need washing. A cleaner who dusts with damp cloths so dust is trapped rather than spread, cleans from top to bottom and pays attention to window channels keeps the house livable. Outdoor sweeping and washing should be done early in the morning, before the heat becomes dangerous, and the cleaner should have cold water and a cool place to rest.

## Air coolers and fans

Many Multan homes run air coolers through the summer, and coolers collect dust, moss and stale water quickly. Cooler pads need cleaning or changing, water tanks need draining and scrubbing so they do not smell or breed mosquitoes, and fan blades need wiping weekly. A cleaner who handles these jobs keeps the air in the house fresher and helps the family avoid dengue risk from standing water.

## Mango season

In mango season, kitchens and courtyards fill with crates, peels and sticky juice that attracts flies and ants. Floors and counters need wiping more often, bins need emptying daily and courtyards need washing. If your family sends mangoes to relatives, the packing area also needs cleaning up afterwards.

## Monsoon and fog

Monsoon rain can flood streets in low lying parts of Multan and bring humidity into homes. Entrances and stairs need frequent mopping, roof drains need clearing and bathrooms need checking for mould. In winter, fog and smog settle over southern Punjab, leaving a grey film on glass and windowsills. Damp dusting and more frequent mopping near entrances help keep the air cleaner indoors.

## Pre-Eid and wedding deep cleaning

Before Eid and weddings, Multan families want the house spotless: walls washed, fans and lights cleaned, curtains taken down, cupboards emptied and wiped, courtyards and floors scrubbed. A deep clean team can do in a day what one person would take a week to finish. Book early, because the weeks before Eid and the winter wedding season are the busiest times. A lighter clean after the functions restores order once guests leave.

## When nobody is home

If the cleaner will work while you are out, decide on keys, lock private rooms and keep valuables away. A WhatsApp message on arrival and departure builds confidence. Be home for part of the first few sessions so you can give feedback and see how the cleaner works.

## Homes with children, elderly parents or pets

Use mild products where babies crawl and lock cleaning chemicals away. Mop when elderly parents are resting so they do not slip, and leave bathrooms dry. Mention pets in your brief, since pet hair and paw marks add to the work.
