## Dust storms and summer cleaning

Faisalabad summers are long, and dust storms can turn the sky brown within minutes. Afterwards, every windowsill, fan blade and surface needs wiping, and courtyards, roofs and cars need washing. A cleaner who closes windows quickly when the wind picks up, dusts with damp cloths so dust is trapped rather than spread, and cleans from top to bottom keeps the house livable through the hottest months. In peak summer, ask the cleaner to do outdoor work early in the morning.

## Fog and smog season

From November to January, fog and smog settle over Faisalabad and the surrounding plains. Families keep windows closed and many run air purifiers. Cleaners should wipe the grey film that builds up on glass, clean purifier filters regularly, mop near entrances more often and use damp dusting so fine particles do not float around the house. For families with asthma or elderly parents, this extra care makes a real difference.

## Monsoon and damp

Monsoon rain floods low lying streets in parts of the city and brings humidity everywhere. After heavy rain, porches and garages may need mud cleared.
