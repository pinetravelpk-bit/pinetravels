## Domestic cleaner Peshawar: supplies and safety

Most domestic cleaners in Peshawar use supplies provided by the family. Keep a basic kit: mop and bucket, brooms, microfibre cloths, glass cleaner, floor cleaner, bathroom cleaner, a fan brush, a carpet brush and gloves. Marble needs pH neutral cleaners. ## Carpets and rugs

Peshawar homes often have heavy carpets and handmade rugs, some of them valuable family pieces. Carpets need regular vacuuming or brushing, rugs should be turned now and then so they wear evenly, and spills should be blotted rather than rubbed. Once or twice a year, rugs benefit from airing in the sun on a dry winter day. For valuable or antique rugs, ask the cleaner not to use strong chemicals, and arrange professional washing when needed.

## Winter cleaning

Peshawar winters are cold, and gas heaters run for months. Heaters leave a film on walls, ceilings and glass, and closed windows trap dust and smells. Cleaners should wipe walls and windows regularly, air rooms on sunny days, dust heaters only when they are off and cool, and keep entrances mopped during the rainy months. Always ensure rooms with gas heaters are ventilated.

## Summer dust and heat

Peshawar summers are hot and dusty. Dust storms coat every surface, and courtyards, roofs and porches need sweeping daily. Damp dusting traps dust rather than spreading it. Outdoor work is best done early in the morning, and cleaners should have cold water and a cool place to rest.
