## Dust storms and summer cleaning

When a dust storm hits Lahore, windows need closing fast, and afterwards every surface, windowsill and fan blade needs wiping. Outdoor areas, balconies and cars need washing. A cleaner who knows to dust with damp cloths, clean from top to bottom and pay attention to window channels keeps the house livable through the dustiest months.

## Monsoon and damp

Lahore's monsoon brings sudden flooding in low lying areas and humidity everywhere. Entrances and stairs need frequent mopping, bathrooms need checking for mould, and cupboards need airing. After heavy rain, some homes need mud cleared from porches and garages.

## Smog season cleaning

From November to January, smog changes how homes should be cleaned. Families keep windows closed, run air purifiers and worry about children and elderly parents with asthma. Cleaners should dust with damp cloths so particles are trapped rather than spread, clean purifier filters regularly, mop near entrances more often and wipe the grey film that builds up on windowsills and glass.

## Pre-Eid and wedding deep cleaning

Before Eid and weddings, Lahore families want the house spotless. A deep clean team washes walls, cleans fans and lights, empties and wipes cupboards and scrubs floors and grout. Book early in the last weeks before Eid and in wedding season.

## Homes with children, elderly parents or pets

Cleaning around vulnerable family members needs extra care. Floors where babies crawl should be cleaned with mild products and rinsed properly, and cleaning chemicals must be locked away. Wet floors are a fall risk for elderly parents, so mopping should happen when they are resting and bathrooms should be left dry. Homes with pets need regular cleaning of hair and paw marks, and some cleaners are nervous around dogs, so mention pets in your brief.

## Keeping a good cleaner

Pay on time, provide proper supplies, keep the workload realistic and give specific, polite feedback. Cleaners stay with families who respect them. A cup of tea during a long shift, a fair workload for the hours agreed and a word of thanks when the house looks good go a long way. Lahore is a big city, but cleaners talk to each other, and households known for treating staff well find reliable people far more easily.

## Marble, tiles and hard water

Many Lahore homes have marble or porcelain floors, and the water in several parts of the city leaves white marks on taps, shower glass and bathroom tiles. A cleaner who knows how to deal with this saves you money on repairs. Taps and shower screens need a mild descaler once a week, used carefully and rinsed well. Marble should never see vinegar, lemon or strong acid cleaners, because they dull and etch the surface. Spills of tea, ghee or juice should be wiped at once. Grout lines in bathrooms and kitchens need scrubbing every few weeks so they do not turn dark. If you share which surfaces your home has, we will shortlist cleaners who have handled them before.
