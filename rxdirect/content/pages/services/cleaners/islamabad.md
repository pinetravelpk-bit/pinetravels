## Domestic cleaner Islamabad: supplies and safety

Most domestic cleaners in Islamabad use supplies provided by the family. Keep a basic kit ready: a good mop and bucket, brooms, microfibre cloths, glass cleaner, floor cleaner, bathroom cleaner, a dusting brush for fans and gloves. Marble needs pH neutral products; acidic cleaners etch the surface. We brief cleaners never to mix bleach with acid based cleaners and to keep windows open with strong chemicals.

## Seasonal cleaning in Islamabad

Islamabad's seasons each bring their own cleaning work. In spring, pollen from the city's trees settles on windowsills, cars and outdoor furniture, and families with allergies need more frequent dusting. In summer, dust storms occasionally sweep in from the plains and coat everything in a fine layer. Monsoon from July to September brings mud to entrances, damp in corners and the risk of mould in bathrooms and cupboards. In winter, gas heaters and closed windows leave a film on glass and walls, and smog from Rawalpindi occasionally drifts in. A good cleaner adjusts the routine through the year without needing to be told, giving more attention to windows in spring, entrances in monsoon and ventilation and mould checks in the damp months.

## When nobody is home

Many Islamabad families want the cleaner to come while they are at work. Decide who will let the cleaner in, or whether a key will be given, and keep a record. Lock rooms or cupboards you want private and keep valuables away. A quick WhatsApp message on arrival and departure gives everyone confidence. In the first weeks, try to be home for part of the cleaning time so you can see how the cleaner works and give feedback. Once trust is built, unsupervised cleaning usually works smoothly with a verified cleaner.

## Homes with children, elderly parents or pets

Cleaning around vulnerable family members needs extra care. Floors where babies crawl should be cleaned with mild products and rinsed properly. Wet floors are a fall risk for elderly parents, so mopping should happen when they are resting, and bathrooms should be left dry. Homes with pets need regular cleaning of hair and paw marks. Mention these in your brief, and we will shortlist cleaners who are comfortable with them.

## Large houses, glass and marble

Islamabad's newer homes in DHA, Bahria Town and the upmarket sectors often have large glass windows, glass railings, double height lounges and acres of marble or porcelain tile. These look beautiful when clean and terrible when streaked. Cleaning glass well means using the right cleaner and a squeegee or microfibre cloth, working top to bottom, and cleaning on a cloudy day or in shade so it does not streak. Marble needs pH neutral cleaners, never vinegar or lemon, and spills of tea or juice wiped quickly to avoid stains. High windows and chandeliers may need a stable ladder and two people. When you share your brief, mention these features so we can shortlist cleaners who have handled similar homes.

## Keeping a good cleaner

Pay on time, provide proper supplies, keep the workload realistic for the hours agreed, and give specific, polite feedback. Cleaners stay with families who respect them. A cup of tea during a long shift and a word of thanks for a job well done cost nothing and are always noticed.
