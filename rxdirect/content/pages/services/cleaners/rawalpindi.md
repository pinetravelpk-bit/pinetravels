## Seasonal cleaning

Summer dust, monsoon mud, autumn leaves in courtyards and winter grime from heaters all change what a cleaner needs to focus on. A good cleaner adjusts through the year without constant reminders. In summer, when dust storms roll in from the plains, windows need closing early and surfaces need wiping more than once a day. In monsoon, entrances, stairs and courtyards need extra attention, and bathrooms and cupboards need checking for damp and mould. In autumn, leaves from Rawalpindi's old trees pile up in courtyards and roof drains. In winter, gas heaters and closed windows leave a film on glass and walls, and sunny days are perfect for airing out rooms. A cleaner who understands these patterns keeps the house comfortable all year.

## When nobody is home

If the cleaner will work while you are out, decide on keys, lock private rooms and keep valuables away. A message on arrival and departure builds confidence. Be home for part of the first few sessions so you can give feedback and see how the cleaner works. Many Rawalpindi families also have a camera at the entrance; if you do, tell the cleaner openly. Honest cleaners rarely mind, and it protects them as much as you. After a few weeks of good work, most families are comfortable leaving the cleaner to work on their own.

## Homes with children, elderly parents or pets

Use mild products where babies crawl, mop when elderly parents are resting so they do not slip, and leave bathrooms dry. Mention pets in your brief. Some cleaners are nervous around dogs, and pet hair and paw marks add to the work, so it is fairer to everyone to know in advance. For homes with asthma or allergies, ask the cleaner to use damp dusting rather than dry brushes, so dust is trapped instead of spread through the air.

## After construction and renovation

With constant building in Bahria Town and DHA, post-construction cleaning is in demand. It needs a team working from ceilings to floors to remove cement dust and paint. Share photos and size for a quote. Post-construction cleaning usually takes a team one to three days, depending on the size of the house and how much paint and cement has been left behind. It is worth doing properly before furniture moves in, because fine cement dust settles into carpets, sofas and cupboards and keeps reappearing for weeks if it is not removed at the start.
