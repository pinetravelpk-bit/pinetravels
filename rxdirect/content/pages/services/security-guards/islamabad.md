## Home security guard Islamabad: the job at a residence

A home security guard in Islamabad usually controls the main gate and checks every visitor, keeps a visitor register with names, times and purpose, receives deliveries and checks them before they enter, opens the gate for family cars and stops unfamiliar vehicles, patrols the boundary at night, switches outdoor lights on and off, checks locks and calls the family or police if anything is suspicious. In homes with several staff, the guard also notes when the maid, driver and cook arrive and leave.

Some families ask guards to water plants or wash the car during quiet daytime hours. Small extra tasks are fine if agreed, but the guard's first job is security, and they should never leave the gate unattended.

## Police registration in Islamabad

Islamabad Police ask residents to register domestic employees, including guards, with the local police station or through the online servant registration system. Registration needs the guard's CNIC, photographs, address and your details. It helps the police verify identity quickly if anything goes wrong and is a sensible step for every family. We provide the documents we have collected and guide you through it.

## Planning cover for your home

One guard on a 12 hour night shift suits most houses. Large plots, farmhouses and homes with frequent visitors may need round the clock cover, which means at least two guards on alternating shifts, plus relief cover for weekly days off. For very large properties, a third guard covering the back of the plot at night closes a gap that a single gate guard cannot watch. CCTV, good lighting and a working intercom make every guard more effective.

## Night shifts and Islamabad winters

Winter nights in Islamabad are cold, and fog occasionally reduces visibility. A night guard needs a proper shelter, a heater that is safe to use, warm clothing, a reliable torch and a charged phone. Guards should do rounds at set times, check doors and gates after midnight and stay awake. A short call from the family once or twice a night, or a WhatsApp check-in, helps keep standards high.

## Monsoon and storms

During monsoon storms, trees fall, power cuts are common and alarms sometimes fail. Guards should check boundary walls and gates after heavy rain, report fallen branches or damaged wires and keep the generator or UPS area clear and dry. A raincoat and boots are part of the basic kit.

## Guards and household staff

In Islamabad homes with a cook, maid, driver and gardener, the guard plays a quiet but important role. They note who comes and goes, make sure staff do not bring unknown people inside and watch for anything carried out of the house. Agree in advance whether staff bags are checked and who the guard reports concerns to, so this happens politely and without friction.

## Training and conduct at the gate

A good guard is polite to genuine visitors and firm with everyone else. They ask for names, call the family before opening the gate, never let a stranger in because someone claims to know the family, check delivery packages and refuse to leave the gate unattended. They keep the visitor register neat and the gate area clean. We brief guards on these basics, and you should repeat your own rules in the first week.

## Keeping a good guard

Pay on time, provide a proper shelter, meals or a meal allowance as agreed, a weekly day off with relief cover and respect. Guards who are treated as part of the household's safety, not as a fixture at the gate, stay longer and stay alert.
