## Home security guard Rawalpindi: the job at a residence

A home security guard in Rawalpindi usually controls the main gate and checks every visitor, keeps a visitor register with names, times and purpose, receives deliveries and checks them before they enter, opens the gate for family cars and stops unfamiliar vehicles, patrols the boundary at night, switches outdoor lights on and off, checks locks and calls the family or police if anything is suspicious. In homes with several staff, the guard also notes when staff arrive and leave.

Some families ask guards to water plants or wash the car during the day. Small extra tasks are fine if agreed, but the guard's first job is security, and they should never leave the gate unattended.

## Planning cover for your home or shop

One guard on a 12 hour night shift suits most houses. Larger houses, homes with frequent visitors and properties on busy roads may need round the clock cover, which means at least two guards on alternating shifts, plus relief cover for weekly days off. Shops and plazas usually need a day guard during trading hours and a night guard after closing. CCTV, good lighting and a working intercom make every guard more effective.

## Night shifts, fog and power cuts

Rawalpindi winters bring cold nights and fog, and load shedding still happens in parts of the city. A night guard needs a proper shelter, a safe heater, warm clothing, a reliable torch, a charged phone and an emergency light. Guards should do rounds at set times, check gates after midnight and stay awake. A short call from the family once or twice a night helps keep standards high.

## Monsoon and Nullah Leh

Monsoon in Rawalpindi can be severe, especially near Nullah Leh, Committee Chowk and low lying parts of the old city. During heavy rain, guards should watch for water entering the premises, move vehicles to higher ground if needed, keep the generator area dry and report damage. A raincoat and boots are part of the basic kit.

## Guards and household staff

In homes with a cook, maid, driver and gardener, the guard notes who comes and goes, makes sure staff do not bring unknown people inside and watches for anything carried out of the house. Agree in advance whether bags are checked and who the guard reports concerns to, so this happens politely and without friction.

## Training and conduct at the gate

A good guard is polite to genuine visitors and firm with everyone else. They ask for names, call the family before opening the gate, never let a stranger in because someone claims to know the family, check delivery packages and refuse to leave the gate unattended. We brief guards on these basics, and you should repeat your own rules in the first week.

## Keeping a good guard

Pay on time, provide a proper shelter, meals or a meal allowance as agreed, a weekly day off with relief cover and respect. In a city where many guards are former soldiers, a family that treats them with dignity earns loyalty that lasts for years.
