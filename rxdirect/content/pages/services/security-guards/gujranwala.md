## Home security guard Gujranwala: the job at a residence

A home security guard in Gujranwala usually controls the main gate and checks every visitor, keeps a visitor register with names, times and purpose, receives deliveries and checks them before they enter, opens the gate for family cars and stops unfamiliar vehicles, patrols the boundary at night, switches outdoor lights on and off, checks locks and calls the family or police if anything is suspicious. In homes with several staff, the guard notes when staff arrive and leave.

In empty houses, the guard's job widens: opening and airing the house, checking for leaks and damage, keeping the gate area clean and sending regular updates to the family abroad.

## Guarding houses for families abroad

This is one of the most common requests we receive in Gujranwala. A closed house attracts attention: thieves look for signs that nobody is home, and squatters or relatives with disputes sometimes try to occupy empty property. A live-in guard keeps the house occupied and watched. Agree a simple routine: open and air the rooms weekly, check roofs and drains after rain, keep the gate and boundary lit at night and send a short video of the gate, boundary and rooms every few days. A trusted relative should hold a spare set of keys and check in in person now and then. When the family returns for Eid or a wedding, the guard should know the dates so the house is ready.

Punjab Police ask residents to register domestic staff, including guards, at the local police station or through the Punjab Police tenant and servant registration system. Registration needs the guard's CNIC, photographs, address and your details. We provide the documents we have collected and guide you or your relative through the process.

## Planning cover for your home

One guard on a 12 hour night shift suits many houses. Large houses, corner plots and closed houses may need a live-in guard or round the clock cover with two guards on alternating shifts, plus relief cover for days off. CCTV that the family can view from abroad, good lighting and strong gates make every guard more effective.

## Fog, heat and monsoon

Winter fog on and around the GT Road can reduce visibility to almost nothing at night. A night guard needs a proper shelter, a safe heater, warm clothing, a strong torch and a charged phone. Summers are hot, and day guards need shade and cold water. During monsoon, guards should check roof drains and boundary walls after heavy rain.

## Weddings and family visits

When relatives return from abroad for weddings, the house fills with guests, caterers and drivers. An extra guard for event days and a family member at the gate to identify guests keep valuables safe.

## Keeping a good guard

Pay on time, provide a proper shelter, meals or a meal allowance as agreed, a weekly day off with relief cover and respect. If you are abroad, a short call of thanks now and then is noticed and valued.

## Leave and relief cover

Many guards in Gujranwala come from Hafizabad, Sheikhupura, Wazirabad, Sialkot and nearby villages, and they go home for Eid, harvest and family weddings. Agree leave dates in advance and arrange a relief guard for those days, so the house or factory is never left uncovered.
