## Home security guard Faisalabad: the job at a residence

A home security guard in Faisalabad usually controls the main gate and checks every visitor, keeps a visitor register with names, times and purpose, receives deliveries and checks them before they enter, opens the gate for family cars and stops unfamiliar vehicles, patrols the boundary at night, switches outdoor lights on and off, checks locks and calls the family or police if anything is suspicious. In homes with several staff, the guard notes when staff arrive and leave.

Some families ask guards to water plants or wash the car in quiet hours. Small extra tasks are fine if agreed, but the guard's first job is security, and they should never leave the gate unattended.

## Factory and mill security

Factories and mills need more than a guard at the gate. They need gate control for workers at shift changes, checks on trucks entering and leaving with yarn, fabric and finished goods, a register of materials going out, and night patrols around stores and machinery halls. A team of guards with a supervisor works best for larger units, with clear instructions on searches, vehicle checks and whom to call. We help plan the number of guards and shifts, and place them with the same checks we apply to homes.

## Wages day and cash movement

Weekly or monthly wage days are a risk for many Faisalabad businesses. Guards should be extra alert on these days, the cash route should change from time to time and nobody outside a small circle should know the timings. At home, avoid discussing cash movements in front of staff or visitors. A guard who notices someone watching the gate or following the car should tell the family straight away.

## Planning cover for your home

One guard on a 12 hour night shift suits most houses. Larger houses, corner plots and homes left empty during travel may need round the clock cover, which means at least two guards on alternating shifts, plus relief cover for weekly days off. CCTV, good lighting and a working intercom make every guard more effective.

## Fog, smog and summer heat

From November to January, fog and smog reduce visibility at night across Faisalabad. A night guard needs a proper shelter, a safe heater, warm clothing, a strong torch and a charged phone. In summer, temperatures pass 45 degrees, and day guards need shade, a fan, cold water and short breaks.

## Weddings and big gatherings

Faisalabad weddings bring caterers, decorators, guests and their drivers to the house over several days. An extra guard for event days, a guest list at the gate and a clear rule about where drivers wait help keep valuables safe.

## Guards and household staff

In homes with a cook, maid, driver and gardener, the guard notes who comes and goes, makes sure staff do not bring unknown people inside and watches for anything carried out of the house. Agree in advance whether bags are checked and who the guard reports concerns to.

## Training and conduct at the gate

A good guard is polite to genuine visitors and firm with everyone else. They ask for names, call the family before opening the gate, never let a stranger in because someone claims to know the family, check packages and refuse to leave the gate unattended. We brief guards on these basics, and you should repeat your own rules in the first week.
