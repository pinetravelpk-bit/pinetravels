## Home security guard Lahore: the job at a residence

A home security guard in Lahore usually controls the main gate and checks every visitor, keeps a visitor register with names, times and purpose, receives deliveries and checks them before they enter, opens the gate for family cars and stops unfamiliar vehicles, patrols the boundary at night, switches outdoor lights on and off, checks locks and calls the family or police if anything is suspicious. In homes with several staff, the guard notes when the maid, cook, driver and gardener arrive and leave.

Some families ask guards to water plants or wash the car in quiet hours. Small extra tasks are fine if agreed, but the guard's first job is security, and they should never leave the gate unattended.

## Police registration in Lahore

Punjab Police ask residents to register domestic staff, including guards, at the local police station or through the Punjab Police tenant and servant registration system. Registration needs the guard's CNIC, photographs, address and your details. It helps the police verify identity quickly if anything goes wrong. We provide the documents we have collected and guide you through it.

## Planning cover for your home

One guard on a 12 hour night shift suits most houses. Larger houses, corner plots, homes with frequent visitors and houses left empty during travel may need round the clock cover, which means at least two guards on alternating shifts, plus relief cover for weekly days off. CCTV, good lighting, a working intercom and a simple panic button for the family make every guard more effective.

## Smog, fog and winter nights

From November to January, Lahore's smog and fog can reduce visibility to a few metres at night. A night guard needs a proper shelter, a safe heater, warm clothing, a mask on bad smog days, a strong torch and a charged phone. Guards should do rounds at set times, check gates after midnight and stay awake. Poor visibility is exactly when an alert guard matters most.

## Summer heat and monsoon

Lahore summers are brutal, with temperatures above 45 degrees. Day guards need shade, a fan, cold water and short breaks. During monsoon, heavy rain floods many roads and causes power cuts. Guards should watch for water entering the premises, keep the generator area dry and report damage. A raincoat and boots are part of the basic kit.

## Weddings and big events at home

Lahore weddings bring caterers, decorators, tent crews, guests and their drivers to the house over several days. Gate control becomes harder and more important. An extra guard for event days, a guest list at the gate and a clear rule about where drivers wait help keep valuables safe and the family relaxed. Tell us the dates early, especially in the winter wedding season.

## Homes left empty during travel

Many Lahore families travel abroad for summer holidays or visit their villages for Eid. An empty house is a target. A live-in guard, with a relative or neighbour checking in, keeps the house occupied and watched. Agree a daily check-in by phone or WhatsApp, ask for a quick video of the gate and boundary each evening, and keep the guard's salary and supplies arranged before you leave.

## Guards and household staff

In homes with a cook, maid, driver and gardener, the guard notes who comes and goes, makes sure staff do not bring unknown people inside and watches for anything carried out of the house. Agree in advance whether bags are checked and who the guard reports concerns to, so this happens politely and without friction.

## Training and conduct at the gate

A good guard is polite to genuine visitors and firm with everyone else. They ask for names, call the family before opening the gate, never let a stranger in because someone claims to know the family, check delivery packages and refuse to leave the gate unattended. We brief guards on these basics, and you should repeat your own rules in the first week.

## Keeping a good guard
