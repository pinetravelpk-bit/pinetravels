## Questions worth asking at the interview

Practical questions reveal real experience quickly. Ask how they press a uniform shirt or a starched kameez, how they keep track of several tablets taken at different times, what they would do if a guest arrived while their employer was resting, and how they handled private matters in previous jobs. Watch how the candidate arrives: neatly dressed, on time and with documents in order usually means the same care on the job.

## Why attendants leave, and how to keep them

Attendants usually leave when they are on call day and night with no rest, when other staff treat them as a general servant for the whole house, or when they are blamed for things outside their control. Protect the role: the attendant serves one person, has fixed rest hours and a weekly day off, and any complaint is handled privately and calmly. A small yearly increase and recognition at Eid show that loyalty is noticed.

Rawalpindi's seasons change the attendant's work. In winter, woollen uniforms, coats and blankets come out of storage and need airing, heaters must be checked and switched off safely at night. In summer, light cotton clothes, cold drinks and a cool room matter most.
