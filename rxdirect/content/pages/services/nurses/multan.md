## Private nurse Multan: what a home nurse does

A private nurse in Multan can give injections, including insulin and blood thinners; manage IV drips and cannulas where the doctor has prescribed them; dress wounds and surgical incisions; care for urinary catheters, colostomy bags, NG and PEG feeding tubes and tracheostomies; monitor blood pressure, pulse, temperature, sugar and oxygen levels; give medicines on schedule and keep a medicine chart; turn and position bedbound patients to prevent bedsores; assist with prescribed exercises; recognise warning signs and call the doctor or arrange hospital transfer; and keep a daily record for the family and the doctor.

A home nurse works under the instructions of the patient's treating doctor. They do not change medicines or diagnose new problems on their own, and a good nurse knows when to call for help.

## Heat and vulnerable patients

Multan's summer is one of the hardest in Pakistan for sick and elderly people. Temperatures above 45 degrees, hot winds and power cuts put bedbound, diabetic and heart patients at serious risk of dehydration and heatstroke. A good home nurse tracks fluids carefully, keeps the room cool with fans, curtains and damp cloths when the power fails, watches for confusion, fever, low urine output and dizziness, and calls the doctor early. Plan a UPS or generator backup for oxygen concentrators, air mattresses and fans.

## Nurse or caretaker?

If the patient needs medical procedures, you need a nurse. If they mainly need help with walking, bathing, meals and tablets on time, a [caretaker in Multan](/services/caretakers/multan) is usually enough and costs less. ## Working with the patient's doctor

The nurse should have the doctor's written instructions, the medicine chart and a contact number for the doctor or ward. Agree how often the nurse should update the doctor and family, and which warning signs mean an immediate call or a trip to the emergency department. Keep reports and prescriptions in one folder.

## Daily records and family updates

A good home nurse keeps a daily chart: vital signs, medicines given and when, food and fluids, output, wound condition and anything unusual. Family members at work or abroad can ask for a short WhatsApp update after each shift.

## Equipment at home

Depending on the condition, the patient may need a hospital bed, an air mattress, a wheelchair, a commode, a blood pressure monitor, a glucometer, a pulse oximeter, an oxygen concentrator or a suction machine. Medical suppliers near Nishtar and in the main markets rent equipment.

## Infection control

A home nurse should wash hands before and after every procedure, use gloves, dispose of needles in a sharps container, keep supplies clean and teach the family simple hygiene steps.

## Night duty

Many families need a nurse most at night, when patients are restless, confused or at higher risk of falls and breathing problems. A night nurse should stay awake, check the patient regularly and keep a record.

## Supporting the family

A good home nurse teaches family members simple skills, such as checking sugar, helping the patient sit up safely or spotting early signs of a bedsore, so they feel confident between shifts. The nurse also gives the family a few hours of proper sleep and the reassurance that someone trained is watching.

## Keeping a good nurse

Pay on time, provide meals during long shifts, give the nurse a cool place to rest during breaks and treat them as a professional. A nurse who feels respected stays longer and gives better care.
