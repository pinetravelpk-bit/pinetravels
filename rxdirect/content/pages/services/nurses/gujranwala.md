## Private nurse Gujranwala: what a home nurse does

A private nurse in Gujranwala can give injections, including insulin and blood thinners; manage IV drips and cannulas where the doctor has prescribed them; dress wounds and surgical incisions; care for urinary catheters, colostomy bags, NG and PEG feeding tubes and tracheostomies; monitor blood pressure, pulse, temperature, sugar and oxygen levels; give medicines on schedule and keep a medicine chart; turn and position bedbound patients to prevent bedsores; assist with prescribed exercises; recognise warning signs and call the doctor or arrange hospital transfer; and keep a daily record for the family and the doctor.

A home nurse works under the instructions of the patient's treating doctor. They do not change medicines or diagnose new problems on their own, and a good nurse knows when to call for help.

## Daily records

A good home nurse keeps a daily chart: vital signs, medicines given and when, food and fluids, output, wound condition and anything unusual. These records help the doctor at follow up visits and reassure family members abroad.

## Infection control

A home nurse should wash hands before and after every procedure, use gloves, dispose of needles in a sharps container and keep supplies clean.

## Night duty

## Supporting the family
