## Private nurse Rawalpindi: what a home nurse does

A private nurse in Rawalpindi can give injections, including insulin and blood thinners; manage IV drips and cannulas where the doctor has prescribed them; dress wounds and surgical incisions; care for urinary catheters, colostomy bags, NG and PEG feeding tubes and tracheostomies; monitor blood pressure, pulse, temperature, sugar and oxygen levels; give medicines on schedule and keep a medicine chart; turn and position bedbound patients to prevent bedsores; assist with exercises prescribed by the doctor or physiotherapist; recognise warning signs and call the doctor or arrange hospital transfer; and keep a daily record for the family and the doctor.

A home nurse works under the instructions of the patient's treating doctor. They do not change medicines or diagnose new problems on their own, and a good nurse knows when to call for help.

## Nurse or caretaker?

If they mainly need help with walking, bathing, meals and tablets on time, a [caretaker in Rawalpindi](/services/caretakers/rawalpindi) is usually enough and costs less. ## Working with the patient's doctor

The nurse should have the doctor's written instructions, the medicine chart and a contact number for the doctor or ward. Agree how often the nurse should update the doctor and family, and which warning signs mean an immediate call or a trip to the emergency department. Keep reports and prescriptions in one folder.

## Daily records and family updates

A good home nurse keeps a daily chart: vital signs, medicines given and when, food and fluids, output, wound condition and anything unusual. Families at work or abroad can ask for a short WhatsApp update after each shift.

## Equipment at home

Depending on the condition, the patient may need a hospital bed, an air mattress, a wheelchair, a commode, a blood pressure monitor, a glucometer, a pulse oximeter, an oxygen concentrator or a suction machine. Medical suppliers near the major hospitals and in Saddar rent equipment. The nurse can advise on what is needed. Load shedding is a concern for oxygen concentrators and air mattresses, so plan a UPS or generator backup.

## Infection control

A home nurse should wash hands before and after every procedure, use gloves, dispose of needles in a sharps container, keep supplies clean and teach the family simple hygiene steps.

## Night duty

Many families need a nurse most at night, when patients are restless, confused or at higher risk of falls and breathing problems. A night nurse should stay awake, check the patient regularly and keep a record. Provide a comfortable chair, a small lamp and tea.

## Supporting the family

Caring for a sick parent or spouse is exhausting, even with a nurse. A good home nurse teaches family members simple skills, such as checking sugar, helping the patient sit up safely or spotting early signs of a bedsore, so they feel confident between shifts. The nurse also gives the family something just as important: a few hours of proper sleep and the reassurance that someone trained is watching.

## Keeping a good nurse

Pay on time, provide meals during long shifts, give the nurse a place to rest during breaks and treat them as a professional. A nurse who feels respected stays longer and gives better care.
