## Home cook Islamabad: what the job usually covers

A home cook is different from a chef. A cook for home is there to run your daily meals, not to cater a dinner party for fifty. In a typical Islamabad household the job covers:

- Breakfast for school and office (paratha, eggs, omelette, oats or a light continental option)
- Lunch boxes for children and working family members
- A full dinner with roti or rice, one salan, one daal or sabzi, and salad
- Weekly grocery lists and keeping an eye on what is running low
- Basic kitchen cleaning after each meal
- Special meals for elderly parents, diabetics or people on a low oil diet

Some families also want the cook to handle tea for guests, bake on weekends or prepare frozen items like kebabs and samosas for Ramadan. Tell us at the start and we will only send cooks who are comfortable with that.

## Cook for home Islamabad: live-in or live-out?

This is the first decision most families make. Both work well. The right choice depends on your house and your routine.

**Live-out cooks** arrive in the morning or afternoon and go home after dinner. They suit families in apartments in E-11, Gulberg Greens or the Centaurus area where there is no servant quarter. Many of our live-out cooks live in Rawalpindi, Bhara Kahu, Tarnol or Golra Mor and come in by Metro or Careem bike.

**Live-in cooks** stay in your servant quarter and are available from early morning. They suit large families, homes with elderly parents and households in DHA Islamabad, Bahria Town or the bigger houses in F-6 and F-7. A live-in cook can also step in when you have unexpected guests, which happens a lot in Islamabad.

If you are not sure, tell us your daily schedule and we will suggest what fits.

## Cook required in Islamabad? Write a better brief

When someone posts "cook required in Islamabad" on a Facebook group, they get fifty replies and no way to check any of them. A clear brief saves you that trouble. Before you contact us, think about:

- **Cuisine:** Mostly desi? Pathan style karahi? Continental for children? Chinese on weekends?
- **Number of people:** Cooking for four is a different job from cooking for twelve.
- **Diet needs:** Diabetes, heart patients, children with allergies, vegetarian days.
- **Timing:** What time should breakfast be ready? When do people come home?
- **Other duties:** Is the cook also expected to do dishes, buy groceries or serve guests?
- **Language:** Urdu, Punjabi and Pashto speaking cooks are all available in Islamabad.

The more detail you give, the better the match. A cook who is happy in their job stays longer, which is what you want.

## Tips for keeping a good cook

Hiring the right cook is half the job. Keeping them happy is the other half. The households that keep a cook for years usually do a few simple things:

- Pay on the same date every month, without reminders.
- Agree on a weekly day off and stick to it.
- Give clear feedback on taste early, not after three months of frustration.
- Keep the kitchen equipped with the basics: a decent pressure cooker, sharp knives, a working exhaust fan.
- Treat them with respect in front of guests.

It sounds obvious, but these small things are why some families in Islamabad have had the same cook for ten years.
