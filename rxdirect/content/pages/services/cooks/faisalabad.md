## Cooking for large joint families

A joint family kitchen is a different job from a nuclear family kitchen. Quantities are large, the pace is constant and different members want different things. A good cook for a joint family plans ahead: kneading dough for the day in the morning, preparing two salans at once, keeping daal ready for anyone who comes home late. For families of ten or more, we usually recommend a cook plus a [kitchen helper](/services/helpers/faisalabad), who handles chopping, washing up and serving. It costs less than you might think and keeps the cook from burning out.

## Guests and business visitors

In Faisalabad business families, guests are part of daily life: traders, clients, relatives from the village, neighbours. Chai and snacks need to appear quickly, and sometimes a full meal. Cooks who have worked in business households understand this rhythm. If your home receives many visitors, mention it in your brief so we can match the right cook, and consider agreeing on extra pay for very large gatherings.

## Summer heat and the Faisalabad kitchen

Faisalabad summers are long and hot, and kitchens become uncomfortable. A working exhaust fan, a pedestal fan and cold water help a cook through June and July. Food spoils faster too, so good storage habits matter: covering food, refrigerating leftovers quickly and buying fresh vegetables daily. Load-shedding makes fridge management harder, and experienced cooks plan around it.

## Cooking for elderly parents

Faisalabad's joint families often include grandparents with diabetes, high blood pressure or heart problems. Their food needs less salt, less ghee and controlled sugar, while the rest of the family eats normally. An experienced cook manages this by taking out portions before adding extra spice or oil, preparing plain daal or boiled vegetables alongside the main dishes, and using less sugar in kheer and halwa for those who need it. Share any doctor's advice with us, and we will look for cooks who have cooked for elderly people before. Ask the cook during the trial to prepare one regular and one diet friendly dish.

## Tiffins for the factory and shop

Many Faisalabad families send lunch to the mill, factory, office or shop every day. A cook handling tiffins needs to cook early, pack food so it stays fresh and hot until lunchtime, and coordinate with the driver or helper who delivers it. If tiffins are part of the job, mention how many and what time they leave the house. It affects the cook's whole morning, and the right cook will plan for it from the first day.

## Kitchen hygiene

During the trial, notice whether the cook washes hands, keeps raw meat separate, uses clean cloths and covers food. In a large family kitchen, good hygiene protects many people at once.

## Ramadan and Eid in Faisalabad

Ramadan means early sehri and big iftars for the whole family. Discuss duties and rest before the month starts. At Eid, many cooks go home to nearby districts, so plan cover in advance and ask us early if you need temporary help.

## Grocery money and records

In large households, grocery spending adds up quickly. Agree with the cook on a weekly budget for daily items like milk, vegetables and bread, keep a simple notebook of purchases, and check it together every few days. For bigger purchases like meat, flour and oil, many families buy in bulk themselves. Clear records protect both the family and an honest cook, and they make it easy to spot when spending creeps up for an innocent reason, like a rise in vegetable prices.
