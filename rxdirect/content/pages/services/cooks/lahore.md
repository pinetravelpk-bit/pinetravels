## Home cook Lahore: what the job covers

A home cook in Lahore usually handles:

- Breakfast: parathas, omelettes, chanay, halwa puri or nihari on weekends, cereal or eggs for children
- Lunch and lunch boxes for school and office
- Dinner with roti or rice, a meat or chicken dish, daal or sabzi, and salad or raita
- Evening chai and snacks: pakoras, samosas, sandwiches when guests visit
- Weekend specials and BBQ for family gatherings
- Menus planned with the family and grocery lists for markets like Liberty, Akbari Mandi or local sabzi mandis
- Kitchen cleaning after every meal

## Cooking for grandparents and children in the same kitchen

Many Lahore homes have three generations at the table. Grandparents may need food with less salt, less oil and softer textures. Parents want the rich Punjabi flavours they grew up with. Children want pasta, nuggets or a sandwich. A good cook can manage all three without making three separate dinners every night, for example by taking out a portion of salan before adding extra chilli, making plain daal alongside the tadka version, or preparing a simple continental dish twice a week. We ask about this in interviews and look for cooks who have handled mixed households before.

## Kitchen hygiene and food safety

Lahore's heat makes food safety especially important. During the trial, notice whether the cook washes hands before cooking and after touching raw meat, keeps raw and cooked food apart, uses clean cloths, covers dishes and refrigerates leftovers promptly. Ask how they clean vegetables and how long they keep cooked food. A cook with good habits protects your family from stomach illnesses that are common in summer and monsoon.

## Lahore summers and the kitchen

Lahore kitchens in June can be unbearable. Cooks stand over hot stoves while the temperature outside crosses 45 degrees. A working exhaust fan, a pedestal fan and cold water make a huge difference to whether a cook stays through the summer. Food also spoils faster, so good storage habits matter: covering food, refrigerating leftovers quickly and not leaving cooked meat out for hours. In winter, smog season brings its own problem, with many families keeping kitchen windows closed, so ventilation needs attention.

## Ramadan, Eid and wedding season in Lahore

Ramadan in Lahore means sehri, big iftar spreads and dinner, often for extended family. Discuss duties and rest before the month starts. Eid takes many cooks home for a week or more, so plan cover early. In wedding season, families host dholkis and dinners at home, and cooks used to cooking for crowds, sometimes with a [helper](/services/helpers/lahore), are worth their weight in gold.

## Grocery money and kitchen budgets

Many Lahore families give the cook money for daily vegetables, milk and bread while doing the main shopping themselves. Keep it simple and fair: a fixed amount, a small notebook for purchases and receipts where possible, and a quick check at the end of each week. Honest cooks appreciate the clarity because it protects them from doubt. If the cook will order meat or groceries by phone from a local shop, agree on which shop and settle the bill yourself.

## Keeping a good cook in Lahore

Pay on time, respect the day off, give feedback early and kindly, and praise good food. In a city that loves its food as much as Lahore, a cook who is appreciated takes real pride in the kitchen.
