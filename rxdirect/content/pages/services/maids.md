## House maid Pakistan: duties and boundaries

A house maid in Pakistan typically handles:

- Sweeping, mopping and dusting all rooms daily
- Cleaning bathrooms and kitchens
- Washing, drying, folding and ironing clothes
- Changing bed sheets and towels
- Organising cupboards, wardrobes and storage
- Cleaning windows, fans and doors on a weekly schedule
- Tidying up after meals and guests

Many families also ask maids to help with children or cooking. That is fine if it is agreed and paid for. The most common mistake we see is hiring a maid for cleaning and then gradually adding cooking, babysitting and errands without changing the salary. That leads to tired maids, lower standards and resignations. If you need help with several jobs, consider a maid plus a [helper](/services/helpers) or a [cook](/services/cooks).

## Home maid Pakistan: live-in or live-out?

**Live-in maids** stay in your home, usually in a servant quarter. They suit large houses, families with young children or elderly parents, and homes where help is needed early in the morning or late at night. You provide a private room, bathroom access, meals and a weekly day off. Many live-in maids come from other cities or villages and go home for Eid and family events.

**Live-out maids** come for fixed hours and return home in the evening. They suit apartments, smaller homes and families who prefer privacy at night. Transport time matters, so we try to match maids who live nearby.

Choosing the right home maid in Pakistan often depends on whether you have a proper servant quarter. If you do not, live-out is usually the better choice.

## Setting clear duties from day one

A simple written list of daily and weekly tasks makes a huge difference. For example: daily sweeping, mopping, dusting, bathrooms and laundry; weekly fans, windows and fridge cleaning; monthly cupboards and curtains. Put it on the fridge for the first month. It helps the maid know what is expected and helps you give fair feedback.

Also agree on hours, breaks, the weekly day off, phone use during work, and whether the maid can have visitors. These small points cause most misunderstandings when left unspoken.

## Protecting your home and your maid

Verified staff are the first layer of safety. A few habits add more: keep jewellery and cash locked away, keep copies of the maid's CNIC and our verification summary, register live-in staff with your housing society where required, and do not leave large amounts of money lying around. These steps also protect an honest maid from being wrongly suspected if something goes missing.

## Respect and retention

Maids stay longest in homes where they are paid on time, spoken to politely, given proper food and rest, and allowed their day off without argument. Small gestures, like remembering Eid clothes or helping with a family emergency, build loyalty that lasts years. Families who treat maids well also find it much easier to hire again, because good workers talk to each other.

## How much work one maid can handle

Families often ask how big a house one maid can manage. As a rough guide from our placements, one full-time maid can keep a three to four bedroom house clean, handle the laundry and ironing for a family of four to five, and do a weekly deep clean of one or two areas. A larger house, a family with small children, frequent guests or a lot of daily laundry usually needs a second person, either another maid, a helper or a part-time cleaner.

Overloading one maid is false economy. Standards drop, she gets tired, and she starts looking for another job. Two people working at a reasonable pace often cost less over a year than the constant hiring that follows burnout.

## Laundry, ironing and delicate items

Laundry is a big part of a maid's day in most Pakistani homes, especially with school uniforms, office shirts and the heavy embroidered clothes that come out for weddings and Eid. During interviews we ask maids how they handle colours, whites, silk and chiffon, and whether they have used automatic and semi-automatic machines. If you own expensive formal wear, show the maid how you want it cared for, or keep it for the dry cleaner. A short demonstration in the first week saves ruined clothes later.

## Seasonal deep cleaning

Pakistani homes need a proper deep clean a few times a year: before summer when fans and air conditioners start, after dust storms, before Ramadan and Eid, and before winter when heaters and blankets come out. A good maid can plan these deep cleans around the normal routine. For very large homes or tight deadlines, such as before a wedding, extra [cleaners](/services/cleaners) for a day or two make the job much easier.

## Common reasons maids leave

From our placements, the most common reasons maids leave are extra duties without extra pay, late salaries, harsh treatment, no proper rest for live-in maids, and homesickness in the first weeks. The first four are in the family's hands. For homesickness, letting a new live-in maid call her family regularly and visit home after the first month helps a lot.
