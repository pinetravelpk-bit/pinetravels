## Home cleaner Pakistan: what is included

A home cleaner in Pakistan usually handles:

- Sweeping and mopping all floors
- Dusting furniture, shelves, decoration pieces and electronics
- Cleaning bathrooms: toilets, basins, showers, tiles and mirrors
- Cleaning the kitchen: counters, stove top, sink and outside of appliances
- Emptying bins and taking out rubbish
- Cleaning fans, switchboards, doors and windows on a weekly schedule
- Cleaning balconies, porches and stairs

Laundry, ironing, cooking and childcare are not part of a cleaner's role. If you need those, consider a [maid](/services/maids) or [helper](/services/helpers).

## House cleaner Pakistan: getting consistent results

A house cleaner in Pakistan does best with a clear routine. Agree on:

- **Daily tasks:** floors, bathrooms, kitchen, bins
- **Weekly tasks:** fans, windows, switchboards, fridge exterior, balconies
- **Monthly tasks:** cupboards, curtains, deep cleaning of tiles and grout
- **Rooms to skip:** any rooms you prefer to keep private
- **Supplies:** which products to use on which surfaces

Write the routine down for the first month. It helps the cleaner and makes feedback easy.

## Domestic cleaner Pakistan: supplies and safety

Most domestic cleaners in Pakistan use supplies provided by the family. Keep a basic kit ready: a good mop and bucket, brooms, microfibre cloths, a toilet brush, glass cleaner, floor cleaner, bathroom cleaner, a dusting brush for fans and gloves. Store chemicals out of children's reach.

We brief cleaners on safety: never mixing bleach with acid based cleaners, keeping windows open when using strong chemicals, and using a stable ladder for fans and high shelves. Marble, wood and granite need gentle products; ask the cleaner to check before using anything new on these surfaces.

## Deep cleaning: what it covers

A deep clean goes far beyond daily cleaning. It typically includes:

- Washing walls, skirting boards and doors
- Cleaning inside cupboards, wardrobes and kitchen cabinets
- Descaling bathroom tiles, taps and showers
- Cleaning behind and under furniture and appliances
- Washing windows inside and out, including grills and frames
- Cleaning fans, lights and air conditioner covers
- Scrubbing floors and grout

Before moving into a new home, a deep clean is one of the best investments you can make. It removes dust and debris left by builders or previous occupants and gives you a fresh start.

## Cleaning through the seasons

Cleaning needs change through the year. Summer dust storms in Punjab and Sindh mean more frequent dusting and window cleaning. Monsoon brings mud and damp, so floors and entrances need extra attention and damp corners should be checked for mould. Winter smog in Lahore leaves a grey film on windowsills. Before Eid, most families want a deeper clean. A good cleaner adjusts the routine to the season without needing to be told every time.

## When nobody is home

Many families want the cleaner to come while they are at work. That needs trust, and a little planning. Decide who will let the cleaner in, or whether a key will be given. If you give a key, keep a record and take it back when the arrangement ends. Lock rooms or cupboards you want private, and keep valuables away. Some families ask the cleaner to send a WhatsApp message on arrival and departure, which takes seconds and gives everyone confidence.

For the first few weeks, try to be home for at least part of the cleaning time, so you can see how the cleaner works and give feedback. Once you are comfortable, unsupervised cleaning usually works well with a verified cleaner.

## Homes with babies, elderly parents or pets

Cleaning around vulnerable family members needs extra care. In homes with babies, floors where children crawl should be cleaned with mild products and rinsed properly, and cleaning chemicals must be locked away. For elderly parents, wet floors are a fall risk, so mopping should happen when they are resting, and bathrooms should be left dry. Homes with pets need regular cleaning of hair and paw marks, and some pets are nervous around strangers. Tell us about these situations in your brief, and we will shortlist cleaners who are comfortable with them.

## Saving water while cleaning

Water shortages are a real problem in many Pakistani cities, especially Karachi and parts of Rawalpindi and Lahore in summer. A careful cleaner uses buckets rather than running hoses, sweeps before mopping, reuses water for outdoor areas where suitable, and reports leaking taps or overflowing tanks. If your home depends on water tankers, mention it, so the cleaner understands how precious each tank is.
