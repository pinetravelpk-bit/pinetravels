## Babysitter Rawalpindi or nanny: which do you need?

A **babysitter in Rawalpindi** looks after children occasionally: an evening out, a few hours on the weekend, or during a wedding or family function. A **nanny** provides regular childcare, usually full time, and becomes part of the child's daily routine. Nannies follow routines for feeding, naps, play and learning, and often stay with a family for years. Most families who contact us need a nanny, and some also want an occasional babysitter. We can arrange both.

## What a nanny does in a Rawalpindi home

Typical nanny duties include feeding babies and children, preparing bottles and simple meals, sterilising feeding equipment, bathing, dressing and changing nappies, settling children for naps and bedtime, playing and reading, age appropriate activities, getting older children ready for school, helping with homework, keeping the nursery and children's clothes tidy, accompanying children to the park, classes or appointments, and watching for signs of illness.

Nannies are not usually expected to do general housework. Keeping a nanny focused on the children is better for everyone. If you also need cleaning or cooking, consider a [maid in Rawalpindi](/services/maids/rawalpindi).

## Grandparents and the nanny

Many Rawalpindi homes still include grandparents, who are closely involved in raising the children. Before the nanny starts, agree as a family who sets the rules on feeding, sleep and discipline, and who the nanny should ask when unsure. A nanny caught between two sets of instructions is in an unfair position.

## Setting house rules

Agree on feeding, sweets and snacks; screen time; how to handle tantrums, never smacking or shouting; who can visit and who can collect the children; where the nanny may take them; phone use, limited to breaks and never near water; and emergencies, including who to call and the nearest hospital. Write these down and review them after the first week.

## Safety at home

A nanny's job is easier and safer when the home is child safe: socket covers, cabinet locks for medicines and cleaning products, gates on stairs, secure windows, balconies and roofs, and no small objects within reach of babies. Older Rawalpindi homes often have open roofs and steep stairs, so check these carefully. Make sure the nanny knows basic first aid.

## Building trust over time

In the first weeks, work from home when you can, come back unexpectedly sometimes and talk to your children about their day. If you use cameras, tell the nanny openly. Over time, a good nanny earns the family's trust, and the relationship often lasts years.

## Keeping a good nanny

Pay on time, give a weekly day off, keep duties focused on the children and treat the nanny with respect in front of the children. Nannies stay with families who value them. A nanny who stays through your child's early years gives them stability that matters, and children often remember a loving nanny for the rest of their lives. Agree leave for Eid in advance and plan cover, so the nanny can visit her own family without worrying about yours.
