## Babysitter Karachi or nanny: which do you need?

A **babysitter in Karachi** looks after children occasionally: a dinner at Do Darya, a few hours on the weekend, or a wedding where the children need a watchful adult. A **nanny** provides regular childcare, usually full time, and becomes part of the child's daily routine. Nannies follow routines for feeding, naps, play and learning, and often stay with a family for years. Most families who contact us need a nanny, and some also want an occasional babysitter. We can arrange both.

## What a nanny does in a Karachi home

Typical nanny duties include feeding babies and children, preparing bottles and simple meals, sterilising feeding equipment, bathing, dressing and changing nappies, settling children for naps and bedtime, playing and reading, age appropriate activities, getting older children ready for school, helping with homework, keeping the nursery and children's clothes tidy, accompanying children to the park, classes or appointments, and watching for signs of illness.

Nannies are not usually expected to do general housework. Keeping a nanny focused on the children is better for everyone. If you also need cleaning or cooking, consider a [maid in Karachi](/services/maids/karachi).

## Choosing the right nanny for your child's age

**Newborns and infants** need a nanny who understands feeding schedules, safe sleep, sterilisation, nappy care and soothing a crying baby. A calm, gentle temperament matters most. **Toddlers** need energy, patience and a sharp eye for safety. **School going children** need someone who manages school routines, helps with homework and keeps screen time in check. Tell us your child's age and personality, and we will shortlist nannies with matching experience.

## Nannies in flats

Many Karachi families live in flats, and that changes how childcare works. Balconies and windows need secure grills or locks, and a nanny must never leave a child alone near an open balcony, even for a moment. Lifts stop during load shedding, so a nanny carrying a baby up several floors needs to be fit and careful. Building parks and play areas are useful for outdoor time, but a nanny should keep the child in sight at all times. If a live-in nanny will stay in a flat, she needs a proper space to sleep and some privacy.

## Heat, humidity and monsoon

Karachi is hot and humid for most of the year. Nannies should keep babies cool, dress them lightly, watch for heat rash and make sure they drink enough. During heatwaves, children should stay indoors in the hottest hours, and power cuts mean a nanny should know how to keep a baby cool without air conditioning. In monsoon, dengue and stomach bugs are common, so mosquito repellent, covered arms and legs and strict hygiene with food and bottles matter.

## Beach outings and water safety

Many Karachi families take children to the beach at Sea View, Clifton or the huts at Hawksbay and French Beach. Water is the biggest danger. A nanny must never take children into the sea without a parent present, must keep small children within arm's reach near water and must never use the phone while children are near a pool or the sea. Agree these rules clearly before any outing.

## Setting house rules

Agree on feeding, sweets and snacks; screen time; how to handle tantrums, never smacking or shouting; who can visit and who can collect the children; where the nanny may take them; phone use, limited to breaks and never near water; and emergencies, including who to call and the nearest hospital, such as Aga Khan, South City or Liaquat National. Write these down and review them after the first week.

## Building trust over time

In the first weeks, work from home when you can, come back unexpectedly sometimes and talk to your children about their day. If you use cameras, tell the nanny openly. Over time, a good nanny earns the family's trust, and the relationship often lasts years.

## Keeping a good nanny

Pay on time, give a weekly day off, keep duties focused on the children and treat the nanny with respect in front of the children. Help with transport on strike days or heavy rain if you can. Nannies stay with families who value them. A nanny who stays through your child's early years gives them stability that matters. Agree leave for Eid in advance and plan cover, so she can visit her own family without leaving yours stranded.
