## Babysitter Lahore or nanny: which do you need?

A **babysitter in Lahore** looks after children occasionally: a dinner on MM Alam Road, a few hours on the weekend, or a mehndi or walima where the children need a watchful adult. A **nanny** provides regular childcare, usually full time, and becomes part of the child's daily routine. Nannies follow routines for feeding, naps, play and learning, and often stay with a family for years. Most families who contact us need a nanny, and some also want an occasional babysitter. We can arrange both.

## What a nanny does in a Lahore home

Typical nanny duties include feeding babies and children, preparing bottles and simple meals, sterilising feeding equipment, bathing, dressing and changing nappies, settling children for naps and bedtime, playing and reading, age appropriate activities, getting older children ready for school, helping with homework, keeping the nursery and children's clothes tidy, accompanying children to the park, classes or appointments, and watching for signs of illness.

Nannies are not usually expected to do general housework. Keeping a nanny focused on the children is better for everyone. If you also need cleaning or cooking, consider a [maid in Lahore](/services/maids/lahore).

## Choosing the right nanny for your child's age

**Newborns and infants** need a nanny who understands feeding schedules, safe sleep, sterilisation, nappy care and soothing a crying baby. A calm, gentle temperament matters most. **Toddlers** need energy, patience and a sharp eye for safety. **School going children** need someone who manages school routines, helps with homework and keeps screen time in check. Tell us your child's age and personality, and we will shortlist nannies with matching experience.

## Smog season and children's health

From November to January, Lahore's smog is a real health concern for young children. A good nanny keeps children indoors on bad air days, keeps windows closed, makes sure air purifiers are running where the family has them, encourages plenty of water and watches for coughing, wheezing or itchy eyes. When outdoor play is not possible, she plans indoor activities so children are not stuck in front of screens all day. Tell your nanny which air quality level means staying inside.

## Summer heat and monsoon

Lahore summers are fierce. Nannies should keep children out of the midday sun, make sure they drink enough water and watch for signs of heat exhaustion. In monsoon, dengue is a risk, so mosquito repellent in the evening, covered arms and legs and no standing water in pots and coolers matter. Stomach bugs are common in the hot months, so hygiene with food and bottles is essential.

## Joint families and grandparents

Many Lahore homes include grandparents, aunts and uncles who all love the children and all have opinions. Before the nanny starts, agree as a family who sets the rules on feeding, sleep and discipline, and who the nanny should ask when unsure. A nanny caught between conflicting instructions is in an unfair position, and the child gets mixed messages.

## Setting house rules

Agree on feeding, sweets and snacks; screen time; how to handle tantrums, never smacking or shouting; who can visit and who can collect the children; where the nanny may take them; phone use, limited to breaks and never near water; and emergencies, including who to call and the nearest hospital. Write these down and review them after the first week.

## Safety at home

A nanny's job is easier and safer when the home is child safe: socket covers, cabinet locks for medicines and cleaning products, gates on stairs, secure windows and balconies, and no small objects within reach of babies. Make sure the nanny knows basic first aid for choking, burns and falls.

## Building trust over time

In the first weeks, work from home when you can, come back unexpectedly sometimes and talk to your children about their day. If you use cameras, tell the nanny openly. Over time, a good nanny earns the family's trust, and the relationship often lasts years.

## Keeping a good nanny

Pay on time, give a weekly day off, keep duties focused on the children and treat the nanny with respect in front of the children. Nannies stay with families who value them, and a nanny who stays through your child's early years gives them stability that matters.
