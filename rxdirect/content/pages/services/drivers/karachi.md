## Security awareness

Security is a real concern for many Karachi families. A good driver stays alert at signals, keeps doors locked and windows up in busy areas, avoids unnecessary stops, varies routes when the family asks, does not share the family's schedule with anyone, and reports anything suspicious. During interviews we ask drivers about their security habits, and we check references specifically on discretion.

## Monsoon and flooding

Karachi's monsoon rains can flood roads like Shahrah-e-Faisal, University Road and parts of Clifton and DHA within an hour. A sensible driver avoids flooded underpasses, never drives through deep water and waits rather than risking the car and passengers. After heavy rain, the car should be checked for electrical problems. Karachi's rain often brings long power cuts and blocked roads, so a driver who keeps the fuel tank reasonably full and a phone charger in the car is better prepared for whatever the day brings.

## Heat, humidity and waiting

Karachi drivers often wait in parked cars in heat and humidity. Agree a sensible rule on running the air conditioning, and let the driver wait in shade or indoors where possible. Cold water in the car helps on long summer days, and so does a short rest break between duties.

## School runs

Karachi's school traffic is notorious. Share timings, gate procedures and who can collect the children. Ask the driver never to leave children alone in the car and always to use seat belts. Arriving early avoids the worst of the jam. Give the school the driver's name, photo and number, and agree what the driver should do if a child is late or the school releases students early because of rain or a security alert.

## Fuel, logs and honesty

Agree on how fuel is bought and recorded, with receipts and odometer readings. Check weekly. A clear system protects both you and an honest driver. Karachi traffic means fuel use can vary a lot from week to week, so look at the monthly pattern rather than single days, and ask calmly if something looks unusual.

## Route closures and planning ahead

Karachi routes close often and suddenly: VIP movements, rallies, religious processions during Muharram, road works and accidents. A good Karachi driver checks traffic updates before leaving, keeps a mental map of alternatives, and calls the family early if a delay is unavoidable. Drivers who know the city well can usually find a way around closures on Shahrah-e-Faisal or in Saddar without losing much time. During interviews we ask candidates how they would get from one part of the city to another if the main road were blocked, and their answers tell us a lot about real experience.
