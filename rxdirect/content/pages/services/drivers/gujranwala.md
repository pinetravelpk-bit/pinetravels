## GT Road driving

The GT Road through Gujranwala is one of the busiest stretches in Punjab. Heavy trucks, overloaded buses, rickshaws pulling out without warning and motorcycles on every side mean a driver must be patient, alert and defensive. During road tests we watch how candidates handle this traffic. Speeding to save a few minutes is the habit that causes most serious accidents on the GT Road.

## Airport runs for relatives from abroad

When family members arrive from Europe or the Gulf, the driver often makes a late night or early morning run to Lahore or Sialkot airport. Flights are frequently delayed. A good driver checks flight status, leaves early, waits patiently, helps with heavy luggage and drives back carefully when tired. Agree in advance how airport runs are paid, and let the driver rest after a night trip.

## Fog season

Gujranwala and the GT Road corridor are known for dense winter fog from December to January. Visibility can drop to a few metres, and the motorway is sometimes closed. A careful driver slows down, uses fog lights, keeps a safe distance and does not insist on travelling when it is dangerous. Plan airport runs with extra time in fog season, and check whether the motorway is open before leaving.

## School runs

Share exact timings, school procedures and who can collect the children. Ask the driver never to leave children alone in the car, never to stop for personal errands with children inside, and always to use seat belts. Give the school the driver's name, photo and phone number. Gujranwala's school roads near the GT Road get very congested at dismissal time, and a driver who arrives early and parks safely away from the main road protects the children and avoids stress for everyone.

## Fuel, logs and honesty

Agree on how fuel is bought and recorded, with receipts and odometer readings. Check weekly, or ask a local relative to check if you are abroad. Airport runs and trips to Lahore can make fuel use vary a lot from month to month, so look at the overall pattern and ask calmly if something seems unusual. A clear system protects an honest driver from doubt as much as it protects you.
