Some Faisalabad homes need a chef simply because the daily cooking is so large. A chef who can organise this kitchen like a small restaurant, often with a cook and helper under them, brings order and quality to daily meals.

## Ramadan and Eid in a Faisalabad kitchen

Ramadan in a large Faisalabad household is a month long operation. Sehri for twenty before dawn, a full iftar spread at Maghrib, and dinner later for those who want it, often with relatives and business contacts joining for iftar several times a week. A chef plans the month's menus in advance, preps ingredients in the afternoon and works with helpers so the family's hospitality never slips. Eid then brings open houses from morning to night. Many chefs from outside the city go home for Eid, so if you need your chef on Eid day itself, agree on it early and consider offering leave on the days before or after instead.

## Desi degs and large quantity cooking

Cooking in degs, the large pots used for biryani, haleem and qorma at gatherings, is a skill of its own. Heat control, layering and timing are very different from cooking for six. Faisalabad families who host frequent large gatherings often want a chef with deg experience, either from catering or from years in big households. If this matters to you, say so in your brief, and the trial can include a large quantity dish so you can judge it.

## Discretion in business households

Chefs in business family homes often overhear conversations about deals, family matters and finances. We brief every chef on discretion and ask references about it. In a city where business families know each other well, a chef who keeps the household's matters private is essential.

## Healthy eating and special diets

Rich Punjabi food is part of Faisalabad life, but many elders live with diabetes, blood pressure or heart conditions. A chef can prepare lighter versions of favourite dishes and separate diet meals without making the family feel deprived. Share any medical guidance, and we will shortlist chefs who have cooked diet meals for elderly family members before.

## Summer heat and food safety

Faisalabad summers are long and hot. Chefs need good storage, quick refrigeration, fresh daily purchases and a working exhaust. Buffets at summer events need careful management so food is not left out too long. Experienced chefs bring out dishes in smaller batches and keep the rest hot or chilled in the kitchen, which keeps food fresh and safe for guests who arrive late.

## Sourcing and costs

Good chefs know Faisalabad's markets, from the bazaars around the Clock Tower to wholesale suppliers. Agree on a grocery budget and purchase records. A skilled chef often reduces waste in large households by planning menus and portions carefully.

## Live-in chefs and accommodation

Many full-time chefs in Faisalabad are live-in, especially those relocating from Lahore. Provide a clean, ventilated room with a fan or cooler, bathroom access, proper meals and a weekly day off. Agree on working hours and how long event days are compensated.

## Working with existing staff

If you already have a cook, decide whether the chef leads the kitchen or handles events. Clear roles prevent friction, and an existing cook often learns new dishes from a skilled chef. Many large households end up with the chef as head of the kitchen and the long serving cook as a trusted deputy who knows the family's tastes.

## Questions to ask in the interview

Ask how they would cook for fifty guests, how they would adjust spice for foreign visitors, how they control costs in a large household, and what they would do if a dish failed before guests arrived. Listen for practical detail: quantities per person, what can be cooked a day ahead, how many helpers they would need and how they would keep food hot and safe. A chef who answers these questions confidently has done the work before, while vague answers usually mean limited experience with large Faisalabad style gatherings.
