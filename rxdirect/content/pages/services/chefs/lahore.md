## Lahori dawats and mixed menus

A typical Lahore dawat now mixes traditions: a main table of karahi, biryani, qorma and kebabs, a continental corner with pasta or grilled chicken for younger guests, and a dessert spread with kheer, gulab jamun and a cake. A good Lahore chef plans this so everything is hot and fresh at the same time, and quantities are right without huge waste. During the trial, ask for a small version of your usual dawat menu.

## Weddings at home

Lahore families often host dholkis and mehndis at home, sometimes for a hundred or more guests. Event chefs with catering experience handle these with a team of helpers. Book early in the November to February season and after Eid, when the best chefs fill up quickly. We can arrange [helpers in Lahore](/services/helpers/lahore) for prep, serving and washing up.

## BBQ evenings

Spring and autumn evenings in Lahore are made for barbecue. Chefs who can manage a charcoal grill, marinate tikka, boti, seekh kebab and fish properly, and coordinate with indoor cooking for naan and sides, are popular with families who entertain on their lawns. Mention outdoor entertaining in your brief.

## Smog, summer and the kitchen

Lahore's summer heat makes kitchens hard work, and smog season from November keeps windows shut. A strong exhaust and good ventilation help chefs work comfortably and keep cooking smells out of the house. Food safety is critical in summer, when meat and dairy spoil fast. Good chefs buy meat fresh, refrigerate quickly, and never leave cream based desserts out on a buffet for hours in June.

## Healthy eating and special diets

More Lahore families now hire chefs for health: diabetic friendly meals for parents, heart healthy food, or high protein meal prep for fitness. Share any medical or dietary guidance, and we will shortlist chefs experienced with it.

## Live-in chefs in Lahore

Full-time chefs in DHA, Bahria Town and Lake City are often live-in, because early breakfasts, late dinners and long event days make commuting impractical. A live-in chef needs a clean, ventilated room with a fan or cooler for Lahore's summer, bathroom access, proper meals and a weekly day off. Agree on working hours, the afternoon break between lunch and dinner service, and how long event days will be compensated. A chef who is treated well in these practical ways stays longer and cooks with more care.

## Ramadan and Eid in a chef's kitchen

Ramadan changes a chef's day completely. Sehri must be ready before dawn, iftar spreads with pakoras, samosas, dahi baray, fruit chaat and sharbat must be on the table at the exact minute of Maghrib, and dinner often follows later for guests. Many Lahore families host iftar dinners for relatives and friends several times during the month. A chef who plans the month's menus in advance, preps in the afternoon and shares the workload with a helper can handle it without burning out. Eid then brings lunches and dinners for large numbers, and the chef's planning skills matter even more.

## Setting up your kitchen

Check counter space, a good range and oven, quality knives and pans, storage and exhaust before the chef starts. Small upgrades suggested by the chef in the first weeks are usually worthwhile.

## Working with existing staff

If you already have a cook, decide whether the chef leads the kitchen or handles events only. Clear roles prevent friction. Some Lahore families keep their long serving cook for daily family meals and bring in the chef three or four days a week for dinners and weekend gatherings. Others make the chef head of the kitchen, with the cook moving into an assistant role and learning new dishes along the way. Both arrangements work well when everyone knows who is responsible for what, and when the existing cook feels respected rather than replaced.

## Questions to ask in the interview

Ask how they would plan a dawat for forty, how they balance Lahori and continental dishes, how they control costs, and what they would do if a dish failed before guests arrived. Specific answers matter. A chef who can tell you quantities per guest, cooking times and what can be prepared the day before has clearly done this many times.
