## Domestic helper Karachi: typical duties

A domestic helper in Karachi supports the household in many small but essential ways. In the kitchen, that means washing dishes, chopping, cleaning fish and meat, kneading dough and serving. Around the house, it means sweeping, mopping smaller areas, cleaning balconies and grills, taking out rubbish and helping with laundry.

Karachi brings its own extra tasks. Helpers often manage water: checking tank levels, switching motors on and off, calling the tanker and making sure it is delivered properly. They run errands to the local market, pharmacy and bakery, and serve tea and snacks to guests who arrive at any hour.

## Water, tankers and motors

In many Karachi neighbourhoods, water supply is irregular and families depend on tankers. A helper who understands the system is invaluable. They know when the line water comes, switch the motor on to fill the overhead tank, keep an eye on levels, call the tanker before the tank runs dry, check that the full amount is delivered and make sure tanks are covered and clean. They also use water carefully when washing cars, floors and dishes. During interviews we ask helpers about these routines.

## Load-shedding and stairs

Power cuts mean lifts stop and fans go quiet. Helpers in apartment buildings often carry groceries, water bottles and gas cylinders up several floors. They also help keep the household running during outages: charging lights and fans in advance, keeping the fridge closed and planning laundry around power hours.

## Kitchen helpers for Karachi hospitality

Karachi homes entertain often, and guests may stay late. A kitchen helper preps, serves and washes up, keeping the cook from working until midnight. In Ramadan, with big iftar spreads and dinner afterwards, a helper is close to essential. Many Karachi families host iftar for relatives several times a week, and a helper who fries pakoras, cuts fruit, sets the dastarkhwan and washes up afterwards lets the cook and the family get through the month without exhaustion.

## Balconies, grills and salt

Homes near the sea in Clifton and DHA collect salt and dust on balconies, windows and grills. A helper who cleans these weekly prevents rust and keeps the home looking cared for.

## Monsoon and cleaning up after rain

When Karachi's monsoon arrives, it often arrives all at once. Streets flood, water seeps under doors, balconies fill up and power can be off for hours. A helper who knows what to do moves furniture and rugs away from water, sweeps water off balconies and courtyards, helps clear roof drains, mops up seepage and keeps the motor switched off until it is safe. After the rain, there is mud to clean from entrances and stairwells and wet laundry that will not dry in the humidity. Families who have been through a Karachi monsoon with a capable helper know how much difference it makes.

## Errands and money

Give a fixed amount for each errand, ask for change and receipts, and keep a simple record. Avoid sending a new helper with large sums early on, and never share bank cards or PIN codes. Water tanker payments are a common source of confusion in Karachi, so agree in advance who pays the tanker driver, how much, and how the payment is recorded. A simple notebook in the kitchen, with every purchase and tanker payment written down, protects both the family and an honest helper from misunderstandings.

## Live-in helpers

Live-in helpers need a ventilated space with a fan, bathroom access, proper meals and a weekly day off. Karachi's humidity makes ventilation especially important. A window, a working fan and a clean bathroom go a long way toward a helper who stays.

## Why helpers leave

Long commutes, duties that keep growing without extra pay, no day off and being treated as the lowest person in the house. Matching by area and fair treatment keep helpers for years. Small courtesies matter in Karachi's heat: cold water, a fan in the quarter, proper meals and a rest during the hottest part of the afternoon. Helpers who are treated well often grow into trusted cooks, drivers or maids with the same family, and they recommend reliable people when you need extra help for a wedding or Eid.
