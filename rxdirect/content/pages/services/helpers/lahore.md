## Domestic helper Lahore: typical duties

A domestic helper in Lahore usually supports the cook and maid. In the kitchen, that means washing dishes, chopping vegetables, cleaning chicken and meat, kneading dough, grinding masalas, serving at meals and cleaning up. Around the house, it means sweeping, dusting and mopping smaller areas, taking out rubbish, cleaning the porch, car and lawn furniture, and helping with laundry and folding.

Errands fill much of a Lahore helper's day: vegetables from the local sabzi mandi, meat from the butcher, bread from the bakery, medicines from the pharmacy, and small purchases from Liberty, Akbari Mandi or the nearest market. When guests visit, the helper serves tea and snacks and clears up afterwards.

A home helper in Lahore usually works with a cook, maid, driver or guard. ## Kitchen helpers for dawats and gatherings

A Lahore dawat is a serious undertaking. Biryani, karahi, kebabs, salads, raita, desserts and endless cups of tea, often for twenty or thirty guests. A kitchen helper preps everything in the afternoon, helps the cook during service, refills dishes and washes up afterwards. In homes that entertain every week, a full-time kitchen helper is one of the best investments a family can make, because it keeps the cook from burning out and leaving.

## Weddings and Eid

Wedding season in Lahore, from November to February and after Eid, brings dholkis, mayun and mehndi functions at home. Eid brings open houses from morning till night. Temporary helpers for these periods handle serving, cleaning and dishes. Book early, because demand rises sharply.

## Lawns, roofs and outdoor areas

Many Lahore homes, especially in DHA, Model Town and Bahria Town, have lawns, porches, garages and roofs that need regular attention. A helper can sweep and wash outdoor areas, clean cars, carry plants for the gardener and help with heavier outdoor work. In summer, when dust storms leave a layer on everything, a daily sweep makes a visible difference.

## Summer and smog

Lahore's summer heat makes outdoor work hard. Helpers should do outdoor jobs early in the morning or in the evening, drink plenty of water and rest in the afternoon. During winter smog, outdoor work should be limited and windows kept closed. Look after your helper's health, and they will look after your home.

## Helpers for elderly parents

Many Lahore parents live alone while children work in other cities or abroad. A helper can keep the house tidy, make tea and simple meals, fetch medicines and groceries and provide company. If your parent needs help with bathing, walking or medicines, a [caretaker](/services/caretakers/lahore) may be the better choice.

## Helper, maid or cleaner?

Choose a **maid** for full responsibility for cleaning and laundry. Choose a **cleaner** for scheduled cleaning of floors, bathrooms and kitchens. Choose a **helper** to support a cook or maid, or for flexible chores and errands.

## Ramadan in a Lahore kitchen

Ramadan doubles the kitchen work in most Lahore homes. Sehri must be ready before dawn, then iftar with pakoras, samosas, fruit chaat, dahi baray and rooh afza at the exact minute of Maghrib, followed by dinner, often for visiting relatives. The cook is fasting too, and the long hours wear everyone down. A helper who preps fruit and vegetables in the afternoon, fries pakoras alongside the cook, sets the table and clears up after iftar makes the month manageable. Many families hire a temporary helper just for Ramadan, and some find the arrangement works so well that they keep the helper on afterwards.

## Joint families in the older neighbourhoods

In Model Town, Garden Town, Shadman and the older parts of the city, many homes house extended families with grandparents, uncles and cousins. These homes produce enormous amounts of dishes, laundry and cleaning, and the cook and maid are often stretched beyond what is fair. A helper restores the balance. In homes like these, we often suggest a helper as the first addition before hiring a second maid, because a helper is flexible enough to cover whatever is most urgent each day.

## Errands and handling money

Give a fixed amount for each errand, ask for change and receipts, and keep a simple record. Avoid sending a new helper with large sums in the first weeks, and never share bank cards or PIN codes.

## Live-in helpers

Live-in helpers need a clean space, bathroom access, proper meals, a fan or cooler for Lahore's summer and a weekly day off. If the helper shares a quarter with the cook or driver, make sure there is enough room and privacy for everyone. Many live-in helpers in Lahore come from Kasur, Okara, Sheikhupura or South Punjab and go home at Eid, so plan for cover during those weeks.

## Training and growth

Show new helpers how things are done, correct calmly and praise good work. Many helpers grow into cooks or maids over time. A helper who spends a year or two beside a good Lahori cook often learns enough to take over the kitchen one day, which gives the family continuity and the helper a better future.

## Why helpers leave

Helpers leave when treated as the lowest person in the house, when duties keep growing without extra pay, or when they get no day off. Fair treatment keeps them for years. In a city as sociable as Lahore, helpers who are respected also tend to recommend reliable friends and relatives when you need extra hands for a wedding or Eid.
