## Domestic helper Gujranwala: typical duties

In busy homes, a domestic helper in Gujranwala supports the cook and maid: washing dishes, chopping, cleaning meat for karahi and kebabs, kneading dough, serving and clearing up. Around the house, they sweep courtyards and roofs, take out rubbish, wash cars, carry gas cylinders and help with laundry.

In quieter homes with elderly parents, a helper keeps the house tidy, makes tea and simple meals, fetches groceries, bread and medicines, accompanies parents to nearby shops or the mosque, and provides company through the day. Errands to the local bazaars, the pharmacy and the bank are common in both kinds of home.

Some Gujranwala homes stay closed while the family is abroad. A helper living on the property, often alongside a guard, can keep the house aired and swept, water plants and report any problems. ## Helper, maid or caretaker?

Families sometimes ask which role they need. A **maid** takes full responsibility for cleaning and laundry. A **caretaker** helps an elderly or unwell person with walking, bathing, toileting and medicines. A **helper** is flexible support: kitchen help, errands, outdoor work and company for parents who are still largely independent. For parents living alone, many families start with a helper and add a caretaker later if health needs change. Describe your situation and we will suggest the right fit.

## Training a new helper

Helpers who come from villages may need guidance in the first weeks: how you like tea served, where supplies are kept, which shops to use and how to keep a simple record of purchases. A relative can help with this if the family is abroad. Correct calmly and praise good work. Many helpers grow into trusted cooks, drivers or maids with the same family.

## Errands and money

Give a fixed amount for each errand, ask for change and receipts, and keep a simple record. For parents living alone, ask a relative to check the record weekly. Children abroad sometimes send a fixed weekly amount for groceries through a mobile wallet, and the helper keeps receipts in a folder. This keeps everything transparent, protects an honest helper from doubt and reassures the family that their parents' money is being handled carefully. Never share bank cards or PIN codes.

## Live-in helpers

Live-in helpers need a clean space, bathroom access, proper meals, a fan in summer, warm bedding in winter and a weekly day off. Many come from Hafizabad, Wazirabad or Sialkot and go home for Eid, so plan cover and agree return dates in advance.

## Why helpers leave

Helpers leave when treated as the lowest person in the house, when duties keep growing without extra pay, or when they get no day off. Fair treatment keeps them for years, and for parents who live alone, a helper who stays becomes a familiar, trusted presence that matters a great deal.
