## Domestic helper Islamabad: typical duties

A domestic helper in Islamabad usually takes on a mix of supporting tasks. In the kitchen, that means washing dishes, chopping vegetables, cleaning meat, kneading dough, grinding masalas and keeping the kitchen tidy so the cook can focus on cooking. Around the house, it means sweeping, dusting and mopping smaller areas, taking out rubbish, cleaning the porch and car, and helping the maid with laundry and folding.

Helpers also handle the errands that eat into everyone else's day: carrying groceries from F-6 Supermarket or Jinnah Super, picking up medicines, collecting dry cleaning and running to the bakery. When guests visit, helpers serve tea and clear up afterwards.

A home helper in Islamabad usually works with a cook, maid, driver or guard, so team fit matters. Before placing a helper, we ask who they will work with. ## Kitchen helpers for entertaining homes

Islamabad homes entertain a lot: official dinners, diplomatic receptions, family gatherings and Eid open houses. In these homes, a kitchen helper is invaluable. They prep ingredients in the afternoon, help plate and serve during the meal, and wash up afterwards so the cook is not working until midnight. If your home hosts often, a full-time kitchen helper can transform how your cook copes and how long they stay.

## Helper, maid or cleaner?

Choose a **maid** if you need one person responsible for cleaning and laundry for the whole house. Choose a **cleaner** if you mainly need floors, bathrooms and kitchens cleaned on a schedule. Choose a **helper** if you already have a cook or maid who needs support, or want a flexible person for lighter chores and errands.

## Errands and handling money

Give a fixed amount for each errand, ask for change and receipts, and keep a simple written record. Honest helpers welcome this because it protects them. Avoid sending a new helper with large amounts of cash in the first weeks. Never share bank cards or PIN codes.

## Training a new helper

Helpers often learn on the job. Show them how you want things done in the first two weeks, correct mistakes calmly and praise good work. Many helpers grow into cooks or maids over time. A helper who spends a year beside a good cook often learns enough to take over the kitchen, which gives your household continuity and the helper a better future.

## Fair treatment for helpers

Because helpers are often the youngest members of staff, they can be the easiest to overlook. Make sure they get proper meals, rest, a weekly day off and their full salary on time, and do not let other staff take advantage of them.

## Live-in helpers and accommodation

Live-in helpers in Islamabad need a clean, private space in the servant quarter, bathroom access, proper meals and a weekly day off. If the helper shares a quarter with other staff, make sure the arrangement is comfortable for everyone. In Islamabad's cold winters, a heater or warm bedding is a basic need, and in summer a fan. Helpers who are housed decently settle quickly and stay longer.

## Why helpers leave

Helpers usually leave when they are treated as the lowest person in the house, when duties keep growing without any change in pay, or when they are not given a proper day off. Fair, respectful treatment keeps them, and helpers who are treated well often grow into trusted cooks and maids with the same family.
