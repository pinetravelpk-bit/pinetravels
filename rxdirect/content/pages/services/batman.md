## Hire personal attendant in Pakistan: dedicated help for one person

In many Pakistani households, especially military families, there is a long tradition of a trusted attendant who looks after one person's daily needs. In the army this role is known as a batman: the soldier who keeps an officer's uniform pressed, shoes polished, room in order and day running on time. After retirement, many officers and their families want the same standard of care at home. Senior professionals, business owners and elderly parents increasingly want it too.

RX Direct helps families hire a personal attendant in Pakistan who is disciplined, discreet and dependable. Every attendant is verified, and many have direct experience serving officers. This page explains what the role involves, who it suits, costs and how to make it work.

## Batman for home Pakistan: what the role involves

A batman for home in Pakistan typically handles:

- Preparing clothes daily: washing, ironing and laying out outfits or uniforms
- Polishing shoes and maintaining accessories
- Keeping the person's room, wardrobe and study tidy and organised
- Serving tea, meals and refreshments at the right times
- Managing medicines on schedule and reminding about appointments
- Running personal errands: bank, pharmacy, tailor, post office
- Receiving guests and serving them
- Accompanying the person to events, appointments, the mosque or the club
- Packing and unpacking for travel
- Helping with light personal care where needed

The role is about attention to detail and anticipating needs before being asked.

## Personal helper Pakistan: attendant or caretaker?

A personal helper in Pakistan can mean different things. To choose the right role:

- If the person is independent and mainly needs their routine, clothes, room and errands managed, choose a **personal attendant**.
- If the person needs help walking, bathing, toileting or eating, choose a **[caretaker](/services/caretakers)**.
- If medical procedures are needed, add a **[home nurse](/services/nurses)**.

Some attendants are comfortable with light personal care, which suits many elderly people who are mostly independent.

## Household attendant Pakistan: standards and presentation

A household attendant in Pakistan represents the family to guests. Good attendants are neatly dressed, speak politely, serve quietly and know when to step back. Many families provide a simple uniform: a clean shalwar kameez in a set colour, or white for formal occasions. Agree on standards for serving guests, answering the door and handling phone calls in the first week.

## Discretion and privacy

An attendant sees a great deal of a person's private life: their health, finances, family matters and visitors. Discretion is essential. We brief every attendant that what they see and hear in the home stays there. References are asked specifically about discretion. Families should also keep sensitive documents and valuables secured, as with any household staff.

## Ex-army attendants

Attendants who served in the forces bring habits that families value: punctuality, routine, cleanliness and respect for hierarchy. They understand how to press a uniform, polish shoes to a parade standard and organise a room. We check service records to confirm backgrounds. Ex-army attendants are especially popular with retired officers, who appreciate someone who understands their routines without explanation.

## Daily routine example

A typical day for a live-in attendant serving a retired officer might look like this: morning tea at 6:30, clothes laid out and shoes polished before breakfast, newspaper ready, medicines given with breakfast, room cleaned while the officer walks, errands to the bank or market mid-morning, lunch served, afternoon rest, evening tea for guests, preparing clothes for the next day and checking the medicine schedule before night. Every household is different, but a written routine helps the attendant settle quickly.

## Uniform and clothing care

For officers and professionals who wear uniforms or formal clothes daily, clothing care is a core part of the job. A skilled attendant knows how to press creases correctly, starch collars, remove stains without damaging fabric, store woollen uniforms with moth protection and keep medals, badges and accessories polished and in order. For civilian clients, it means suits pressed, shirts ironed and hung by colour, shalwar kameez ready for Friday prayers, and formal wear cared for before weddings and events. Show the attendant how you like things done in the first week. Good attendants learn quickly and then keep to your standard without reminders.

## Travel and events

Many clients ask attendants to help with travel: packing suitcases with a checklist, carrying medicines and documents, managing luggage at airports and hotels, and unpacking at the destination. For official events, weddings and club functions, the attendant may prepare the outfit, accompany the client and look after their needs during the event. If travel is part of the job, discuss how often, how far, and how travel days will be compensated.

## Attendants for elderly parents

When the person being served is an elderly parent, the attendant role often becomes a blend of companion, organiser and helper. The attendant keeps the parent's day structured, makes sure meals and medicines happen on time, accompanies them on walks and visits, handles small errands and keeps the family informed. For parents who are still largely independent, this can be exactly the right level of support, preserving their dignity while making daily life easier. As needs grow, a caretaker can be added.

## Respect works both ways

Personal attendants work closely with one person, which can create a strong bond of trust. It can also lead to the attendant being on call at all hours. Agree on working hours, rest times and a weekly day off. Pay on time and speak respectfully. A loyal attendant who feels valued often stays with a family for decades.

## Interview questions for a personal attendant

When meeting candidates, ask practical questions that reveal real experience. How do you press a uniform shirt? How do you keep track of medicines for someone who takes several tablets a day? What would you do if a guest arrived while your employer was resting? How have you handled confidential matters in previous jobs? Ask ex-army candidates about their unit and the officers they served, then confirm with references.

Watch how the candidate presents themselves at the interview. An attendant who arrives neatly dressed, on time and with documents in order usually brings the same care to the job.

## Common reasons attendants leave

Attendants usually leave when they are on call day and night with no clear rest, when other household staff treat them as a general servant for everyone, or when they are blamed unfairly for things outside their control. Protect the role: make it clear the attendant serves one person, give fixed rest hours and a weekly day off, and handle complaints privately and calmly. A small yearly increase and recognition at Eid show that their loyalty is noticed.
