## The RX Direct team behind every placement

When a family receives a shortlist from RX Direct, they see two or three profiles and a summary of checks. What they do not see is the work behind it: the calls to previous employers in villages with patchy mobile coverage, the CNIC that did not match and was rejected, the cook who was asked to make daal and rice at our office before we would send her anywhere. That work is done by the RX Direct team, and it is the reason our placements last.

This page explains who does what, how the team works together and what you can expect when you deal with us. Team profiles with photos are shown above this section and are updated from our admin system as the team grows.

## Domestic staffing recruitment team Pakistan: how we are organised

Our domestic staffing recruitment team in Pakistan is organised around the three stages every placement goes through.

**Recruitment.** Recruiters build and maintain our pool of candidates. They source workers through referrals from placed staff, community contacts in the regions where many domestic workers come from, and direct applications through our website and WhatsApp. They run first interviews, judge experience and attitude, and decide who moves forward.

**Verification.** Verification officers take each shortlisted candidate through our checks: CNIC, address, references, and role specific documents. They record the results in our system and flag anything that concerns them. No candidate is sent to a family until verification signs off.

**Placement.** Placement coordinators work directly with families and businesses. They take the brief, match it against verified candidates, arrange interviews and trials, handle the paperwork at placement and follow up afterwards. Each client has one coordinator from start to finish.

## Staff verification team Pakistan: what they actually do

The staff verification team in Pakistan has the least glamorous job in our company and the most important one. A typical day includes:

- Examining original CNICs and comparing photos, names and dates carefully
- Calling previous employers and asking specific questions, not just "was he good?"
- Confirming addresses through family contacts, local references or a visit where needed
- Checking police character certificates for security guards
- Checking driving licences and testing drivers in real traffic
- Checking nursing diplomas and registration for home nurses
- Recording every result so it can be shared with the family

When a reference sounds rehearsed, or a story about why the worker left does not add up, our verification officers dig further. Sometimes the answer is innocent. Sometimes it is not, and that candidate does not go forward.

## Recruitment agency team Pakistan: what makes ours different

Many recruitment agency teams in Pakistan work on volume: collect as many CVs as possible and send them all to the client. We work the other way. Our recruiters are measured on how long placements last, not on how many candidates they send. That changes behaviour. A recruiter who knows a placement must last six months will be honest about a candidate's weaknesses, because hiding them only creates a replacement request later.

Our team also works across languages. Recruiters speak Urdu, English, Punjabi, Pashto and Saraiki, which matters when interviewing a cook from Swat, a driver from Chakwal or a caretaker from Bahawalpur, and when speaking with elderly family members who are more comfortable in their own language.

## Domestic staff recruiters Pakistan: how they judge candidates

Our domestic staff recruiters in Pakistan look at more than experience. In interviews they assess:

- **Honesty.** Does the candidate's story match their documents and references?
- **Attitude.** How do they talk about previous employers? Blaming everyone is a warning sign.
- **Skill.** Can they actually do the work? We test rather than take their word for it.
- **Reliability.** How far do they live from likely placements? How did they handle leave in past jobs?
- **Fit.** Will they be comfortable with the kind of household or office they are likely to join?

A candidate does not need to be perfect. They need to be honest, capable and suited to the placement.

## RX Direct recruiters and the families they serve

RX Direct recruiters do not just fill vacancies. They listen. A family that asks for "a maid" may really need a cook with a part-time helper. A son who wants "a nurse" for his father may find that a patient caretaker is the better fit. Our team asks the questions that get to the real need, and they will tell you honestly when your brief and your budget do not match the market.

## Staff placement team Pakistan: what happens after placement

Our staff placement team in Pakistan stays involved after the worker starts. Coordinators call the family and the worker during the first week, after the first month and periodically through the 6 month guarantee period. These calls catch small problems early, such as a misunderstanding about duties or a delayed salary, before they turn into resignations.

If a placement does not work out, the same coordinator handles the replacement. You do not have to explain everything again to someone new.

## Verified staffing team Pakistan: our standards

As a verified staffing team in Pakistan, we hold ourselves to a few standards that apply to every member of staff:

- Every candidate is checked to the same standard, whatever the role or urgency.
- Families get honest information, including anything that concerns us.
- Workers are treated with respect and never charged for placement.
- Client and worker information is kept confidential and used only for placement.
- Problems are reported to management the same day.

## A day in the life of a placement coordinator

To give a sense of how the team works, here is what a normal day looks like for one of our placement coordinators. The morning starts with WhatsApp messages that came in overnight: a family in Islamabad whose cook did not turn up, a new request for a nanny in Lahore, a follow up question from a family in Peshawar about a candidate's previous job.

The coordinator replies to each, then checks with recruitment on candidates for open requests. By late morning, two shortlists are ready to send. The afternoon is spent on calls: a family interviewing candidates by phone, a worker who wants to confirm their start date, a one month follow up with a household in Bahria Town. In between, the coordinator updates every file in our system so nothing depends on memory.

By evening the urgent cook request has a replacement candidate scheduled for a trial the next morning. That is a good day. Not every day is that smooth, but the habits stay the same: answer quickly, keep records, be honest.

## How the team handles complaints

Things sometimes go wrong. A worker leaves without notice, a family feels a candidate was oversold, or a worker reports that salary has not been paid. Every complaint goes to the coordinator first, who logs it and responds the same day. If the issue is serious, such as a safety concern or an allegation of theft, it goes straight to management.

We do not take sides automatically. We listen to both the family and the worker, look at the facts and act on what we find. Families get a replacement under the guarantee when the placement fails. Workers get support and a new placement when a family breaks the agreement. And when we get something wrong ourselves, we say so and fix it.

## Training and development

Our team members are trained on our verification procedures before they handle any candidate on their own. New verification officers shadow experienced colleagues for several weeks. Recruiters learn how to run structured interviews and how to spot common warning signs. Coordinators learn how to write a clear brief, set salary expectations and handle difficult conversations with both families and workers.

We review placements that did not work out to understand why. Sometimes the lesson is about our process, sometimes about the brief, sometimes about the match. Each review makes the next placement more likely to succeed.

## Where the team works

Our core team works from our office at Al-Bilal Plaza, Chandni Chowk, Rawalpindi. This is where candidates come for interviews and document checks, and where families can visit by appointment. For placements in Lahore, Karachi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar and Gujranwala, we work with local contacts and video interviews, and our coordinators manage the placement from Rawalpindi.

Being in one office matters more than it might seem. When a recruiter has a doubt about a candidate, the verification officer is at the next desk. When a coordinator needs a replacement urgently, the whole team can look at the pool together. Decisions are quicker, and information does not get lost between branches. As we grow, we plan to keep this close way of working, adding local interview points in other cities rather than separate offices that run on their own rules.
