## Domestic staff verification Pakistan: why it exists

Families in Pakistan want to hire honest, skilled workers. Honest, skilled workers want families to trust them. The problem is that neither side has an easy way to prove it. A worker can say "I have worked with good families for ten years", but a new employer has no way to check. A family can say "we pay on time", but the worker has to take their word for it.

Domestic staff verification fixes half of that problem. When a worker is verified by RX Direct, their identity, address, references and background have been checked by a registered company, and the result is recorded. Families can see it. The worker gets hired faster and on better terms. This page explains how to register, what we check and what the Verified ID means.

## Register domestic worker Pakistan: who can apply

Any adult domestic worker can register, whether or not they are currently looking for a job through us. You can register domestic worker details here if you work as a:

- Cook or chef
- Maid, helper or cleaner
- Nanny or babysitter
- Driver
- Security guard
- Gardener
- Home nurse or caretaker
- Office boy
- Personal attendant
- Electrician, plumber, carpenter or painter

You must be at least 18 years old and hold a valid CNIC. Workers from all cities can apply, and the process can be completed online with an interview at our Rawalpindi office or by video.

## How to register: step by step

1. **Fill in the form** on this page with your personal details, city, role and experience.

## Staff background verification Pakistan: the 7 checks

Our staff background verification in Pakistan covers seven checks. Each one is marked as pending, passed or not cleared, so you and any family can see exactly where things stand.

| Check | What we look at |
|---|---|
| CNIC | Original details match the person, card is valid |
| Address | Current and permanent address confirmed |
| Police record | Police character certificate reviewed where provided or required |
| References | Two references contacted and asked about honesty, skill and reasons for leaving |
| Medical | Medical certificate reviewed where provided or required |
| Interview | Face to face or video interview with our team |
| Skills | Practical check for cooks, drivers, nannies, nurses and trades |

An application is only approved when the required checks are passed. If any check is marked as not cleared, approval is not possible until it is resolved.

## Tips for a smooth verification

- **Use clear photos.** Blurry CNIC photos are the most common reason for delays. Take photos in good light, with all four corners of the card visible.
- **Give working phone numbers for references.** Tell your references we will call.
- **Get a police certificate if you can.** It is required for guards and helpful for every role. You can apply through your local police station or the Punjab Police Khidmat Markaz.
- **Be honest.** If you left a job on bad terms, explain it in the interview. Honest explanations are better than surprises from references.
- **Keep your reference number.** You need it with your phone number to check your status.

## Getting a police character certificate

Many workers ask how to get a police character certificate. In Punjab, you can apply at a Khidmat Markaz or through the Punjab Police online service, usually with your CNIC, a photo and a small government fee. In Islamabad, applications go through the Islamabad Capital Territory Police, and in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Sindh through their provincial police services. The process usually takes a few days to a couple of weeks.

You can register with us before you have the certificate. The police record check will stay pending until you upload it, and the other checks can go ahead in the meantime. For security guard roles the certificate is required before approval. For other roles it is strongly recommended, because families value it highly.

## Getting a medical certificate

A medical certificate is a short letter from a registered doctor confirming that you are fit to work and do not have an infectious illness. It is most useful for live-in roles, cooks, nannies and caretakers, where you work closely with food, children or elderly people. Any registered doctor or government hospital can issue one after a basic check. Some families also ask for a hepatitis test for cooks and nannies, and having one ready can help you get hired faster.

## What families see

A status check needs two things: your reference number or Verified ID, and the phone number you registered with. Without both, nothing is shown. When you share these with a family and they check your status, they see your name, role, overall verification status and the result of each check. They do not see your full CNIC number, your address or your documents. If you are shortlisted for a job, the family receives a verification summary from our team, which includes the information they need to make a fair decision.
