## Check staff verification status in seconds

Anyone can claim to be verified. A printed card, a photo of a certificate on WhatsApp or a confident "I have been checked by an agency" proves very little on its own. That is why every RX Direct Verified ID can be checked on this page, live, against our own records. If the details match, you know the worker has genuinely been through our process. If they do not, you know to ask more questions before letting that person into your home.

This page lets you check staff verification status for any worker registered with RX Direct, whether they were placed by us or registered for verification independently.

## How to check: step by step

1. **Get the reference or ID from the worker.** A reference number starts with RXA and is given when the worker registers. A Verified ID starts with RXD and is issued after approval, for example RXD-2026-0001.
2. **Get the worker's registered phone number.** This must be the number they gave us when registering.
3. **Enter both** in the form on this page and press check.
4. **Read the result.** You will see the worker's name, role, overall status and each individual check.

Both pieces of information are needed. This protects workers' privacy and prevents strangers from looking up personal details with just an ID number.

## RX Direct verification status: what each result means

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Received, waiting for review | The worker has registered and submitted documents. Checks have not started yet. |
| Verification in progress | Our team is working through the checks. Some may be passed, others pending. |
| Verified | All required checks have been passed and a Verified ID has been issued. |
| Not approved | One or more required checks were not cleared. The worker has not been verified. |
| Suspended | The worker was verified, but the ID is on hold, usually after a complaint under review. |

Only a status of **Verified** with a matching Verified ID means the worker has fully passed our process.

## Check verified staff ID: the seven checks

When you check a verified staff ID, you see the result of each of our seven checks:

- **CNIC:** identity confirmed against the original card
- **Address:** current and permanent address confirmed
- **Police record:** police character certificate reviewed
- **References:** previous employers contacted
- **Medical:** medical certificate reviewed
- **Interview:** interviewed by our team
- **Skills check:** practical skills tested for the role

Each check shows as Pending, Passed or Not cleared. Some checks, such as police record for non-security roles or medical for live-out roles, may remain pending for a verified worker if they were not required for that role. The overall status tells you whether the worker was approved.

## Domestic staff verification status Pakistan: when to check

We suggest checking the domestic staff verification status in these situations:

- **Before an interview**, if a worker tells you they are RX Direct verified
- **Before a trial**, to confirm the details match the person who will come to your home
- **When a worker shows you an RX Direct ID card**, to make sure the card is genuine
- **Every few months**, for live-in staff who have been with you a long time, to confirm the status has not changed
- **When your housing society asks** for verified details of household staff

## Verify domestic worker Pakistan: what to do with the result

If you want to verify a domestic worker in Pakistan and the result is Verified, compare the name and role with what the worker told you. Ask to see their original CNIC and confirm the name matches. That combination, a live Verified status plus matching original CNIC, gives you strong confidence.

If the result is In progress, the worker is genuinely registered but not yet fully checked. You can wait for verification to finish, or proceed carefully with your own checks in the meantime.

If the result is Not approved or Suspended, do not rely on the worker's claim of being verified. You are welcome to contact us to ask general questions, though we may not share every detail of why an application was not approved.

If there is no result at all, either the details were typed incorrectly or the ID is not genuine. Try again carefully. If it still fails, contact us before going further.

## Staff ID verification Pakistan: spotting fake IDs

Staff ID verification in Pakistan matters because fake cards are easy to make. Warning signs include:

- An ID number that does not follow the RXD-year-number format
- A worker who refuses to share their registered phone number for a check
- A card with spelling mistakes, a blurry logo or details that do not match the CNIC
- A worker who says the website "is not working" and asks you to trust the card instead

A genuine RX Direct Verified ID can always be checked on this page. If a worker cannot or will not let you check, treat their claim with caution.

## Verified worker status and privacy

The verified worker status shown on this page is limited on purpose. You see the worker's name, role, status and check results. You do not see their CNIC number, address, family details, documents or references. Those stay private with our verification team and are shared only with families who are seriously considering the worker for a job through us, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

We believe this balance is fair. Families get enough to confirm the worker is genuine. Workers keep control over their personal information.

## What if a status changes

A Verified ID is not permanent if circumstances change. If we receive a serious complaint about a verified worker, we review it, and the ID may be suspended while the review takes place. If the complaint is confirmed, the ID may be withdrawn. This protects the trust that families place in every Verified ID and protects the reputation of every honest worker who holds one.

If you are an employer and have a serious concern about a verified worker, contact us with details. We take every report seriously.

## Why some checks take longer

Families sometimes wonder why a worker's status stays "in progress" for several days. The slowest part is usually references. Previous employers may be abroad, may have changed numbers, or may only answer in the evening. Address checks can take time when a worker's family lives in a remote village. Police certificates depend on the worker applying at their local police office, which can take a week or more.

We would rather take a few extra days than mark a check as passed without real confirmation. If a worker's verification is urgent because you want to hire them, tell us and we will prioritise it, but we will not skip steps.
