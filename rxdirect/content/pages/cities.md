## Domestic staff Islamabad Rawalpindi Lahore Karachi: the big four

**[Islamabad](/cities/islamabad).** The capital has the highest demand for professional household staff, driven by government officers, diplomats, overseas families and professionals in the F, E and G sectors, DHA and Bahria Town. Many workers commute from Rawalpindi, Bhara Kahu, Tarnol and Golra. Salaries are among the highest in the country, and families often want staff with experience in larger homes or with foreign households.

**[Rawalpindi](/cities/rawalpindi).** Our home city. We place staff across Bahria Town, DHA, Askari, Chaklala Scheme III, Airport Housing Society, Westridge, Satellite Town and the old city. Rawalpindi has a large community of military families who value discipline and punctuality, and many of our personal attendants and drivers work with retired officers here.

**[Lahore](/cities/lahore).** Lahore's food culture means cooks are always in demand, and families expect them to handle everything from nihari to continental breakfasts. We work across DHA, Gulberg, Model Town, Johar Town, Bahria Town, Askari, Cantt and the newer societies along Raiwind Road and Bedian Road. Summers are brutal and winters bring smog, both of which shape what families need from staff.

**[Karachi](/cities/karachi).** The country's largest city has the longest commutes, so live-in staff are more common than elsewhere. We place staff in Clifton, DHA, PECHS, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, North Nazimabad, Bahria Town Karachi and Malir Cantt. Karachi households often need staff comfortable with humidity, water tank management and frequent guests.

## Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar and Gujranwala

**[Faisalabad](/cities/faisalabad).** Pakistan's textile capital has many business families in large houses in Peoples Colony, Madina Town, Wapda City, Eden Valley and along Canal Road. Joint families are common, which means bigger kitchens, more laundry and often a need for drivers and guards for factories as well as homes.

**[Multan](/cities/multan).** Summers in Multan regularly cross 45 degrees, which affects everything from gardening schedules to how much water tanks need checking. We place staff in DHA Multan, Gulgasht, Multan Cantt, Wapda Town, Citi Housing and along Bosan Road. Saraiki speaking staff are often preferred by older family members.

**[Peshawar](/cities/peshawar).** Peshawar households value privacy and hospitality in equal measure. Female staff for homes with women at home, Pashto speaking workers and cooks who can manage Peshawari dishes are common requests. We work in Hayatabad, University Town, DHA Peshawar, Regi Model Town, Cantt and Warsak Road.

**[Gujranwala](/cities/gujranwala).** A busy industrial city on the GT Road with many families who have relatives abroad. Caretakers for elderly parents and guards for houses that stay empty part of the year are frequent requests. We place staff in DHA Gujranwala, Model Town, Satellite Town, Citi Housing, Palm City and DC Colony.

## Domestic staffing agency near me Pakistan: does location matter?

People often search for a domestic staffing agency near me in Pakistan because they want someone local who understands their area. That instinct is right, but the agency's office does not need to be next door. What matters is that the workers live near you and that the agency knows your city.

For live-out staff, distance is the biggest reason for late arrivals and early resignations. A cook who spends two hours on a bus each way will not stay long. When we shortlist, we look at where each candidate lives and how they will reach your house. For live-in staff, distance matters less, but local knowledge still helps: a driver who knows Lahore's back routes or a guard who understands Karachi's security habits is worth more than one who does not.

## Household staff services Pakistan: what changes from city to city

Some things are the same everywhere: the need for honest, skilled, reliable people. Other things change a lot.

**Language.** Urdu works across all cities, but older family members often prefer Punjabi in Lahore and Gujranwala, Pashto in Peshawar, Saraiki in Multan and Sindhi or other languages in parts of Karachi. We match language where it matters.

**Food.** A cook who is excellent in Peshawar may need guidance on Karachi style biryani or Lahori breakfasts. We ask about your family's food habits and match accordingly.

**Housing.** Islamabad and DHA areas have more large houses with servant quarters. Karachi and central Lahore have more apartments, where live-out staff are the norm.

**Climate.** Summer heat in Multan and Lahore, humidity in Karachi, winter cold in Islamabad and Peshawar, and monsoon rain in the twin cities all affect gardening, cleaning and maintenance needs.

**Salary levels.** Salaries are higher in the capital and the two biggest cities, lower in the others. See our [Pricing page](/pricing) for details.

## Housing society rules for domestic staff

Most large housing societies in Pakistan have their own rules for household staff, and it helps to know them before a worker starts. DHA in every city, Bahria Town and the Askari schemes usually ask residents to register domestic staff with the society's security office, sometimes with a copy of the CNIC, a photo and a police verification form. Some societies issue staff entry cards. Gated communities in Karachi and Lahore often check staff at the gate every morning.

We share copies of the worker's CNIC and our verification summary at placement, which makes society registration simple. If your society asks for police verification, we can guide the worker through the local police station process. Doing this in the first week saves trouble at the gate later and is a sensible safety step even where it is not required.

## Seasonal demand across cities

Demand for staff rises and falls through the year. Before Ramadan and the two Eids, many workers go home and some do not come back, so families look for replacements. Wedding season in the winter months brings requests for chefs, helpers and cleaners. Summer in Lahore and Multan brings more requests for gardeners and maintenance staff as lawns struggle and air conditioners trip breakers. If you know you will need someone at a busy time, contact us a couple of weeks early.
