## Domestic staffing agency contact Pakistan: what to send

You will get a faster, better answer if your first message includes the basics. A good first message to any domestic staffing agency contact in Pakistan looks like this:

- **City and area.** For example, "Bahria Town Phase 7, Rawalpindi" or "Gulberg III, Lahore".
- **Role.** Cook, maid, driver, nanny, nurse, guard and so on.
- **Hours.** Full time, part time, or live-in.
- **Salary range.** What you are comfortable paying.
- **Household details.** Family size, children, elderly members, anything relevant to the role.
- **Start date.** How soon you need someone.

A voice note in Urdu, English or Punjabi is completely fine. If you are not sure about some details, send what you have and your coordinator will ask the rest.

## Urgent requests

Some situations cannot wait. A live-in maid leaves the night before your guests arrive, a driver quits before the school term starts, or a parent comes home from hospital sooner than expected and needs a nurse the same week. When you contact us with an urgent request, start your message with the word "urgent" and include the start date. We move urgent requests to the top of the queue and look first at candidates who are already verified and available. We cannot promise the impossible, but we will tell you honestly what we can do and how quickly, so you can plan.

For urgent care needs, it helps to send a photo of the discharge summary or prescription. For urgent household roles, tell us the bare minimum the worker must handle from day one, and we can fine tune the match later.

## Questions we will ask on the first call

To save time, here are the questions your coordinator is likely to ask, so you can think about them beforehand:

- Who lives in the house, and who will the worker mainly deal with?
- What does a normal day look like, from morning to night?
- What went well and what went wrong with previous staff?
- Is there a servant quarter for live-in staff?
- Do you have a preference for language, age or experience?
- Is there anything the worker must not do, or must always do?
