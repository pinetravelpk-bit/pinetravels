## Domestic staffing terms Pakistan: what we provide

Under these domestic staffing terms for Pakistan, RX Direct provides:

- Sourcing and shortlisting of candidates for household, care, office, security and trade roles
- Verification of candidates as described on our [How It Works page](/how-it-works)
- Arrangement of interviews and trials
- Support with a written agreement between client and worker
- Follow up after placement
- Replacement candidates under the guarantee described below
- For trade staff, booking of technicians for specific jobs at an agreed price
- A free verification service and Verified ID for workers who register

We do not guarantee that a suitable candidate will be available for every request, in every city, by a particular date. We will always tell you honestly what is realistic.

## Staff placement terms Pakistan: how a placement works

Our staff placement terms in Pakistan follow these steps:

1. **Request.** The client shares accurate requirements, including role, location, hours, duties and salary range.
2. **Shortlist.** We share profiles of suitable candidates with a summary of our checks. This is free and creates no obligation.
3. **Interview.** The client interviews candidates. This is free.
4. **Trial.** The chosen candidate works a short trial at the client's premises. The client pays the worker an agreed daily rate for trial days.
5. **Confirmation.** If the client wishes to proceed, the client confirms the placement and pays the placement fee.
6. **Start.** The worker starts under the agreed terms, and the replacement guarantee begins from the start date.

A placement is not final until the client confirms it. Clients may decline any candidate at any stage before confirmation without charge.

## Domestic staff agency terms: fees and payments

These domestic staff agency terms apply to payments:

- **Placement fee.** A one time fee for each confirmed placement. The amount depends on the role and city and is shared with the client before candidates are met.
- **When it is due.** On confirmation of the placement, before or on the worker's first day.
- **Salary.** The worker's monthly salary is paid by the client directly to the worker. RX Direct does not take any part of the worker's salary.
- **Trade work.** For electricians, plumbers, carpenters and painters, the price is agreed per job before work starts. Materials are paid by the client or billed at cost with receipts.
- **No hidden charges.** We do not charge anything beyond what was agreed in writing or on WhatsApp before placement.
- **Workers pay nothing.** No fee is ever charged to workers.

## RX Direct service terms: the replacement guarantee

Under RX Direct service terms, every confirmed placement includes a 6 month replacement guarantee from the start date:

- If the worker leaves, or the client reasonably finds the worker unsuitable, within six months, the client informs us.
- We provide replacement candidates as soon as reasonably possible, without another placement fee.
- The guarantee covers one role. If the client changes the role significantly, for example from maid to cook, a new placement fee may apply.
- The guarantee applies when the client has kept the agreed terms: salary paid in full and on time, the agreed hours and weekly day off, reasonable living conditions for live-in staff, and respectful treatment.
- The guarantee does not apply where a worker leaves because the client breached these conditions.

Replacement is our remedy for an unsuccessful placement. Placement fees are not normally refunded unless agreed otherwise in writing.

## Staffing agency terms and conditions Pakistan: client responsibilities

Under these staffing agency terms and conditions for Pakistan, clients agree to:

- Give accurate information about the role, premises, household and working conditions
- Interview and approve any candidate before they start work
- Pay the worker the agreed salary in full and on time
- Provide one full day off each week and reasonable working hours
- Provide a clean, safe room, bathroom access and adequate meals for live-in workers
- Treat workers with respect and never subject them to abuse, harassment or unlawful treatment
- Not retain a worker's original CNIC or other personal documents
- Not employ anyone under 18
- Inform us promptly of any problem with a placement
- Comply with applicable Pakistani law, including labour standards and housing society rules

## Domestic worker placement terms: worker responsibilities

Our domestic worker placement terms ask workers to:

- Give truthful information about identity, address, experience and references
- Provide genuine documents
- Attend interviews and trials as arranged
- Work honestly and respect the client's property, privacy and family
- Keep the client's household information confidential, during and after the placement
- Give reasonable notice, usually two weeks, before leaving a placement
- Inform RX Direct of any problem, including unpaid salary or mistreatment

Workers who provide false information or documents may be removed from our records and their Verified ID withdrawn.

## Ending a placement

Either side can end a placement. We ask clients and workers to give reasonable notice, usually two weeks, so the other side can make arrangements. A client who ends a placement should pay the worker all salary due up to the last working day, plus any agreed leave or bonus that has been earned. A worker who leaves should hand back keys and any property belonging to the household.

Where a placement ends because of serious misconduct, such as theft, violence or abuse, notice does not apply. In such cases the client or worker should inform RX Direct immediately, and where appropriate, the police.

## Complaints procedure

If a client or worker has a complaint about a placement or about our service, they should first raise it with their placement coordinator. Complaints are logged and answered, normally within two working days. If the response does not resolve the matter, the complaint can be escalated to RX Direct management by asking the coordinator or writing to info@rxdirect.pk. We aim to resolve complaints fairly, listening to both sides before reaching a view.

## Trials

Trials let the client see a candidate in the real working environment before committing. A trial usually lasts one to three days, depending on the role. During a trial the client should give the candidate a normal workload, explain expectations clearly and supervise as needed, especially for nannies and caretakers. The client pays the candidate an agreed daily rate for trial days, even if the client decides not to proceed. Either side may end a trial early if it is clearly not working.

## Verification and conduct

We verify every candidate carefully, but verification is based on documents, references and interviews available at the time. We cannot guarantee the future conduct of any individual. Clients should take sensible precautions, such as securing valuables and supervising new staff in the early weeks, and report concerns to us immediately. If a serious incident occurs, we will cooperate fully, including sharing the worker's verified details with the police where appropriate.
