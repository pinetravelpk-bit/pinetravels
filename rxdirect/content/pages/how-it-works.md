## How to hire domestic staff Pakistan families can trust

Most people in Pakistan learn how to hire domestic staff the hard way. You hire someone a relative recommends, it goes wrong, and next time you ask a few more questions. After three or four bad experiences you have built your own process. We built ours over years of placements so you do not have to learn it through trial and error.

This page explains exactly how hiring works with RX Direct, step by step, and what we check at each stage. It also includes advice you can use even if you decide to hire on your own.

## Domestic staff hiring process Pakistan: the five steps

Our domestic staff hiring process in Pakistan is the same for every role and every city.

**Step 1: You tell us what you need.** Send a message on WhatsApp or use our contact form. The essentials are your city and area, the role, hours, live-in or live-out, and your salary range. Extras that help: family size, ages of children or elderly members, language preference, food habits and any special needs. A voice note is fine.

**Step 2: We shortlist verified candidates.** Your placement coordinator matches your brief against candidates who have already passed our checks, or puts new candidates through verification. You receive two or three profiles, usually within 24 to 48 hours, each with experience details and a summary of our checks.

**Step 3: You interview.** Speak to candidates by phone, video call or in person. Ask about their experience, their last job and how they would handle your routine. We can join the call if you want.

**Step 4: Trial at home.** The candidate you choose works a trial at your home. For cooks this means cooking your regular meals. For nannies it means a day with your children while you are present. For drivers it means your normal routes. This is where you learn the most.

**Step 5: Placement and follow up.** Once you are happy, the placement is confirmed, the fee is paid and the 6 month guarantee starts. We follow up during the first weeks and stay available for anything that comes up.

## Domestic worker verification process Pakistan: why each check matters

Each step in the domestic worker verification process in Pakistan exists because we have seen what happens when it is skipped.

- **CNIC checks** catch borrowed and fake cards. A worker using someone else's identity is a serious warning sign.
- **Address checks** mean you know where the worker's family lives, which matters if anything ever goes wrong.
- **Reference calls** reveal why someone left their last job. "He left to get married" and "we asked him to leave" lead to very different decisions.
- **Interviews** show attitude and communication, which documents cannot.
- **Skills tests** separate people who can cook from people who say they can cook.

## Hire household staff safely Pakistan: advice for any family

Whether you use RX Direct or not, these steps will help you hire household staff safely in Pakistan:

1. Always see the original CNIC, not a photo on WhatsApp.
2. Call previous employers yourself and ask why the person left.
3. Never pay a large advance to someone you have not verified.
4. Hold a trial before committing.
5. Agree salary, duties, hours, day off and leave in writing.
6. Keep a copy of the CNIC and contact details of the worker's family.
7. Register live-in staff with your housing society or local police where required.
8. Keep valuables locked away for the first months.

## Background checked staff hiring Pakistan: the trial period

The trial is where background checked staff hiring in Pakistan turns from paperwork into reality. Our advice for a useful trial:

- **Make it a normal day.** Do not ask a cook for a showpiece dinner. Ask for your usual daal, sabzi and roti.
- **Be present.** Watch how the worker handles instructions, your children, your elderly parents.
- **Talk honestly.** Explain what you liked and what you would want done differently.
- **Notice small things.** Do they clean as they go? Do they ask before using things? Do they arrive on time?

If the trial does not go well, tell us. We will arrange another candidate without pressure.

## Staff placement process Pakistan: after the worker starts

The staff placement process in Pakistan does not end on day one. In the first weeks we call both the family and the worker. Most problems at this stage are small: a misunderstanding about duties, a question about the day off, a worker who is homesick. Catching them early prevents resignations.

If the placement does not work out within six months, we replace the worker without another placement fee. You tell your coordinator, we discuss what went wrong so the next match is better, and new candidates are shared as quickly as possible.

## Writing a brief that gets the right match

The single biggest factor in a good placement is a clear brief. Families often send "need a maid urgently" and nothing else. We will always ask follow up questions, but the more you tell us at the start, the faster and better the match.

A strong brief describes a normal day in your home. When do people wake up? Who is home during the day? What meals are cooked, and for how many people? Are there children, and how old are they? Is there an elderly parent who needs help? How big is the house, and are there stairs, a lawn or a roof terrace that needs cleaning? Are guests frequent?

It also explains what has gone wrong before. If your last cook left because the hours were too long, or your last maid struggled with the children, tell us. It helps us avoid repeating the same mismatch.

Finally, be honest about salary. If your budget is below the market rate for what you need, we will tell you and suggest options, such as part-time help or splitting duties. A candidate who accepts a low salary out of desperation usually leaves as soon as something better comes along.

## Replacements under the guarantee

Our 6 month replacement guarantee works simply. If the worker leaves, is asked to leave for a genuine reason, or is not suitable, tell your coordinator. We talk through what happened so we understand what to look for next time, then share new candidates. You do not pay another placement fee within the guarantee period.

The guarantee does not cover situations where the family has not kept the agreed terms, such as unpaid salary or no day off, because those placements were not given a fair chance. In every other case, we stand by our placements.
