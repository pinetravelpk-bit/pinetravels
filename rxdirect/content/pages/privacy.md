## RX Direct privacy policy: summary

This RX Direct privacy policy explains how RX Direct (SMC-Private) Limited ("RX Direct", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and protects personal information. It applies to families and businesses who use our services, workers who apply for jobs or verification, and visitors to rxdirect.pk.

We run a domestic staffing agency, which means we handle sensitive information on both sides of every placement: details about families' homes and routines, and identity documents and histories of workers. We take that responsibility seriously. Our approach is simple: collect only what we need, use it only for placements, protect it properly, and never sell it.

Our registered office is Office No. 4, 2nd Floor, Al-Bilal Plaza, Chandni Chowk, Murree Road, Rawalpindi, Pakistan. You can contact us about this policy on +92 322 1223666 or at info@rxdirect.pk.

## Who this domestic staffing privacy policy Pakistan covers

This policy covers four groups of people:

- **Clients:** families, individuals and businesses who contact us to hire staff
- **Workers:** people who apply for jobs, register for verification or are placed through us
- **References:** previous employers and other contacts whose details workers share with us
- **Visitors:** anyone who uses our website, including people who leave blog comments or fill in forms

## Information we collect from clients

When you contact us to hire staff, through WhatsApp, phone, email, the contact form, the blog sidebar form or in person, we may collect:

- Your name, phone number and WhatsApp details
- Your email address, if you provide one
- Your city, area and address, when needed for trials and placement
- Details of your requirements: role, hours, salary range, household size and relevant family details such as the age of children or the needs of an elderly parent
- Notes from our conversations and the history of your placements
- Payment records for placement fees

## Information we collect from workers

Staffing agency privacy, Pakistan wide, matters most for workers, because their information is the most sensitive. When you apply for a job or register for verification, we may collect:

- Name, phone number, date of birth and gender
- CNIC number and photos of your CNIC
- Current and permanent address
- A recent photo
- Work history, skills, languages and salary expectations
- Names and phone numbers of references
- Police character certificate, medical certificate, driving licence or nursing qualification where relevant
- A CV, if you choose to upload one
- Notes from interviews, skills checks and reference calls
- Your verification status and Verified ID

## Information we collect automatically

When you visit rxdirect.pk, our website and analytics tools may collect basic technical information: pages visited, approximate location based on IP address, device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. Your browser may also store your language preference (English or Urdu). We use this information to understand how the website is used and to improve it.

## How we use personal information

We use personal information only for purposes connected to our services:

| Purpose | Information used |
|---|---|
| Matching clients with suitable workers | Client requirements, worker skills, location and experience |
| Verifying workers | CNIC, address, references, certificates, interview notes |
| Arranging interviews, trials and placements | Contact details, addresses, schedules |
| Managing the replacement guarantee | Placement history and notes |
| Following up on placements | Contact details and placement records |
| Responding to complaints and safety concerns | Relevant placement and verification records |
| Meeting legal and tax obligations | Payment and business records |
| Improving our website | Aggregate analytics data |

We do not use personal information for unrelated purposes, and we do not sell it.

## RX Direct data privacy: who we share information with

RX Direct data privacy rests on sharing as little as possible. We share information only in these situations:

- **With clients considering a worker:** a verification summary, the worker's name, photo, experience and relevant check results. Full documents are not shared unless the worker agrees.
- **With workers being considered for a job:** the information needed to attend an interview or trial, such as the family's area and the nature of the work.
- **With references:** when we call a reference, we tell them who has listed them and ask about that person's work.
- **On the status page:** anyone with a worker's reference or Verified ID and registered phone number can see the worker's name, role and verification status.
- **With service providers:** hosting and analytics providers that help us run the website, under appropriate safeguards.
- **With authorities:** when required by law, or when necessary to investigate a serious incident such as theft or harm, in cooperation with the police.

## Domestic staff data policy: how worker documents are protected

Our domestic staff data policy treats worker documents with extra care:

- Documents uploaded through the website are stored privately on our own server, outside the public part of the site.
- Only authorised members of our verification team can open them, through a password protected admin system.
- Uploaded files are checked by their content to confirm they are images or PDFs, and size limits apply.
- Forms are protected against automated abuse with rate limits and spam filters.
- Paper copies at our office are kept in locked storage.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold.

## Recruitment privacy policy Pakistan: references and third parties

Workers often give us details of previous employers as references. Under our recruitment privacy policy for Pakistan, we use these details only to contact the reference about the worker's employment, and we record the outcome of the call. We do not use reference contacts for marketing. If you are a reference and do not want to be contacted again, tell us.

## Staff verification data privacy

Staff verification data privacy is central to the verification system. The public status check is designed to reveal as little as possible: it requires both a reference or ID and the registered phone number, and shows only name, role, overall status and check results. CNIC numbers, addresses, documents and reference details are never shown publicly.

## Blog comments

Comments on our blog are held for review before publishing. Only the name you provide and your comment text are shown publicly. Contact details submitted with comments are never displayed.

## How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, manage guarantees and follow up on placements, handle any complaints or safety concerns, and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. After that, information is deleted or anonymised. Verification records for workers may be kept longer so that a Verified ID remains meaningful, unless the worker asks us to close their record.

## Security incidents

If we become aware that personal information we hold has been accessed without permission, lost or misused, we will act quickly to contain the problem, assess the risk and inform the people affected where there is a real risk to them. We will also cooperate with the relevant authorities where required.

## Your rights and choices

You can contact us at any time to:

- Ask what information we hold about you
- Correct information that is wrong or out of date
- Ask us to delete your information
- Withdraw from verification or close your worker profile
- Stop receiving follow up messages

We will respond within a reasonable time. In some cases we may need to keep certain records, for example to meet legal requirements or because of an ongoing complaint, and we will explain this when it applies.

## RX Direct personal data policy for children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information about children except where parents share details relevant to a role, such as a child's age for a nanny placement. We do not place anyone under 18 in work.

## Information shared through WhatsApp

Most of our conversations with clients and workers happen on WhatsApp because it is quick and familiar. Messages, photos and documents sent to our WhatsApp number are seen by our team and kept in our records for as long as needed for the placement. WhatsApp itself is operated by a third party under its own privacy terms. If you prefer not to send documents over WhatsApp, you can upload them through our website forms or bring them to our office.

## Information about households

When a family shares details about their home, such as the address, routines, who lives there and any health conditions of family members, we treat that information as confidential. We share it with shortlisted workers only to the extent needed for them to understand the job and attend a trial. We ask workers to keep household information private, both during and after a placement, and we take breaches seriously.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, systems or legal requirements change. The "last updated" date on this page shows the latest version. Significant changes will be highlighted on the website.
