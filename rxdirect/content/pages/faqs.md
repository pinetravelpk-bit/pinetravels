## How to hire verified staff Pakistan wide through RX Direct

Send us your city, area, the role you need, hours, live-in or live-out preference and your salary range. Our team shortlists candidates who have passed our verification checks and shares two or three profiles, usually within 24 to 48 hours. You interview them, the candidate you prefer does a trial at your home, and once you confirm, the placement starts. That is how to hire verified staff in Pakistan without guesswork: every step is recorded, and you see the results of our checks before anyone enters your home.

## What checks do you do on staff?

Every worker goes through CNIC verification, address confirmation, reference calls to at least two previous employers, a face to face or video interview and a practical skills check for their role. Security guards must provide a police character certificate, drivers must hold a valid licence and pass a driving test, and nurses must show their nursing qualification. Medical certificates are available on request and recommended for live-in roles. Workers who pass receive an RX Direct Verified ID.

Karachi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar and Gujranwala can take a little longer, and specialised roles such as nurses and chefs take a few days more. Urgent requests are prioritised, and we always tell you honestly how long a request is likely to take.

## Maid hiring questions Pakistan families ask

**Live-in or live-out?** Live-in maids suit larger homes with a servant quarter and families who need help early and late. Live-out maids suit apartments and families who prefer privacy in the evening.

**How much work can one maid handle?** A full-time maid can usually manage cleaning, laundry and ironing for a typical family home. If she is also expected to cook and look after children, add a helper or a cook.

Every household is different, so the salary for a domestic worker is set after looking at the hours, duties, experience required and whether food and accommodation are provided. Message us with your needs and we will help you settle on a fair offer.

These are the maid hiring questions in Pakistan we hear every week. Our [maids page](/services/maids) has much more detail.

Our domestic staff replacement policy is a 6 month guarantee from the placement date. ## Staff hiring FAQ Pakistan: trials and agreements

**How long is the trial?** Usually one to three days, depending on the role. For nannies and caretakers, we suggest you are present during the trial.

**Do I pay for the trial?** It is usual to pay the worker a daily rate for trial days. We agree the amount beforehand.

**Is there a written agreement?** Yes. We prepare a one page agreement covering salary, duties, hours, weekly day off, leave and notice period, signed by both sides.

**Can the worker leave without notice?** We ask workers to give notice, usually two weeks, and most do. If someone leaves suddenly, the replacement guarantee covers you.

## Household staff questions Pakistan employers often forget to ask

Some household staff questions in Pakistan only come up after the worker starts. It is better to settle them at the beginning:

- What happens on public holidays and during Ramadan?
- How will leave for Eid or family events be handled?
- Is the worker allowed visitors in the servant quarter?
- What is the rule on phone use during working hours?
- Who buys groceries, and how is grocery money recorded?
- What should the worker do in an emergency when you are not home?

Agreeing on these early prevents misunderstandings later.

## Questions about specific roles

**What is the difference between a cook and a chef?** A cook handles daily family meals. A chef plans menus, cooks for events and works at a higher level, usually for families that entertain often. Most households need a cook.

**What is the difference between a nurse and a caretaker?** A nurse handles medical tasks such as injections, dressings and catheter care. A caretaker helps with daily living: walking, bathing, meals, medicines on schedule and company.

**What does a personal attendant or batman do?** A personal attendant looks after one person, often a retired officer or elderly parent, handling clothes, medicines, errands and daily needs.

**Can a driver also work as a guard?** Sometimes, but one person cannot drive and guard the gate at the same time. For homes that need both, we usually suggest a domestic couple or two separate workers.

**Do you provide electricians and plumbers for one job?** Yes. Trade staff are booked per job, and we share the price before work starts.

**Can I change the worker's duties after they start?** Small changes are normal, but adding a whole new job, such as asking a maid to take over cooking, should come with a conversation and usually a salary review. Sudden extra duties are one of the most common reasons staff resign. If your needs have changed a lot, talk to your coordinator; adding a helper is often cheaper than losing a good worker.

## Questions about safety

**How do I protect my home in the first months?** Keep valuables locked, keep copies of the worker's CNIC and our verification summary, register live-in staff with your housing society where required, and save our number. If anything feels wrong, call us early.

**What if something goes missing?** Contact us immediately. We share the worker's verified details with you and, where needed, with the police. Our records exist so that problems can be investigated properly.

**Do you place workers under 18?** No. We never place anyone under the age of 18 in domestic work.
