## SECP registered staffing agency Pakistan: what the SECP record shows

When you look up an SECP registered staffing agency in Pakistan on the SECP portal, you will see the company's name, CUIN, date of incorporation, company type and status. For RX Direct, the record shows RX Direct (SMC-Private) Limited, CUIN 0347436, incorporated on 24 July 2026 as a single member private limited company.

A single member company is a private limited company owned by one person. It is a common structure for growing businesses in Pakistan and carries the same legal standing as any other private limited company. The company must keep proper books, file annual returns with the SECP and maintain its registered office.

If you search and do not find a company that claims to be registered, or the details differ from what you were told, treat that as a serious warning. Registration is public precisely so that people can check it.

## How to verify any staffing agency in Pakistan

We encourage families to check every agency they consider. Here is how:

1. **Ask for the full legal name and SECP CUIN.** A company should give these without hesitation.
2. **Check the SECP record.** Search the SECP eServices company verification page with the CUIN or name. Confirm the name and incorporation date match.
3. **Ask for labour department registration.** In Punjab this is a Form C certificate. Check the validity date.
4. **Ask for an NTN.** Registered businesses can share their National Tax Number.
5. **Visit the office.** A real agency has a real address. If an agent refuses to meet anywhere but a roadside, be careful.
6. **Ask how workers are verified.** A clear answer with specific steps is a good sign. Vague promises are not.
7. **Ask about replacement terms.** Get the guarantee period and conditions in writing.
