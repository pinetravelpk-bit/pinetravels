## RX Direct Pakistan: who we are

RX Direct Pakistan began with a simple frustration. Families around us kept hiring household staff the same risky way: a phone number from a relative, a quick chat at the gate, and a hope that things would work out. Sometimes they did. Often they did not. Cooks left after two weeks, drivers turned out to have no valid licence, and in the worst cases valuables disappeared along with a worker whose real name nobody knew.

We started placing staff in Rawalpindi and Islamabad in 2018 with one rule: check everyone properly before they enter anyone's home. That rule has not changed. What has changed is the size of the operation. Today RX Direct (SMC-Private) Limited is a registered company working in eight cities, placing 18 types of workers with families, offices and businesses.

## RX Direct domestic staffing: what we do

RX Direct domestic staffing covers four groups of workers:

- **Household staff:** cooks, chefs, maids, helpers, cleaners, nannies, gardeners, domestic couples and personal attendants
- **Care staff:** home nurses and caretakers for patients and elderly parents
- **Office and security staff:** drivers, security guards and office boys
- **Home repair staff:** electricians, plumbers, carpenters and painters

For every role, our job is the same. We understand what the client needs, find candidates who fit, verify them, arrange interviews and trials, and stay involved after placement. If a placement does not work out within six months, we replace the worker without another placement fee.

## SECP registered staffing agency Pakistan: why it matters to you

Many people offering staff in Pakistan are individual agents working from a mobile phone. Some are honest and helpful. But if something goes wrong, there is often nobody to hold responsible. An SECP registered staffing agency in Pakistan is different in three practical ways:

1. **Accountability.** A registered company has a legal identity and a registered address. It cannot simply switch off its phone.
2. **Records.** We keep files on every worker and every placement: CNIC copies, verification results, references and placement dates.
3. **Continuity.** Your replacement guarantee is with a company, not with one person who may stop answering.

## Verified domestic staffing company Pakistan: how we check

A verified domestic staffing company in Pakistan should be able to tell you exactly what it checks. Ours is the same for every worker:

- **CNIC verification.** We see the original card and confirm the details match the person.
- **Address check.** We confirm current and permanent addresses.
- **References.** We call at least two previous employers and ask specific questions about honesty, punctuality and why the worker left.
- **Interview.** Every candidate is interviewed face to face by our team, or by video for remote candidates.
- **Skills.** Cooks cook, drivers drive, nannies answer childcare scenarios, technicians do practical tasks.
- **Role specific checks.** Police certificates for security guards, licences for drivers and nursing qualifications for nurses.

Workers who pass receive an RX Direct Verified ID. Families can [check any ID](/staff/status) on our website, and workers can [apply for verification](/staff/register) themselves.

## Domestic staff company Pakistan: what we believe

A domestic staff company in Pakistan sits between two groups who both need protecting: families who open their homes, and workers who depend on fair treatment for their livelihood. We try to be fair to both.

**For families,** that means honesty. We tell you if we do not have the right person yet. We share what our checks found, including anything that concerns us. We do not oversell candidates.

**For workers,** that means respect. We never charge workers for placement. We explain the job, salary and conditions before they agree. We ask families to commit to timely salary, a weekly day off and decent living conditions for live-in staff. We do not place anyone under 18.

We believe that when both sides are treated fairly, placements last longer, and that is good for everyone.

Overseas Pakistanis began asking for caretakers and drivers for their parents. Businesses asked for office boys and guards. ## What a typical placement looks like

To make this concrete, here is how a common request moves through our process. A family in Bahria Town Rawalpindi messages us on a Monday evening. Their cook is leaving at the end of the month, they are a family of six including an elderly mother on a low salt diet, and they want a live-out cook from 10 am to 8 pm.

On Tuesday our placement coordinator calls them back with a few questions: what the family usually eats, whether the cook should also buy groceries, and what salary they have in mind. By Wednesday afternoon they have three profiles on WhatsApp, each with a photo, experience summary and the results of our checks. They speak to two candidates by phone on Wednesday evening and invite one for a trial on Thursday.

The trial goes well. The cook starts the following Monday, the family pays the placement fee, and the 6 month guarantee begins. Our coordinator calls the family after the first week and again after the first month. Nothing about this is dramatic. That is the point. A good placement should feel simple.

## What we do not do

Being clear about what we refuse to do says as much about us as what we offer:

- We do not send candidates we have not checked, even when a family is in a hurry.
- We do not charge workers any fee, advance or commission.
- We do not place anyone under the age of 18.
- We do not hide negative findings from references to make a placement happen.
- We do not take a share of the worker's monthly salary.
- We do not ask families to pay large advances to workers before they start.
- We do not place workers with families who refuse basic conditions such as a weekly day off.

## Supporting workers after placement

Our relationship with workers does not end on the day they start. We check in with them during the first weeks, the same way we check in with families. Workers can contact us if they are not being paid on time, if their duties change without discussion, or if they face any mistreatment. We take these reports seriously and raise them with the family. In the rare cases where a family does not respect the agreement, we help the worker find a new placement and we stop placing with that household.

We also help workers grow. A helper who shows promise in the kitchen may move into a cook role. A driver with good references may move to a corporate placement with better pay. Workers who stay with us over several placements build a record that helps them get better jobs.
