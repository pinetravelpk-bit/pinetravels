## Domestic staff provider Lahore: what makes the city different

As a domestic staff provider in Lahore, we see a few patterns that set the city apart.

**Food expectations are high.** Lahoris know their food. A cook who would pass in another city may be found lacking here. We test cooks on everyday Punjabi dishes as well as anything special the family wants.

**Joint and extended families.** Many Lahore homes, especially in Model Town, Garden Town, Gulberg and the older parts of the city, house several generations. That means bigger kitchens, more laundry and often a need for more than one worker.

**A city that keeps expanding.** New societies along Raiwind Road, Bedian Road, the Ring Road and towards Kasur mean long commutes for live-out staff and more demand for live-in arrangements.

**Extreme weather.** Summer heat, monsoon flooding in low areas and winter smog all affect what households need, from gardeners to electricians.

**Workers from across Punjab.** Many domestic workers in Lahore come from Kasur, Okara, Sheikhupura, Sahiwal and South Punjab, and travel home at Eid.

## DHA and Gulberg households

DHA Lahore and Gulberg have some of the city's largest homes and busiest social calendars. Families here often keep a full team: a live-in cook, a maid, a driver or two, a night guard and a gardener, sometimes with a nanny for young children. Dinner parties and family gatherings are frequent, so cooks with experience in larger kitchens and chefs for special occasions are common requests. DHA's society rules require staff registration, and its sheer size means live-out workers need careful matching by phase. A worker who lives near Phase 8 should not be placed in Phase 1 if a closer candidate is available.

## Model Town, Garden Town and the older neighbourhoods

The older planned areas of Lahore, such as Model Town, Garden Town, Faisal Town and Shadman, are full of family homes that have stood for decades. Many house joint families with grandparents, and staff here often stay for years. Older houses mean more maintenance: aging wiring, plumbing in walls and damp after monsoon. Elderly residents also mean strong demand for caretakers, nurses and drivers for hospital visits.

## Johar Town and the newer societies

Johar Town, Wapda Town, Valencia, Lake City, Bahria Town and the Raiwind Road societies attract younger families and professionals. Homes are often smaller or semi-detached, both parents frequently work, and the most common needs are a maid, a part-time cook and a nanny. Because many of these areas are far from where workers live, live-in arrangements or workers from nearby villages work best.

## Summer, monsoon and smog

Lahore's seasons shape household needs. In June heat, lawns burn, air conditioners overload old wiring and workers need extra care to avoid heatstroke. Monsoon floods low lying areas and brings damp walls and roof leaks. From November, smog fills the city, windowsills turn grey and families with elderly or asthmatic members keep windows shut and air purifiers running. Gardeners, electricians, plumbers and cleaners are busiest at these times, and planning ahead helps.
