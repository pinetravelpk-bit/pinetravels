## Domestic staff provider Islamabad: what makes the capital different

Every city has its own hiring pattern. As a domestic staff provider in Islamabad, we see a few things that set the capital apart.

**Larger houses, more live-in staff.** The older sectors such as F-6, F-7, F-8 and E-7 have big houses with servant quarters, and families often keep a live-in cook, maid and guard. Newer areas like DHA and Bahria Town follow the same pattern.

**A large commuting workforce.** Live-out staff often travel from Rawalpindi, Bhara Kahu, Tarnol, Golra and Koral. The Metro Bus helps, but distance still matters for early starts.

**Diplomatic and expat households.** Islamabad has embassies, international organisations and many foreign families. They often need English speaking staff familiar with international food, schedules and expectations.

**Government transfers.** Officers move in and out of Islamabad on postings, which creates sudden demand when a family arrives and needs a full household team quickly.

**Seasonal extremes.** Cold winters bring geyser and heater issues, while monsoon rain and summer heat test gardens and roofs.
