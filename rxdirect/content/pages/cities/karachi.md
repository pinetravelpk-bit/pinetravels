## Domestic staff provider Karachi: what makes the city different

As a domestic staff provider in Karachi, we see patterns that do not appear in other cities.

**Distance and commuting.** Karachi is huge. A worker living in Orangi, Landhi or Korangi may spend two or three hours a day travelling to Clifton or DHA. That is why live-in staff are more common here, and why we match live-out workers by area.

**Apartments and bungalows.** Many families live in apartments in Clifton, PECHS, Gulshan and North Nazimabad, where there is no servant quarter. Others live in large DHA or Bahria Town bungalows with quarters. The type of home shapes the arrangement.

**Humidity and sea air.** Karachi's damp climate means clothes take longer to dry, metal rusts, and mould appears in corners. Maids and cleaners need to keep on top of it.

**Water and power.** Many areas depend on water tankers and face load-shedding. Staff who understand tank management, water saving and UPS routines are valuable.

**A diverse workforce.** Karachi's workers come from every part of Pakistan and speak many languages. Matching language and food habits matters more here than anywhere.

In many Karachi neighbourhoods, water arrives by tanker and needs careful management. Staff who know how to check tank levels, avoid waste, switch motors on and off at the right times and keep tanks clean are a real asset. Load-shedding also means UPS systems and generators need attention. During interviews we ask candidates about their experience with these routines, and we brief new staff on the household's arrangements.

## Karachi's climate and household needs

Karachi does not have a cold winter, but its humidity and heat shape daily life. Clothes dry slowly in monsoon, mould appears in corners, metal grills rust and paint peels faster than in drier cities. Pre-monsoon roof checks, regular painting of grills and good ventilation all help. Heatwaves in May and June are dangerous for outdoor workers, so guards and gardeners need shade and water. Water tank cleaning is especially important where tanker water is used. A household that plans for these seasonal needs, rather than reacting when something breaks, spends less and keeps its staff more comfortable.
