Staff who understand both sides of this, who are discreet inside the home and gracious with guests, are what Peshawar families look for.

## Domestic staff provider Peshawar: what makes the city different

As a domestic staff provider in Peshawar, we see clear local patterns.

**Privacy and female staff.** Many households prefer female staff for work inside the home and male staff for driving, guarding and outdoor work. We respect these preferences and match accordingly.

**Language.** Pashto is the main language of most households and workers, with Hindko and Urdu also common. Older family members especially prefer Pashto speaking staff.

**Hospitality.** Guests are frequent and important. Staff must be comfortable serving tea, preparing food quickly and conducting themselves well around visitors.

**Security awareness.** Peshawar families take security seriously, which makes careful verification of guards, drivers and live-in staff essential.

**Large joint families.** Extended families under one roof mean big kitchens and heavy housework.

Security is a real concern in Peshawar, which makes background checked staff essential. Guards and drivers in particular must be checked carefully, with police certificates for every guard. ## Female staff and women's comfort

Many Peshawar families want female staff for work inside the home, especially where women are at home during the day or where purdah is observed. Female workers, in turn, want to know who lives in the house and whether other women are present. We discuss this openly with both sides before placement. Female maids, cooks, nannies and caretakers are available, and we pay attention to their safety and comfort as much as the family's.

## Peshawari food and hospitality

Peshawar's food is a point of pride: chapli kabab, namkeen gosht, kabuli pulao, karahi, lamb dishes and fresh naan. Many families also want everyday daal, sabzi and lighter food for children and elders. Cooks need to handle guests quickly, serving green tea and qehwa, snacks and full meals at short notice. We test cooks on the dishes your family eats and ask about their experience with large gatherings.

## Care for elderly parents in Peshawar

Peshawar has major hospitals including Lady Reading Hospital, Khyber Teaching Hospital, Hayatabad Medical Complex and Rehman Medical Institute. Patients discharged from these hospitals often need nursing or caretaking at home. Many Peshawar families have members working in the Gulf, and caretakers for parents are a frequent request. We manage these placements on WhatsApp and video calls with local relatives involved.

## Hayatabad households

Hayatabad is Peshawar's largest planned township, with seven phases of homes ranging from compact houses to large bungalows. Families here are often professionals, doctors, traders and officials, many with children studying or working elsewhere. Common requests in Hayatabad include a live-in maid or cook, a driver for school and office runs, and a guard for the gate. Because Hayatabad is close to the Karkhano markets and the Torkham road, families also value staff who know the area well and are alert about who comes to the door. The BRT has made commuting easier for live-out workers from other parts of the city.

## University Town and Cantt

University Town and the Cantonment are among Peshawar's oldest established residential areas. University Town has many large family homes, NGO offices and guest houses, which creates demand for cooks, maids, guards and office boys. The Cantt has military and civil officers' homes with their own security procedures, where personal attendants and ex-army guards are often preferred. Older homes in both areas need regular maintenance of wiring, plumbing and roofs.

## Hujra, guests and staff conduct

In many Peshawar homes the hujra or guest room is the public face of the family, where male guests are received and served. Staff who serve here need to be well presented, quick with tea and food, respectful in their manner and discreet about what they hear. Household staff inside the home, often women, serve the family and female guests. We brief staff on these customs before placement and ask families to explain any specific expectations during the first week.

## Winters and summers in Peshawar

Peshawar winters are cold, with gas heaters, geysers and sometimes low gas pressure making plumbers and electricians busy. Summers are hot and dusty, stressing wiring and lawns. Monsoon storms are shorter than in Punjab but can still cause leaks and power faults. Plan geyser servicing in autumn and electrical checks before summer. Gas heaters should never be left burning overnight in closed rooms, and staff rooms need the same care, with ventilation and a safe heater in the cold months.
