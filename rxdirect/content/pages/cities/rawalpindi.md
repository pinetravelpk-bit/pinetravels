## Domestic staff provider Rawalpindi: what makes the city different

Rawalpindi is not just Islamabad's neighbour. It has its own character, and as a domestic staff provider in Rawalpindi we see it in our placements.

**Military families.** Rawalpindi is home to GHQ and a large military community in the Cantt, Chaklala, Askari schemes and DHA. Many retired officers want the discipline and standards they are used to, which is why personal attendants and ex-army drivers and guards are so popular here.

**Old city and new societies.** The narrow lanes of Raja Bazaar, Saddar and the old mohallas sit alongside the planned phases of Bahria Town and DHA. Staff for older family homes in Satellite Town or Westridge often need different skills from staff for a modern Bahria Town house.

**Commuting to Islamabad.** Many Rawalpindi families work in Islamabad, so drivers who know Murree Road, the Expressway and the Metro routes are valuable.

**Joint families.** Larger households with several generations are common, which means more cooking, more laundry and often a need for more than one worker.

**Close links to surrounding areas.** Many workers come from Kahuta, Gujar Khan, Murree, Kotli Sattian, Chakwal and Azad Kashmir, and stay connected to home, so leave patterns around Eid matter.
