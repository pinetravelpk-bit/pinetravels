Tell us the dates in advance and we will arrange temporary staff, so the family can enjoy the visit without worrying about the housework.

## Seasons in Gujranwala

Gujranwala's summers are hot and humid, winters bring dense fog on the GT Road and motorway, and monsoon can flood low lying streets. Electricians are busy before summer, plumbers before winter, and drivers need to be careful in fog season. Planning maintenance ahead of each season saves money and stress. For empty homes, a pre-monsoon roof check and a pre-winter geyser service are especially important, since nobody is there to notice a leak or a fault until the damage is done.
