Askari 10 and Askari 11 are among the more established Askari sectors in Islamabad, home to a mix of serving and retired military families. Both sectors maintain their own gate security, and residents here tend to hold hiring standards that go a step beyond what's typical elsewhere, which fits exactly how RX Direct operates.

## What we hear most often from Askari 10 & 11 households

- "We need someone whose CNIC and background we can actually verify, not just take their word for it."
- "Our last helper couldn't produce ID at the gate and it became a whole issue, we don't want that again."
- "We want a guard with a police character certificate, not just someone in a uniform."

These are reasonable expectations, and they're the baseline of our screening process, not an upgrade or add-on.

## Our process for Askari 10 & 11 placements

1. CNIC and address verification, cross-checked against the details the candidate provides.
2. Reference checks with at least two previous employers.
3. A personal, in-person interview.
4. A police character certificate for any security-related role.
5. A trial period before any long-term commitment, so both sides can confirm the fit.

## Questions we get asked most about Askari 10 & 11 placements

**Can staff get through the gate on their first day without prior notice?**
Usually not smoothly. Both sectors expect the resident to inform gate security in advance when a new domestic worker starts, so we tell clients to flag this the moment a candidate is confirmed rather than the morning of.

**Do you place staff who've never worked in a cantonment area before?**
Yes, but we're upfront with clients about it, someone without cantonment experience may take a day or two to get used to the ID checks and logging procedures. If that's a concern, we can prioritize candidates who've worked in Askari or DHA sectors before.

**What happens if the guard or helper doesn't work out after we've hired them?**
That's what the trial period is for. If the fit isn't right, we arrange a replacement rather than leaving the household to start the search over from scratch.

**Is there a difference in hiring for Askari 10 versus Askari 11?**
Not in our process, both sectors fall under the same gate-security norms and the same screening standard. The difference is usually just which candidates happen to live closer, which we factor into the shortlist so commute isn't an issue.

## Why documentation matters more here than in open housing schemes

In sectors without gate security, a helper or driver who lacks paperwork is inconvenient. In Askari 10 and Askari 11, it can mean the person simply isn't let in, gate staff will turn away anyone whose CNIC doesn't match what's on file for that house, or who can't explain their purpose of visit. We build our verification process around that reality: every document we collect during screening is the same document your household will need to hand to gate security, so there are no surprises once someone's ready to start.

## Household types in Askari 10 & 11

Both sectors host a mix of serving officers' families who've relocated on posting and retired officers who've settled long-term. Newly posted families typically need a full staff setup, cook, helper, sometimes a driver, arranged quickly around a move-in date, while established households are usually replacing a single role and expect the new hire to fit into a routine that's already in place. We ask early on which situation applies, since it changes how we build the shortlist and how much we brief the candidate on the household's existing habits.

## What a typical Askari 10 & 11 booking looks like

A resident messages us with their sector, house number if they're comfortable sharing it, and the role they need. We confirm live-in versus live-out, any language preference, and whether a police character certificate is required. Within about 48 hours we send a shortlist of two or three candidates with verification already complete, the household interviews by phone or in person, and once a choice is made we confirm the gate-notification details so the candidate isn't turned away on day one. A trial period follows, and if it doesn't work out we arrange a replacement rather than leaving the household to start over.
