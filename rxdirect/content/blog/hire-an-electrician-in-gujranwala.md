## Our verification process for Gujranwala placements

Every electrician we shortlist for a Gujranwala placement goes through the same four-step screening, with no shortcuts, because the trust placed in someone working inside your home or business is real and the risks of cutting corners are too:

- **CNIC and address verification.** Every candidate's CNIC and home address are verified, so you know exactly who is being placed in your household and where they come from. This is the baseline, not a formality.
- **Previous employer and client references.** We speak directly with at least one previous employer or client by phone rather than relying only on a written reference, and we ask specifically about the kind of work the candidate handled, whether they showed up on time, and whether the client would use them again.
- **Practical skills assessment.** We run a practical assessment so a candidate's actual hands-on ability is confirmed, not just their confidence in describing it. An electrician who talks fluently about wiring but fumbles a basic breaker or motor connection reveals it here, which is exactly the point.
- **Tool and safety-equipment check.** We confirm the candidate arrives with their own tools and basic safety equipment, since an electrician who relies on borrowing tools from the household or workshop is not set up to do the job properly or safely.

## Seasonal and local considerations in Gujranwala

Gujranwala's electrical demands shift noticeably through the year, and an electrician with real experience in the city tends to already account for these patterns. Summer here is genuinely harsh, with temperatures regularly pushing past the high thirties, and that means AC units, water pumps and cooling systems run almost continuously across residential and commercial properties alike. Older wiring in Civil Lines and central Gujranwala homes is most likely to show strain under that load, with scorch marks at socket points and breakers tripping during peak afternoon hours. The industrial belt runs heavy loads through the hot months too, and motor winding and connection faults cluster in this period. Winter brings a smaller but real shift, with heating appliances and a rise in inverter and generator setup requests as loadshedding patterns change. The monsoon stretch in July and August brings water ingress into rooftop junction boxes and exterior points, and industrial dust through the rest of the year can also trip outdoor units and fittings. If your booking starts right before one of these periods, mention it so we prioritize candidates who have already worked through a full summer or monsoon cycle in Gujranwala rather than someone only tested in calmer months.
