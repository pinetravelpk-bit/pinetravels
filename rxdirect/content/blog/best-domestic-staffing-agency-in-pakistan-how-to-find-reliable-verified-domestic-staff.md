## *Why Verification Is Important When Hiring Domestic Staff*

*Hiring someone for your home is different from hiring for many ordinary jobs.*

*A domestic worker may spend several hours inside your home every day. A driver may transport your family members and children. A cook may prepare meals for your family. A caretaker may assist an elderly family member.*

*For these reasons, clients should take the hiring and verification process seriously.*

*Before selecting domestic staff, families should consider:*

### *1. Identity Verification*

*The worker's identity and basic information should be checked before placement.*

### *2. Previous Employment*

*Information about previous employers can help clients understand the worker's experience and employment history.*

### *3. Experience*

*Different jobs require different skills.*

*A cook should have cooking experience. A driver should have appropriate driving experience and a valid licence. A helper should understand household duties.*

### *4. Personal Interview*

*An interview allows the agency and client to understand the candidate's skills, communication and suitability.*

### *5. Clear Job Requirements*

*The client and worker should clearly understand duties, salary, working hours, accommodation arrangements and other employment terms before starting work.*
