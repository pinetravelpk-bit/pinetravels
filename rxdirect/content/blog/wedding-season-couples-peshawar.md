Wedding season in Peshawar does not arrive as a single event. From the cooler months into early spring, households across Hayatabad, Regi Model Town, Cantt, and the older central areas like University Town and Dalazak Road move through weeks of henna, baraat, and walima commitments that pull the whole family out of the house for long stretches. The house does not stop needing to run in their absence. Guests arrive from out of town and need to be fed, laundry piles up faster than any normal week, the kitchen runs on a shifted schedule, and the regular maid or cook is often stretched past their usual hours. That is the gap a temporary domestic couple fills. Two people who come in as a unit, take over the cooking, cleaning, and basic household upkeep for the wedding weeks, and step back once the season closes and the household returns to its permanent staff. We place temporary couples across Peshawar specifically for this window, and the process is built around the fact that you need cover that settles in fast, works as a team without supervision, and is verified to the same bar as a permanent placement.

## Why wedding season creates a couple gap in Peshawar households

In a typical Peshawar household, the daily load is split between family members and one or two permanent staff. The cook handles the kitchen. The maid handles cleaning and laundry. A family member manages guests and grocery coordination. Wedding season breaks that balance in a specific way. The family member who coordinated the household is now at the marquee, the tailor, or hosting out of town relatives. The permanent cook is asked to scale up from cooking for six to cooking for twenty, often while the kitchen itself is being used to prepare function food. The maid is cleaning guest rooms and common areas daily instead of the usual weekly rotation. The result is that the baseline household work, the everyday meals, the everyday laundry, the everyday cleaning, gets squeezed out, and the household starts the day already behind. A temporary couple absorbs that baseline load so your permanent staff can focus on the wedding-specific surge without burning out halfway through the season.

## How a temporary domestic couple is used during wedding season

The requests we get from Peshawar during wedding season tend to fall into a few clear patterns. The most common is a live-in couple brought in for a two to four week stretch covering the full cluster of events, taking over daily cooking, cleaning, and laundry while the household focuses on the wedding. The second is a live-out couple who come in for the daytime hours during the peak function week, handling meals and cleanup while the family is out at events. The third is a couple brought in specifically to manage guest quarters, cooking for and cleaning up after out of town relatives who are staying with the household through the wedding period. Each pattern solves a different gap, which is why we ask about the actual wedding calendar and the guest situation before suggesting a placement shape.

## Temporary versus permanent couple placement

A temporary couple placement is not a lighter version of a permanent one. The couple is doing the same work, running the kitchen, managing laundry, keeping the house in order, often with the added pressure of a household that is busier and more disrupted than usual. The difference is in how we brief the candidates and frame the engagement. A temporary couple knows the assignment has a defined end date, which means we look for a pair who can settle into an unfamiliar household quickly and work without needing the first week to learn the routine. We also look for couples who have done short engagements before, since not every couple that works well in a long term placement adjusts easily to a fast paced wedding season household. The verification bar is exactly the same as for a permanent placement. There is no reduced screening for a shorter assignment, because a live-in couple is in your home, around your family, and around your wedding guests, regardless of whether the engagement is two weeks or two years.

## Lead time for a wedding season couple in Peshawar

The earlier you reach out, the wider the shortlist. Ten days of lead time is usually enough for us to send two or three verified couples and for you to do a phone screen and a short in person meeting before confirming. Two weeks is comfortable, it lets us match a couple whose previous experience lines up with your household size and the kind of cooking your guests expect. A week is the realistic minimum, we can move that quickly because our couples are already verified and in our pool, but the shortlist is narrower and you may have to compromise on a preference like a specific cuisine or a live-in versus live-out arrangement. Peshawar's wedding season dates often cluster, which means multiple households are sometimes asking for cover in the same week. Reaching out as soon as your dates are set, even roughly, holds you a better place in the queue than waiting until the week of the first function.

## Managing your existing staff alongside a temporary couple

Most Peshawar households that ask for a temporary couple already have a maid, a cook, or both. The temporary couple is not there to replace them, and the brief we give the couple is explicit about that. The couple takes over the baseline household load, everyday meals, everyday cleaning, everyday laundry. Your permanent cook, if they are being asked to scale up for function food or guest cooking, is freed from the daily routine. Your permanent maid, if they are handling guest rooms, is freed from the everyday cleaning. We flag this boundary during the placement conversation because the most common friction in a temporary engagement is role overlap, the temporary couple and the permanent maid both trying to clean the same room, or the temporary cook and the permanent cook both in the kitchen at the same time. A clear division on day one prevents that. We also ask you to introduce the temporary couple to your permanent staff yourself on the first day, since a direct introduction settles the working relationship faster than anything we can arrange remotely.

## Our verification process for temporary couple placements

Every temporary couple we place in Peshawar goes through the same four verification steps as a permanent placement, with no shortcuts for the shorter engagement, and both individuals are checked, not just the partner who does most of the talking.

**1. CNIC and address verification for both individuals.** We check each person's CNIC separately and confirm the address and family details on each document. This matters because couples sometimes present themselves as married when they are not, or one partner may be using documents that do not belong to them. We verify both CNICs against each other as well, confirming the relationship they claim is consistent with what the documents show. A couple coming into your home for even two weeks, around your wedding guests, needs to be exactly who they say they are.

**2. Reference checks with previous employers.** We speak with previous households where the couple has worked as a unit, not just separate references for each person. Working as a couple is a skill of its own, and a previous employer who has seen them do it together can tell you things a single person reference cannot. We ask about how they divided responsibilities, whether they had any conflict that affected the household, and why they left. For temporary placements, we specifically ask whether the couple has handled short wedding season engagements before, since settling into a busy unfamiliar household fast is a skill not every couple has.

**3. Joint interview.** We interview the couple together, in person, in Peshawar. A joint interview reveals dynamics that separate interviews hide. Who answers first, who defers, whether they contradict each other on basic facts like previous employers or working hours, and how they handle a difficult question when they have to answer in front of each other. These are small observations, but they are the ones that predict how the couple will behave inside your household during a high pressure wedding week.

**4. Health screening.** Because a live-in couple is in constant proximity to the family, the kitchen, and the guests, we require a recent health screening for both individuals, including basic bloodwork and a chest X-ray. This is basic protection for the household and a reasonable expectation for any live-in placement, temporary or permanent.

A temporary placement still carries our replacement guarantee. We do not ask you to restart the search. The verification we did upfront is what makes this possible, since the replacement couple has already been through CNIC and address verification for both partners, reference checks, joint interview, and health screening. In a wedding season context, this matters more than usual, because you do not have the time to run a fresh search mid-season while functions are ongoing and guests are in the house.

## Questions Peshawar families ask us about wedding season couples

**Can we book a couple for just the week of the baraat and walima?** Yes. A one week engagement covering the main function days is a common request. Reach out a few days in advance so we can confirm availability, since those specific weeks tend to be in high demand across multiple households.

**Will the couple be comfortable cooking for out of town guests as well as the family?** Most experienced temporary couples are used to scaling up meals during wedding season. We ask about your expected guest numbers upfront so the couple we shortlist is prepared for the volume rather than surprised by it.

**What if the couple and our permanent maid do not get along?** This is exactly what the day one role division is meant to prevent. If friction still develops, tell us early and we will either re brief the couple on boundaries or arrange a replacement. We do not wait it out during a wedding week.

**Do you handle society gate registration for a temporary couple in Hayatabad or Regi Model Town?** We prepare the couple with the documents the society security typically asks for and brief them on the registration process. The final registration is handled between your household and the society administration per their current procedure.

**Can the same couple come back for the next wedding in the family a few months later?** Often yes, if they are still on our pool and available. Mention this possibility when you first book and we will flag the couple as a returning placement preference.
