Lahore's housing stock ranges from older kothis in Gulberg and Model Town to newer builds across DHA, Bahria Town, and Wapda Town, and that mix shapes how households here think about painting. Some want a fresh coat before a wedding, others need a full exterior repaint after a monsoon, and businesses along Main Boulevard and in commercial plazas need regular interior refreshes that fit around trading hours.

## What families and businesses in Lahore usually need

The requests we get from Lahore generally split into a few clear patterns:

1. **A full house repaint before a wedding or family event.** Lahore's wedding season runs through winter, and households across DHA and Model Town often want every room, the exterior facade, or both repainted to a tight deadline before guests arrive.
2. **Exterior repainting after monsoon damage.** July and August rains streak exterior walls across Cantt and Township areas, and by September many families want the facade restored before the dry season sets in.
3. **Office and shop refreshes.** Businesses along Main Boulevard Gulberg, MM Alam Road, and in Johar Town commercial areas call us for quick interior repaints that need to happen overnight or on a closed day so the shop or clinic can reopen without disruption.

## Our verification process for Lahore placements

Every painter we shortlist for a Lahore booking goes through CNIC and address verification, previous employer and client reference checks, a practical skills assessment where we ask them to talk through wall preparation and finish choices, and a tool and equipment check so we know they arrive with their own rollers, drop sheets, ladders, and sanding gear rather than borrowing on site. We do this before a candidate ever meets a client, because the gap between a painter who preps properly and one who just rolls paint over damp or flaking plaster shows up within months, not days.

## Trial period and replacement, explained simply

For larger multi-day jobs we treat the first day or two as the practical trial, where a painter's actual pace, surface preparation, and tidiness become clear. If the work isn't matching what was discussed, tell us the same day rather than letting it run, and we arrange a replacement from the shortlist instead of asking you to start over. For single-room touch-ups the trial is effectively the job itself, but the same guarantee applies: if the finish or cleanup isn't right, we follow up rather than leave you to chase it.

## What a typical Lahore booking looks like

Most bookings start with a WhatsApp message to /contact: the area (DHA Phase 5, Gulberg, Johar Town, or wherever the property is), the scope (one room, full house, exterior only, office), preferred dates, and a rough idea of finish, whether matte, satin, or a specific paint brand you already trust. We ask a few follow-up questions about wall condition, whether furniture needs to be moved or just covered, and whether the household will be occupied during the work. Then we send two or three verified painter profiles, usually within 48 hours. For larger jobs we encourage a short site visit before the start date so the painter can quote materials accurately, and we stay reachable through the first days in case the plan needs adjusting.

## Seasonal and local considerations in Lahore

Lahore's painting calendar is shaped by two things more than anything else: the winter smog and the monsoon. Exterior work is difficult from late November into February because high humidity and poor air quality slow drying and trap dust in the finish, so households planning an exterior repaint often book it for March or early April instead. Indoor work continues through winter but ventilation matters, paint fumes in a sealed room during smog season are worse than usual, so we brief candidates on keeping windows cracked where possible and working room by room. Monsoon in July and August brings its own issues, water streaks on freshly painted exterior walls, and damp patches that won't hold paint until the wall dries out. Wedding season, which peaks from November to February, is the single biggest driver of tight-deadline repaint requests, so if you have a shaadi date locked, reach out two to three weeks ahead rather than the week of, because good painters get booked early.

## Questions Lahore households ask us most

**Do the painters bring their own paint, or do we supply it?**
Either works. Most households prefer to buy the paint themselves so they control the brand and quantity, and the painter handles tools, rollers, drop sheets, and prep. If you want us to handle materials too, tell us and we'll quote it as part of the booking.

**Can you paint just one room, or is there a minimum job size?**
Single-room touch-ups are a normal booking for us, no minimum. We do ask that you tell us the room size and condition upfront so we match a painter whose daily rate fits the scope rather than sending someone used to larger projects.

**How do you handle furniture during a full house repaint?**
Painters we place bring drop sheets and masking tape and will cover or shift light furniture as part of the job. For heavier pieces we ask the household to arrange shifting in advance, or flag it and we'll quote an extra pair of hands for moving day.

**Is exterior painting safe during Lahore's smog season?**
We generally advise against full exterior repaints in peak smog months, the finish doesn't cure cleanly. Spot repairs and touch-ups are fine, but a full exterior is better scheduled for spring.
