Informal hiring is still how most households in Pakistan find a driver, and in a city like Gujranwala, where trade and manufacturing run the local economy, the informal route is especially common. A factory contact recommends someone, a relative passes along a name, or a driver from another household brings a friend who is "looking for driving work." It feels efficient because it skips paperwork, and for many families it has been the only option for years. The trouble is that a driver is not just someone who steers a car. They are alone with your vehicle, often with your children on the school run, and frequently trusted with cash and errands. When that person arrives with nothing but a verbal recommendation, the household is carrying the entire risk, and most families only find that out after something has already gone wrong.

## What informal hiring actually risks

A driver hired through a casual referral comes with none of the checks that should accompany any hiring decision. There is no driving license authenticity check, so a license that is expired, fake, or wrong for the vehicle class can go unnoticed until a traffic stop or, worse, an accident. There is no traffic violation history review, which means a pattern of reckless driving or outstanding fines stays invisible until it becomes your problem on the road. There is no CNIC and address verification, so if the person's identity turns out to be false, you have no way to trace them after a theft or a disappearance.

There are no reference calls either. You are relying entirely on whoever made the introduction, and that person often has no real idea how the driver behaves on the road, around children, or under pressure in Gujranwala's dense GT Road traffic. And when the placement goes wrong, which is common, there is no fallback. A driver who stops showing up, drives carelessly, or simply does not fit the family's routine leaves you back at zero. You restart the search yourself, ask around again, and hope the next referral works out. No one is accountable, and no one replaces the failed placement for you, which means the time you already spent is simply lost.

## What a verified agency actually does differently

A verified agency takes that risk off the household and runs it through a structured sequence of checks instead. For [drivers](/services/drivers) in Gujranwala, that sequence is built around what actually matters for someone who will be behind the wheel of your car every day.

The first step is a driving license authenticity check. We confirm the license is genuine, current, and valid for the vehicle class the household needs, whether that is a sedan, a larger SUV, or a commercial vehicle for factory runs. An expired or incorrect license is not something you want to discover during a traffic stop with your children in the back seat, and it is exactly the kind of detail an informal introduction never surfaces.

The second step is a traffic violation history review. We look at whether a candidate has a pattern of violations or outstanding fines that signal how they actually behave on the road, not just how they describe themselves in an interview. A clean record does not guarantee a careful driver, but a history of repeated violations tells you something a confident interview answer never will.

The third step is CNIC and address verification. We confirm the identity document is genuine and that the address on it matches where the candidate actually lives. For Gujranwala specifically, this also matters for commuting, a driver who lives far from DHA Gujranwala or Model Town may struggle with early school-run start times, and an accurate address helps us match someone who can actually get to your gate on time every morning.

The fourth step is a road test evaluation. We do not rely on paperwork alone. A candidate drives for us, on the kind of roads they will actually face in this placement, including the calmer internal roads of a gated society and the far more congested routes into Gujranwala's older commercial and industrial areas. How someone handles a crowded intersection or a narrow lane in the older parts of the city tells you more than any license ever can, and it is a check no informal introduction will ever run for you.

## The replacement guarantee, specifically

This is the part that genuinely separates an agency from an informal hire. Every driver we place comes with a replacement guarantee. If a placement is not working out, during the trial period or shortly after, we go back to the shortlist and arrange a replacement rather than leaving the household to start the search from zero.

That matters most in exactly the situations where informal hiring falls apart. A driver who interviews well but turns out to be careless in traffic. A candidate who is reliable for a month and then starts arriving late for the school run. A placement where the driver is comfortable with family errands but panics on the longer factory and warehouse trips that DHA Gujranwala households frequently need. In every one of these cases, an informal hire leaves you with nothing, you either tolerate a bad fit or start the search over by yourself. With a verified agency, you message us, we return to candidates we have already screened, and we line up an alternative. The time you spent is not wasted, because the screened pool is still there and ready to draw on.

The replacement guarantee is not a sign that we expect placements to fail. Most of them do not. It exists because even careful screening cannot predict every fit, and a household should not absorb the full cost of a mismatch when one does happen.

## The time and effort you actually save

The hidden cost of informal hiring is the time it consumes. Asking around, meeting candidates yourself, inspecting their licenses, calling references, and then restarting when a placement fails, all of that is unpaid work that falls on the household. For a family running a business in Gujranwala, that time is genuinely scarce, and the search often drags on far longer than expected because there is no structure behind it.

A verified agency compresses all of that into a single conversation. You tell us the area, the vehicle, whether the role is full-time or part-time, and what kind of driving you need, school runs, business travel to factories and warehouses, or general errands. We take that brief and return with a shortlist of two or three candidates who have already cleared every check. You do the final interview and a short trial drive, but you are choosing from a screened pool rather than assembling one yourself. Most Gujranwala households we work with have a shortlist in hand within 48 hours of first contact, which is a fraction of the time an informal search tends to take.
