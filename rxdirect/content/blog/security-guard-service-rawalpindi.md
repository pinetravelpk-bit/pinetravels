Rawalpindi's proximity to multiple cantonment areas and Askari sectors means many households expect a security standard that goes beyond a uniform, genuine documentation, a clean record and comfort with structured entry procedures.

## Our screening process

Rawalpindi sits next to multiple cantonment boundaries and Askari sectors, and residents there are used to structured entry procedures, a guard who can produce ID and documentation without being asked twice, and who follows a consistent gate routine rather than an improvised one. During reference checks we specifically probe past employers about how candidates handled visitor logging and access control in similar cantonment-adjacent settings, alongside the standard CNIC verification, police character certificate and physical fitness check every guard goes through.

## Shift options

Because cantonment-adjacent streets often already have a baseline of activity and patrols, many Rawalpindi households opt for a single day or night guard who reinforces that existing routine rather than duplicating full coverage. For properties further from cantonment boundaries, or those wanting round-the-clock coverage regardless, we run full 24-hour rotations split between two guards on 12-hour shifts.

## Questions we're asked most in Rawalpindi

**Do guards come with their own uniform?** Yes, every guard we place arrives uniformed and presentable from day one.

**Can we request a guard with prior cantonment or ex-forces experience?** Where available, yes, let us know upfront and we'll prioritize candidates with that background during shortlisting, though availability varies by shift and timing.

**What happens if the guard doesn't show up one day?** This is exactly what our replacement policy covers, tell us immediately and we'll arrange interim or replacement coverage rather than leaving your property unattended.

**Is a single guard enough for a household, or do we need two?** Most single-family homes are well covered by one guard on a day or night shift; round-the-clock coverage is really only necessary for larger properties or households specifically wanting zero gaps in coverage.

## Trial period and replacements

Fit matters as much as credentials for a security placement, how a guard actually handles your street, your household's routine, and your specific access points only becomes clear after a few real shifts. If something isn't working, tell us and we'll arrange a replacement rather than leaving you to manage an unsuitable placement.

## Documents and verification you can expect

Before a guard is ever shortlisted to you, we've completed CNIC verification, obtained a police character certificate, run a physical fitness check, and spoken with at least one previous employer about reliability and conduct. We share a summary of this background with you before the introductory meeting, and for cantonment-adjacent placements specifically, we confirm the guard's documentation is in order well before their first shift so gate registration doesn't cause a delay.

## Uniform, equipment and reporting

Every guard arrives uniformed from the first shift, this isn't something you need to arrange or supply. Standard equipment typically includes a torch for night shifts and a visitor/gate log for recording entries and exits, which we encourage every household to use even where a gated society already keeps its own separate record. On reporting, guards are expected to flag anything unusual, an unfamiliar vehicle, a repeated visitor, a late-night disturbance, directly to you rather than deciding on their own whether it's worth mentioning. If you want a more formal daily handover note or log, let us know and we'll brief the guard accordingly during placement.
