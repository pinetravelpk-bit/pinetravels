DHA Multan's first phase alone spans roughly 9,500 acres across six phases, which means a driver who only knows one part of the community will struggle once your household's routine covers school runs, office commutes and errands across different sections.

## What we check before placing a driver in DHA Multan

- **Original driving license verification**, checked against the CNIC, not just a photocopy.
- **Traffic violation and accident history**, cross-referenced with references where possible.
- **A road test evaluation**, including questions about specific routes between phases.
- **Comfort with gate security procedures**, since DHA Multan requires staff registration at entry.

## Full-time or part-time

Drivers are available for full-time schedules (typically including school pickup/drop-off and office commuting) or part-time arrangements for households with lighter needs. Some DHA Multan families also ask for an hourly arrangement during a specific season, exam months when school timing gets unpredictable, or a stretch when one family member needs a daily hospital run. We can usually structure a placement around that instead of pushing everyone into the same full-day contract.

## What a typical booking looks like

Most requests start with a short WhatsApp message: which phase you're in, whether the driver needs to handle school pickup, office commuting, or both, and roughly what hours you need covered. From there we pull candidates from our existing pool who've already cleared license verification and reference checks, and run the DHA-specific part of the screening, the road test questions about moving between phases and the gate registration conversation, before anyone gets shortlisted to you. You'll typically see two or three profiles with their verification notes, not just a CV. Once you pick someone, we handle the introduction and the first few days are treated as a settling-in period where we stay reachable if anything about the routine needs adjusting.

## Trial period and replacements

We don't consider a placement finished the moment a driver starts. If a driver isn't working out in the first stretch, timing issues, a mismatch in how they handle the car, or anything else, tell us and we'll find a replacement rather than leaving you to start the search over on your own. It's a much smaller ask than vetting a stranger off a classified ad, since the replacement still comes from the same verified pool.

## Questions we get asked most in DHA Multan

**Do you place drivers who can also handle the school run alone, without a parent in the car?** Yes, though for younger children most families prefer the driver's first few runs to be supervised so everyone's comfortable before it becomes routine.

**Can the same driver cover both a Phase 1 house and a Phase 5 office?** That's exactly what the road test screens for, we specifically avoid shortlisting candidates who only know one phase for households with a split routine like this.

**What if our schedule changes week to week?** Tell us upfront if your hours vary, we'll flag it during matching so the driver you get is someone who's already comfortable with a less fixed routine, rather than someone expecting a rigid 9-to-5.

## How we match a driver to your household

Matching isn't just picking whoever's next on the list. We weigh which phase your house sits in, whether the routine is school-run heavy or office-commute heavy, and whether you need someone who can handle a child in the car unsupervised or would rather ease into that. A driver whose recent placements were all corporate commutes in Phase 5 isn't automatically the right pick for a family in Phase 2 needing a patient school-run driver, even if both are technically "DHA Multan experience", we look at the actual mix of duties, not just the address.

## Documents and verification you should expect

Before a driver is shortlisted to you, we've already verified the original CNIC and driving license in person, taken down at least two employer references with direct phone contact, and confirmed there's no unresolved traffic or accident history. When the driver starts, it's reasonable to ask to see this documentation again yourself, and to keep a copy of the license and CNIC number on file at home, most DHA Multan households do this anyway as part of registering the driver with gate security.

## Seasonal considerations in DHA Multan

Multan's summer heat changes the daily rhythm more than people expect. School timing often shifts earlier in the hottest months, which means driver schedules need to flex around that rather than stay fixed to a normal-season routine. We flag this during matching for placements starting in summer, since a driver used to a rigid winter schedule can be caught off guard by an early-morning-only school run that then leaves a long midday gap before the office pickup.
