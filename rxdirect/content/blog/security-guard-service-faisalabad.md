Faisalabad's newer gated communities like Wapda City and Citi Housing Faisalabad maintain their own community security desks, and many households additionally want a dedicated private guard for their own property.

## Our screening process

Faisalabad's economy runs on textile mills and small manufacturing units, and a fair number of our guard placements are for owners who live next to, or manage, a warehouse or workshop alongside their home. Beyond the standard CNIC verification and police character certificate, we specifically ask candidates about experience with goods movement, vendor check-ins and gate logs, skills that matter when a property sees regular delivery and staff traffic rather than just family visitors. Reference checks with previous employers and a physical fitness check round out the process before anyone is shortlisted.

## Shift options

Because many Faisalabad clients are managing a home alongside a shop, mill office or rental unit, we often set up split coverage: a day guard focused on gate and delivery management, handing over to a stricter night shift once commercial activity around the property winds down. Straight 24-hour rotations with two guards on 12-hour shifts are available too, tell us your property's daily traffic pattern and we'll recommend a schedule.

## What a typical booking looks like

Most Faisalabad inquiries start on WhatsApp with the area, whether the property is purely residential or has an attached workshop or shop, and the preferred shift. We follow up with a few questions about the gate setup and how much delivery or vendor traffic the property sees day to day, since that shapes which candidates get shortlisted. From there we typically send two or three profiles with background summaries, arrange a short introductory meeting between the client and the guard, and confirm placement once both sides are comfortable. Most requests move from first message to a guard on duty within 48 hours.

## Residential vs. mill-adjacent commercial placements

A guard suited to a straightforward family home isn't automatically the right fit for a house that shares a wall or gate with a workshop or small mill office, even though both fall under "residential" on paper. The first calls for someone attentive to family routines and visitor courtesy. The second needs someone comfortable questioning an unfamiliar delivery vehicle and keeping a basic gate log without prompting. We ask clients to flag this distinction upfront so we're not matching a purely household-minded guard to a property with regular commercial foot traffic.

## Trial period and replacements

A guard's fit with a household often only becomes clear after a week or two on the job. If something isn't working, a scheduling mismatch, a personality clash, or anything else, let us know and we'll arrange a replacement rather than leaving you to manage the gap. The goal is a guard who actually suits your property's daily rhythm, not just someone who cleared screening on paper.

## Documents and verification you can expect

Every guard we place has completed CNIC verification, holds a police character certificate, has passed a physical fitness check, and has had at least one previous employer contacted directly about reliability and conduct. For Faisalabad placements involving an attached workshop, mill office or shop, we go a step further and ask past employers specifically about the candidate's comfort with vendor check-ins and gate logs, not just standard household visitor traffic. We share this background summary with you before the introductory meeting so you're deciding with full information, not just a first impression.

## Uniform, equipment and reporting

Guards arrive uniformed from their first shift, so there's nothing for you to arrange or launder. Standard equipment includes a torch for night duty and a visitor/gate log, which we recommend keeping even on properties with modest traffic, since it becomes a useful record if anything ever needs to be checked later. Given how many Faisalabad placements involve some mix of household and commercial activity, we specifically train guards here to log delivery and vendor visits with a bit more detail than a purely residential posting would need, vehicle description, rough time, and who they were there to see. On reporting, guards are expected to flag anything unusual directly to you rather than deciding on their own it isn't worth a mention.
