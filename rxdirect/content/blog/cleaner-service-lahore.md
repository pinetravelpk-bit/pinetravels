Lahore's dense mix of homes, offices and short-let apartments in areas like Gulberg and DHA means cleaning needs vary a lot, some households want a quick daily tidy, others need a thorough weekly deep clean.

## Corporate office cleaning in Gulberg and Model Town

A large share of our Lahore requests come from small-to-mid-size offices in Gulberg and Model Town rather than households, law firms, agencies and consultancies wanting a consistent after-hours clean a few times a week. These placements typically prioritize discretion (working around a live office) and reliability over speed, since the same cleaner returning consistently matters more to an office than a household.

## What we check before placing a cleaner

Every candidate goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, and a personal interview. For office placements, we weight reliability and consistency heavily in reference checks, since a corporate client is trusting the same person with access to their workspace repeatedly.

## One-time deep clean vs. a recurring arrangement

A one-time deep clean tends to get booked around a move, a renovation wrap-up, or ahead of a specific event, and we look for a candidate comfortable with a heavier single session rather than routine light maintenance. A recurring arrangement is a different match altogether, for a Gulberg or Model Town office, it usually means the same person coming in a few evenings a week, which matters because a live office is trusting that individual with repeated access rather than a one-off visit from a stranger. Households sometimes start with a one-time clean to see how a candidate works before deciding whether to move to a weekly routine, and that's a reasonable way to approach it if you're not sure yet.

## Seasonal demand in Lahore

Requests pick up noticeably ahead of Eid and other family occasions, when both DHA households and Gulberg offices want a heavier clean done before guests or clients arrive. Lahore's smog season also drives a real increase in bookings, since dust settling on floors, furniture and window sills means many households want extra attention during those months rather than sticking to a lighter routine. Offices see a smaller but steady seasonal bump too, often tied to year-end or new-year deep cleans before a fresh business quarter starts. Letting us know about an upcoming busy stretch a little in advance means we can have suitable candidates ready rather than searching cold.

## What documents and verification you should expect to see

Every candidate goes through CNIC verification and an address check, and we speak with at least one previous employer directly rather than relying on a written reference alone. For office placements specifically, we also confirm a candidate has no issue being registered as a recurring visitor if the building requires it, which is common in parts of Gulberg. Clients can ask to see a candidate's CNIC copy before confirming, and we're happy to walk through what the reference check covered if that would help a household or office feel more confident before the first visit.

## Trial period and replacements

The first couple of visits are where most fit issues become obvious, pace, attention to detail, comfort with an office's particular routine, so we treat that early window as an informal trial even without a separate contract step. If a Gulberg office or a DHA household finds the placement isn't working, the sooner we hear about it the faster we can shortlist someone else; waiting weeks to raise it just makes the eventual switch more disruptive than it needs to be.

## What a typical booking looks like

Most office clients start with a message describing their space, preferred cleaning window, and how many times a week they need coverage. We shortlist candidates with relevant experience, law firm and agency clients in Gulberg often want someone used to working around a live office rather than an empty one, share a short profile, and arrange next steps once the client picks someone. Household bookings in DHA follow a similar but simpler path, usually landing on a weekly slot that fits the family's own weekend.
