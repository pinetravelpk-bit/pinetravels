## Our verification process for Faisalabad placements

Every cleaner we shortlist for a Faisalabad home or business has already completed four checks before you meet them: CNIC and address verification, reference checks, a personal interview, and a punctuality and reliability track record review. ## Seasonal and local considerations in Faisalabad

Faisalabad's industrial dust is a year-round factor, but it worsens in the hotter months when drier conditions and factory activity keep particulate in the air, so households in open areas like Peoples Colony and Garden Town often want their weekly clean moved to a twice-weekly schedule through May and June. The summer heat, regularly past forty degrees, also affects a live-out cleaner's commute, so we weight travel distance more heavily during these months. Ramadan compresses the working day for businesses, which shifts a commercial cleaner's ideal arrival time earlier, and Eid brings a predictable spike in one-time deep-clean requests ahead of family gatherings. The textile and trading calendar also creates busier stretches for many households, when a more frequent clean is worth adding temporarily. Letting us know about these seasonal patterns in advance helps us prepare the candidate and adjust the schedule smoothly.
