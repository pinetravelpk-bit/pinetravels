Lahore's residential culture has always valued outdoor space, and neighborhoods like DHA, Gulberg, Model Town, Garden Town and Johar Town are full of houses with lawns, planted driveways and kitchen gardens that need regular attention. The city's long, hot summer and distinct monsoon mean a garden left unattended for even a couple of weeks can go from healthy to stressed very quickly. A dependable gardener, or mali, is not a luxury for Lahore households with outdoor space, it is what keeps that space from becoming a problem.

## What families and businesses in Lahore usually need

The requests we get from Lahore generally follow three patterns:

1. **A live-out gardener visiting two to three times a week** for a household in DHA or Gulberg, covering mowing, watering, pruning and seasonal upkeep on a fixed schedule.
2. **A daily gardener for a larger property** in Model Town or Garden Town, where the outdoor footprint is significant enough to need someone on site every day through the peak growing season.
3. **A gardener for an office, school or commercial property** with landscaped outdoor space, where the role includes maintaining appearance for visitors alongside routine plant and lawn care.

## Our verification process for Lahore placements

Every gardener we shortlist for a Lahore placement goes through the same screening before we share their profile with you. We run CNIC and address verification to confirm identity and residence, which is particularly relevant in Lahore where the working population includes people coming in from surrounding towns and villages. We conduct reference checks by calling previous employers directly, whether households or commercial properties, rather than accepting a written reference alone, because a short phone conversation reveals how someone actually maintains a garden day to day in a way no signed letter can. We hold a personal interview to assess communication and reliability. And we run a practical skill assessment, asking candidates about seasonal plant care, lawn treatment in Lahore's heat, irrigation timing and tool handling, because the gap between an experienced mali and someone with minimal knowledge shows up quickly when you ask specific questions about soil, watering depth and how to handle summer stress on grass.

## Trial period and replacement, explained simply

Every placement begins with a trial period. This is not a formality or a lack of confidence in our own screening, it is a realistic safeguard. Even a verified, experienced gardener may not suit your specific outdoor space, your plant varieties or your expectations for how the garden should look through the seasons. If the first candidate does not work out during trial, we go back to the shortlist we already built, incorporate your feedback about what was not working, and arrange a replacement without asking you to restart the search from scratch. The trial exists to catch mismatches early, when they are still straightforward to resolve.

## What a typical Lahore booking looks like

Most Lahore bookings start with a WhatsApp message. You tell us the area, the rough size of the outdoor space and whether you need daily visits or a few times a week. We follow up with a few targeted questions: what kind of plants and lawn you have, whether there are specific landscaping features like hedges, flower beds or kitchen gardens, and whether tools and equipment are available at the property or the gardener needs to bring their own. From there we send a shortlist of two or three verified candidates, usually within 48 hours. You do a phone screen with your preferred candidate, have them visit the property if you want them to see the space before confirming, and set a start date. Because Lahore's residential areas are spread across a very wide footprint, we prioritize candidates who already live within a manageable commute of your property, since a gardener traveling from Township to DHA every day is a placement at risk of attrition.

## Seasonal and local considerations in Lahore

Lahore's seasonal cycle is one of the most demanding for garden maintenance in Pakistan. Summer from May through August is brutal on lawns, and a gardener who understands how to manage watering depth, shade timing and heat stress is essential, not optional. Monsoon from July to September brings rapid growth but also fungal issues, waterlogging and drainage problems that need active management. Winter is milder than Islamabad but lawns still go dormant and the focus shifts to pruning, clearing and preparing for spring. Spring and autumn are the busiest periods for planting and lawn recovery, and demand for experienced gardeners peaks during those windows. Ramadan shifts working hours across the city, and many households want their gardener to come earlier or later in the day, so we confirm candidates are comfortable with adjusted timings before placement. If your garden has specific seasonal needs or particular plant varieties, tell us upfront so we shortlist someone whose experience matches rather than discovering the gap after placement.

## Questions Lahore households ask us most

**Can the gardener bring their own tools, or do we need to provide equipment at the property?** Many gardeners we place own basic hand tools like shears, but larger equipment like mowers, trimmers and blower machines is usually expected to be available at the property. Tell us what you already have so we match accordingly.

**What happens if the gardener we hire does not maintain the lawn to the standard we expect?** Tell us as soon as you see the issue. The trial period exists for exactly this, and we will arrange a replacement from the existing shortlist rather than asking you to wait it out and hope it improves on its own.

**Can you place a gardener who knows how to manage a kitchen garden or seasonal vegetables?** Yes, some of the gardeners we place have experience with kitchen gardens, seasonal vegetables and herbs. If you have a kitchen garden or want one started, flag it when you first reach out so we screen for that specific experience during the skill assessment.
