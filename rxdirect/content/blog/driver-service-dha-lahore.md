DHA Lahore households rely on drivers for a wider range of daily routines than most other parts of the city, school runs to campuses on the far side of the society, office commutes into the city center, and errand-running across a road network that, in some phases, is still being extended. Finding a driver who knows the area well, has a clean record, and can be trusted with a family car isn't a small ask, which is why most families we work with want someone properly vetted before they hand over the keys.

## What families in DHA usually need

Requests from DHA Lahore fall into a few common patterns:

1. **A daily family driver** handling school drop-offs, pickups, and errands during the day, usually live-out.
2. **A full-time driver** for households where one or more family members commute daily to offices outside DHA.
3. **An experienced driver for a specific vehicle type**, some households have a particular car (manual transmission, larger SUV, or a vehicle requiring extra care) and want someone already comfortable driving it.

## Our verification process for DHA placements

Every driver we place goes through CNIC verification, license validation, a driving record check, reference checks with previous employers, and an in-person interview that includes an assessment of local road knowledge. For DHA specifically, we confirm familiarity with the internal road layout, the newer phases toward the ring road have a different, less familiar network than the older sections near Defence Mall, and knowing the difference matters for punctuality.

## Trial period and replacement, explained simply

Every driver we place starts with a trial period rather than an immediate long-term arrangement. A driver can have a clean record and solid references and still not be the right fit for your car, your family's schedule, or your household's expectations around communication and punctuality, that's a normal part of finding the right match, not a failure of screening. If the trial doesn't work out, we go back to the shortlist and arrange someone else rather than starting the process over.

## What a typical DHA booking looks like

Most requests begin with a WhatsApp message: phase, vehicle type, daily schedule (school runs, office hours, or both), and live-in or live-out preference. We ask a few follow-up questions, any specific routes, preferred working hours, whether occasional weekend or evening availability is needed, and put together a shortlist of two or three candidates. Families typically do a phone interview, then a short in-person trial drive with the top choice before confirming. Given how spread out DHA is, we also weigh which phase a candidate currently lives in, since that affects both commute reliability and familiarity with your specific part of the society.

## Questions DHA families ask us most

**Can you find a driver who already knows DHA's newer phases near the ring road?**
Yes, we track which candidates have prior driving or placement experience in the newer sections and prioritize them for households located there, since the road layout and access points differ from the older, more established phases.

**Do you place drivers experienced with larger vehicles or manual transmission?**
Often, yes. We ask about this directly during screening and only shortlist candidates who've actually driven that vehicle type professionally.

**How do you handle households needing a driver for both school runs and evening events?**
We flag this combination upfront so the shortlist only includes candidates comfortable with a split schedule and evening availability, rather than assuming it will work out after hiring.

## What documents and verification you can expect

Every driver we place comes with a documented verification history rather than just an interview and a handshake. This includes a verified CNIC, a checked driving license, a driving record review, at least one reference check conducted directly with a past employer, and notes from an in-person interview that includes an assessment of local road knowledge. For DHA Lahore placements, we also record which phase a candidate has prior experience in, since that's directly relevant to punctuality and route familiarity here. If you'd like to review any part of this before confirming a placement, we're happy to walk you through it.

## Questions DHA families ask us most (continued)

**Our household is right on the edge of the older and newer phases, does that complicate the match?**
Not usually, though we do flag it during intake. Candidates with experience straddling both the established areas near Defence Mall and the newer sections toward the ring road tend to be a good fit for households in this kind of location, and we prioritize them where available.
