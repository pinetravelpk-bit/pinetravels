## The three business models

Most home staff hiring agencies in Islamabad work in one of three ways. Understanding which one you are dealing with explains a lot about how the agency will behave.

### 1. One-time placement fee

The family pays a fee once a worker is confirmed. The worker receives their full salary directly from the family. The agency's income depends on placing workers who stay, because failed placements lead to free replacements.

**Good for families because:** the agency's interest is aligned with a lasting match, and the worker is paid in full.

### 2. Monthly commission

The family pays the agency every month, and the agency pays the worker, keeping a share. Some agencies call this "managed staff".

**Watch for:** the worker may receive much less than you pay, which makes them more likely to leave. Ask exactly how much reaches the worker.

### 3. Charging the worker

Some middlemen charge workers a fee to be placed, or take part of their first months' salary. The family may pay little or nothing.

**Why to avoid it:** workers who start a job in debt to a middleman are under pressure, often leave quickly and are sometimes encouraged to move on so the middleman can place them again and earn another fee. This model also harms vulnerable workers.

## Legal registration

A legitimate home staff hiring agency should be a registered business. Ask to see registration documents, such as company incorporation with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan or registration with the relevant provincial labour department. Registration does not guarantee quality, but it does mean the business has a legal identity, an address and accountability.

## The paperwork you should receive

When you confirm a worker through a serious agency, you should receive:

- A copy of the worker's CNIC
- The worker's current and permanent addresses
- A recent photograph
- An emergency contact for the worker
- A summary of reference checks
- A written note of the placement fee paid
- The replacement terms in writing

Keep these together. You will need them for police registration and if anything goes wrong.

## Warning signs to walk away from

Be cautious if a home staff hiring agency in Islamabad:

- **Has no office,** only a mobile number, and avoids meeting in person
- **Asks for the full fee before you have seen any candidate**
- **Will not tell you the fee** until you have "chosen" someone
- **Sends a worker the same day** with no checks possible
- **Wants to collect the salary** and pay the worker themselves, without explaining how much the worker receives
- **Has no written replacement policy,** or offers only a few days
- **Discourages you from calling references** or police registration
- **Sends candidates who seem coached,** giving identical answers or unable to describe their own past jobs
- **Pressures you** with "this worker will go elsewhere if you do not decide now"
- **Offers very young workers.** Children cannot legally be employed as domestic workers, and any agency offering them should be avoided entirely

## Questions about the replacement guarantee

The replacement guarantee is where agencies differ most. Ask:

- How long does the guarantee last from the date of placement?
- What reasons are covered: the worker leaving, unsuitable work, misconduct?
- How many replacements are included?
- Is there any extra charge for a replacement?
- How quickly will a replacement be sent?

## How agencies source workers, and why it matters

Ask where the agency's workers come from. Good agencies build pools of workers through referrals from staff they have placed successfully, registrations at their office and returning workers. Agencies that rely on whoever happens to call that day tend to have weaker candidates. Workers who can [register](/staff/register) in person, bring their documents and be interviewed by staff are more likely to be genuine and committed.

## The first call: what a good agency asks you

A good agency asks as many questions as you do. Expect to be asked about:

- Your sector or society and how workers would reach it
- The size of the house and number of family members
- Children, elderly parents, pets
- The duties and hours
- Live-in or live-out, and the quarters if live-in
- Your budget
- Any previous staff and why they left

If an agency asks almost nothing and promises to send someone straight away, it is matching on availability rather than fit.

## Comparing timelines

Fast is good, but not at any price. A realistic timeline from a careful agency in Islamabad is:

- **Shortlist:** one to three days for common roles
- **Interviews:** within the same week
- **Trial:** three to seven days
- **Confirmation:** at the end of the trial

Specialised roles such as experienced nurses, English speaking nannies or chefs may take longer. An agency that promises a fully checked worker within hours for every role is probably skipping checks.

## Reading reviews and recommendations

Online reviews help, but read them carefully. A page full of five star reviews posted on the same day, all in the same style, tells you little. Look for reviews that mention specific details: the role, the area, how long the worker stayed and how the agency handled a problem. Recommendations from neighbours and colleagues are often more useful, especially if they have used the agency more than once. Ask them what happened when a placement did not work out, because that is when an agency shows its real quality.

## Aftercare: what happens after the worker starts

A good home staff hiring agency Islamabad families stay with does not disappear once the fee is paid. Ask what aftercare looks like. At minimum, the agency should check in during the first weeks, be reachable if there is a problem with the worker, help resolve misunderstandings about duties or salary, and arrange a replacement quickly if needed. Workers also need someone to speak to if they are being treated unfairly. An agency that supports both sides after placement prevents many small problems from becoming reasons to leave.

## Specialised roles need specialised checks

For a helper or cleaner, standard checks are usually enough. For specialised roles, ask the agency what extra steps it takes. For [home nurses](/services/nurses/islamabad), it should check nursing qualifications and registration and discuss clinical experience. For [nannies](/services/babysitters-nannies/islamabad) caring for newborns, it should test infant care knowledge and call families with babies. For [caretakers](/services/caretakers/islamabad) looking after a parent with dementia or after a stroke, it should ask about similar past cases. For [drivers](/services/drivers/islamabad), it should see the licence and road test them. If the agency treats every role the same way, the specialised roles will suffer.

## Using more than one agency

Some families contact two or three agencies at once to see more candidates. That is fine, but be open about it, and confirm with only one. Make sure you understand each agency's fee terms so you do not owe a fee to an agency whose candidate you did not hire.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I know a home staff hiring agency Islamabad families use is genuine?

Visit its office, check its registration, ask how it verifies workers and ask for the fee and replacement terms in writing. A genuine agency will answer all of these clearly.

### Should I pay the fee before meeting candidates?

No. A fair agency charges the placement fee only after you confirm a candidate, usually after a trial.

### Is it normal for the agency to pay the worker's salary?

Some agencies do this, but ask exactly how much reaches the worker. We recommend paying the worker directly so they receive the full amount.

### What if the agency closes or stops answering after placement?

This is why a physical office, legal registration and written terms matter. Choose an agency that is easy to find and accountable.
