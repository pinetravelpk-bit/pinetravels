Lahore's mix of young dual-income families in Johar Town and larger established households in DHA and Model Town means helper requests range from a few hours of daily cleaning to full live-in household support.

## Two very different household patterns in Lahore

Johar Town's younger dual-income couples mostly want a live-out helper for a fixed window each day, cleaning and laundry done before or after work hours, with minimal supervision needed. DHA and Model Town's larger, more established households tend to want the opposite: a live-in helper who becomes part of a standing team alongside a cook or nanny, managing the home day to day rather than working a fixed slot. We ask upfront which pattern fits before shortlisting, since the two need genuinely different candidates.

## What we check before placing a helper

Every helper we place in Lahore goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, a personal interview and a health screening. For live-in placements specifically, we also weigh experience managing a household independently, since there's less day-to-day supervision than in a fixed-hours live-out role.

## How a Lahore placement usually goes

It starts with a message telling us your area and what kind of help you need, live-in, live-out, and roughly how many hours or how large the household is. From there we shortlist candidates already vetted through our verification process and send over profiles for you to review. Most households ask for a short interview, either in person or by phone, before deciding. Once you've picked someone, we coordinate the start date and check in during the first few weeks to make sure the arrangement is working the way you expected, whether that's a Johar Town family confirming the fixed-hours schedule suits them, or a DHA household making sure the live-in helper is settling into the existing team.

## Part-time hours vs. full-time live-in

Johar Town's younger households are a good example of why this distinction matters: a couple both working office hours often just needs someone for a defined block, cleaning and laundry done in a set window, no live-in arrangement required. Larger DHA and Model Town homes tend to need the opposite, someone present through the day who can flex with whatever comes up. Neither is "better", it depends on household size, whether there's already other staff in place, and how much day-to-day coordination the home actually needs. We ask about this early so we're not proposing a live-in candidate for a household that only needs a few hours a day.

## What's covered under general helper duties

Standard helper work covers cleaning, laundry, dishwashing and general tidying. It doesn't automatically include cooking (a separate role), driving, or childcare unless that's discussed and screened for specifically at the outset. If you need a blended role, say, housework plus keeping an eye on kids after school, say so when you first reach out, so we can match someone who's actually suited to it rather than finding out it's a mismatch after they've started.

## Language and communication fit matter more than people expect

Lahore households vary a lot in what language they run the home in, some DHA and Gulberg families communicate mostly in English, others in Urdu, and many switch between Urdu and Punjabi depending on who's talking. We ask about this before shortlisting, because a helper who's fluent in Punjabi but shaky on Urdu instructions can still be a poor fit for a household that expects everything explained in Urdu, and vice versa. For live-in placements especially, where the helper is around for meals, guests and daily routine, we try to match not just language but general comfort with how formal or informal the household is, a Model Town family running a fairly structured routine needs a different temperament than a Johar Town couple who mostly just want the basics handled without much back-and-forth.

## Questions we get asked most in Lahore

**Can I ask for a helper who's worked in DHA or Gulberg before, not just anywhere in Lahore?** Yes, tell us if you'd prefer someone with experience in a similar type of household or neighborhood, and we'll prioritize that in the shortlist, though it can mean a slightly longer search than taking the first available match.

**What if I need someone who can also open the gate for guards or gated society security checks?** Common in DHA and some Model Town blocks, let us know at the inquiry stage and we'll confirm the candidate is comfortable with the ID and registration process your society requires.

**Is it normal to change the schedule after the helper has started, say from live-out to a couple of live-in nights a week?** It happens, particularly around exams or when a family travels. Tell us as early as you can, we'd rather help you adjust the existing placement than have you start from scratch with someone new.
