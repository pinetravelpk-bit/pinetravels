"Trained" is one of the most overused words in domestic staffing. A cook who has made dal and roti for one family for a year is described as a trained cook. A young woman who has looked after a neighbour's child is described as a trained nanny. Families then discover, a week into the job, that trained meant something very different to the person who used the word.

This guide sets out what trained domestic staff Islamabad families should expect in each common role. It describes practical skill standards, simple ways to test them before hiring, and how to keep building skills once a worker has joined your household. Use it as a checklist when you interview, or as a training plan for staff you already have.

## What "trained" should mean

A trained domestic worker is someone who:

- **Knows the core tasks** of the role and can do them without supervision
- **Follows hygiene and safety standards** that protect the family
- **Uses equipment and products correctly,** without damaging the home
- **Understands household etiquette:** how to speak to family and guests, how to serve, how to respect privacy
- **Can handle common problems:** a burnt dish, a stain, a sick child, a flat tyre

Formal certificates in domestic work are rare in Pakistan, so most training is on the job. That makes testing skills before hiring essential. It also means that a worker's training depends heavily on the households they worked in before. A maid who spent four years with a careful family in F-8 may be better trained than one who spent ten years in a home where nobody cared how things were done. That is why we ask previous employers not only whether a worker was honest, but how they did the work. The answers tell us what training the worker actually received, and where they may need a little more guidance from their next family.

## Trained maids: the standard

A trained [maid](/services/maids/islamabad) in Islamabad should be able to:

- Clean every room in a logical order, top to bottom, dry to wet
- Clean bathrooms properly, including tiles, fittings and drains, with the right products
- Care for different floors: marble, tiles, wooden flooring, carpets and rugs
- Use a washing machine correctly, separate whites and colours, and read basic care labels
- Iron cotton, lawn, chiffon, silk and khaddar at the right heat
- Make beds neatly and change sheets regularly
- Keep cleaning cloths separate for kitchen, bathroom and general use
- Store cleaning chemicals safely, away from children and food

**Quick test:** ask the maid to clean one bathroom and iron two different fabrics while you observe.

## Trained cooks: the standard

A trained [cook](/services/cooks/islamabad) should:

- Cook everyday desi food consistently: salans, dal, rice, roti, sabzi, chicken and meat dishes
- Adjust oil, salt and spice for health needs, such as diabetes, blood pressure or heart conditions
- Handle meat and vegetables hygienically: separate boards, washed hands, proper thawing
- Store leftovers safely and label them
- Plan menus and grocery lists for a week
- Keep the kitchen clean while cooking, not only at the end
- Cook for guests: larger quantities, presentation and timing

Many cooks in Islamabad also offer Chinese, continental, baking or BBQ. Test these separately rather than accepting a list.

**Quick test:** ask the cook to prepare two dishes of your choice in your kitchen, and watch how they work as well as how the food tastes.

## Trained drivers: the standard

A trained [driver](/services/drivers/islamabad) should:

- Hold a valid licence for the vehicle type
- Drive calmly and defensively in Islamabad and Rawalpindi traffic, including the Expressway, Kashmir Highway, Murree Road and busy sector markets
- Know main routes and alternatives during road closures
- Perform daily checks: tyres, oil, water, lights
- Handle a flat tyre and basic breakdowns
- Keep the car clean inside and out
- Follow child safety rules: seat belts, child seats, doors locked, no phone while driving
- Keep fuel and mileage records honestly

**Quick test:** a twenty minute drive including a busy road, a U-turn, tight parking and a short stretch at speed.

## Trained nannies: the standard

A trained [nanny](/services/babysitters-nannies/islamabad) should know:

- Safe handling, bathing, feeding and sleeping for infants
- Bottle sterilisation and formula preparation
- Age appropriate play and learning activities
- How to manage tantrums calmly, without hitting or shouting
- Basic first aid: choking, burns, falls, fever
- When to call parents or a doctor
- Hygiene: hand washing, nappy changing, clean feeding areas
- Routines for meals, naps and bedtime

**Quick test:** ask scenario questions. "The baby has a temperature of 102 at night. What do you do?" "A toddler is choking on a grape. Show me what you would do."

## Trained caretakers: the standard

A trained [caretaker](/services/caretakers/islamabad) for an elderly person should be able to:

- Help with walking, transfers from bed to chair and toilet safely
- Assist with bathing and dressing while protecting dignity
- Give medicines on time according to a chart, and record them
- Recognise warning signs: confusion, breathlessness, chest pain, a fall, not eating
- Prevent bedsores by changing position for bedridden patients
- Prepare soft or special diet food when needed
- Offer patient company and conversation

**Quick test:** ask how they would help a person with weak legs get from the bed to the bathroom, and what they would do if the person fell.

## Trained nurses: the standard

A [home nurse](/services/nurses/islamabad) should have formal nursing training and registration, and be able to handle the clinical tasks your patient needs: injections, IV lines, wound dressing, catheter care, vital signs monitoring, feeding tubes or post-surgery care. Always check qualifications and ask about experience with similar patients.

## Trained security guards: the standard

A trained [security guard](/services/security-guards/islamabad) should:

- Control the gate: check visitors, confirm with the family, keep a register
- Stay alert, especially on night shifts
- Know what to do in an emergency: fire, break-in, medical emergency
- Handle deliveries and couriers safely
- Communicate politely but firmly
- Where ex-service, have records to confirm it

## Training common to all trained domestic staff Islamabad homes employ

Whatever the role, trained domestic staff in Islamabad should also understand:

### Hygiene

Hand washing after toilet, before food and after cleaning; clean uniforms or clothes; covered cuts; staying home or reporting when ill with something infectious.

### Safety

Gas safety for stoves, geysers and heaters, including ventilation and what to do if they smell gas; electrical safety with wet hands and overloaded sockets; safe storage of chemicals and medicines; locking doors and gates.

### Etiquette

Greeting family and guests politely; serving tea and food properly; knocking before entering rooms; keeping family matters private; not using family phones or belongings without permission.

### Communication

Reporting problems honestly and quickly, such as a broken item, a child's injury or a strange visitor at the gate.

## Real training versus claimed training

In interviews, many candidates say they are trained. A few signs help you tell genuine skill from a confident claim:

- **They describe the process, not just the result.** A trained maid explains that she cleans the toilet last and uses a separate cloth. An untrained one says she "cleans everything nicely".
- **They ask about your equipment.** A trained cook asks whether you have an oven, a chopper or a pressure cooker before promising a dish.
- **They know what not to do.** A trained nanny can list things she would never do with a baby, such as propping a bottle and leaving, or shaking a crying infant.
- **Their references confirm the specific skill.** A previous family says "she made the best biryani for our dinners", not just "she was good".
- **They are comfortable being tested.** Genuine trained domestic staff Islamabad agencies place rarely object to a practical test. Reluctance is a warning sign.

## Seasonal training for Islamabad homes

Islamabad's climate adds a few skills that staff from other cities may not have.

**Summer food safety.** From May to August, heat and humidity make food spoil quickly. Cooks should know to refrigerate cooked food within two hours, avoid leaving yoghurt, raita and cut fruit outside, and throw away anything that smells or looks wrong. Upset stomachs in children often start in the kitchen.

**Monsoon hygiene.** Damp clothes and cupboards grow mould. Maids should know how to air rooms on dry days, keep wardrobes slightly open and dry laundry fully before folding. Standing water near the house should be cleared to keep mosquitoes away during dengue season.

**Winter heating safety.** Staff should know to switch off gas heaters before sleeping, never use them in closed rooms without ventilation, keep blankets and curtains away from heaters and report the smell of gas immediately. This one piece of training can save lives.

These homes often need staff who can cook continental breakfasts and Western dishes, serve formally at dinners, follow strict allergy instructions, communicate in basic English and respect different cultural practices around food and privacy. ## How to train staff on the job

Even trained domestic staff need to learn your household's way of doing things. A simple approach:

1. **Show, do not just tell.** Demonstrate how you want a task done the first time.
2. **Watch them do it.** Give calm feedback.
3. **Write it down.** A short list on the kitchen wall for routines, such as the cleaning order or meal plan.
4. **Check after a week.** Has the routine stuck?
5. **Praise improvement.** Recognition motivates more than criticism.

For more complex skills, consider short courses or online videos. Many cooks learn new dishes from Urdu cooking channels. First aid courses are offered by organisations such as the Red Crescent and private trainers in Islamabad. Paying for a course is an investment that also shows the worker you value them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do trained domestic staff in Islamabad have certificates?

Some do, especially nurses, and some drivers have defensive driving training. Most domestic workers learn on the job, so practical tests are more reliable than certificates.

### How can I test a worker's skills quickly?

Ask them to perform the main task in your home: cook a meal, clean a bathroom, drive a short route or answer scenario questions about childcare or elderly care.

### Should I pay for my staff to take courses?

It is often worth it, especially for first aid, cooking and driving. Agree in advance whether there will be a salary increase once the skill is used.

### What if a trained worker does not meet the standard?

During the trial and within six months of placement, we replace the worker without another placement fee.
