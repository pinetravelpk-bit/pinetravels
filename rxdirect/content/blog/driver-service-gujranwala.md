Gujranwala's spread between established areas like Satellite Town and newer developments like DHA Gujranwala and Palm City means a driver needs genuine familiarity with the full city, not just one neighborhood.

## Route familiarity across old and new Gujranwala

A driver who's only worked the older grid around Satellite Town often struggles once a household's routine stretches out to DHA Gujranwala or Palm City, where street layouts are newer and still filling in. During the road test, we specifically have candidates describe a route connecting one of the established areas to one of the newer developments, rather than just navigating a single familiar loop, it's a quick way to tell whether someone can actually cover a family's full day, from a Satellite Town school run to an evening pickup out toward Palm City.

## What we check before placing a driver

Every driver goes through original driving license verification against the CNIC (not a photocopy), a check of traffic violation and accident history cross-referenced with references where possible, and calls to at least two previous employers. Given how much of Gujranwala's growth is happening in newer societies, we weight the road test toward candidates who can comfortably work across both the old city core and the newer developments, since most households here don't stay confined to one part of town.

## Full-time, part-time, or hourly

Not every household needs a driver on the road all day. We place drivers on full-time schedules covering school runs and office commuting, part-time arrangements for families who mainly need morning and evening coverage, and hourly setups for households that already have someone driving most days but want backup during a busy week or a family trip. Tell us upfront which shape fits your household and we'll match accordingly rather than defaulting everyone to a full-day contract.

## What a typical booking looks like

A booking usually starts with a message telling us your area, Satellite Town, DHA Gujranwala, Palm City, or wherever, along with the hours and routine you need covered. We match candidates from our existing screened pool rather than starting from zero, run the route-familiarity part of the road test if it hasn't already been done recently, and send you a short list with notes on license verification, reference checks, and how they did on the route description. You choose, we introduce, and the first week or two functions as a settling-in period where we're still reachable if the routine needs tweaking.

## Trial period and what happens if it's not a fit

Placements aren't a one-shot deal. If the driver you choose turns out not to suit your household's pace or communication style, let us know during the early weeks and we'll work on a replacement instead of leaving you to restart the search independently. The replacement is pulled from the same vetted pool, so it's a faster process than the initial search.

## Questions we get asked most in Gujranwala

**Do you place drivers who can also run errands outside school and office hours, like grocery runs?** Yes, most family placements here end up covering some combination of school, office, and general errands, and we ask about that mix upfront so the driver you get is prepared for it rather than surprised by it.

**Is it a problem if our house is in a newer society without a firmly established address?** Not usually, though we flag it during matching so we prioritize candidates who've already worked in newer developments and are used to navigating without a long-established street grid.

**Can a driver split time between two households, say ours and a relative's nearby?** That's something to raise directly with us, it changes the screening and scheduling conversation, so it's better discussed before the placement than adjusted after.

## How we match the right driver to your household

We don't just send the first available name. Matching starts with the shape of your daily routine, school runs, office commuting, errands, or some mix, and cross-references it against a candidate's recent placement history, not just their general years of experience. A driver who's spent the last year doing quiet, fixed-hour corporate commutes isn't necessarily the right fit for a household with a chaotic, ever-changing school and activity schedule, even if both are technically "family driving" on paper. We also weigh communication style: some households want a driver who proactively flags delays and route changes, others prefer someone quieter who just gets the job done, and we ask about that preference before shortlisting.

## Documents and verification you should expect

Before any candidate reaches your shortlist, we've already checked their original CNIC against their driving license in person, called at least two previous employers directly, and reviewed their traffic and accident history as far as it's traceable. When the driver joins your household, it's worth asking to see this documentation yourself and keeping a note of the license number and CNIC on file, useful if you ever need to reference it with a housing society's gate security or in the rare event of a dispute.

## Traffic patterns and timing across the year

Gujranwala's traffic thickens noticeably during the Ramadan and wedding-season months, when both school and office schedules tend to compress into narrower windows. Drivers who've worked through a full year here tend to build in more buffer time during those stretches rather than treating every month as identical. If your booking starts during one of these busier periods, mention it, we'll prioritize candidates with a demonstrated track record of steady timing under compressed traffic rather than someone only tested in quieter months.
