Multan's rental-heavy areas like Gulgasht Colony and established Cantt-area homes both generate steady demand for household helpers, ranging from a few hours of daily cleaning to full live-in support.

## Rental turnover vs. settled households

Gulgasht Colony's rental market means a good share of our Multan requests come from tenants who've just moved in and need a helper matched quickly, often on a live-out basis while they settle into a new routine. Cantt-area households, by contrast, are usually long-settled families looking for continuity, a live-in helper who stays with the household for years rather than a short-term arrangement. We flag this distinction early, since a helper who thrives in a fast-turnover rental setting isn't necessarily the right fit for a household wanting long-term stability.

## What we check before placing a helper

Every helper we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, a personal interview and a health screening. For Cantt-area long-term placements, we weight reference checks toward candidates with a track record of staying with one household, rather than frequent short-term stints.

## Questions we get asked most in Multan

**I've just moved into a rental in Gulgasht, how fast can someone start?** New tenants are a big share of our Multan requests, so we prioritize speed for move-in situations, matching a live-out helper who can start once you've had a chance to interview them.

**We've had the same helper for years and she's retiring, can you find someone similar?** This comes up a lot in Cantt-area households. We ask about the outgoing helper's routine and household expectations so the replacement isn't a jarring change for a family used to years of continuity.

**Do you only place live-in helpers, or can I get someone for a few hours a day?** Both, plenty of Multan households, especially newer renters, want part-time or hourly help rather than a full live-in commitment, and we match accordingly.

**What happens if it's not a good fit after a few weeks?** Tell us early. We'd rather adjust the placement in the first few weeks than have you stay quiet and unhappy with the arrangement.

## Trial period in practical terms

Because Multan has such a mix of short-term renters and decades-long settled families, we don't treat every placement the same way. Early weeks function as a settling-in period for both sides, the household gets a feel for the helper's pace and habits, and the helper adjusts to the home's routine. If it's clearly not working, raise it with us and we'll look at alternatives rather than treating the first match as final.

## What's included in helper duties

General helper work in Multan placements covers cleaning, laundry, dishwashing and everyday tidying of the home. It doesn't typically extend to cooking full meals, driving or dedicated childcare, those are separate roles we screen for differently. If a Gulgasht renter or a Cantt-area family wants a combined role, such as light housework alongside occasional supervision of children, mention it at the inquiry stage so we can match someone genuinely comfortable with the mix, rather than assuming it can be added on later.

## Language and communication fit

Multan households vary in what language they expect a helper to be comfortable with day to day, some Cantt-area families run the household mostly in English or Urdu with domestic staff, while other homes, including many in Gulgasht Colony, communicate primarily in Saraiki or Punjabi. We ask about this at the inquiry stage rather than assuming, since a helper who's perfectly capable but struggles to follow instructions comfortably in the household's usual language tends to create friction that has nothing to do with the quality of their work. For households with elderly members or young children who mainly speak one language at home, we try to prioritize candidates who are already fluent in it, rather than expecting everyone to adapt to a helper's language over time.

## How we match the right helper to your household

We start with what kind of continuity your household actually needs, a Gulgasht renter who just moved in usually wants someone who can start quickly on a live-out basis, while a settled Cantt-area family is often looking for a helper who'll stay for years, which changes who we prioritize from our reference checks. Household composition matters too: homes with young children or elderly residents tend to do best with a candidate who's already comfortable working around people who are home all day, rather than someone used to empty houses. We also weigh language fit and the specific mix of duties you need, cleaning only, or cleaning alongside occasional supervision, before narrowing the shortlist, so the first candidate you meet is already a reasonable fit rather than a starting point for trial and error.
