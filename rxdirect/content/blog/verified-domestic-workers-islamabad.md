Almost every agency and middleman now says its workers are "verified". The word appears on WhatsApp statuses, Facebook posts and business cards. But verified can mean anything from "we saw a photocopy of his CNIC" to a careful, multi step background check. If you are looking for verified domestic workers Islamabad families can genuinely trust, you need to know what a real check involves, so you can ask the right questions and judge the answers.

This guide breaks verification into layers. For each layer, it explains what should be checked, how, what it tells you and what it cannot tell you. It also explains how you can verify a worker yourself if you are hiring through a referral.

## Why verification matters more in Islamabad

Islamabad is a city where many households hold sensitive positions. Senior government officers, judges, military officers, diplomats, journalists and business leaders live in the sectors and societies, often with official documents, valuables and security concerns at home. Many homes also have elderly parents or young children left in the care of staff for long hours.

At the same time, a large share of the domestic workforce comes from outside the city, from villages in Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Azad Kashmir, Gilgit-Baltistan and beyond. Most are honest, hard working people. But without proper checks, families have no way to know who they are letting into their homes. Verification closes that gap. It also protects the many honest workers in the city, who lose out when families become suspicious of everyone because of a few bad experiences. When checks are routine and fair, good workers are trusted faster, paid better and treated with more respect from the first day.

## Layer 1: Identity

**What is checked:** The worker's original Computerised National Identity Card.

**How:** The original card is examined, not a photocopy or a photo on a phone. The photo is matched to the person in front of you. The CNIC number, full name, father's or husband's name, date of birth and permanent address are recorded. The card is checked for signs of tampering and for expiry.

**What it tells you:** That the person is who they say they are and is an adult Pakistani citizen.

**What it cannot tell you:** Anything about character, history or skills.

**Watch out for:** Candidates who only have a photocopy, say the original is "with a relative", or have an expired card and no renewal receipt. Ask them to bring the original. A genuine candidate will.

For further assurance, the CNIC details can be checked against NADRA's verification services where available. Islamabad Police registration of the worker also links their CNIC to your address.

## Layer 2: Address

**What is checked:** Both the permanent address on the CNIC and the current address where the worker lives in Islamabad or Rawalpindi.

**How:** The current address is confirmed through a visit, a reliable local contact or a landlord. The permanent address is noted, and where possible a family contact there is recorded.

**What it tells you:** Where the worker can be found, and that they have roots and family who know them.

**What it cannot tell you:** Whether they will stay at that address.

Many domestic workers in the capital live in areas such as Bhara Kahu, Tarlai, Golra, Sohan, Khanna, Dhok Kala Khan and the older katchi abadis near the sectors. An agency that knows these areas can confirm addresses properly.

## Layer 3: Employment history and references

**What is checked:** Where the worker has worked before, for how long, and what previous employers say.

**How:** Previous employers are called directly, not just listed. Specific questions are asked:

- How long did they work for you?
- What were their duties?
- Were they trusted with cash, keys or children?
- Were they punctual?
- Why did they leave?
- Would you hire them again?

**What it tells you:** How the worker actually behaved in a real household over time. This is the most predictive layer of all.

**What it cannot tell you:** Whether a previous employer is being fully honest. That is why more than one reference is better, and why the tone of the answer matters as much as the words.

**Watch out for:** References who cannot be reached, numbers that belong to the worker's own relatives, or employers who are vague about dates.

## Layer 4: Police and legal record

**What is checked:** Whether the worker has a criminal record.

**How:** Workers can obtain a police character certificate from the police in their home district or current area. In Islamabad, employers should also register domestic workers with the local police station, which creates a record linked to the household.

**What it tells you:** Whether there is a known criminal record.

**What it cannot tell you:** Anything that was never reported. Many minor thefts in households are never taken to the police, which is why references still matter.

## Layer 5: Skills

**What is checked:** Whether the worker can actually do the job.

**How:** Practical tests suited to the role:

| Role | Practical check |
|---|---|
| [Cook](/services/cooks/islamabad) | Prepares two or three dishes, observed for hygiene, taste and timing |
| [Driver](/services/drivers/islamabad) | Road test in Islamabad traffic, licence checked, basic car knowledge |
| [Maid](/services/maids/islamabad) | Cleaning and ironing demonstration, knowledge of products and surfaces |
| [Nanny](/services/babysitters-nannies/islamabad) | Scenarios on feeding, sleep, fever, choking and discipline |
| [Nurse](/services/nurses/islamabad) | Qualifications and registration checked, clinical scenarios discussed |
| [Security guard](/services/security-guards/islamabad) | Experience, alertness, visitor handling, ex-service record where claimed |

**What it tells you:** Whether the worker is capable now.

**What it cannot tell you:** Whether they will perform consistently. That shows in the trial and the first months.

## Layer 6: Health and fitness

**What is checked:** Whether the worker is fit for the role.

**How:** A basic health conversation and, for roles involving food, children, the elderly or patients, a medical check covering common infectious conditions such as hepatitis and tuberculosis.

**What it tells you:** That the worker is fit to work and unlikely to pass on serious infections to the family.

**What it cannot tell you:** Future health. Families should allow for illness like any employer.

## Layer 7: Interview and judgement

**What is checked:** Attitude, honesty, communication and expectations.

**How:** A face to face interview by someone experienced in placing domestic staff. The interviewer looks for consistency between what the worker says, what the documents show and what references report. They also explain the job honestly to the worker and watch how they respond.

**What it tells you:** Whether the overall picture holds together.

**What it cannot tell you:** Everything. Experienced judgement reduces risk but cannot remove it.

## The limits of any verification

No check can guarantee future behaviour. A worker with a clean record and excellent references can still make a bad decision. Verification shifts the odds strongly in your favour, but it works best together with:

- A paid trial before confirming
- Clear house rules and duties
- Sensible handling of cash, keys and valuables
- Fair pay and treatment, so the worker has every reason to stay honest

Be wary of anyone who promises "100 percent guaranteed" workers. Honest providers explain what they check and what they cannot.

## Red flags during verification

Most workers are open about their background. A few patterns should make you slow down and look more carefully:

- **Different names.** The name the worker uses does not match the CNIC, with no clear explanation such as a nickname.
- **Gaps that change.** The worker says they were in one job for two years, but the reference says six months.
- **Every reference is a relative.** Previous "employers" turn out to share the worker's surname or village.
- **Pressure to skip steps.** A middleman insists the worker must start today and there is no time for references.
- **Reluctance about police registration.** A genuine worker has nothing to fear from being registered.
- **No contact at home.** The worker cannot give any family contact at the permanent address.

None of these alone proves anything, but two or three together mean the check is not complete.

## Verification does not end on day one

Circumstances change. A worker who was verified three years ago may have moved house, changed phone numbers or had family changes. Every year or so, update the worker's current address, phone number and emergency contact, check that the CNIC has not expired and renew the police registration if your area requires it. This is also a good moment to ask how the worker is doing and whether anything at home is worrying them. Keeping records current protects you both and shows the worker that you take the arrangement seriously.

## Live-in, live-out and part-time workers

The level of checking should match the level of access. A live-in maid or nanny who sleeps in your house and looks after your children needs every layer above, including health checks. A full-time live-out cook or driver needs the same identity, address, reference and skill checks. Even a part-time cleaner who comes twice a week sees your home and possibly your valuables, so identity, address and at least one reference are the minimum. Verified domestic workers Islamabad households employ part-time should never be treated as "too small to check".

## How to verify a worker yourself

If you are hiring through a friend or relative's recommendation, you can still do the essential checks:

1. See the original CNIC and take a clear photo of both sides.
2. Ask where they live now and, if possible, visit or confirm through someone you trust.
3. Call at least one previous employer yourself.
4. Ask for a police character certificate, or register the worker with Islamabad Police.
5. Test the main skill before confirming.
6. Run a paid trial of a few days.

It takes a little time, but it is far less work than dealing with a problem later.

## Questions to ask any agency about verification

When an agency says its workers are verified domestic workers Islamabad families can trust, ask:

- Do you see the original CNIC yourselves?
- How do you confirm the current address?
- Do you call previous employers, and can you share what they said?
- Do you arrange or ask for a police character certificate?
- Do you test skills before sending candidates?
- Do you share copies of documents with me?
- What happens if something goes wrong after placement?

A good agency will answer each one clearly and without irritation.
