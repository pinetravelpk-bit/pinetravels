If you drive for a living in Karachi, you already know this city does not forgive a driver who does not know it. The traffic, the choke points, the routes that work at ten in the morning and are unusable by five in the evening, all of that is knowledge you carry in your head, and it is worth real money when a family or a company needs someone they can trust behind the wheel. The difficulty is that most households and offices in Clifton, Korangi, DHA and the business districts no longer hire a driver on a verbal reference alone. They want to see that your license is genuine, that your record is clean, and that someone has actually watched you drive. That is what the verified badge with RX Direct gives you. This guide explains what getting verified involves, what to bring, what the road test is like, and what happens once you are placed.

## Why the verified badge matters for you as a driver

A driver in Karachi usually finds work through a contact, and that can take weeks with no guarantee. When you are verified, a family or company does not have to guess about you. They can see your license has been checked against your CNIC, that your traffic history has been reviewed, and that you passed a real road test rather than a five-minute spin around the block. That trust is what opens up the better placements, family drivers in DHA and Clifton with steady hours, and corporate commutes where punctuality is non-negotiable. Verification is not just a screen we put you through for the client's benefit, it is what gets you paid fairly and stops you being swapped out for the next cheaper driver who walks in.

## Documents you need to bring

Get these together before you apply:

1. **Your original driving license.** Not a photocopy, not a photo on your phone, the original card. We check it in person and we verify it against your CNIC, because a license that does not match the person holding it is the first thing that ends a placement and the easiest thing to catch. If your license is expired or up for renewal, renew it first, we cannot place you on an invalid license.
2. **Your original CNIC**, plus a photocopy. The CNIC has to match the name on the license. If there is any discrepancy, a spelling difference, an old card, a mismatched father's name, sort it out with NADRA before you come in, because we cannot verify around it.
3. **References from at least two previous employers.** These can be families you drove for or companies you drove for. We will call them to ask about your punctuality, your driving, and why you left. A reference who remembers you well and speaks positively makes the process faster.
4. **A recent photograph** for your profile.

If you only have one reference because you are early in your driving career, tell us. We can sometimes work with one strong reference and a more detailed road test, but do not give us a fake contact, we will find out, and it ends your registration with us immediately.

## Step one, driving license authenticity check

The first check is on the license itself. We confirm the license is genuine, that it is current, and that it belongs to you and matches your CNIC exactly. This is not a formality. Karachi has a real problem with forged and borrowed licenses, and the families and companies we place with know that, which is exactly why they come to us instead of hiring off the street. Once your license clears this check, we move on. If it does not, we tell you straight rather than wasting your time.

## Step two, traffic violation history review

Next we review your traffic violation and accident history. We are not looking for a perfect record, anyone who has driven Karachi traffic for years has a story. What we are looking for is a pattern, repeated speeding, reckless driving complaints, or a history of accidents that suggests a habit rather than bad luck. A couple of old challans will not disqualify you. A string of recent serious violations will, and we will tell you why rather than leaving you to guess. Be honest about anything on your record when we ask, because finding it ourselves later is worse than hearing it from you first.

## Step three, CNIC and address verification

We then verify your CNIC and the address on it. For drivers this matters in two ways. First, it confirms who you are, which the family or company will want on file for their own security registration. Second, your actual address affects which placements make sense for you. A driver who lives in Korangi and gets matched to a family in DHA can end up with a commute that makes punctuality impossible on bad traffic days, so an accurate address helps us match you to a household or office you can reliably reach. If you move, tell us, an outdated address becomes a problem the day a family needs to reach you in an emergency.

## Step four, the road test evaluation

This is the part every driver asks about, so here is what it actually involves. You drive, and we watch. We are not just checking that you can start the car and move it forward. We look at how you handle the car in real traffic, how you manage a junction, how you position yourself in a lane, how you brake, and how you react when someone cuts you off. We also ask you to describe, from memory, a route between two points in Karachi, say from Clifton to an office in Korangi, and to talk through how you would adjust that route during evening rush hour. A driver who can only recite one memorized path usually shows it here, and a driver who genuinely knows the city usually shines here. There is no trick, drive the way you actually drive every day, and if you know the city, you will do fine.

## How placement works

Once you are verified, you do not have to chase work on your own. When a family in DHA or Clifton needs a driver, or a company needs someone for a corporate commute, we send them verified profiles that fit the schedule and the area. If you are the match, we set up a meeting between you and the household or office. You get to ask questions too, what the hours are, whether it is a company car or your own, whether there are school runs or night shifts involved. A placement only goes ahead when both sides are comfortable. You are not pushed into a role that does not suit you.

## How pay works

We keep pay transparent. Before you start, we agree your monthly figure, your off days, and any overtime or extra-duty terms with the household or company, so there is no confusion after a week of driving. We do not encourage clients to undercut verified drivers, and we will not push you into a placement that pays below what your experience and your clean record warrant. Fair wages and clear terms are part of the arrangement from day one, not something you have to negotiate alone after the fact.

## What happens after placement

After you start, the first week or two is a settling-in period. We stay reachable, if the schedule is not what was agreed, or if the household's expectations change, or if something about the role is not working for you, message us and we step in. Every placement comes with a replacement guarantee for the client, but for you it means this: if a placement genuinely is not a fit, we would rather move you to a better-matched role than have you stuck somewhere unhappy and putting your record at risk. We do not disappear once you are behind the wheel.
