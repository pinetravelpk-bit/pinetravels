## Bosan Road commerce vs. Cantt's established offices

Bosan Road's newer commercial offices are mostly trading and distribution businesses, where office boys handle a steady stream of supplier visits and outgoing courier packages alongside the usual pantry work. Cantt's older, more established offices and clinics run a quieter, more fixed routine, filing, scheduling support and general tidiness matter more than fast turnaround. We ask which pattern fits before shortlisting candidates.

## What we check before placing a candidate

Every office boy we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, and a personal interview. For Bosan Road's busier trading offices, we also confirm the candidate can manage a higher volume of courier and supplier traffic without losing track of tasks.

We ask a few clarifying questions about the daily workload, whether it's mostly courier and supplier traffic or a quieter filing-and-scheduling role, and shortlist candidates who've handled that kind of pace before. ## Full-time vs. part-time support

Bosan Road's trading and distribution offices generally want full-time coverage, since supplier visits and courier pickups happen throughout the day without much of a lull. Cantt's older offices and clinics are more likely to need part-time or fixed-hours coverage tied to clinic timings or a smaller office's actual working hours. We talk through which pattern fits before shortlisting, since it narrows the pool of realistic candidates either way.
