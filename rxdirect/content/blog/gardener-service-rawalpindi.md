DHA Rawalpindi and Bahria Town households with dedicated lawns are our most frequent gardener requests in the city, though established Cantt-area homes with mature gardens ask for regular upkeep too.

## Cantonment gardens vs. newer suburban lawns

Rawalpindi's Cantt-area homes often sit on land with long-standing garden layouts and mature trees, and getting a gardener onto the property can involve the same cantonment gate and CNIC-check procedures that apply to other household staff there, something we prepare candidates for in advance. DHA and Bahria Town households, further out, tend to have newer, larger open lawns without the same access formalities, so the work there is more about regular mowing and getting new planting established.

## Our verification process

Every gardener we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks, a personal interview, and a skills assessment. For Cantt-area placements, we also confirm candidates have valid CNIC documentation ready ahead of time, since cantonment gate procedures require it before they can be cleared onto the property.

## Questions we get asked most in Rawalpindi

**Why does a Cantt-area gardener need CNIC documentation ready in advance?** Cantonment areas require gate clearance for household staff, and having a candidate's CNIC and details sorted before their first visit avoids delays getting them onto the property.

**Is a full-time mali necessary for a DHA or Bahria Town lawn?** Not always, a lot of newer suburban lawns are manageable with a gardener visiting a few times a week, especially while the planting is still being established rather than fully mature.

**Do Cantt gardens need a more experienced gardener?** Generally yes, since mature, long-standing layouts call for more careful pruning and tree care than a newer open lawn does, so we lean toward more experienced candidates for those placements.

**What's included in a standard visit?** Mowing, edging, weeding, pruning, and a check of irrigation lines, with seasonal planting or new layout work agreed separately depending on what the household wants done.

## Trial period and replacement

Given the extra step of cantonment clearance for Cantt-area placements, we try to get the fit right the first time, but if a gardener isn't working out, in Cantt or in DHA and Bahria Town, tell us and we'll shortlist a replacement rather than making the household navigate the process again from scratch.

## What a typical booking looks like

Most requests start with a WhatsApp message naming the area, Cantt, DHA, or Bahria Town, along with a rough sense of the garden's size and whether it's an established layout or a newer plot. If it's a Cantt placement, we flag early that the candidate will need CNIC details ready for gate clearance, so that step doesn't hold up the first visit. Once a household reviews the shortlist and confirms someone, we arrange the introduction directly, and the first visit is usually set up within a day or two so both sides can see how the working relationship feels before committing to a regular schedule.

## Full-time vs. part-time arrangements

Not every Rawalpindi household needs a gardener on site daily. A newer DHA or Bahria Town lawn that's still filling in often works well with a gardener visiting a few times a week, since the workload, mowing, edging, basic upkeep, is fairly predictable once the planting is established. Cantt-area gardens with mature trees and long-standing layouts more often benefit from a full-time or near-daily mali, particularly where pruning and tree care need closer, ongoing attention rather than a fixed weekly visit. We talk through the property and how hands-on the household wants to be before recommending one arrangement over the other.

**What if I only need seasonal help, not a year-round arrangement?** We can discuss shorter-term placements, though many households find it simpler to keep the same gardener throughout the year so they already know the property's layout and quirks.
