As Pakistan's largest commercial hub, Karachi has no shortage of offices, clinics and small businesses that rely on a dependable office boy to keep daily operations running, pantry management, courier runs, filing support and general errands.

## High-rise offices bring their own building rules

Clifton and Defence office towers typically run their own front-desk security and visitor-registration procedures, so an office boy placed there needs to be comfortable being logged in daily and following a building's own access rules, not just showing up to a normal office door. Gulshan-e-Iqbal's clinics and smaller trading offices are more relaxed on that front but expect faster turnaround on courier runs given the area's traffic. We place with the specific building type in mind.

## What we check before placing a candidate

Every office boy we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, and a personal interview. For high-rise placements specifically, we also confirm the candidate has documentation ready for building security sign-in, so there's no delay getting them cleared on day one.

## What a typical booking looks like

Requests usually come in through WhatsApp with a short description of the office, a high-rise in Clifton, a clinic in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, a trading office somewhere in between, and the hours needed. We ask whether the building has its own visitor and security sign-in process, since that changes which candidates we shortlist and what paperwork they'll need on day one. Once we've got a shortlist ready, most clients meet the candidate briefly or run a short trial shift before confirming, particularly for high-rise placements where building access has to be arranged in advance.

## Questions we get asked most in Karachi

**Does the office boy need his own documentation for building security?** Yes, for Clifton and Defence tower placements especially, we make sure candidates have what's needed for sign-in before they're placed, so there's no delay at the front desk.

**Can one office boy cover both pantry duties and courier runs in heavy traffic areas?** Generally yes, but for Gulshan-e-Iqbal and similarly congested areas we factor in realistic travel time when discussing coverage, since courier turnaround expectations there tend to be tighter.

**What happens if the placement doesn't work out?** Tell us and we'll find a replacement, we'd rather get it right than leave you short-staffed with someone who isn't a fit.

## Full-time vs. part-time support

Clifton and Defence high-rise offices almost always need full-time coverage matched to standard business hours, with some flexibility for late meetings. Smaller Gulshan-e-Iqbal offices and clinics sometimes only need part-time or fixed-hours help, particularly clinics running set morning and evening sessions. We place for both and are upfront about how each choice affects the pool of available candidates.

## Range of duties we match for

Karachi placements span a wide range depending on the office: pantry and tea service, courier and document handling, filing, general errands to banks or suppliers, and for high-rise offices, coordinating with building reception on visitor logs. We ask what mix your office actually needs rather than assuming a one-size answer.

## Documents and verification you should expect

Before we shortlist anyone, every candidate has been through CNIC verification, address confirmation and a reference check with at least one previous employer. When we introduce a candidate to you, we share a summary of that verification along with a contact for their most recent reference, so you're not just taking our word for it, you can call and ask directly about reliability, punctuality and conduct. For high-rise placements, we also make sure the candidate's CNIC copy is ready in advance so building security can process it without a first-day delay.

## How we match a candidate to your office

Matching isn't just about filling a vacancy, we weigh the building type (high-rise with its own security desk versus a standalone office or clinic), the mix of duties you actually need, whether the pace is fast and interruption-heavy or calmer and routine-driven, and whether the role is full-time or part-time. A candidate who's thrived in a quiet Gulshan clinic for two years won't necessarily suit a Clifton high-rise with tighter visitor protocols, and vice versa. We ask enough questions upfront to shortlist people who've actually worked in a similar setting before, rather than sending whoever's next on a generic list.
