## Our verification process for Faisalabad placements

Every plumber we shortlist for a Faisalabad placement goes through the same four-step screening, with no shortcuts, because the trust placed in someone working inside your home or business is real and the risks of cutting corners are too:

- **CNIC and address verification.** Every candidate's CNIC and home address are verified, so you know exactly who is being placed in your household and where they come from. This is the baseline, not a formality.
- **Previous employer and client references.** We speak directly with at least one previous employer or client by phone rather than relying only on a written reference, and we ask specifically about the kind of work the candidate handled, whether they showed up on time, and whether the client would use them again.
- **Practical skills assessment.** We run a practical assessment so a candidate's actual hands-on ability is confirmed, not just their confidence in describing it. A plumber who talks fluently about fittings but fumbles a basic joint or trap identification reveals it here, which is exactly the point.
- **Tool and equipment check.** We confirm the candidate arrives with their own tools and standard fittings, since a plumber who relies on borrowing tools from the household is not set up to do the job properly.

## Seasonal and local considerations in Faisalabad

Faisalabad's plumbing demands shift noticeably through the year, and a plumber with real experience in the city tends to already account for these patterns. Summer here is intensely hot, with temperatures regularly climbing past the high thirties, and that puts water-tank, pump and float-valve setups under heavy strain as consumption rises across residential and commercial properties alike. Older piping in central Faisalabad and Gulistan Colony homes is most likely to show stress in this period, with joints giving way and exposed lines cracking under heat and continuous use. The industrial belt runs high water volumes through the hot months too, and pump and suction-line faults cluster in this season. The monsoon stretch in July and August is shorter than the coastal cities see but still brings a real cluster of drainage and seepage callouts, particularly in lower-lying parts of the older residential areas. Winter cold snaps in December and January can crack exposed rooftop pipes in Madina Town and D-Type homes. If your booking starts right before one of these periods, mention it so we prioritize candidates who have already worked through a full summer or monsoon cycle in Faisalabad rather than someone only tested in calmer months.
