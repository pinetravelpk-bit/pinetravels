Lahore and Faisalabad are both major residential and commercial hubs in Punjab, and our driver placements in each city follow the same non-negotiable checklist regardless of which one you're hiring in.

## What we check before placing a driver

Both cities go through the same core screening: original driving license verification against the CNIC (not a photocopy), a check of traffic violation and accident history, and reference calls with at least two previous employers focused on punctuality and safety record. Where the two cities diverge is the road test itself, what counts as passing depends entirely on local geography, which is why we don't use a generic test for both.

## Local route familiarity matters

In Lahore, that means comfort with DHA's many phases, Gulberg's traffic patterns and the Ring Road. In Faisalabad, it means knowing the routes between newer societies like Wapda City and the older commercial core near Jinnah Colony. We specifically test for this during the driver's road test evaluation.

## What a typical booking looks like

Whether you're in Lahore or Faisalabad, the process starts the same way, you tell us your city, area, and what the driver needs to cover, whether that's a school run, an office commute, or both. We match from drivers already in our pool who've cleared license and reference checks, then run the city-specific road test before shortlisting. You'll usually get a couple of profiles with notes on their verification and route test rather than a bare CV, and once you've picked someone we stay reachable during the first week or two while the routine settles in.

## Trial period and replacements

If the driver you choose doesn't end up being the right fit, a mismatch in pace, communication, or reliability, let us know early and we'll find a replacement from the same vetted pool rather than leaving you to restart the search from scratch. This applies the same way in both cities; there's no separate policy for Lahore versus Faisalabad.

## Full-time, part-time, or hourly arrangements

Households in both cities hire drivers under different structures. Full-time typically covers school runs plus office commuting across a standard day. Part-time suits households that mainly need morning and evening coverage. Some clients, particularly businesses in Faisalabad's commercial areas or families in Lahore managing overlapping schedules, ask for hourly or on-call arrangements instead. Let us know which shape fits before we start matching, since it changes who we shortlist.

## Questions we get asked most

**Is there a difference in how you screen drivers for Lahore versus Faisalabad?** The core checks, license verification, accident history, reference calls, are identical. Only the road test route differs, since it needs to reflect local geography.

**Can a driver who's worked in DHA Lahore also handle a Gulberg household?** Not automatically, we test specifically for route familiarity across the areas relevant to your booking rather than assuming experience in one part of the city transfers cleanly to another.

**Do you place drivers for both a home and a small business in Faisalabad?** Yes, though it's worth mentioning both locations upfront so scheduling and route testing account for the full day, not just one stop.

## How we match a driver to your household or business

Because Lahore and Faisalabad have such different traffic textures, matching starts with where exactly you are rather than just which city. A driver whose road-test strengths are DHA Lahore's phase layout isn't a natural fit for a Gulberg-based household, and someone who's mainly worked Faisalabad's Wapda City societies may need a closer look before being placed near the older Jinnah Colony core. We also weigh whether the booking is corporate or family, corporate placements lean harder on punctuality and traffic-law discipline, family placements lean harder on patience and a clean safety record, and match against whichever the road test and reference calls were actually weighted toward.

## Documents and verification you should expect

Every driver we shortlist has had their original CNIC checked against their driving license in person, along with direct phone calls to at least two previous employers about punctuality and safety record. Once you've selected a driver, keep a copy of the verified license number and CNIC on file yourself, it's a small step that most households and offices in both cities do anyway, and it gives you your own record independent of ours.

## Seasonal and timing considerations

Both cities see traffic shift meaningfully around Ramadan and wedding season, when office hours compress and evening traffic thickens earlier than usual. Lahore's smog-heavy winter months also reduce visibility on the Ring Road and outer DHA phases, which experienced drivers adjust for by allowing more time rather than driving at a normal-season pace. If your booking starts during one of these periods, tell us, we'll prioritize drivers who've already worked through a similar stretch in that specific city rather than someone only tested in easier conditions.
