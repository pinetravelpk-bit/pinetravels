There is no single price list for hiring a security guard in Faisalabad, and a flat rate sheet would be misleading. A retired soldier standing day shift at a single gate in Peoples Colony is a different hire from a trained guard covering a commercial property on the Sitara Bridge industrial stretch with night patrols and visitor logging. Same job title, completely different cost profile. Any agency quoting one number for "a guard" is either giving you the cheapest possible option or planning to revise the figure once they understand the actual assignment.

The honest approach is to break down what moves the cost, and then give you a real number for your specific property rather than a generic estimate that falls apart on the first day.

## Why guard costs in Faisalabad vary so widely

Faisalabad's staffing demand splits in a way most cities do not. On one side are the established industrial families in places like Madina Town and Jinnah Colony, who typically want a residential guard for a gated independent house, often a live-in arrangement with a room on the property. On the other are the commercial and industrial premises, factories, warehouses, and trading offices, that need shift-based coverage with formal logging and often multiple guards. The compensation for a residential live-in guard and a commercial shift guard are not directly comparable, and a single price list cannot capture both.

That split is why we quote per requirement rather than publish a rate card. The factors below are what actually shape the number.

## The real cost factors

**Experience and background.** The biggest single driver. A guard with an ex-military or ex-paramilitary background, or one who has completed a recognized security training course, sits at the upper end of the range. A young worker with no formal training but a clean record costs less, and may be fine for a low-risk residential gate, but is not the right fit for a commercial property that needs someone who can actually manage access control and respond to an incident. We verify training background as part of screening, so the experience level you are paying for is the experience level you get.

**Live-in versus shift-based.** Residential guards in Faisalabad are frequently live-in, with the household providing a room and meals, and the cash compensation adjusted accordingly. Commercial and industrial premises run shift-based coverage, typically twelve-hour shifts, either day or night, and the cost is usually quoted per guard per month for a defined shift pattern. A round-the-clock residential setup with two guards on rotation obviously costs more than a single live-in guard, and a one-shift commercial arrangement costs less than full coverage through the day and night.

**City demand and area profile.** Faisalabad's industrial character means commercial security demand runs steadily through the year, and certain industrial zones carry a premium because of the risk profile and the commute. Residential demand concentrates in the planned societies, where gated properties want a dedicated guard rather than relying on society-level security. A guard placed in a higher-demand industrial area is not priced the same as one in a quiet residential pocket.

**Scope of duties.** A guard whose only job is to sit at a gate and log visitors costs less than one expected to do periodic patrols of a large property, manage a visitor register, coordinate with society security, or handle cash escorts. The more a household or business bundles into the role, the more the worker's monthly compensation shifts. Two clients both asking for "a guard" often mean genuinely different jobs.

## What our placement fee covers versus the guard's monthly compensation

There are two separate numbers, and keeping them clear is what makes pricing transparent.

The guard's own monthly compensation is paid directly by you to the worker, on a monthly or per-shift basis as you agree. That figure is shaped by the factors above and is settled once the scope and shift pattern are clear. We do not take a cut of the guard's monthly pay.

Our placement fee is separate and covers the verification work that happens before placement: CNIC verification and a police character certificate, previous employer references, a physical fitness check, and verification of any basic security training the candidate claims. It also covers the shortlisting, matching a candidate to your property type and risk profile, coordinating the trial or initial shift period, and the replacement guarantee. If a placement does not work out in the early weeks, whether because the guard is not the right fit or the assignment scope shifts, we go back to the shortlist and arrange a replacement rather than asking you to start over or pay a second placement fee.

So the full cost of hiring a guard is the one-time placement fee for the verified, guaranteed placement plus the ongoing monthly compensation the guard earns. We give you both numbers up front.

## How seasonal demand shifts cost

Faisalabad has a few demand windows worth planning around. Wedding season, which runs late in the year into early the next, pushes up requests for temporary residential and event security, and the available pool of experienced guards tightens during that stretch. Election periods, whenever they fall, also create a short, sharp spike in demand for trained guards, which can pull experienced candidates toward higher-paying temporary contracts. On the commercial side, the run-up to Eid and the end of the fiscal year tends to mean more factories and warehouses wanting additional or upgraded coverage, which again tightens the pool.

Booking outside these peaks usually means a smoother shortlist and a more settled negotiation. If you have to hire during one, a couple of weeks of lead time lets us line up candidates before demand spikes rather than competing for whoever is left.
