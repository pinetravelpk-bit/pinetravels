DHA Lahore spans more phases than almost any other DHA development in Pakistan, from the older, established sections near Barki Road to the newer phases still under active development. That scale means household needs vary a lot from one phase to the next, but the hiring standard shouldn't.

## What to look for regardless of phase

Whether you're in an established DHA Lahore phase or a newer one still filling in, the fundamentals of safe hiring don't change:

- Verified CNIC and address on file before the candidate ever enters your home.
- Contactable references from at least two previous employers.
- An in-person interview, phone screening alone isn't enough.
- A trial period before committing to a long-term arrangement.

## Gate and entry procedures across DHA Lahore phases

Most DHA Lahore phases operate their own security desk at each entry point, and staff working in the phase regularly are usually expected to register there, CNIC on file, sometimes a photograph, and in several phases a resident-staff card or sticker for the vehicle bringing them in. Because this varies slightly phase to phase, we recommend confirming your specific phase's requirement with your Defence Raya, Phase 6, Phase 8 or other local management office directly, and having your staff member's CNIC ready on their first day to avoid a hold-up at the gate.

## Household types across DHA Lahore

DHA Lahore's scale means the resident mix is wide. Newer phases skew toward young families and recently returned expats setting up a home for the first time, often needing several roles filled together. Older, established phases have a larger share of long-settled families and retirees who tend to need single, stable long-term placements, a cook or helper who stays for years rather than a full household team.

## Staff categories most requested in DHA Lahore

Across both established and newer phases, the roles we place most often here are cooks, drivers and maids/helpers, reflecting how many DHA Lahore households run a car-dependent daily routine with school runs and office commutes. Security guards and gardeners see more demand in the newer, larger-plot phases where private coverage supplements the phase's own security.

## Questions we get asked most about hiring in DHA Lahore

**Do I need to inform DHA security before staff start working?**
Most phases prefer staff to be registered at the gate before they begin regular visits, so it's worth doing this in the first few days rather than after the fact.

**Can the same driver or cook serve more than one house in the same street?**
Yes, this is common in DHA Lahore, and many of our clients share a driver informally with a neighbor. We can discuss scheduling if that's what you're looking for.

**How do you handle placements for a newly built house with no existing staff at all?**
This is a frequent request in the newer phases. We typically start with a cook and helper first, then add a driver or guard once the household routine settles.

## Documents worth collecting before hiring

Regardless of which DHA Lahore phase you're in, keep a simple file on any staff member you hire: their original CNIC (with a photocopy kept separately), a current photo, their home address, and the phone number of at least one previous employer. This is useful beyond our own verification, many phase security desks will ask for the same details when issuing a resident-staff card, so having it organized in advance means one less delay once a candidate is ready to start.

## How we structure a trial period

A two- to four-week trial is standard for most roles we place in DHA Lahore, longer for live-in positions. Before the trial starts, agree on working hours, specific duties and a day off, in writing if possible, and set a short check-in around the one-week mark rather than waiting until the end to raise any concerns. This window is also when you confirm the practical things an interview can't show: whether a cook's food actually suits the household, whether a driver is comfortable with the specific routes between phases, whether a helper's pace matches the home's routine.

## Newer phases vs. established sections

Households in DHA Lahore's newer phases often need a wider range of staff at once, cook, helper, guard and gardener together, as a new home gets fully staffed. Established phases more often need single-role replacements or additions to an existing household setup.
