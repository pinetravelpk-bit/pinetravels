Multan is one of South Punjab's fastest-growing residential markets, and DHA Multan alone spans roughly 9,500 acres in its first phase. That scale, combined with established areas like Gulgasht Colony and Multan Cantt, means household staffing needs vary a lot depending on where in the city you are.

## What to expect when hiring in Multan

Whether you're in a newer gated society or a long-established neighborhood, the fundamentals stay the same: verified CNIC and address on file, contactable references from at least two previous employers, an in-person interview, and a trial period before any long-term commitment.

## Red flags to watch for at interview stage

A few warning signs are worth taking seriously regardless of how strong a candidate otherwise seems: an address that can't be pinned down to a specific location, reluctance to provide a previous employer's phone number, or a work history with unexplained gaps. In Multan's DHA and Citi Housing sections specifically, where society management requires a verified staff registration before issuing a gate pass, a candidate who can't produce consistent CNIC and address details will also cause delays getting them cleared for entry, so it's worth catching these issues before an offer is made, not after.

## Why a phone reference beats a letter every time

Written reference letters are simple to produce and impossible to verify on the spot. A phone call to the previous employer is the more reliable check. Ask direct questions: how long did they actually work there, why the employment ended, whether the employer noticed any issues with honesty, punctuality or handling household responsibilities, and whether they'd hire the person again. Employers who genuinely worked with the candidate answer naturally and in detail; answers that feel scripted are worth following up on with a second reference.

## Setting up a fair trial period

For live-in roles particularly, a two- to four-week trial gives both the household and the candidate a real chance to assess fit before making a longer commitment. Agree on hours, core duties and days off before day one, and set a specific check-in, a short conversation at the one-week mark works well, so any issues surface early rather than after weeks of friction.

## What Multan households request most

Cooks and household helpers are the most consistently requested roles across Multan, particularly in the larger joint-family homes common in Gulgasht Colony and Cantt. Drivers see strong demand in DHA Multan given the distances involved in a 9,500-acre first phase, and guards are increasingly requested in the newer gated sections as those societies mature.

## Documents to ask for before hiring

Before any candidate starts, collect their original CNIC, a recent photo, and the phone number of at least one previous employer, ideally the most recent one. For placements in DHA Multan or Citi Housing, keep this information organized in one place, since the society's security office will usually request the same details before issuing a staff gate pass, and having it ready in advance saves a delay once the candidate is due to start.
