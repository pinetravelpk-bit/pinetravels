Karachi's wedding season turns a lot of private lawns into event venues overnight. Between the cooler winter weeks and the early spring shaadi rush, houses in DHA, Clifton, Malir Halt and the older established pockets of the city pull out marquees, lay down carpet runways across the grass, and host mehndi, barat and walima functions for hundreds of guests at a time. For a household with a garden, that is not just a social event, it is a logistical one for the lawn itself, and the regular twice-a-week gardener is rarely enough to carry it through.

That is where temporary gardener staffing comes in. Not a permanent hire, not a replacement for your regular gardener, but a verified extra pair of hands who steps in for the weeks around the functions and steps back once the garden has recovered.

## How Karachi households use a temporary gardener during wedding season

The requests we get during Karachi's wedding weeks tend to fall into a few clear patterns.

The first is the pre-function prep. A lawn that has been maintained at a casual level all year suddenly needs to look pristine for a barat night with two hundred guests. That means a heavier mow, edging, weed clearance along the boundary, the hedges shaped, and the flowerbeds tidied. A temporary gardener comes in for the three or four days before the function to get the garden to that standard.

The second is the function days themselves. Marquee setup, carpet laying, and generator cables all trample the lawn, and someone needs to be on hand to manage the bits of the garden that are still visible, water the plants that are drying out under the lights, and handle any last-minute touch-ups before guests arrive.

The third is the post-function recovery. This is the part families underestimate. After a two-night wedding on a Karachi lawn, the grass is compacted, there are oil stains from the catering area, broken glass in the beds, and sections of lawn that have been killed off entirely under the carpet. A temporary gardener comes in for the week after the functions to aerate, reseed the dead patches, and get the garden back to where it was before the season started.

The fourth is the multi-wedding stretch. Some Karachi households host three or four functions across a single season, sometimes their own children's, sometimes relatives using their lawn, and the regular gardener simply cannot keep up with the cycle of prep, function and recovery on top of his normal weekly rounds.

## Temporary placement versus a permanent gardener

A temporary gardener booking is structured differently from a permanent placement, and the expectations on both sides reflect that.

A permanent gardener is matched to your garden's ongoing needs, the plants you keep, the size of the lawn, the Karachi climate realities of salt air and monsoon humidity. The screening and matching investment is built around someone who will stay and learn the property over months.

A temporary gardener is matched to a specific job: get the lawn function-ready, handle the function days, or do the post-event recovery. We do not lower the verification bar, the same CNIC, reference and skill checks apply, but we prioritise candidates who have done event-prep work before and understand that a wedding lawn has a deadline, not a gentle weekly schedule.

Pricing for a temporary booking is usually structured per day or for a defined package of days rather than as a monthly retainer, and we agree the scope and the rate upfront. There is no ambiguity about what the booking covers, which matters when the work is tied to a fixed function date.

## Lead time: when to message us

Karachi wedding season is busy, and the lead time on temporary gardener staffing makes a real difference to what we can deliver.

If you message us two to three weeks before the function, we can shortlist properly, you can meet the candidate, and there is time for a prep plan that does not rush the lawn. Lawns need a few days to recover from a hard mow and look green again, so the lead time is not just for our screening, it is for the garden itself.

If you message us a week out, we can usually place someone, but the prep window is tighter and the lawn may not have time to green up fully before the function. We will be honest about what is achievable in the time available.

If you message us two days before the function, we can sometimes help, but the verification steps are not optional and they take a fixed amount of time. For the post-function recovery work there is usually more flexibility, since the deadline pressure has passed, but for pre-function prep the earlier you reach out the better.

For peak wedding weeks in Karachi, we recommend reaching out as soon as the function date is set. Holding a verified temporary gardener early is straightforward. Finding one the day before a barat is not.

## Managing your existing gardener alongside temporary help

Most Karachi households that call us for wedding-season gardening already have a regular gardener, often someone who comes two or three days a week. The temporary gardener is not there to replace him, he is there to absorb the extra workload the regular gardener cannot take on, and managing that handoff keeps both working well.

A few things make the arrangement work. First, be clear with your regular gardener about why the temporary help is coming in, so he does not feel his role is under threat, which is the most common cause of friction. Second, let the regular gardener keep his normal days and routine, and slot the temporary gardener around him rather than restructuring the whole schedule. Third, define the split. If the regular gardener handles the ongoing maintenance and the temporary gardener handles the function-prep and recovery work, write that down so neither is stepping on the other's work.

We brief temporary candidates on this before they start. A gardener who walks into a property expecting to take over when there is already someone on it causes problems, and we screen for candidates who are comfortable working alongside an existing gardener rather than replacing him in practice.

## How we verify a temporary gardener in Karachi

The verification steps for a temporary booking are the same as for a permanent one, because the person is on your property and handling your landscaping regardless of how long the engagement lasts.

**CNIC and address verification.** We confirm the candidate's CNIC and verify the home address he has given us. A traceable identity matters as much for a short engagement as for a long one, and it filters out the kind of casual, untraceable labour that disappears when a problem comes up.

**Reference checks.** We contact previous households or commercial properties the candidate has worked for and ask specific questions about the size of the gardens maintained, the plants and lawn types involved, and whether the candidate handled irrigation and pesticide work himself. For temporary placements we also ask whether the candidate has done event-prep or post-event recovery work before.

**Personal interview.** We talk through how the candidate handles a lawn that needs to be ready for a fixed date, how he manages watering around marquee setup, and what he does with compacted grass after a function. Someone who has done wedding-prep work answers with specifics. Someone who has only done casual weekly mowing tends to give vague responses.

**Skill assessment.** Where possible we have the candidate demonstrate practical work, mowing, edging, or soil prep, so we are not taking skill claims on trust. This is the step that most reliably separates a real gardener from someone who simply owns a pair of shears, and it matters more when the work has a deadline.

## Questions Karachi families ask us about wedding-season gardeners

**Can we book the same gardener for pre-function prep and post-function recovery?**
Yes, and we usually recommend it, since the same person who prepped the lawn knows where the vulnerable patches are and can plan the recovery work accordingly.

**Do you place gardeners for the function days themselves?**
Yes. For a two-night wedding on a Karachi lawn, having a gardener on hand during setup and on the function days keeps the visible parts of the garden looking right and lets someone manage watering around the marquee and lighting rig.

**What if our regular gardener is uncomfortable with a temporary gardener on the property?**
We brief temporary candidates on working alongside an existing gardener, and we encourage families to introduce the two before the prep begins. Most friction comes from unclear roles, so defining the split upfront usually resolves it.

**Can you send a team of two for a large lawn?**
Yes. For bigger Karachi lawns, especially in DHA Phase 6 and 8 where the garden areas are substantial, two temporary gardeners are often the right call for a function-prep window, and we can arrange that as a single booking.

**How far in advance do we need to book for peak wedding weeks?**
Two to three weeks is ideal for the lawn to green up properly after prep, a week is workable but tighter, and last-minute requests are hard to fill with a verified candidate. The earlier you reach out, the better the result.
