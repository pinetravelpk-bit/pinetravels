## Why verification is worth your time as a painter

Most painters in Islamabad still get work through a contractor who takes a cut, or through a single contact at a paint shop who passes jobs along. Both work, but both leave you with thin margins and no real control over which jobs you take. A verified listing with RX Direct changes that in a few concrete ways. Households that come to us have already been told you passed a CNIC and reference check, so the trust question is settled before you arrive. You are not negotiating your rate from scratch each call. And you are not taking a job at 9 PM across the city for a touch up that turns out to be a full wall. Verification does not mean guaranteed daily work, but the work that does come is better matched to what you actually do, better paid, and less of a scramble.

## Documents you need to bring

Get these together before you come in for screening. We will not schedule the skills assessment until the paperwork is complete, and missing documents are the main reason applications get delayed.

1. **Original CNIC and one clear photocopy.** We verify the CNIC against NADRA records and confirm the address on the card. If your permanent address is in Rawalpindi or a nearby town but you currently live and work inside Islamabad, that is fine, we just need your actual current address on file.
2. **Two reference contacts from past employers or clients.** These should be people you have painted for in the last two to three years, not relatives. We call them. A reference who says "he did two rooms for us in F-11 last year and the finish is still good" is exactly what we are looking for.
3. **Proof of contractor or shop affiliation.** If you have worked under a named painting contractor or are regularly referred by a specific paint shop, bring the contact. This is not required, but it speeds up the reference stage and often means a higher starting placement tier.
4. **Your tool kit.** Not a document, but it is checked at the skills assessment, so bring the actual kit you work with day to day.

## CNIC and address verification, step by step

The first thing that happens after you submit your CNIC copy is a verification check. We confirm the CNIC number is valid, that the name on it matches the name you gave us, and that the card is not flagged. We also confirm your current residential address, usually through a utility bill in your name or a verifiable landlord contact if you rent. This is not about prying into your private life, it is about being able to tell a household that the person they are letting into their home for several days is who they say they are. If there is a mismatch, for example your CNIC shows a Rawalpindi address but you tell us you live in Bhara Kahu, we will ask you to clarify, we will not reject you outright. Honesty here saves everyone time.

## Previous employer and client references

Once CNIC is cleared, we contact your two references. We ask them three things: what painting work you did, when, and whether they would hire you again. We are not after flattery, we are after consistency. If one reference calls you neat and reliable and the other says you left splattered trim and walked off early, we will raise it with you directly before deciding. Painters who have been working steadily for two or more years usually clear this stage without trouble. The ones who struggle are typically those who listed a brother in law or someone they did one small patch job for years back. Choose references who have seen your real work, recently.

## The practical skills assessment

This is the part that sets your placement tier, so it matters. There is no written test. We run a hands-on assessment at a designated space where you are asked to complete a short set of typical painting tasks within a reasonable timeframe. For painters this typically includes:

- Preparing a patched wall surface, sanding, and applying a primer coat evenly.
- Cutting in along a ceiling line and a window frame without tape, clean and straight.
- Rolling a full coat on a wall section without lap marks or heavy buildup.
- Matching a supplied paint color to an existing swatch by eye, close enough that a touch up would not stand out.

You are observed but not rushed. The assessor is a senior painter, not an office worker with a checklist, so they understand that there is more than one correct way to cut in or load a roller. What they are watching for is whether your prep is thorough, whether you protect floors and fixtures, whether you maintain a wet edge, whether your finish is even without drips or runs, and whether you clean up after yourself. Bring your own tools to this assessment. A painter who shows up without a decent brush, a roller frame, or drop sheets is telling us something we need to know before placing them in someone's home.

## Tool and equipment check

Right after the skills assessment we do a quick inventory of the kit you brought. We are not checking for branded or expensive gear. We are checking that you actually own the basics: a set of brushes in a couple of widths, a roller frame and sleeves, a tray, sandpaper of at least two grits, filler and a putty knife, masking tape, drop cloths or plastic sheeting, and a step ladder. If you are missing something core, we note it and may ask you to get it before we activate your listing. This is not about keeping you out, it is about making sure that when an Islamabad household books you for a two room job, you can actually start on the agreed day without borrowing the client's ladder.

## How placement works

Once you are verified, your profile goes active in our Islamabad painter pool. When a request comes in that fits your skill level and your part of the city, our coordinator reaches out on WhatsApp or by call with the details: the sector or area, the scope, the expected duration, and the offered rate. You can say no. Declining a job does not remove you from the pool. What does remove you is saying yes and not showing up, or showing up and quoting a higher rate than was agreed, or steering the client toward a specific paint shop for a kickback. We collect feedback after every placement, and a pattern of complaints means your listing gets suspended pending review.

## How pay works

This is the question we get most, so let us be straight about it. You are paid directly by the household, at the rate agreed when the job is confirmed. RX Direct does not take a cut of your daily or per job wage. Our coordination fee is billed to the household, not to you. For longer jobs, like a full house repaint over a week or two, the rate and payment schedule are set upfront and we keep a written record so there is no ambiguity about what is owed at each stage. If a household delays payment without reason, tell us and we will follow up. We cannot legally force payment, but a household that does not pay our verified painters does not get painters from us again, and that is usually enough to resolve it.

## What happens after placement

After your first few jobs, expect a follow up call from us asking how it went and whether the client treated you fairly. Feedback runs both ways. If your prep is thorough, your finish is clean, and your communication is clear, you rise in priority for the next matching request. If a client asks for you by name for follow up work, that request reaches you first before anyone else in the pool is offered it. Over time, the painters who show up on the agreed day, keep the site protected, and finish on schedule build a steady repeat client base through us without having to chase work themselves.

## What can disqualify you

We will not verify you if the CNIC check comes back invalid or flagged, if both references decline to vouch for you, if you badly fail the skills assessment and refuse a retest, or if it becomes clear during screening that you overstated your experience. A past criminal record is not an automatic disqualification, but undisclosed charges that surface later are. Tell us the truth at the start and we will work with it.
