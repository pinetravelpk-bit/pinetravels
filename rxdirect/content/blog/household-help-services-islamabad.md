Running a home in Islamabad involves far more than cleaning and cooking. There are school runs and grocery trips, a garden that grows wild in the monsoon, geysers to service before winter, an elderly parent who needs company, a leaking tap, a wedding dinner for forty guests and a house to clean top to bottom before Eid. Household help services Islamabad families use cover all of this, from staff who come every day to tradesmen who visit once a year.

This guide sets out the full menu of help available, grouped by how often you need it. Use it to see what your home might need, what each service involves and how to arrange it.

## Everyday help: staff who work in your home regularly

These are the services most families think of first: people who come every day or live in, and keep the household running.

### Housekeeping

A [maid](/services/maids/islamabad) handles cleaning, dusting, mopping, bathrooms, laundry, ironing and keeping rooms in order. Full-time maids manage the whole house; part-time maids come for a few hours a day. In larger homes, two maids may split upstairs and downstairs.

### Cooking

A [cook](/services/cooks/islamabad) plans meals, buys or lists groceries, cooks daily food and keeps the kitchen clean. Skills range from everyday desi cooking to continental, Chinese, baking and diet food for diabetic or heart patients. For formal entertaining or special menus, a [chef](/services/chefs/islamabad) brings a higher level of skill.

### Childcare

[Nannies and babysitters](/services/babysitters-nannies/islamabad) care for infants and children: feeding, bathing, naps, play, homework help and safe supervision. Newborn nannies specialise in the first months, including night feeds and helping the mother recover.

### Elderly care

[Caretakers](/services/caretakers/islamabad) help elderly family members with daily living: walking, bathing, dressing, meals, medicines on time and company. For medical needs such as injections, wound care, catheters or post surgery monitoring, a qualified [home nurse](/services/nurses/islamabad) is the right service.

### Driving

A [driver](/services/drivers/islamabad) handles school runs, office drops, shopping, doctor visits and trips to Rawalpindi, Murree or Lahore. Drivers also keep the car clean and handle basic checks.

### Outdoor and support work

[Helpers](/services/helpers/islamabad) take care of porches, driveways, rubbish, errands and lifting. [Gardeners](/services/gardeners/islamabad) look after lawns, hedges, flower beds and pots.

### Security

[Security guards](/services/security-guards/islamabad) manage the gate, check visitors, receive deliveries and keep watch at night.

### Couples

A [domestic couple](/services/couples/islamabad) covers two roles, often cook and driver, or maid and helper, and lives in the quarters together.

## Weekly and monthly help

Some household help services Islamabad homes need are regular but not daily.

**Deep cleaning visits.** A [cleaner](/services/cleaners/islamabad) can come weekly or monthly to clean what daily housekeeping misses: kitchen cabinets, fans, windows, grills, behind furniture and bathroom tiles.

**Garden maintenance.** Many homes without a full-time gardener use a weekly visit for mowing, trimming and planting.

**Car washing.** Some families use a helper or a visiting car washer on a weekly basis.

**Laundry and ironing.** Part-time maids sometimes take on ironing only, two or three times a week.

## One-off help

Some needs come only once or occasionally.

### Move-in and move-out cleaning

Moving into a new house in DHA, Bahria Town or one of the sectors usually means a full clean: dust from construction, kitchen grease from previous tenants, bathrooms, windows and floors. Moving out often requires the same to get a security deposit back. A team of cleaners can finish a typical house in a day.

### Post-construction and renovation cleaning

After painting, tiling or renovation, fine dust settles everywhere. A specialised clean removes paint spots, cement marks and dust from fittings and fans.

### Event help

Weddings, dholkis, milads, birthdays and formal dinners need extra hands: cooks, servers, cleaners before and after, and sometimes extra guards for parking and the gate. Temporary help for the day or week of an event can make the difference between enjoying your own event and spending it in the kitchen.

### Short-term care

After surgery, childbirth or a fall, a nurse or caretaker may be needed for a few weeks. Short-term placements can be arranged for the expected recovery period.

### Help while staff are on leave

When your regular cook or maid goes home for Eid or a family wedding, temporary cover keeps the house running until they return.

## Home repair trades

Household help also includes the skilled trades that keep the house itself in good condition. Families often struggle to find honest, careful tradesmen, especially for small jobs.

- **[Electricians](/services/electricians/islamabad):** wiring faults, switches and sockets, fans and lights, UPS and inverter installation, breaker problems, earthing
- **[Plumbers](/services/plumbers/islamabad):** leaks, blockages, motors and pumps, geyser installation and servicing, tank cleaning, bathroom fittings
- **[Carpenters](/services/carpenters/islamabad):** door repairs, wardrobes, kitchen cabinets, furniture repair, polish work
- **[Painters](/services/painters/islamabad):** interior and exterior painting, damp treatment, distemper and emulsion, wood polish

These are usually priced per job, with materials bought by you or billed at cost. Many Islamabad families keep a list of trusted tradesmen, but when the usual electrician has moved to another city, finding a new one at short notice is hard. The result is often a stranger found through the market, who may cut corners, overcharge for parts or leave a job half done. Household help services Islamabad families rely on should include tradesmen who are checked in the same way as daily staff, who explain the problem before starting, agree the price upfront and clean up afterwards.

For larger jobs, such as rewiring an old house in F-7, replacing rusted GI pipes in G-9 or fitting a new kitchen in a DHA house, ask for a written quote that lists labour and materials separately. Ask the tradesman to show you old parts that were replaced. And for anything involving gas, mains electricity or structural changes, never accept shortcuts to save a little money. These are the jobs where a careful, qualified tradesman protects your family's safety, not just your budget.

## How to decide which household help services Islamabad homes like yours need

Start by listing the tasks that are currently not getting done, or that are taking too much of your own time. Then group them:

1. **Tasks needed every day:** these point to full-time or part-time staff
2. **Tasks needed weekly or monthly:** these point to visiting cleaners, gardeners or part-time help
3. **Tasks needed occasionally:** these point to one-off or seasonal services
4. **Repairs:** these point to tradesmen

Many homes find they need one or two daily staff plus a few regular visits, rather than a large full-time team.

## A pre-winter checklist for Islamabad homes

Winter is the season when household help is most in demand and hardest to book, so many families work through this list in October and early November:

1. **Geysers:** service gas geysers, check pilot lights, thermostats and flues, and drain sediment
2. **Gas heaters:** check pipes and connections for leaks, clean burners, confirm every room has ventilation
3. **Electric heaters and blowers:** check plugs, wiring and sockets, as overloaded sockets are a common cause of winter fires
4. **Pipes:** insulate exposed pipes on roofs and outer walls against frost
5. **Windows and doors:** fix gaps that let in cold air, re-hang sticking doors after the monsoon
6. **Bedding:** wash and air quilts and blankets, clean carpets that were rolled up for summer
7. **Staff quarters:** check heaters and hot water for live-in staff

A plumber, an electrician and a carpenter can usually cover all of this in two or three visits.

## A sample month of household help for a family house

To see how these services fit together, here is how a typical family of five in a house in G-13 might use household help over one month:

- **Every day:** a full-time maid for cleaning and laundry, a full-time cook for three meals, a driver for school and office runs
- **Twice a week:** a gardener for the lawn and pots
- **Once a month:** a cleaner for a deep clean of the kitchen and bathrooms
- **This month only:** a plumber to service the geyser and an electrician to check heater sockets before winter
- **For one evening:** two extra servers and a helper for a family dinner

Planned like this, the house runs smoothly without hiring more full-time staff than needed.

## Household help for apartments

Apartment living is growing fast in Islamabad, with towers along the Expressway, in E-11, B-17, Gulberg and Bahria Enclave. Apartments need less help than houses but have their own needs: building rules on staff entry, no servant quarters, small kitchens and shared water tanks managed by the building. Most apartment households do well with a part-time maid, a part-time cook if both partners work, a cleaner once a month and a plumber or electrician on call. Check your building's rules on registering domestic staff with the management office before anyone starts.

## Coordinating several services

Larger homes may use five or six types of household help. Keep it manageable:

- **Keep a contact list** of all regular and occasional help, with phone numbers and CNIC copies
- **Use one point of contact** for arranging services where possible
- **Let your guard know** who is expected each day
- **Plan seasonal work in advance,** before demand peaks
- **Combine trades visits** where possible, such as electrician and plumber on the same day during a renovation
