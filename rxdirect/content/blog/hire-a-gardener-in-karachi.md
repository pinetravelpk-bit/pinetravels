Karachi's coastal climate is unlike any other city in Pakistan, and that shapes what gardening actually involves here. The humidity, the salt air in areas near the coast, and the long hot stretch from April through October mean that lawns and plants in DHA, Bahria Town, Clifton and Gulshan face conditions that kill off species that thrive in Lahore or Islamabad without breaking a sweat. A good gardener, or mali, is the difference between a lawn that survives the Karachi summer and one that burns out by May because nobody with the right knowledge was managing watering depth, shade timing and soil condition.

## What families and businesses in Karachi usually need

The requests we get from Karachi generally split into three patterns:

1. **A live-out gardener visiting two to three times a week** for a household in DHA Phase 6 or Clifton, covering mowing, watering, pruning and basic seasonal upkeep on a fixed schedule.
2. **A daily gardener for a larger property** in Bahria Town Karachi or a farmhouse on the outskirts, where the outdoor footprint is significant enough to need someone on site every day through the growing season.
3. **A gardener for an office building or commercial property** with landscaped entrance areas or courtyards, where maintaining appearance for visitors and clients is part of the role alongside routine plant care.

## Our verification process for Karachi placements

Every gardener we shortlist for a Karachi placement goes through the same screening before we share their profile with you. We run CNIC and address verification to confirm identity and residence. We conduct reference checks by speaking directly with previous employers, whether households or commercial properties, rather than relying on a written reference alone, because a short phone call reveals more about how someone actually maintains a garden than any paper can. We hold a personal interview to assess communication and reliability. And we run a practical skill assessment, asking candidates about seasonal plant care suited to Karachi's climate, lawn treatment, irrigation timing and tool handling, because the gap between someone who has worked as a mali for years and someone who simply owns a pair of shears shows up quickly when you ask specific questions about soil, watering depth and coastal plant selection.

## Trial period and replacement, explained simply

Every placement begins with a trial period. This is not a formality or a sign that we doubt our own screening, it is a realistic safeguard. Even a verified, experienced gardener may not suit your specific outdoor space, your plant varieties or your expectations for how the garden should look. If the first candidate does not work out during trial, we go back to the shortlist we already built, incorporate your feedback about what was not working, and arrange a replacement without asking you to restart the search from zero. The trial exists to catch mismatches early, while they are still simple to resolve.

Most Karachi bookings start with a WhatsApp message. You tell us the area, the size of the outdoor space roughly, and whether you need daily visits or a few times a week. We follow up with a few questions: what kind of plants and lawn you have, whether there are any specific landscaping features like hedges or kitchen gardens, and whether tools and equipment are available at the property or the gardener needs to bring their own. A gardener commuting from Malir to DHA every day is a placement at risk, and we would rather match someone closer from the start.

## Seasonal and local considerations in Karachi

Karachi's climate is the single biggest factor in gardening here, and it is different from the rest of the country. The long hot season from April through October means watering needs to be timed carefully, early morning or late evening, because midday watering in peak heat damages plants rather than helping them. Coastal humidity in areas like DHA and Clifton encourages fungal issues on certain plant varieties, so a gardener who recognizes those signs early and adjusts treatment is worth more than one who simply waters on a fixed schedule. Monsoon from July to September brings humidity spikes and drainage challenges, particularly in properties where lawn grading does not channel water away effectively. Winter is mild compared to northern cities, so lawns do not go fully dormant the way they do in Islamabad, but growth slows and the focus shifts to pruning and clearing. Ramadan shifts working hours, and many households want their gardener to come earlier or later in the day, so we confirm candidates are comfortable with adjusted timings before placement. If you know your garden has specific seasonal issues, tell us upfront so we shortlist someone whose experience matches.

**Can the gardener bring their own tools, or do we need to provide them?** Many of the gardeners we place own basic tools like shears, but larger equipment like mowers and trimmers is usually expected to be available at the property. Tell us what you already have so we match accordingly and brief the candidate before day one.

**What happens if the gardener we hire does not maintain the lawn to the standard we expect?** Tell us as soon as you see the issue. The trial period exists for exactly this, and we will arrange a replacement from the existing shortlist rather than asking you to wait it out and hope it improves.

**Can you place a gardener who knows how to manage a kitchen garden or specific plant varieties?** Yes, some of the gardeners we place have experience with kitchen gardens, seasonal vegetables and specific plant types suited to Karachi's climate. If you have particular plants or a kitchen garden, flag it when you first reach out so we screen for that specific experience during the skill assessment.
