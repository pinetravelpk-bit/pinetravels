Karachi's coastal climate means gardens need different seasonal care than inland cities like Islamabad or Lahore, households in DHA Clifton and Bahria Town Karachi in particular ask for gardeners experienced with the local growing conditions.

## Gardening in a coastal, humid climate

Salt-laden air and high humidity near the coast affect which plants thrive in DHA Clifton gardens in a way that doesn't apply further inland, and households there often ask specifically for gardeners who already know which local varieties tolerate the coastal conditions. Bahria Town Karachi properties, being further from the immediate coastline, deal less with salt exposure but still share the city's humidity, so watering and fungus management come up more than they would in a drier inland city.

## Our verification process

Every gardener we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks, a personal interview, and a skills assessment. For coastal-area placements we also ask about direct experience with salt-tolerant planting, since it's a recurring, practical concern for Karachi households near the water.

## What's typically included

A regular Karachi gardening visit covers mowing and edging, weeding, pruning, and, because of the coastal air, a closer check on fungus and mildew than you'd need inland. Irrigation checks matter more here too, since humidity can mask early signs of both overwatering and blocked lines. Seasonal or salt-tolerant planting is usually discussed separately from the standard visit, since it depends on what the household wants to introduce and whether the property is close enough to the coast for salt exposure to be a real factor.

## Questions we get asked most in Karachi

**Do I need a gardener who's worked in DHA Clifton specifically, or is general Karachi experience enough?** General coastal experience is usually enough, the key is whether the candidate has worked with salt-tolerant or humidity-tolerant planting, not the exact neighbourhood.

**How often should a gardener visit in Karachi's climate?** It depends on the property, but the humidity here means fungus and pest issues can develop faster than in drier cities, so many households prefer slightly more frequent visits than they might in Lahore or Islamabad.

**Can I get a part-time gardener instead of full-time?** Yes, a lot of Bahria Town Karachi households in particular go with two or three visits a week rather than a live-in mali, especially where the lawn is a manageable size.

**What happens if the gardener isn't a good fit?** Let us know and we'll shortlist a replacement. It's more common early in a placement, and we'd rather households flag it than stay stuck with a mismatch.

## Full-time vs. part-time mali

Coastal DHA Clifton properties with more demanding, salt-tolerant planting sometimes benefit from more frequent visits, particularly while a garden is being established with the right varieties for the conditions. Once planting is settled, many of these gardens are manageable with a gardener visiting several times a week rather than daily. Bahria Town Karachi households, being further from direct salt exposure, more often go with a straightforward part-time schedule, since the main ongoing concern there is humidity and general upkeep rather than salt damage. Full-time or near-daily mali arrangements tend to come up for larger properties with more extensive grounds, regardless of exact location.

This gives both sides a chance to see whether the gardener's plant knowledge and pace actually suit the property, rather than assuming a strong interview automatically means a good fit once they're working with the specific mix of salt exposure, humidity, and existing planting on site. If it's not the right match, tell us what's off and we'll shortlist someone else.
