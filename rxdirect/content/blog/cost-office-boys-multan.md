There is no standard price list for hiring an office boy in Multan, and pretending there is one would not help anyone. A two-person trading office near Bosan Road that needs someone to make tea, receive parcels, and run the occasional bank errand is a completely different hire from a busy medical clinic on Gulgasht that wants front-desk support, courier coordination, and basic filing handled reliably through the day. Same job title, two different cost profiles. Any agency that quotes a single flat rate for "an office boy" is either giving you the entry-level number or planning to adjust it upward the moment the real scope comes out.

The better question is what actually drives the cost, and how to land on a real number for your specific office instead of a ballpark that drifts.

## Why office boy costs in Multan vary

Multan's commercial market is smaller and steadier than Karachi's or Lahore's, which actually makes the cost picture clearer in some ways, but it is not uniform. Offices along Bosan Road and in the cantonment area tend to be established trading and professional setups with consistent hours, while the newer commercial pockets around Citi Housing and Wapda Town are a mix of new businesses, clinics, and branch offices with different rhythms. An office boy who has worked a structured corporate branch is not priced the same as one moving over from an informal shop assistant role, and the duties bundled into the job change the figure further.

That local texture is why we do not publish a rate card. The cost a Multan office ends up paying is built from specific factors, and those factors move the relative price even within the same city.

## The real cost factors

**Experience level.** An office boy who has already worked in a formal office environment, who knows how a visitor register works, how to handle courier dispatch, and how to manage a basic filing system, sits at the higher end of the range. A first-timer or someone shifting from a retail or shop assistant role costs less but usually needs a longer settling-in period and more supervision. For offices where reliability matters more than polish, a less experienced but punctual candidate can be a sensible fit, and we match accordingly rather than defaulting to the most experienced option.

**Shift coverage and hours.** Office boys in Multan are almost always live-out, day-shift roles, so the live-in versus live-out question that applies to domestic staff matters less here. What does matter is the shift pattern. A standard nine-to-five office costs one figure; an office that needs early coverage, say from eight in the morning, or late coverage past seven in the evening, shifts the compensation expectation upward because the worker's commute and day get longer. Half-day and part-time office support is also possible, and is quoted per day or per week rather than monthly.

**City demand and location.** Multan's office support demand runs steady rather than spiky, but the cantonment and Bosan Road corridors carry slightly higher expectations simply because of the area profile and the commute from the residential pockets where most candidates live. A placement in a newer commercial area, where the worker can reach the office on a shorter commute, is not directly comparable.

**Scope of duties.** An office boy handling tea, parcels, and basic cleaning of the office space costs less than one who also takes on filing, courier coordination, stationery procurement, assisting with photocopying and basic document handling, or covering the front desk when the receptionist is away. The more an office bundles into the role, the more the monthly compensation moves. This is where most flat-rate estimates fail, because two offices both calling the role "office boy" often mean very different workloads.

## What our placement fee covers versus the office boy's monthly compensation

Two separate numbers are involved, and mixing them up is what makes pricing feel unclear.

The office boy's own monthly compensation is paid directly by the office to the worker, typically monthly. That figure is shaped by the factors above and is settled once the scope and hours are clear. We do not take a portion of the worker's monthly pay.

Our placement fee is separate and covers what happens before the worker joins your office: CNIC and address verification, previous employer references, a personal interview, and a punctuality track record review, since for an office role, lateness is the single most common reason a placement breaks down. The fee also covers the shortlisting itself, matching a candidate to your office's size and rhythm, coordinating the initial trial period, and the replacement guarantee. If a placement does not settle in during the first weeks, whether because of punctuality or a scope mismatch, we go back to the shortlist and arrange a replacement rather than asking you to restart the search or pay again.

So the full cost of hiring an office boy is the one-time placement fee for the verified, guaranteed placement, plus the ongoing monthly compensation the worker earns. We give you both numbers clearly.

## How seasonal demand shifts cost

Multan has a couple of seasonal shifts worth knowing. Summer heat, which is intense here, tends to push offices toward earlier start times and shorter afternoons, and some offices bring in extra help for beverage and water runs through the hotter months, which tightens the available pool slightly. Ramadan shortens office hours across the board, and we see a brief dip in new hiring during the month itself followed by a pickup immediately after, as offices return to full hours. Admission and exam seasons near the education corridors can also create short bursts of demand from coaching centers and branch offices that want temporary support.

If your hiring timeline is flexible, booking just before or just after these windows usually means a smoother shortlist. If you have to hire during a peak, giving us a couple of weeks of lead time lets us line up candidates before the pool tightens.
