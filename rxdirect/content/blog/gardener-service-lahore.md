Lahore's older, established areas like Model Town, built around a well-known circular park, and larger DHA and Bahria Town properties generate steady demand for regular lawn and garden upkeep.

## Established gardens vs. newer plantings

Model Town's homes back onto a well-known, decades-old circular park, and many of the gardens in the area are similarly mature, households there usually want ongoing pruning, hedge-shaping and upkeep of established planting rather than starting anything new. Newer DHA and Bahria Town properties tend to be the opposite: larger open lawns that are still being landscaped, with more demand for new plant beds and layout work than for maintaining decades-old greenery.

## Our verification process

Every gardener we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks, a personal interview, and a skills assessment. We factor in whether a placement is for maintaining a mature, established garden or building out a newer one, since the two call for a different mix of pruning and planting skills.

## What a typical booking looks like

We usually start with a WhatsApp message about the area, Model Town, DHA, Bahria Town or elsewhere, plus roughly how large the garden is and what's already planted. That's enough for us to shortlist candidates whose background matches: pruning-heavy experience for an established Model Town garden, or planting and layout experience for a newer DHA plot still being landscaped. Households then meet the shortlisted candidate directly, and once there's a fit, the first visit is typically arranged within a couple of days.

## Part-time visits vs. full-time mali

Lahore households split fairly evenly between the two arrangements. A Model Town garden that's already mature and just needs regular pruning and tidying often works fine with a gardener visiting a few times a week. A newer DHA or Bahria Town property that's still being landscaped, new beds, larger open lawns, ongoing planting, more often benefits from a full-time mali who can be on site more consistently while the garden takes shape. We talk through what stage the garden is at before recommending which fits better.

## Trial period and replacement

The first couple of weeks with a new gardener are effectively a trial period, it's the best way to see whether their pace and approach suit an established garden versus a newer one being built out. If it's not working, tell us what's off and we'll shortlist a replacement rather than leaving the household to restart the process alone.

## What's included in a typical visit

A standard Lahore visit covers mowing and edging, weeding, pruning of hedges and shrubs, and a check of irrigation lines for leaks or blockages. In Model Town, that pruning work tends to be a bigger part of the visit, since mature trees and long-established hedges need more careful shaping than a young lawn. In DHA and Bahria Town, visits often lean more toward maintaining new plant beds and monitoring how recently-laid grass is taking hold. Larger jobs, redesigning a bed, introducing new plant varieties, reworking a layout, are usually agreed as a separate conversation once the gardener has seen the property.

## Questions we're asked most in Lahore

**Is a full garden redesign something a regular gardener can handle, or do I need a landscaper?** Many of our gardeners can manage layout changes and new planting themselves, but for a more elaborate redesign we'd tell you upfront if the scope is really better suited to a dedicated landscaper.

**How do you decide whether a candidate is right for Model Town versus DHA?** Mostly around experience, we look for stronger pruning and mature-tree handling for Model Town-style gardens, and stronger planting and lawn-establishment experience for newer DHA and Bahria Town plots.

**Can I change the visit schedule after starting?** Yes, households often adjust frequency once they see how the garden responds, particularly moving from a newer property's more frequent early visits to a lighter routine once planting is established.

**What if I only need someone seasonally, like before a family event?** We can discuss shorter-term arrangements, though most households find it more practical to keep a regular gardener so the property doesn't fall behind between one-off visits.
