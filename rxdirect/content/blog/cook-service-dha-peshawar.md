Peshawar already had well-established upscale neighborhoods long before DHA arrived, University Town and Hayatabad have decades of history and their own deep networks of household staff built up over generations. DHA Peshawar is newer and still growing into that landscape, which means families moving in often can't just ask a neighbor who's been there for twenty years. They're building their household staff from scratch in a society that's still finding its footing, and that's usually where we come in.

## What families in DHA Peshawar usually need

Requests from DHA Peshawar tend to fall into a few recurring patterns:

1. **A daily home cook** for a nuclear family, usually a few hours a day, focused on Pashtun and broader Pakistani home cooking.
2. **A live-in cook** for larger joint-family households, often families who've relocated from University Town or Hayatabad and want to keep the same level of full-time domestic support.
3. **A cook experienced with regional specialties**, households that want someone genuinely comfortable with Peshawar's local cuisine, not just generic Pakistani cooking, especially for family gatherings and guests.

## Our verification process for DHA Peshawar placements

Every cook we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, an in-person interview, and a hygiene and food-safety orientation. For DHA Peshawar specifically, we also confirm a candidate's willingness to commute to a society that, while growing quickly, is still less centrally connected than older areas like University Town, and we check whether they have prior experience cooking Peshawar's regional dishes if that's what the household is looking for.

## Trial period and replacement, explained simply

Every cook we place starts with a trial period rather than an immediate long-term commitment. This gives both sides a real chance to confirm the fit, a candidate who interviews well can still turn out to be the wrong match once daily routines, spice preferences, and kitchen habits are actually tested. If the trial period doesn't work out, we return to the shortlist and arrange a replacement rather than starting the search over.

## What a typical DHA Peshawar booking looks like

Most requests start with a WhatsApp message: phase or sector, household size, live-in or live-out, and the kind of cooking needed. We follow up with a few quick questions, dietary preferences, whether the household wants Peshawar's regional dishes specifically, preferred working hours, and build a shortlist of two or three matching candidates. Families typically do a phone interview first, then meet the top candidate in person before confirming. Because DHA Peshawar draws residents both from established Peshawar neighborhoods and from families new to the city, we also check whether a candidate already has commuting experience to the society, since that affects day-to-day reliability more than it would in an older, more central neighborhood.

## Questions DHA Peshawar families ask us most

**Can you find a cook who actually knows Peshawar's local dishes, not just generic Pakistani cooking?**
Yes, we screen for this specifically and only shortlist candidates with genuine hands-on experience cooking regional specialties, rather than candidates who just claim general familiarity.

**We moved from University Town, will it be harder to find good household staff in DHA?**
It can take a bit more effort since DHA Peshawar hasn't built up the same decades-deep informal staff networks that University Town or Hayatabad have. That's exactly the gap we help fill, since we draw from our own vetted pool rather than relying on neighborhood referrals.

**Is commute an issue for cooks working in DHA Peshawar?**
It can be, depending on the sector, so we prioritize candidates who already live nearby or have reliable transport, to avoid attendance problems a few weeks into the placement.

**We host large extended-family gatherings a few times a year, do we need someone different from our everyday cook?**
Not necessarily a different person, but it does change who we shortlist. We look for candidates who've handled bigger, occasional-scale cooking before, even if their day-to-day role was for a smaller household, so the same cook can manage both without being caught off guard.

## How we match the right cook to your household

Cuisine specialization is usually the first thing we sort for in DHA Peshawar. Households that specifically want Peshawar's regional dishes need a candidate with real hands-on experience cooking them, not just someone broadly comfortable with Pakistani food, we screen for that distinction directly rather than taking a candidate's self-description at face value. Household size is the second factor: a nuclear family's daily cooking needs look very different from a joint family that relocated from University Town or Hayatabad and wants the same full-time kitchen support they had before. We also ask whether the household expects the cook to handle guest cooking for larger family gatherings, since that changes who we prioritize on the shortlist.
