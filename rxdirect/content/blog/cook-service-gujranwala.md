Gujranwala's established family homes in Model Town and Satellite Town, along with newer households in DHA Gujranwala and Citi Housing, most often request daily home cooks for Pakistani meals.

## A manufacturing city's household schedules

Gujranwala's economy runs heavily on manufacturing and trade, and a lot of Model Town and Satellite Town households have earners who leave early and return late from factories or family businesses, which puts real emphasis on a cook who can have breakfast ready before dawn and dinner ready on a firm, predictable schedule rather than a flexible one. DHA Gujranwala and Citi Housing's newer households tend to be more relaxed on timing but ask more often for a cook who can also handle guest cooking, since business entertaining at home is common in the city's trading families.

## Our verification process

Every cook we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, an in-person cooking-skills interview, and a hygiene and food-safety orientation. For early-schedule households, we also confirm the candidate's commute and living arrangement can realistically support a pre-dawn start.

## Trial periods and replacements

We encourage every Gujranwala household to treat the first few weeks as a trial, even after a candidate clears our verification steps, schedule fit is easier to judge in practice than on paper, especially for the pre-dawn starts many households here need. If the routine isn't sustainable for either side, or the cuisine or pace turns out not to match what the family expected, we'll work on finding a replacement rather than leaving the household to start the search over from nothing.

## What a typical booking looks like

Most Gujranwala inquiries start with a WhatsApp message telling us the area, household size, and whether the family needs an early-morning start. We follow up on cuisine preference, live-in or live-out, and whether guest cooking is part of the role, then shortlist candidates whose availability genuinely matches a pre-dawn schedule if that's what's needed. For Model Town and Satellite Town families, we usually have two or three profiles ready within a day or two, since punctuality and a firm routine matter more there than cooking range. DHA Gujranwala and Citi Housing bookings can take a little longer when a household wants strong guest-cooking experience layered on top of the daily routine, since we're matching for both at once. Once a family shortlists someone, we arrange an introductory meeting before any commitment, so both sides can confirm the fit face to face.

## Live-in or live-out, how it plays out locally

Gujranwala's older Model Town and Satellite Town houses more often have a spare room or servant quarter, so live-in arrangements are common there, especially for households that need breakfast ready before sunrise. Live-in cooks in these homes also tend to pick up light morning helper duties simply because they're already on site. In DHA Gujranwala and Citi Housing, live-out is more typical, the cook arrives on a set schedule and leaves once dinner service is done, which suits the more flexible timing these households usually prefer. We ask about this upfront so we're not shortlisting a live-in specialist for a live-out role or vice versa.
