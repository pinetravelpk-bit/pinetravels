Multan's growing family base in DHA Multan and Wapda Town has made childcare one of our steadiest request categories in South Punjab.

## A South Punjab hub with newer housing pressure

Multan's role as South Punjab's main commercial and medical hub has pulled in professional families from surrounding districts, many of whom settle in DHA Multan without the extended family network they'd have had back home. That's a common driver behind our full-time nanny requests here, households that need consistent, independent childcare because there isn't a grandparent or relative nearby to lean on. In older Wapda Town households, we see more requests for part-time or after-school help layered on top of existing family support rather than a sole caregiver arrangement.

## What we check before placing a nanny

Every candidate goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks focused specifically on childcare experience, an in-depth in-person interview, and a health screening. Because a number of our Multan placements are for families without local relatives nearby, we place extra weight on candidates who can work independently and reliably, not just under supervision.

## What a typical booking looks like

Because a good share of our Multan nanny requests come from families who've recently relocated for work, often without relatives nearby to fall back on, we try to move quickly once we hear from you. A typical inquiry starts with the children's ages and the hours that need covering, followed by a short shortlist of candidates with relevant independent-care experience. Families usually do an introductory conversation, then meet the candidate in person before confirming. For households without local family support, we also walk through backup plans upfront, what happens on a nanny's day off or if she's unwell, since there isn't always another relative to step in.

## Full-time versus part-time in Multan

DHA Multan's newer, relocated families tend toward full-time arrangements, since the nanny is often the household's only childcare support during the working day. In Wapda Town, where extended family or long-settled households already share the load, part-time and after-school bookings are more common, a few hours in the afternoon rather than a full day. Occasional babysitting, for an evening or a weekend commitment, comes up in both areas and we handle it as a separate short-term match.

## Questions we get asked most in Multan

**We just moved to Multan and don't know the city, can you still help quickly?** Yes, this is one of the most common situations we handle here, and we keep a pool of screened candidates ready specifically because of it.

**Is it possible to get a nanny who can also help if our child gets sick and can't go to school?** Let us know this preference upfront, some candidates are comfortable with flexible, at-home sick-day coverage and others prefer a fixed routine.

**What happens if the nanny needs to take leave?** For full-time households without nearby family support, we try to flag this early so you're not left without coverage; ask us about backup arrangements when you book.

## What the first couple of weeks actually look like

The first few days are mostly about the nanny learning your child's routine rather than the other way around, nap times, feeding preferences, what calms them down, and how the household generally runs. We suggest parents stay reachable and, where possible, around for at least part of the first day or two, especially for younger children, so the nanny isn't figuring out the routine cold. By the end of week one, most families have a clear sense of whether the fit is working, not just whether tasks are getting done, but whether the nanny's manner with the child feels right. If something feels off, the earlier you flag it to us the better; we'd rather adjust in week one than have a family push through discomfort for a month hoping it improves. A placement "not working out" usually isn't dramatic, it's more often a mismatch in pace or communication style, and swapping to a better-suited candidate early is far easier than doing it after the child has bonded with someone who isn't the right long-term fit.

## Language and comfort with the household routine

Multan households differ in whether Urdu or Punjabi is the main language spoken at home, and for relocated families in DHA Multan who've come from other cities, this is worth raising early, a nanny more comfortable in Punjabi may not follow instructions the same way an Urdu-speaking family expects, particularly around daily routine and any specific rules about screen time, food or discipline. We confirm language comfort as part of the shortlist rather than assuming it, and for families without local relatives nearby, we also check that the candidate is comfortable communicating directly with parents by phone during the day, since there isn't always another adult in the house to relay information.
