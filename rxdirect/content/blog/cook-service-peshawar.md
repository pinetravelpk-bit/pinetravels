Peshawar's Hayatabad and University Town households, along with newer DHA Peshawar families, most often ask for daily home cooks focused on Pashtun and Pakistani home cooking.

## Regional cuisine is a real hiring filter here

A cook who's excellent at standard Pakistani home cooking isn't automatically a fit for a Hayatabad or University Town household that wants proper Pashtun dishes, chapli kebab, kabuli pulao, and regional meat preparations that call for different technique and timing than Punjabi-style cooking. We treat this as a distinct skill to verify rather than assuming it overlaps, and we ask upfront whether a household wants Pashtun cuisine specifically, general Pakistani cooking, or both. DHA Peshawar's newer families lean more often toward the latter, wanting a cook flexible across both traditions.

## Our verification process

Every cook we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, and a hygiene and food-safety orientation. The in-person cooking-skills interview is adjusted to the household's cuisine request, so a candidate is only confirmed for Pashtun-cuisine placements after demonstrating it directly.

## What a typical booking looks like

An inquiry usually starts with a message telling us the area and, most importantly for Peshawar, whether the household wants Pashtun cuisine specifically, general Pakistani cooking, or both. That single answer shapes the entire shortlist, since we don't put forward a candidate for Pashtun-cuisine work until they've demonstrated it directly in the cooking-skills interview rather than relying on a resume claim. Hayatabad and University Town households asking for chapli kebab, kabuli pulao and similar regional dishes sometimes take a little longer to match than a general Pakistani-cooking request, simply because the pool of verified specialists is narrower. DHA Peshawar's more flexible briefs usually move faster. Once we've shortlisted, we set up an introductory meeting so the family can talk through the household's actual weekly menu before anything is confirmed.

## Trial periods and replacements

We encourage a short trial period on every Peshawar placement, particularly where cuisine specialization is involved, it's the fastest way for a household to confirm a candidate's Pashtun or Pakistani cooking genuinely matches what the family is used to at home, rather than discovering a mismatch weeks in. If a placement isn't working out during or shortly after that trial, we'll work with the household to find a replacement rather than leaving them to restart the search from scratch. This matters more in Peshawar than in cities where cuisine is less of a differentiator, since a technically skilled cook can still be the wrong fit if their regional cooking background doesn't align with what the household actually eats.

## How we match the right cook to your household

Cuisine specialization is the single biggest factor in matching a Peshawar placement, but it isn't the only one. We also weigh household size, how often the family entertains, and whether the role is strictly daily cooking or includes occasional larger gatherings. A Hayatabad household hosting extended family regularly needs a different profile than a University Town couple wanting straightforward daily meals, even if both want Pashtun cuisine specifically. We ask about all of this before shortlisting, since a candidate who's an excellent match on cuisine alone can still be the wrong fit if the household's actual pace and scale don't line up with their experience.

## Live-in vs. live-out arrangements

Both arrangements are common across Peshawar, and the right one usually comes down to household size and the family's daily rhythm rather than area alone. Larger Hayatabad and University Town homes with space for a staff room more often go live-in, particularly when the household wants a cook available across a longer stretch of the day for breakfast through dinner. Live-out suits households that prefer a clearer boundary between working hours and family time, with the cook arriving on a fixed schedule and leaving once the day's meals are prepared. DHA Peshawar's newer, more flexible households often lean toward live-out by default, though we've placed live-in cooks there too when the family's routine calls for it. We confirm this preference upfront so we're not shortlisting a live-in specialist for a live-out role.
