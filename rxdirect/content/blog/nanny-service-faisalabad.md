Faisalabad's growing base of young families in newer developments like Eden Valley and Wapda City has made childcare one of our fastest-growing request categories in the city.

## Extended-family households, different needs

Many Faisalabad families still live in joint or extended-family setups, especially in older sections of the city, which changes what "nanny" actually means here. It's often less about a single caregiver covering a full day solo and more about supplementing grandparents or an aunt who's already home, someone to handle school pickup, homework time, or evening supervision while the primary carer rests or manages the household. In the newer developments like Eden Valley, where both parents commonly work, the request shifts back toward full daytime coverage. We ask upfront which pattern fits your household so we're not matching a live-in candidate to a family that really just needs a few structured hours.

## What we check before placing a nanny

Every candidate goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks focused specifically on childcare experience, an in-depth in-person interview, and a health screening. Because so many of our Faisalabad placements are part-time or shared-care arrangements rather than solo full-day roles, we also confirm candidates are comfortable working alongside family members rather than expecting to run the household independently.

## What a typical booking looks like

Most Faisalabad inquiries start with a WhatsApp message describing the household, ages of the children, whether grandparents or other relatives are already involved, and roughly what hours need covering. From there we usually have two or three candidate profiles ready within a day or two, each with a short summary of relevant experience rather than just a CV. Families typically do a phone or video introduction first, then an in-person meeting where the candidate spends time with the children before anything is finalized. For the shared-care arrangements common in older parts of Faisalabad, we also try to include whichever family member the nanny will be working alongside in that meeting, since that working relationship matters as much as the fit with the children.

## Trial period and replacements

We recommend treating the first couple of weeks as a trial, particularly for part-time and shared-care placements where day-to-day dynamics only become clear once everyone's settled into a routine. If a placement isn't working, whether that's a scheduling mismatch, a personality fit issue, or the candidate turning out to have less independent-care experience than the role needs, tell us and we'll find a replacement. This applies whether the household is full daytime coverage in Eden Valley or a few structured after-school hours in an extended-family setup.

## Questions we get asked most in Faisalabad

**Can a nanny start immediately, or is there always a wait?** It depends on the specific requirements, but because we maintain an active pool of screened candidates in Faisalabad, many requests are matched within a couple of days rather than weeks.

**Do you place nannies who've only worked in joint-family homes before?** Yes, and for households that want supplemental support rather than a sole caregiver, that background is often a better fit than someone used to running a home independently.

**What if our needs change from part-time to full-time later?** Let us know and we'll discuss whether your current nanny can extend their hours or whether a different match makes more sense.

## Documents and verification, in detail

Beyond the CNIC and address verification, reference checks and health screening mentioned above, we keep a documented record for every nanny we place in Faisalabad: a copy of the candidate's CNIC, at least one contactable reference from a previous household (not just a phone number, but someone we've actually spoken with about the candidate's childcare experience specifically), and confirmation of the health screening results. For shared-care households where the nanny will be working alongside a grandparent or relative, we also share this documentation with the family before the introductory meeting, so there's nothing to chase down after a candidate has already started spending time with the children.

## Age-group matching

A nanny who's spent years with infants isn't automatically the right fit for a household of school-age children needing homework help and after-school supervision, and the reverse is also true. When you describe your children's ages at the inquiry stage, we use that to narrow our candidate pool specifically, prioritizing infant and toddler experience for younger children, and candidates comfortable with schoolwork, routines and more independence for older kids. For Faisalabad households with children spanning both age groups, we look for candidates who've handled a similar mixed-age arrangement before, rather than assuming experience with one age group transfers automatically to another.
