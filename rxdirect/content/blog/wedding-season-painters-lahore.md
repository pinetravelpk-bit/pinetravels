Wedding season in Lahore stretches from late October well into April, and the moment a shaadi date is set, the painting list starts. A household in Gulberg, DHA, or Johar Town that is hosting the barat at home wants the guest room repainted, the exterior facade touched up, the living room walls that will appear in every photograph given a fresh coat, and the scuffed skirting and door frames that no one noticed all year suddenly addressed. The painter your household calls for the occasional touch-up is rarely set up for the compressed, before-the-wedding rush, and that gap is where temporary painter staffing comes in.

## How wedding season painting work is different

The painting calls we see during shaadi season in Lahore are not the usual repaint calls. They cluster around a few specific jobs:

1. **Guest room and living room refresh**, a full repaint or a careful touch-up of the rooms guests will actually see and sleep in, done on a deadline because the family cannot move out for a week while it dries.
2. **Exterior facade and gate wall**, the wall guests walk past on the way in, often given a fresh coat in the two or three days before the function so it looks maintained in the photographs.
3. **Stage and backdrop painting**, painting the temporary stage backdrop, the signage board, or a feature wall behind the bride and groom, often in a specific color matched to the wedding theme.
4. **Quick fixes on the day before**, touching up scuffed corners, marks on the staircase wall, and finger marks on the door frames that suddenly matter once the decorators point them out.

The work is visible, deadline-driven, and judged by guests and cameras, which is a different kind of pressure than a planned repaint no one is waiting on.

## Our verification process for wedding season placements

Every painter we shortlist for a Lahore wedding season placement goes through the same four-step screening, with no shortcuts, because the trust placed in someone working inside your home around your family is real and the risks of cutting corners are too:

- **CNIC and address verification.** Every candidate's CNIC and home address are verified, so you know exactly who is being placed in your household and where they come from. This is the baseline, not a formality.
- **Previous employer and client references.** We speak directly with at least one previous employer or client by phone rather than relying only on a written reference, and we ask specifically about the kind of work the candidate handled, whether they showed up on time, and whether the client would use them again.
- **Practical skills assessment.** We run a practical assessment so a candidate's actual hands-on ability is confirmed, not just their confidence in describing it. A painter who talks fluently about finishes but leaves runs on a test patch or cannot cut a clean edge reveals it here, which is exactly the point.
- **Tool and equipment check.** We confirm the candidate arrives with their own brushes, rollers, drop sheets, and ladders, since a painter who relies on borrowing tools from the household is not set up to do the job properly.

## Temporary placement versus permanent placement

A temporary wedding season painter is a different ask from a permanent household repaint, and we treat it that way. A permanent repaint is built around scheduling, weather windows, and a pace that lets the household keep living in the house. A temporary placement is built around availability, speed, and the ability to deliver a clean finish on a deadline, often the day before the function. We screen temporary candidates specifically for fast turnaround and night-work willingness, because a painter who is excellent on a planned two-week exterior repaint is not automatically the right fit for a guest room that has to be dry and furniture-back-in-place by morning.

## Lead time, and why it matters more than usual

During peak shaadi weeks in Lahore, lead time is the single biggest factor, and the city's winter smog makes it harder. Drying times stretch when the air is heavy, and a paint job that would normally be a one-day turnaround in spring can take an extra day in December. A week's notice gives us room to shortlist two or three verified candidates, confirm their availability for the paint day and the touch-up day, and line up a backup. Three days' notice usually means we can place someone, but the shortlist is shorter and the weather buffer is thinner. If your function falls on a weekend in peak season, tell us as early as you can, and mention the smog, because the painters who know how to work around Lahore's winter air get booked first.

## Managing your existing staff alongside temporary help

A common worry we hear from Lahore households is how a temporary painter fits alongside the household's existing staff. The two roles rarely overlap. Your regular painter handles the house's planned repaints and knows which walls have damp issues and which colors were mixed custom; the temporary painter is there for the before-the-wedding rush and is gone once the last touch-up is dry. What helps is a short handoff on the morning of the job, where you or your regular painter points out which walls are being done, where the leftover paint from the last job is stored, and who to flag if a surface turns out to need more prep than expected. That handoff is usually enough to keep the two working without stepping on each other.

## Trial period and replacement, even for temporary work

Every temporary placement still starts with a trial window, and our replacement guarantee applies in the same way it does for permanent placements. If the temporary painter does not show on the paint day, is unreachable the day before the function, or the finish is not up to the standard you saw in the assessment, you tell us and we arrange a replacement for the next event or the next coat rather than leaving you to scramble. For a single-evening function this usually means a backup name on standby, and for a multi-day wedding it means we keep the shortlist warm through the whole stretch.

We confirm the candidate's availability across both days before placement, and we keep a backup on standby in case of a conflict.
