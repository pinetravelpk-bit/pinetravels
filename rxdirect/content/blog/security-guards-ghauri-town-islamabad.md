Ghauri Town's zones spread across a large area of Zone 5, and unlike some of Islamabad's more centrally-gated societies, many homes here depend on their own private guard rather than a single society-wide security perimeter. That makes the individual hiring decision, and the vetting behind it, matter even more.

## Our screening process for security guard placements

Security guard roles get the most rigorous check in our process, given the nature of the job:

- **CNIC verification** and a **police character certificate**, non-negotiable for every guard we place.
- **Reference checks** with previous employers, specifically asking about reliability and any incidents.
- **A physical fitness check.**
- **Basic security training verification**, access control, visitor logging, and emergency response basics.

## Shift options for Ghauri Town households

We place guards on day shifts, night shifts, or full 24-hour rotations (typically covered by two guards working 12-hour shifts each). Many Ghauri Town households opt for a night-only guard given the residential, lower-traffic nature of the area during the day, but full-day coverage is available too.

## Typical household and gate setup in Ghauri Town

Because Ghauri Town's zones are spread out rather than centrally gated like some newer societies, most homes here rely on their own boundary wall, gate and guard rather than a single society checkpoint controlling access to the whole area. That means the guard is often the sole line of security for the property, not a supplement to broader perimeter coverage, which is part of why households in Ghauri Town tend to be more particular about vetting than in more centrally-secured societies. Many homes are also on larger plots typical of Zone 5, so guards here often manage a longer boundary wall and a wider driveway or gate area than a typical guard posting elsewhere in Islamabad.

## What the booking process looks like

Households typically get in touch with their zone or street within Ghauri Town and the shift pattern they need. We shortlist two or three background-checked guards, usually within 48 hours, and the household does a short interview before confirming. Because many homes here run a single guard rather than a rotating team, we place extra weight on reliability and long-term fit during screening, a guard who doesn't show up consistently leaves a Ghauri Town property without any backup coverage, unlike homes inside a centrally-guarded society.

## Questions we get asked most

**Since Ghauri Town doesn't have a single central checkpoint, is one guard really enough?**
For most homes, yes, a single guard covering day, night or a 24-hour rotation (via two guards on 12-hour shifts) is the standard setup. Some households on corner plots or near less-trafficked lanes choose to add a night-only second guard for extra coverage.

**Can the guard also handle gate duties like receiving deliveries or guests?**
Yes, this is a normal part of the role, visitor logging, checking ID before entry and managing deliveries are all part of the basic access-control training every guard we place has been through.

**What if we need to change our shift pattern after the guard has started?**
Just let us know, we can adjust the shift arrangement or bring in a second guard for expanded coverage without restarting the whole hiring process.

## Uniform, equipment and reporting

Guards arrive uniformed from their first shift, so there's nothing for the household to source or launder separately. Standard equipment includes a torch for night shifts and a visitor/gate log, something we especially encourage in Ghauri Town given that most homes here rely on their own boundary and gate rather than a shared society checkpoint. Because a single guard is often the sole line of security for the property, we train guards placed here to flag anything unusual immediately rather than waiting to mention it at the end of a shift: an unfamiliar vehicle parked along the boundary wall, a visitor who can't be placed, or anything that breaks the property's normal pattern. If you'd prefer a written daily handover in addition to verbal reporting, let us know and we'll brief the guard accordingly.

## Trial period and replacements

Because most Ghauri Town homes depend on a single guard rather than a rotating team, fit and reliability matter even more here than in a centrally-secured society. We treat the first couple of weeks as a settling-in period, if the guard's routine doesn't suit your household, your zone's specific layout, or your gate setup, tell us and we'll arrange a replacement rather than leaving a mismatched placement in place. We'd rather adjust early, especially given how much a single property here depends on one person showing up reliably.
