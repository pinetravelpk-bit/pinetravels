## Seasonal and local considerations in Rawalpindi

Rawalpindi's seasons and calendar shape office work in ways worth planning for. Summer heat from May through July makes the morning commute harder, especially for candidates coming in from the older, denser parts of the city, which is why the punctuality track record review matters more during placement in those months and we prioritise candidates who already live within a reasonable distance of Saddar or Commercial Market. Winter fog from December into February disrupts early-morning arrivals across the city, so we ask about commute distance and confirm the candidate has a reliable way in during poor visibility. Ramadan compresses both office and trading hours, which shifts the workday earlier in Saddar's wholesale markets and the Murree Road plazas, and an office boy used to a Ramadan routine needs less hand-holding through the month. Eid and the wedding season bring a surge of foot traffic to the retail and wholesale areas, when shops temporarily want extra hands for a few weeks at a stretch, and Rawalpindi's commercial belts also see a real uptick around stock-taking and end-of-quarter periods. Mentioning any of these seasonal windows when you first reach out helps us shortlist someone who is already used to that rhythm rather than one adjusting on the job.
