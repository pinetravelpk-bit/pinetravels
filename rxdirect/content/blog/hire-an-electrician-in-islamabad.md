## Trial period and replacement, explained simply

Every placement starts with a trial period, and it isn't there because we doubt our own screening. It exists because an electrician who passes the skills assessment can still turn out to be a poor fit for a specific household's expectations, a particular property's wiring quirks, or the way you want ongoing maintenance handled. Those first few visits are where you see whether they turn up on time, diagnose problems accurately, and explain what they're doing rather than just getting on with it silently. If something isn't working, tell us sooner rather than later. We go back to the shortlist and arrange a replacement, so you're not restarting the search from scratch. An early flag is always easier to act on than one raised weeks into an ongoing arrangement.

## Seasonal and local considerations in Islamabad

Islamabad's seasons have a real effect on electrical systems. Summer pushes inverter and AC loads hard, and a weak breaker or aging wiring often reveals itself the first time the whole house runs coolers at once, which is exactly when a same-day fix matters most. The monsoon, typically July through August, brings humidity and sudden downpours that can trip breakers and expose any outdoor wiring or fixture issues, so an electrician with local experience checks those points before the rains rather than after a fault. Winter is gentler on the system but brings a spike in heating and geyser loads, and older G-sector wiring sometimes struggles with the combined demand. If your booking starts near a seasonal shift, mention it. We prioritise candidates who have already worked through a full Islamabad year, because the load patterns and the weather catch out electricians new to the city.
