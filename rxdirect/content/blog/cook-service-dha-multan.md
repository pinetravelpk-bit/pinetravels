Multan has traditionally been a Cantt-and-old-city kind of place, where domestic staff got hired through neighborhood referrals and family networks that had built up over decades. DHA Multan changed that picture. As a newer, planned development, it's attracted a mix of families relocating from the older parts of the city and returning overseas Pakistanis building homes from scratch, and both groups tend to run into the same problem: the informal referral networks that worked in Cantt or Shah Rukn-e-Alam colony don't really extend into a brand-new sector yet. That's the gap we get asked to fill most often.

## What families in DHA Multan usually need

Requests from DHA Multan tend to fall into a few recurring patterns:

1. **A daily home cook** for a newly built or newly occupied house, usually a nuclear family wanting straightforward Pakistani home cooking a few hours a day.
2. **A live-in cook** for larger households, especially joint families who've moved from an older part of the city and want the same kind of full-time domestic support they had before.
3. **A cook comfortable with a wider menu**, households that entertain relatives and business guests regularly and want someone who can handle both everyday meals and larger gatherings.

## Our verification process for DHA Multan placements

Every cook we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, an in-person interview, and a hygiene and food-safety orientation. Because DHA Multan is still filling in, some blocks are fully built up while others have scattered construction next door, we also confirm that a candidate is genuinely willing to commute to a sector that may not yet have easy public transport access, rather than just saying yes to get the job.

## Trial period and replacement, explained simply

Every cook we place starts with a trial period rather than an immediate long-term commitment. This gives both sides a real chance to confirm the fit, spice preferences, kitchen setup, and household rhythm all vary, and a candidate who interviews well can still be the wrong match once daily routines set in. If the trial period doesn't work out, we return to the shortlist and arrange a replacement instead of restarting the search from scratch.

## What a typical DHA Multan booking looks like

Most requests start with a WhatsApp message: block or phase number, household size, live-in or live-out, and the kind of cooking needed. We follow up with a few quick questions, dietary preferences, whether the household entertains often, preferred working hours, and put together a shortlist of two or three candidates whose experience fits. Families usually do a phone interview first, then meet the top candidate in person before confirming. Because DHA Multan draws residents from across the city rather than one established local pool, we also check which candidates already have experience commuting to or working within DHA specifically, since that affects reliability more than it would in an older, more centrally located neighborhood.

## Questions DHA Multan families ask us most

**Is it harder to find domestic staff for DHA Multan compared to older parts of the city?**
It can take a little longer simply because DHA Multan doesn't have the same decades-deep informal referral network that areas like Cantt or the old city have built up. That's exactly why families come to us, we maintain our own vetted pool rather than relying on neighborhood word of mouth.

**Can you find a cook who's used to cooking for guests, not just daily family meals?**
Yes, we ask about this during screening and shortlist candidates who've actually cooked for larger gatherings professionally, since DHA Multan households often host relatives and business contacts more frequently than a typical nuclear-family setup.

**What if our house is in a newer, still-developing block with limited nearby transport?**
We factor that into the shortlist and prioritize candidates who either live nearby or have reliable transport arrangements, so commute issues don't turn into attendance problems a few weeks in.
