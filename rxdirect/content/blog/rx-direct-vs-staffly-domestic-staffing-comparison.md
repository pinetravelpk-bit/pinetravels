If you're choosing between domestic staffing agencies in Pakistan, the marketing language on most agency websites sounds nearly identical: "verified staff," "fast placement," "trusted by families." The differences that actually matter are underneath that language, how deep the verification actually goes, whether the guarantee period is a real number or a vague phrase, and whether the company itself is something you can check exists as a legal entity or just a phone number and a WhatsApp chat. Here's an honest look at where RX Direct stands, with sources you can verify yourself rather than take our word for.

## What both agencies offer

RX Direct and Staffly both place cooks, drivers, maids, helpers, cleaners, security guards and office boys across major Pakistani cities, both use a one-time placement fee model with no recurring commission, and both advertise fast turnaround for common roles. On the surface, the offerings look similar. The differences show up once you look at what's actually documented and verifiable behind each offering.

## Verification depth: what we actually do

Every candidate RX Direct places goes through a defined process before ever being shortlisted to a client:

1. **CNIC and address verification**, cross-checked against the history the candidate describes.
2. **Reference checks by phone** with at least two previous employers, not a written letter. A phone call surfaces what a letter can't: why they left, whether the employer would rehire them, how specific issues were actually handled.
3. **A police character certificate**, required for [security guards](/services/security-guards) and [office boys](/services/office-boys), and available for any role on request.
4. **An in-person interview**, not just a phone screening.
5. **Health screening**, relevant for cooks, helpers and any role working closely with family members.

This is the same standard described in our [hiring guide](/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-hiring-domestic-staff-in-pakistan-how-to-avoid-common-pitfalls-and-find-the-right-fit-for-your-home), and it's the standard every candidate on our roster is held to, regardless of city or role.

## How to verify any of this yourself

Don't take a comparison blog post, ours or anyone else's, at face value. Before you commit to any agency:

- Ask for the specific number of months in their replacement guarantee, in writing.
- Ask whether references are called directly or only provided as letters.
- Ask if the company is a registered legal entity, and ask to see proof, not just a claim.
