Ramadan changes the rhythm of nearly every household in Peshawar, and it does so in ways that quietly affect the person who keeps the house running the rest of the year: the cleaner. Between sehri before dawn, the long fasting day, iftar at sunset, tarawih prayers late into the night, and a steady stream of guests through the month, the cleaning load does not shrink. If anything, it grows, because an iftar and dinner spread for a family of six in a Hayatabad Phase 7 home produces more dishes and kitchen mess than a usual weekday, and the dust that settles across University Town and Regi Model Town does not take the month off. Planning around your cleaner before Ramadan begins is genuinely easier than scrambling in the second week, and this guide lays out how Peshawar households are doing it.

## How a cleaner's schedule shifts during Ramadan

Through most of the year, a Peshawar household's cleaner arrives on a predictable morning slot, does the floors, kitchen, and general tidy, and leaves. Ramadan disrupts that routine for a few reasons. Mornings are harder because the household has been up for sehri and often sleeps in afterwards, so an early arrival can walk into a house that is still quiet and not ready to be cleaned. Afternoons are when the fasting cleaner's energy is lowest, which is the wrong time to hand them the heaviest jobs. And evenings, which used to be downtime, become the busiest part of the day as iftar prep, dinner, and post-tarawih tidying stack up.

What works for most Peshawar families is one of a few adjusted patterns. Some move the cleaner's visit to late morning, after the household has surfaced and before iftar prep begins, so the house is clean before the evening rush. Others split the visit, a lighter morning tidy and a shorter second visit after iftar to handle the dinner dishes and kitchen. A few households in larger Hayatabad homes shift the main clean to the hour before iftar so the kitchen and dining area are reset in time for guests. There is no single right answer, which is exactly why this needs to be agreed in advance rather than worked out on day three.

## Temporary extra help through the month

Even with a shifted schedule, many Peshawar households find that their regular cleaner cannot cover everything Ramadan throws at a home. More iftar gatherings mean more dishes, more sweeping, and more kitchen deep cleans. Families hosting relatives for the month often have laundry and general upkeep double. And then there is the pre-Eid rush, when nearly every household wants a thorough deep clean in the last ten days, which is also when cleaners are most stretched.

This is where temporary extra help makes a real difference. A second cleaner for two or three short visits a week, an extra pair of hands for a few hours on the days you host iftar, or a dedicated deep-clean session booked for the week before Eid can take the pressure off your regular cleaner without you having to supervise more. Temporary help also covers the days your regular cleaner needs off, whether for tarawih, ithikaf in the last ten days, or simply a slower fasting day. The key is to book this before the month starts, because the same verified cleaners are in demand across Peshawar and the Eid window fills up fastest.

## Managing your existing cleaner during Ramadan

Your regular cleaner is fasting too, and that shapes how the month goes for them as much as for you. A little planning on your side makes the difference between a placement that holds through Ramadan and one that frays by the second week.

Move the heaviest tasks to the earlier part of the day when energy is higher, and keep the late-afternoon visit light. If you are changing their hours, talk it through with them directly and confirm the new schedule with us so everyone is on the same page. Be clear about expectations, if you want the kitchen reset before iftar each day, say so upfront rather than expecting it to be inferred. If your cleaner asks for time off for tarawih or wants reduced hours in the last ten days, take it seriously and let us know early so we can arrange verified cover rather than leaving the house uncleaned for a stretch. And if your cleaner also works elsewhere, remember their whole day has shifted, so the small courtesies, a place to rest, water available for iftar, and a predictable end time, go a long way in keeping the placement stable through the month.
