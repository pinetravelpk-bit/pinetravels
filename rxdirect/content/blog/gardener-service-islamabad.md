Islamabad's greener sectors and larger properties, from Bani Gala's hillside villas to F-sector and Bahria Town homes with dedicated lawns, mean gardening is a genuinely year-round need, not just a seasonal one.

## Hillside terraces and household teams

Bani Gala's hillside plots often involve terraced or sloped gardens rather than flat lawns, which changes how planting and drainage need to be handled compared to the flatter F-sector and Bahria Town properties. Because many Islamabad homes also employ a guard or helper alongside a gardener, we look for candidates who are used to coordinating within a small household staff rather than working entirely on their own.

## Our verification process

Every gardener we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks, a personal interview, and a skills assessment before being shortlisted. For hillside and terraced properties specifically, we confirm the candidate has handled sloped or multi-level garden layouts before, since flat-lawn experience alone doesn't always translate.

## What a typical booking looks like

Islamabad requests usually come in with a sector or area mentioned upfront, Bani Gala, F-sector, Bahria Town, since the property type varies so much across the city. We ask whether the garden is flat or terraced, whether there's already household staff on site, and how many days a week the household wants coverage. From there we shortlist a small set of candidates whose experience matches, arrange an introduction, and the household confirms directly before the gardener starts on a regular schedule. Because Islamabad's growing season runs longer than most Pakistani cities, we also flag whether the household wants year-round visits or wants to scale down over winter.

## Part-time visits vs. a full-time mali

Sector properties with a modest lawn often work well with a gardener visiting two or three times a week. Larger Bani Gala villas and Bahria Town homes with terraced planting, ornamental beds, and more extensive grounds tend to lean toward a full-time or near-daily arrangement, especially where the gardener is also expected to coordinate with an on-site guard or helper. We talk through the property size and the household's schedule before recommending one over the other.

## Trial period and replacements

We understand that hillside and terraced gardens in particular can be harder to judge from a single interview, so households are welcome to treat the first couple of weeks as a trial period. If the fit isn't right, whether it's the pace of work, communication, or unfamiliarity with sloped layouts, tell us and we'll shortlist a replacement rather than leaving you to restart the search.

## What's included in a typical visit

A standard visit in Islamabad covers mowing, edging, weeding, pruning, and a check of irrigation lines. For Bani Gala's terraced properties, that also usually includes checking drainage across levels, since water runoff on a slope behaves very differently than on a flat F-sector lawn and can wash out new planting if it isn't managed properly. Seasonal planting, whether it's swapping out beds for the season or introducing new shrubs on a terrace, is typically agreed separately once the gardener has walked the property and understood its layout.
