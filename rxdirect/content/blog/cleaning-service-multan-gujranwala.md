## Flexible scheduling in both cities

Cleaning staff are available on a daily, weekly or one-time deep-cleaning basis in both Multan and Gujranwala. Popular with working families, small offices and clinics who need reliable, recurring support without a full-time live-in hire.

## Our verification process

Every cleaner we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks, a personal interview, and a review of their punctuality and reliability track record, the same process in both cities, no exceptions.

## What a cleaning booking typically looks like

Most clients in Multan and Gujranwala start by telling us their preferred schedule, daily, a few days a week, or a one-time deep clean, along with the size of the home or office. We shortlist verified cleaners who match that schedule and location, share their background with you, and arrange a start date. For recurring arrangements, the same cleaner returns on the agreed days so the household or office builds familiarity with them over time, rather than a different person showing up each visit.

## How the trial period works for cleaning staff

Before committing to a long-term recurring arrangement, we recommend a short trial period, typically the first one or two visits, where you can assess thoroughness, punctuality and how the cleaner handles your specific home or office layout. If anything isn't working during this window, let us know and we'll arrange a replacement before the arrangement is locked in as ongoing.

## Questions customers commonly ask

**Can I switch from a one-time deep clean to a recurring schedule later?**
Yes, many clients start with a single deep clean to assess the service, then move to a weekly or daily arrangement once they're satisfied.

**Do cleaners bring their own supplies and equipment?**
This varies by arrangement and is worth confirming when you book, some households prefer to provide their own cleaning products, others ask the cleaner to bring standard supplies.

**What happens if our regular cleaner is unavailable one week?**
Message us and we'll arrange a temporary, equally verified replacement so your schedule isn't disrupted.

## Household and office types we serve in both cities

In Multan, most of our cleaning requests come from newer society homes in DHA Multan and Citi Housing, where families are setting up a cleaning arrangement for the first time, alongside older, established homes in Gulgasht Colony and Cantt areas replacing long-time help. In Gujranwala, a similar split shows up between newer DHA and Citi Housing homes and established houses in Model Town and Satellite Town. Small offices and clinics in both cities are a growing share of our bookings too, usually wanting a fixed daily or alternate-day schedule rather than a one-time clean.

## What documents and checks we do before placing a cleaner

Before a cleaner is shortlisted, we verify their CNIC against the address they've given us, contact at least two previous employers by phone to confirm dates of work and general reliability, and sit down with the candidate for an in-person interview rather than relying on a phone conversation alone. A phone reference check tends to surface more than a written letter, tone, hesitation, and how specific someone is willing to get about a past employee's habits all tell you something a reference letter can't. We ask directly whether they'd rehire the person, and how they handled attendance and communication when something went wrong.

## Red flags we watch for during screening

A candidate who can't name specific previous employers, gives an address that doesn't match their CNIC, or grows vague when asked about why they left a previous position are all reasons we slow down before shortlisting someone. We also treat a total absence of any prior employment history with some caution, not disqualifying on its own, but something we probe further in the interview.
