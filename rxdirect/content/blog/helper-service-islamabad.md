Islamabad's household help requests come from every part of the city, F-sector apartments needing a few hours of daily cleaning, DHA and Bahria Town villas wanting live-in support, and G-sector family homes looking for someone to help with both housework and light childcare.

## Dual-income households need after-school coverage

A large share of Islamabad's F-sector and DHA households are dual-income, with both parents working office hours, so the request we hear most isn't just cleaning, it's someone who can be present when kids get home from school, help with homework routines and manage the house until a parent is back. That's a different placement than a live-out helper who leaves by early afternoon, and we screen specifically for candidates comfortable with light after-school supervision alongside housework.

## What we check before placing a helper

Every helper we place in Islamabad goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, a personal interview and a health screening. For households needing after-school coverage, we also confirm the candidate's comfort level working independently with children present and match on language preference so instructions are never lost in translation.

## Questions we get asked most in Islamabad

**Can a helper watch my kids while I finish a late meeting?** Some can, but not all, after-school supervision is a distinct skill set from general housework, so we flag which candidates are genuinely comfortable being alone with children versus those who prefer cleaning-only roles.

**Do you place helpers in gated sectors and Bahria Town without hassle?** Yes, we handle the ID documentation most societies ask for at their gates, and candidates come prepared with CNIC copies for security registration.

**What if the helper and my family just don't get along?** It happens occasionally, and it's not something you have to push through, let us know within the early weeks and we'll work on a replacement rather than leaving you stuck with a poor fit.

**Is live-in help common in F-sectors, or mostly DHA and Bahria Town?** Live-in requests skew toward the larger villas in DHA and Bahria Town, while F-sector apartments more often want a live-out helper for a set number of hours.

## Trial period and replacement

We don't expect you to commit blind. Early weeks are treated as a settling-in period, if the working style, routine or communication isn't clicking, tell us and we'll find an alternative candidate rather than leaving the placement as-is. The goal is a helper who actually fits your household, not just someone who filled the role fastest.

## Full-time live-in vs. part-time hourly

Not every Islamabad household needs someone living in. F-sector apartments with one or two residents often just want a helper for a fixed morning or afternoon block, while larger DHA and Bahria Town villas, especially those with kids needing after-school coverage, tend to need someone present for a longer stretch of the day, if not full live-in. Tell us your household size and daily schedule upfront and we'll narrow the shortlist to candidates suited to that pattern rather than showing you live-in profiles when a few hours a day would do the job.

## Language and communication matching

Islamabad's household mix means language expectations vary block by block, G-sector and older family homes often run mostly in Urdu, while some F-sector and DHA households, especially ones with school-age kids doing English-medium homework, prefer a helper who's at least comfortable following basic English instructions alongside Urdu. We ask about this before shortlisting rather than after, since it matters most in households where the helper is also handling after-school supervision and needs to follow homework instructions or communicate with a parent by phone during the day. Some households in Islamabad also have staff from Punjab or KP backgrounds who are more fluent in Punjabi or Pashto than Urdu, if that's a better fit for your home, tell us and we'll factor it into the shortlist.

## What documents and verification you can expect

Every helper we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with prior employers, a personal interview and a health screening before being shortlisted. For households in gated sectors or Bahria Town, we make sure the candidate's CNIC copy is ready ahead of the start date, since society gates typically log domestic staff on entry. If you'd like to see a candidate's CNIC or hear more detail about a reference conversation before confirming a placement, just ask, we're glad to share that rather than asking you to take verification on faith.
