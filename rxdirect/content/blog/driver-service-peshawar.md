Hayatabad's multiple numbered phases and Regi Model Town's five separate zones mean a driver in Peshawar needs genuine familiarity with which specific section a household is in, not just the general area.

## Knowing your phase, not just your neighborhood

Telling a candidate "I'm in Hayatabad" or "I'm in Regi Model Town" isn't specific enough on its own, each has multiple distinct phases or zones with their own entry points and internal layouts, and a driver who's only worked one phase can genuinely get lost trying to find a house in another. During the road test, we ask candidates to name which specific phase or zone they're most familiar with and have them describe how they'd get between two different ones, since that's closer to what an actual week of school runs and errands looks like.

## What we check before placing a driver

Every driver goes through original driving license verification against the CNIC (not a photocopy), a check of traffic violation and accident history cross-referenced with references where possible, and calls with at least two previous employers. For Peshawar placements specifically, we also confirm the candidate's familiarity extends beyond a single phase or zone, since most households need a driver who can reliably reach the right gate or block on the first attempt.

## Full-time, part-time, or hourly

Peshawar households hire drivers under a few different structures. Full-time usually means the driver is covering school pickup and drop-off plus office commuting across the day. Part-time works for families who mainly need mornings and evenings covered. A smaller number of households, often ones that already have a family member who drives sometimes, ask for hourly or occasional coverage instead, mainly for days when the usual driver is unavailable. Tell us which arrangement fits and we'll match accordingly.

## What a typical booking looks like

Most requests come in through WhatsApp with the phase or zone you're in, the hours you need, and whether it's a family or a more business-focused placement. Because Hayatabad and Regi Model Town both have multiple distinct sections, we try to match you with a driver who's already worked your specific phase or zone where possible, or at minimum has demonstrated in the road test that they can navigate between sections confidently. You'll typically see a couple of shortlisted profiles with verification notes before you decide, and we stay reachable during the first stretch after placement in case the routine needs adjusting.

## Trial period and replacements

A placement isn't final the day the driver starts. If something isn't working, timing, communication, or comfort with the route, tell us during the early weeks and we'll look for a replacement rather than leaving the search to you. Since the replacement comes from the same screened pool, it's a quicker process than starting over independently.

## Questions we get asked most in Peshawar

**Does the driver need to know both Hayatabad and Regi Model Town, or just one?** Only if your routine actually spans both, we match based on the specific phases or zones you tell us matter, rather than assuming broader coverage is needed.

**Can a driver handle a school run in one phase and an office commute in another?** Yes, and it's common enough that we ask about the full daily route upfront so the driver we shortlist is prepared for a multi-stop day, not just a single fixed loop.

**What if we're new to a phase and don't have a fixed routine yet?** That's fine, mention it when you reach out, and we'll prioritize a driver who's comfortable adapting rather than one used to a rigid, unchanging schedule.

## How we match the right driver to your household

Matching in Peshawar leans heavily on phase- and zone-specific experience rather than general city familiarity, since Hayatabad and Regi Model Town are large enough that "knows Peshawar" doesn't tell us much on its own. We look at which specific phase or zone a candidate has actually driven in most recently, whether their prior placements were family-paced school runs or business-paced commutes, and whether the household wants a driver who can start immediately unsupervised or would rather have the first week or two of runs supervised while trust builds. A candidate's road-test description of navigating between zones matters more in our matching decision here than it would in a smaller, simpler city layout.

## Documents and verification you should expect

Before a driver reaches your shortlist, we've already verified the original CNIC against the driving license in person, checked traffic violation and accident history as far as it's traceable, and called at least two previous employers directly. Once a driver joins your household, ask to see this documentation yourself and keep the license number and CNIC on file, it's a simple step that also helps if the driver ever needs to be registered with a gated community's security desk.

## Seasonal and timing considerations in Peshawar

Winter fog in Peshawar can delay early-morning school runs, and Ramadan compresses both school and office schedules into tighter windows than the rest of the year. Drivers with a full year of local experience tend to already build in extra time during foggy winter mornings and adjust pickup timing during Ramadan without needing repeated reminders. If your placement is starting right before one of these periods, mention it so we can prioritize a driver who's already handled a similar stretch rather than one only tested in easier conditions.
