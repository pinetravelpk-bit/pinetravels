## What families and households in Islamabad usually need

The requests we receive from Islamabad tend to fall into a few clear patterns, and identifying which one fits your household helps us match the right candidate from the start:

1. **A live-out daily maid** for a household in F-11, E-11, G-sector or Bahria Town, covering general cleaning, laundry, kitchen help and daily upkeep on a fixed schedule. This is the most common booking, and it suits a maid who lives nearby and arrives each morning.
2. **A live-in maid or helper** for larger households, joint families or homes with elderly members who need round-the-clock support, often combining housework with basic care and companionship for older relatives.
3. **A helper focused on childcare support**, working alongside a parent rather than replacing one, handling feeding, play, and daily routines for young children while also keeping the home in order.

## Our verification process for Islamabad placements

Every maid or helper we place in Islamabad goes through four screening steps, with no step skipped, because the trust placed in someone working inside your home is real and we treat it that way:

- **CNIC and address verification.** Every candidate's CNIC and home address are verified, so you know exactly who is being placed in your household and where they come from. This is the foundation, and we do not move forward without it.
- **Reference checks from prior families.** We speak directly with at least one previous employer family by phone, rather than relying only on a written reference. We ask about reliability, honesty, and how the helper actually handled the daily routine, not just whether she showed up.
- **Personal interview.** We sit with each candidate for an in-person interview, talking through her experience, her comfort with different household setups, and her expectations around hours, duties and live-in versus live-out arrangements.
- **Health screening.** Every candidate completes a health screening before placement, so the household knows the helper is fit for the work and there are no surprises around ongoing health concerns.

## Trial period and replacement, explained simply

Every placement starts with a trial period. This is not a sign that we doubt our own screening, it is there because even a strong interview and solid references do not always predict how a helper will fit a specific household's rhythm, the way a family likes things done, or the dynamic with children or elderly members. If a placement does not work out during the trial, we go back to the shortlist and arrange a replacement rather than asking you to restart the search from scratch. Our replacement guarantee is straightforward: tell us what is not working, and we fix it. You are not locked into a mismatch, and we would rather hear about a problem in the first week than have it drag on for a month.

## Seasonal and local considerations in Islamabad

Islamabad's household needs shift through the year in ways that affect domestic help, and a helper with real local experience tends to already account for these patterns. Winter in Islamabad is quieter but brings its own demands, with more time spent indoors and households wanting a heavier clean ahead of family gatherings and the wedding season that runs from late autumn into spring. Ramadan shifts the entire household rhythm, with earlier sehri prep, iftar cooking support, and late evening routines that mean a live-in helper or adjusted live-out hours become more common. Summer brings the monsoon from July onward, with humidity and rain meaning more laundry challenges and a need for extra attention to cleaning and drying. Many Islamabad households also have members who travel for work, and the rhythm of a partly occupied home affects what kind of help fits best. If your booking starts right before Ramadan or the wedding season, mention it so we prioritize candidates who have already worked through these cycles in an Islamabad household.
