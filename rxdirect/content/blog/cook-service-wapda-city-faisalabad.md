## What we look for in a Wapda City cook placement

- Genuine hands-on experience with the specific cuisine your household needs, Pakistani home cooking, continental, or both.

## Our verification process

Every candidate completes CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, and a hygiene and food-safety orientation. Given how many Wapda City placements involve entertaining-scale cooking, the in-person cooking-skills interview specifically tests a candidate's ability to hold quality and timing steady when cooking for a full guest table, not just a household-sized portion.

## Live-in vs live-out in Wapda City

Given the size of many Wapda City plots, live-in arrangements with a dedicated staff area are common, particularly for households doing regular entertaining where a cook needs to be available across a longer stretch of the day. Smaller households or those wanting a stricter division between work hours and personal time more often go with live-out, where the cook works a fixed daily schedule and leaves once the day's meals are done. We confirm which setup a household wants before shortlisting, since it changes which candidates are realistically a fit.

## How we match the right cook to your household

Wapda City's larger plots tend to produce a wider range of briefs than a typical neighborhood, some households want a straightforward daily-meals cook, others want someone who can handle a full guest table on short notice, and a fair number want both Pakistani and continental cuisine covered by the same person. We start by asking how often the household entertains, whether continental cooking is a genuine requirement or a nice-to-have, and how large the daily household is versus the guest-table scale the family expects a cook to handle occasionally. Candidates are shortlisted against that specific combination, since a cook who's excellent at daily Pakistani home cooking for a family of five isn't automatically ready to hold quality and timing steady across a table of twenty guests, and the two skills don't always come packaged together.
