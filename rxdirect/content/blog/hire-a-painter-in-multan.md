Multan's heat is the single biggest factor in how painting works here. Summer temperatures regularly climb above 45 degrees from May through July, and paint applied in that kind of heat can blister and peel before it properly cures, so households across Cantt, Bosan Town, and Gulgasht plan their painting around the cooler months rather than fighting the weather.

## What families and businesses in Multan usually need

The requests we get from Multan generally split into a few clear patterns:

1. **Full house repaints in the cooler months.** Households in Cantt and Bosan Town typically plan major interior and exterior repaints for November through February, when the weather is workable and the finish has time to cure properly.
2. **Exterior repaints after dust-storm season.** Summer dust storms leave exterior walls across the city coated in fine grit, and many families want the facade cleaned and repainted once the worst of the heat passes.
3. **Shop and clinic refreshes.** Businesses along Nishtar Road, near Nishtar Hospital, and in the Bosan Town commercial areas call us for interior repaints done on a closed day or overnight so they can reopen without losing trading hours.

## Our verification process for Multan placements

Every painter we shortlist for a Multan booking goes through CNIC and address verification, previous employer and client reference checks, a practical skills assessment, and a tool and equipment check to confirm they bring their own rollers, brushes, drop sheets, ladders, and prep gear. Because Multan's dust and heat are tougher on paint than in most cities, we specifically ask candidates about their approach to surface cleaning and working in heat, since a painter who tries to push an exterior job through a 45-degree afternoon is going to produce a finish that fails.

## Trial period and replacement, explained simply

The first day of a painting job is where a painter's real preparation and pace show, whether they prep surfaces properly or rush to the finish coat. We treat that early period as a working trial, and if the standard isn't what was discussed, tell us the same day so we arrange a replacement from the shortlist instead of letting a poor job continue. For smaller touch-ups the same guarantee applies: if the cleanup or finish isn't right, we follow up.

## Seasonal and local considerations in Multan

Multan's seasonal calendar for painting is more restricted than most cities. From May through July, exterior painting is genuinely difficult, the heat blisters fresh paint and dust storms coat surfaces in grit before they cure, so we advise clients to plan exterior work for November through February. Interior work can continue through summer but needs ventilation, and even then, paint fumes in a sealed room during peak heat are uncomfortable, so we brief painters to work room by room with windows open where possible. Wedding season in Multan runs through winter, which overlaps with the best painting weather, so households planning a shaadi often want a repaint done at the same time, which makes verified painters busier in that window. Reach out two to three weeks ahead of an event date rather than leaving it to the last week. Mango season in summer brings its own small uptick in requests, as households hosting guests for the season want refreshes done before the heat makes painting impractical.

## Questions Multan households ask us most

**When is the best time to paint a house exterior in Multan?**
November through February is the most reliable window. The heat from May to July makes exterior paint blister, and dust storms in that period coat fresh surfaces in grit. If you have flexibility, schedule exterior work for the cooler months.

**Can you do a quick interior refresh before a wedding in December?**
Yes, but reach out early. Winter is both wedding season and the best painting weather, so verified painters get booked up. Two to three weeks' notice gives us time to shortlist properly rather than scramble at the last minute.

**How do you handle the dust from storms before repainting?**
Painters we place are briefed to wash down and prep exterior walls thoroughly before any primer goes on, especially after dust-storm season. Skipping that step is the fastest way to a finish that peels within months.

**Do you handle both interior and exterior in the same booking?**
Yes, most full house repaints cover both. Tell us the scope upfront and we'll match a painter or small team who can handle interior rooms and the exterior facade in sequence, with the exterior timed for the cooler morning hours so the finish cures properly in the Multan heat.
