## Start with the house, not the job titles

Before deciding on roles, write down what actually has to happen in your home every day and every week. For most Islamabad households, the list looks something like this:

- **Daily:** sweeping and mopping, dusting, bathrooms, kitchen cleaning, dishes, laundry, ironing, three meals, tea for guests, school drops and pick ups, errands, gate duty, rubbish out
- **Weekly:** deep cleaning of one or two rooms, bed sheets, fridge cleaning, grocery runs, car washing, garden mowing and watering
- **Seasonal:** heater and geyser checks before winter, curtains and carpets, cleaning water tanks, monsoon drain clearing, planting in spring and autumn

Once the list is written, the roles almost choose themselves. A household staff agency in Islamabad that asks you for this list, or helps you draw it up, is thinking about your home rather than its own placement numbers.

## Dividing duties clearly

Most staff problems are not about honesty or skill. They are about unclear duties. Two maids each think the other should clean the guest bathroom. The driver thinks washing the car is the helper's job. The cook refuses to clean the kitchen floor. Avoid this by writing duties down and sharing them with everyone.

Here is an example split for a family house with a cook, a maid, a driver and a helper:

| Task | Responsible |
|---|---|
| Meals, kitchen counters, utensils | Cook |
| Kitchen floor and fridge cleaning | Maid, twice a week |
| Bedrooms, lounge, bathrooms | Maid |
| Laundry and ironing | Maid |
| Serving tea and meals to guests | Cook, maid helps when busy |
| School and office runs, errands by car | Driver |
| Car washing and basic checks | Driver |
| Garage, porch, lawn and outdoor cleaning | Helper |
| Grocery shopping | Driver takes the cook, or helper on foot |
| Gate when guard is off | Helper |

Pin a copy in the kitchen or staff room. When something changes, change the list.

## A daily schedule that works

Islamabad mornings start early. Schools begin around 7:30 to 8:00, and many offices by 9:00. A workable schedule for a family house might look like this:

- **6:30** Cook prepares breakfast and lunch boxes. Driver checks the car.
- **7:15** Driver leaves for school drops.
- **8:00** Maid begins with bedrooms after the family leaves.
- **9:00** Driver drops the working parent, or returns for errands.
- **10:00 to 12:30** Maid on bathrooms and laundry. Cook starts lunch. Helper on porch and lawn.
- **12:30 to 2:00** School pick ups. Lunch.
- **2:00 to 4:00** Rest break for staff, in rotation so someone is always available.
- **4:00 to 7:00** Ironing, evening cleaning, dinner preparation, tuition or activity drops.
- **8:00** Dinner served. Kitchen cleaned after.

Breaks matter. Staff who work from 6:30 in the morning until 9 at night without rest will not stay long, however good the salary. Build rest into the schedule from day one.

## Staff quarters in Islamabad homes

Many houses in Islamabad have servant quarters at the back or above the garage. If you have live-in staff, the condition of the quarters directly affects how long they stay. A good standard is:

- A clean room with a window, fan and a heater for winter, because Islamabad nights get cold from November to February
- A separate bathroom with a geyser or hot water access
- Proper bedding and a cupboard
- Separate rooms for male and female staff
- A lock for the worker's own room

Spending a modest amount on quarters often saves far more in replacement costs later.

## Managing the team day to day

A household of four or five staff needs a little structure:

- **One point of contact.** Decide who in the family gives instructions. Mixed instructions from several family members confuse staff.
- **A senior worker.** In larger teams, the cook or senior maid can be trusted to coordinate others.
- **A short weekly check.** Ten minutes on a Sunday to discuss the week ahead, guests, travel and any problems.
- **Fair off days.** Rotate days off so the house is never empty of help, and so no one always misses Friday.
- **Equal respect.** Treat all staff fairly. Favouring one worker openly creates resentment.

## When staff do not get along

Disagreements between staff happen. Common causes are unequal workload, language or regional differences, and gossip. Listen to both sides privately, check whether the duty list is fair, and make a decision. If one worker keeps causing problems with everyone, it may be time for a replacement, which a household staff agency in Islamabad should arrange without fuss.

Prevention works better than cure. Keep workloads balanced, pay everyone on the same day so nobody feels second in line, and do not discuss one worker's mistakes with another. Where staff come from different regions, a few simple shared rules about the kitchen, the television in the staff room and prayer times usually settle most tension. If you notice two workers avoiding each other, ask about it early rather than waiting for a blow up in front of guests.

## Settling a new member into an existing team

The first two weeks decide whether a new worker stays. Introduce them to the rest of the staff yourself, explain their duties in front of everyone so there is no confusion, and show them where things are kept. Ask the senior worker to help, but do not leave the whole induction to them, because existing staff sometimes protect their own territory. Check in privately with the new worker at the end of the first week. Ask what is unclear, whether they have what they need and whether anyone is making things difficult. Small problems raised early are easy to fix.

## Ramadan, Eid and the wedding season

Islamabad households run differently through the year, and the team plan should allow for it. In Ramadan, the cook may need to start before sehri and prepare iftar for guests, so shift hours and lighter daytime duties help staff who are fasting. Around Eid, most staff want leave to visit families in Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Azad Kashmir or Gilgit-Baltistan. Stagger leave so that someone is always at home, and plan early, because every family in the city is doing the same. The winter wedding season brings late nights, extra guests and more driving. Agree overtime or extra pay in advance rather than expecting staff to absorb it.

## Hosting guests and dinners

Many Islamabad families entertain often, from family lunches on Sunday to formal dinners for colleagues or diplomats. Hosting goes smoothly when the team knows its roles: the cook plans and prepares, a maid sets the table and serves, the helper manages the porch, shoes and parking, and the guard keeps a list of expected guests at the gate. For larger events, a household staff agency Islamabad hosts rely on can add temporary help for the day. Brief the team the day before, not one hour before guests arrive.

## Safety and records

With several staff, records matter more:

- Keep CNIC copies, photos, addresses and emergency contacts for every worker
- Register all staff with Islamabad Police
- Keep a simple record of salary payments and advances
- Change gate codes and locks when a worker with keys leaves
- Give the guard a list of who is allowed in and when

It depends on your routine and quarters. Live-in staff suit early starts, late dinners and households with elderly parents or infants. Live-out staff suit families who value privacy and have predictable hours.
