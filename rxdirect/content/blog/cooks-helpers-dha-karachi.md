DHA Karachi's phases, from the older sections near Khayaban-e-Ittehad to the newer developments further out, are home to a large, established resident base, and cooks and helpers are consistently our two most-requested roles in the area.

## Cooks in DHA Karachi

Requests range from daily home cooks focused on everyday Pakistani meals, to more experienced cooks for households that entertain often and need comfort with continental or larger-scale menus. Every cook goes through CNIC verification, reference checks with prior employers, an in-person cooking-skills interview, and a hygiene and food-safety orientation.

## Helpers in DHA Karachi

General household help, cleaning, laundry, daily upkeep, and in some households light childcare support, is the second most common request. We match candidates to your household's routine and language preference, with both live-in and live-out arrangements available.

## Household types across DHA's phases

DHA Karachi isn't one uniform market, the older phases near Khayaban-e-Ittehad tend to have long-settled, multi-generational families with established routines and lower staff turnover, while the newer phases further out attract younger nuclear families who are often setting up a household's staffing from scratch. We adjust our approach accordingly: for established households, we prioritize candidates who can slot into an existing routine with minimal adjustment; for newer households, we spend more time upfront understanding what the family actually needs day to day before shortlisting.

## Hiring a cook and helper together

Many DHA Karachi households prefer to hire both roles at once, especially when setting up a new home or replacing existing staff. We can shortlist candidates for both roles in parallel so you're interviewing everyone around the same time rather than running two separate searches.

## Questions DHA Karachi families ask us most

**Is it better to hire a cook and helper from the same agency or separately?**
Hiring both through one process saves time and means both candidates are vetted to the same standard, but we don't push households to bundle roles they don't need, some homes genuinely only need one or the other.

**What if the cook we choose doesn't suit our household's taste after a week or two?**
That's exactly what the trial period is for. Taste and kitchen habits are hard to judge from an interview alone, so if the fit isn't right, we arrange another candidate rather than leaving you to restart the search.

**Do helpers in DHA typically handle childcare as well as cleaning?**
Some do, but not all, and it's not something we assume by default. If light childcare support is part of what you need, tell us upfront so we only shortlist candidates who are actually experienced and comfortable with it.

**How long does it usually take to get someone placed in DHA?**
Once we understand your requirements, we aim to send a shortlist within 48 hours. Actual start dates depend on the candidate's notice period with any current employer.

## What a typical DHA Karachi booking looks like

A household messages us with their phase, the role or roles needed, and whether it's live-in or live-out. We ask about cuisine preference for cooks, or the specific mix of duties expected for helpers, then return a shortlist of two or three verified candidates, usually within 48 hours. The family interviews candidates directly, by phone first, then in person for the top choice, and a trial period follows before the arrangement becomes long-term. If a household is hiring a cook and helper together, we run both shortlists in parallel so interviews can happen around the same time.

## How we verify references before a DHA placement

We call at least two previous employers directly rather than relying on a written reference letter, since a phone conversation tends to reveal more, how specific the previous employer is willing to get, whether they'd rehire the candidate, and how issues like attendance or notice periods were actually handled. Written letters are easy to produce and harder to verify; a phone call lets us ask direct follow-up questions a letter can't answer. We treat vague or unreachable references as a reason to pause and dig further before shortlisting someone to a DHA household.
