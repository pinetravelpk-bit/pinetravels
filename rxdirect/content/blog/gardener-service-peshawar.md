Hayatabad's larger planned plots and University Town's established gardens both generate steady demand for regular lawn and garden upkeep across Peshawar.

## Planned plots and older gardens side by side

Hayatabad was built as a planned township, and its larger, uniformly laid-out plots tend to generate steady, straightforward lawn-maintenance requests. University Town's older homes, by comparison, often have more established gardens with mature trees and shrubs planted over many years, so upkeep there leans more toward careful pruning and preserving existing greenery than mowing open lawns. We ask which situation a household is dealing with before shortlisting, since the two need slightly different gardening strengths.

## Our verification process

Every gardener we place goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks, a personal interview, and a skills assessment. For University Town's older, established gardens, we also check a candidate's comfort working around mature trees and long-standing plantings rather than open, uniform lawns.

## Part-time visits vs. full-time mali

Hayatabad's uniform, planned plots often suit a gardener visiting a set number of times a week, since the work, mowing, edging, keeping beds tidy, tends to be fairly predictable from one visit to the next. University Town's older gardens, with mature trees and shrubs that need more careful, ongoing attention, more often lean toward a full-time or near-daily mali, especially where the household wants someone consistently present to catch pruning needs as they come up rather than on a fixed schedule. We talk through the property before recommending which suits it better.

## What a typical booking looks like

A household usually messages us with the area, Hayatabad or University Town, and a rough sense of the garden's size and condition. We ask a few follow-up questions, particularly around whether the garden has mature trees that need specialist pruning care, then shortlist candidates accordingly. Once a household confirms someone, we set up the introduction and the first visit typically happens within a day or two, giving both sides a chance to see the fit before locking in a regular schedule.

## Trial period and replacement

Because Hayatabad and University Town gardens can need quite different skills, we treat the early visits as a practical trial rather than assuming the first match is automatically right. If a gardener isn't handling mature plantings well, or the pace doesn't suit a straightforward Hayatabad lawn, let us know and we'll shortlist a replacement.

## What's included in a typical visit

A standard visit covers mowing and edging, weeding, pruning of hedges and shrubs, and a check of irrigation lines for blockages or leaks. In University Town's older gardens, that pruning work tends to take longer and matter more, since mature trees and shrubs need more careful shaping than a young, open lawn does. Seasonal planting, new flower beds, replacing thinning patches, introducing shade-tolerant varieties under established trees, is usually agreed as a separate conversation once the gardener has seen the property firsthand.
