## Part-time visits vs. full-time mali

Not every home needs a gardener on site every day. Smaller courtyard gardens in older Jinnah Colony-style houses often do fine with a gardener visiting a few times a week for mowing, weeding and general tidying, while larger Wapda City lawns with more extensive planting tend to need more frequent attention or, in some cases, a full-time mali who also handles general upkeep around the property. We ask about lawn size and how hands-off the household wants to be before recommending which arrangement makes sense.

## What's typically included

A standard gardening visit covers lawn mowing and edging, weeding, pruning of hedges and shrubs, and a check of any irrigation lines or sprinklers for blockages or leaks. Seasonal planting, swapping out beds for the season, adding new shrubs, or reseeding thin patches of lawn, is usually discussed and agreed separately, since it depends on what the household wants to plant and when. Given Faisalabad's dust, we also flag leaf-wiping and general plant cleaning as part of the routine, since dust buildup on foliage is more of an issue here than in less industrial cities.
