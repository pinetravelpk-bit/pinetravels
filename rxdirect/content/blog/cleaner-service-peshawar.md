Peshawar's mix of large Hayatabad homes and Cantt-area offices means cleaning needs vary widely, some households want a quick daily tidy, others need a thorough weekly deep clean.

## Dust and seasonal deep cleans

Peshawar households ask for one thing more consistently than most other cities: extra deep-cleaning frequency during dusty spring and pre-monsoon months, when window sills, ceiling fans and outdoor-facing rooms in large Hayatabad homes need far more attention than a typical weekly routine covers. We factor seasonal demand into scheduling so households aren't stuck waiting during the months they need it most.

## What we check before placing a cleaner

Every candidate goes through CNIC and address verification, reference checks with previous employers, and a personal interview. For larger Hayatabad homes specifically, we prioritize candidates with experience handling a full property rather than a compact apartment routine.

## What a typical booking looks like

A Hayatabad household usually starts by telling us the size of the property and whether they're after a standard weekly routine or extra seasonal coverage during the dustier months. We shortlist candidates with experience handling a full house rather than a compact apartment, share a profile, and set up a brief introduction before confirming a start date. Cantt-area offices follow a slightly different path, the request usually specifies a before-opening or after-closing window, and we screen specifically for candidates comfortable with those hours rather than assuming daytime availability is enough.

## Questions we get asked most in Peshawar

**Can we increase cleaning frequency just for the dusty season and go back to normal after?** Yes, this is a common request and one we plan for, let us know roughly when you'd like the change to start and end.

**Do Hayatabad placements cost more because the homes are larger?** We match candidates based on the property's actual scope, which is discussed and agreed with the client directly rather than fixed in advance.

**Can the same cleaner work both an office and pick up extra household hours?** It depends on the individual's availability and preference, which we check before confirming any placement rather than assuming it works for everyone.

**What if we need someone on short notice before a family visit?** Reach out as early as you can, we can't always guarantee same-day placement, but the more notice we have, the better the match we can find.

## Live-in vs. live-out arrangements

Live-in cleaning is genuinely uncommon for cleaner placements in Peshawar, most households and Cantt offices we work with prefer a live-out arrangement, where a cleaner arrives for a set visit and leaves once the work is done. This tends to suit larger Hayatabad homes just as well as smaller properties, since a live-out schedule can still cover a full-property clean without requiring the household to manage a live-in staff member's separate living space. Where a household genuinely wants live-in help, it's usually paired with broader helper duties beyond cleaning alone, and we'd typically point that request toward our helper placements rather than a dedicated cleaner role.

## One-time deep clean vs. a recurring arrangement

A one-time deep clean suits a specific need, pre-Eid preparation, a move, or catching up after the dustiest weeks of spring, and we match it with someone comfortable doing a heavier single session. A recurring arrangement, whether weekly for a household or a few evenings a week for a Cantt office, works best when the same person returns each time, since they get familiar with the property and don't need re-briefing on layout or routine. Many Hayatabad households find it useful to start with a one-time clean and decide on a recurring schedule once they've seen how a candidate works.

## What documents and verification you should expect to see

Every candidate completes CNIC verification and an address check, and we speak directly with at least one previous employer by phone before confirming a placement. For Hayatabad's larger homes, we also ask about a candidate's experience with multi-room properties during the reference call, not just their general cleaning background. Households are welcome to ask to see a copy of the candidate's CNIC or to hear more detail about the reference conversation before confirming, we'd rather answer those questions upfront than have a client feel unsure on the first visit.
