Multan sits at the crossroads of southern Punjab, and the driving here has its own character shaped by the city's layout, its role as a regional hub, and the long stretches of highway that connect it to the rest of the province. A driver in Multan is often doing more than city errands, they are regularly running the route to Lahore, navigating to and from the airport, and handling the connections to nearby towns like Muzaffargarh and Dera Ghazi Khan that many Multan families and businesses rely on. RX Direct places drivers who understand this specific geography, verified and screened before they ever take the wheel of your vehicle.

## What families and businesses in Multan usually need

The bookings we receive from Multan tend to follow a few clear patterns, and identifying which one fits your situation helps us match the right candidate quickly:

1. **A full-time family driver** for a household in Bukhari Colony, Cantt, Shah Rukn-e-Alam Colony or DHA Multan, covering school runs, office drop-offs, errands and the regular family travel that comes with living in a regional hub. This is the most frequent request and typically suits a live-out driver on a fixed daily schedule.
2. **A business or trader driver** for the business community around the Chowk Bazaar and commercial areas, where punctual transport to meetings, warehouse visits, and regular intercity trips are the priority. These placements favor candidates comfortable with both city navigation and longer highway routes.
3. **A driver for elderly or family members needing medical travel**, since Multan's hospitals serve patients from across southern Punjab, and safe, patient driving for these trips matters as much as route knowledge.

## Our verification process for Multan placements

Every driver we place in Multan goes through four screening steps, with no step skipped, because the trust placed in a driver is real and the roads, both city and highway, demand it:

- **Driving license authenticity check.** We verify the original driving license against the candidate's CNIC in person. A photocopy or a scanned image is not enough, since that is the easiest document to fake and the first thing we would rather catch during screening than discover after a placement.
- **Traffic violation history review.** We review the candidate's traffic violation and accident history to understand how they actually behave on the road, not just how they perform in a brief interview. Patterns of repeated violations tell us more than a single clean month.
- **CNIC and address verification.** Every candidate's CNIC and home address are verified, so you know exactly who is being placed in your household and where they commute from each day.
- **Road test evaluation.** We run a practical road test where the candidate describes, from memory, a route between two points relevant to the booking, and talks through how they would adjust that route during Multan's busier hours. Someone who can only recite a single memorized path usually reveals it here, which is the point of the exercise.

## Seasonal and local considerations in Multan

Multan's driving conditions shift through the year in ways a driver with genuine local experience tends to already account for. Summer heat from May through July is among the most intense in Punjab, and a driver who manages the car's cooling, avoids midday standstill traffic, and plans routes around the hottest hours saves both comfort and fuel. The dust storms that sweep through southern Punjab in late spring and early summer reduce visibility on the highway stretches, especially on the routes toward Lahore and Bahawalpur, and a calm, defensive driver matters far more than a fast one in those conditions. Ramadan compresses the workday and shifts traffic peaks earlier, so a driver used to the adjusted schedule keeps things smoother than one learning it mid-month. The mango season in summer brings heavier truck traffic on the regional roads, which an experienced local driver knows to account for. Winter brings cooler mornings but also fog on the highway exits, which can slow a routine trip considerably. If your booking involves regular highway travel or starts right before the dust storm or fog season, mention it so we prioritize candidates who have already driven through these conditions.

**What happens if the driver is late because of Chowk Bazaar traffic?** We look for candidates with a track record of building buffer time into their routing rather than cutting it close.
