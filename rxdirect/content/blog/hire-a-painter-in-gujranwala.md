## Seasonal and local considerations in Gujranwala

Gujranwala's summers are hot, with temperatures regularly above 40 degrees from May through July, and paint doesn't cure well in that heat, so we advise clients planning exterior work to aim for the cooler stretch from November to February. Spring and early autumn are also workable for interior jobs, with better ventilation than peak summer. Industrial dust is a year-round factor but is worst in the dry months, so exterior repaints benefit from a thorough wash-down first, and we brief painters accordingly. Wedding season in Gujranwala runs through winter, and households across Cantt and Civil Lines often want a fresh coat before a shaadi, which overlaps with the best painting weather, so reach out two to three weeks ahead of an event date because verified painters get booked early in that window. Ramadan compresses working hours, and many families prefer to get painting done before or after the month rather than during.

## Questions Gujranwala households ask us most

**How do you deal with industrial dust before painting?**
Painters we place are briefed to wash down and sand exterior walls before applying any primer, especially for properties closer to the manufacturing areas. Skipping that step is the most common reason a finish fails early in Gujranwala, so we make sure candidates understand it's non-negotiable.

**Can you repaint a shop overnight so we don't lose a trading day?**
Yes, overnight and closed-day commercial repaints are a normal booking. Tell us the hours you need the work done within and we'll match a painter comfortable with that pace.

**When is the best time for an exterior repaint here?**
November through February is the most reliable window. Summer heat can blister exterior paint, and dust is worst in the dry months before monsoon, so the cooler stretch gives the best chance of a finish that lasts.

**Do you handle both interior and exterior in the same booking?**
Yes, most full house repaints cover both. Tell us the scope upfront and we'll match a painter or small team who can handle interior rooms and the exterior facade in sequence, with the exterior timed for the cooler part of the day so the finish cures properly.
