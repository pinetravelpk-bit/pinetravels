If you are about to hire domestic staff in Islamabad, you probably want two things: the right person, and as little stress as possible getting there. The good news is that hiring well is not complicated. It is a sequence of small, sensible steps. Families who follow them usually hire once and keep the worker for years. Families who skip them often find themselves searching again within weeks.

This article is a practical plan in ten steps. It includes interview questions for the most common roles, a sample trial plan and a simple agreement you can adapt. Use it whether you hire through an agency like RX Direct or on your own.

## Step 1: Write down the job

Before you speak to anyone, write the job down in plain words. Include:

- **The role:** maid, cook, driver, nanny, caretaker, helper or a combination
- **Duties:** the daily and weekly tasks, as specifically as you can
- **Hours:** start and finish times, break times, which day off
- **Living arrangement:** live-in, full-time live-out or part-time
- **Household:** number of adults and children, any elderly family members, pets, typical number of guests
- **Location:** your sector or society, because this decides who can reach you

A clear job description does two things. It helps you compare candidates fairly, and it gives the worker a fair picture of what they are agreeing to.

## Step 2: Set a realistic budget

The fastest way to fail at hiring is to offer a salary from five years ago. Salaries in Islamabad have risen with inflation, rent and transport costs. As a guide for 2026:

| Role | Starting salary per month |
|---|---|
| Helper, cleaner, gardener | From PKR 25,000 |
| Cook | From PKR 35,000 |
| Driver | From PKR 35,000 |
| Full-time maid | PKR 40,000 to 55,000 |
| Nanny | From PKR 40,000 |
| Caretaker | From PKR 30,000 |
| Home nurse | From PKR 60,000 |

Experienced workers, live-in roles with long hours and staff with special skills earn more. Also budget for an Eid bonus, a yearly increase and food. See our [pricing page](/pricing) for the full picture.

## Step 3: Decide where to look

When you hire domestic staff in Islamabad, there are three common routes:

1. **Personal referrals** from family, friends or your current staff. Often good, but checks are usually skipped.
2. **Informal middlemen** who supply workers for a fee. Quick, but checks and replacements are often missing.
3. **A registered agency** with an office, verification process and replacement guarantee.

Whichever route you choose, the checks in Step 5 still apply.

## Step 4: Shortlist on the phone

A short phone call before an interview saves a lot of time. Ask:

- Where do you live now, and how would you reach my sector?
- What work have you done before, and for how long?
- Why did you leave your last job?
- What salary are you expecting?
- Are you comfortable with live-in or live-out work?
- When could you start?

If the answers do not fit, thank them and move on.

## Step 5: Check identity, address and references

Do not skip this step, even for a referral.

- **CNIC:** see the original, match the photo, note the number and permanent address.
- **Current address:** confirm where the worker lives in Islamabad or Rawalpindi.
- **References:** call at least one previous employer. Ask how long the worker stayed, how they handled cash and keys, why they left and whether they would hire them again.

## Step 6: Interview with the right questions

Interviews work best at your home, so the worker can see the house and you can see how they react to it. Ask open questions and listen. Here are role specific questions that tell you a lot.

### Questions for a maid

- How do you clean a bathroom from start to finish?
- How do you wash and iron delicate clothes like chiffon or silk?
- What would you do if you broke something valuable while cleaning?
- How do you organise your day in a large house?

### Questions for a cook

- What do you cook best? Which dishes would you make for guests?
- How do you plan meals and groceries for a week?
- How do you keep the kitchen clean while cooking?
- Can you adjust oil, salt and spice for someone with diabetes or high blood pressure?

Then ask the cook to prepare one or two dishes in your kitchen. This is the single most useful test.

### Questions for a driver

- How long have you driven in Islamabad and Rawalpindi?
- Which routes would you take from your sector to Blue Area, the Expressway or the airport?
- What do you check on the car every morning?
- What would you do if a child in the car felt unwell?

Then take a short test drive, including a busy road and parking.

### Questions for a nanny

- What ages have you looked after?
- How would you settle a baby who will not stop crying?
- What would you do if a child had a high fever at night?
- How do you handle a toddler's tantrum?
- What would you never do with a child?

### Questions for a caretaker

- Have you cared for someone with dementia, a stroke or limited mobility?
- How do you help an elderly person bathe safely?
- How do you keep track of medicines?
- What would you do if the person fell?

## Step 7: Run a fair paid trial

A trial of a few days lets both sides see whether the job works. Pay for the trial days. During the trial:

- Give the full duty list on day one
- Show how you like things done
- Observe without hovering
- Note what goes well and what needs improvement
- Ask the worker at the end how they found it

A worker who is shy in an interview may be excellent in practice, and a confident talker may struggle. The trial reveals the truth.

## Step 8: Put a simple agreement in place

A written agreement prevents most disputes. It does not need legal language. Here is a sample you can adapt:

> **Employment agreement**
>
> Employer: (name, address in Islamabad)
> Worker: (name, CNIC number, permanent address)
>
> Role: Cook
> Start date: (date)
> Hours: 7:00 am to 3:00 pm and 6:00 pm to 9:00 pm, Monday to Saturday
> Day off: Sunday
> Salary: PKR (amount) per month, paid on the 1st of each month
> Accommodation and meals: (if live-in, describe room and meals)
> Leave: (number) days paid leave per year, plus Eid
> Notice: Either side gives (number) days' notice
> Duties: (attach the duty list)
>
> Signed by both, with date.

Give the worker a copy and keep one yourself.

## Step 9: Register and keep records

Register the worker with Islamabad Police, keep copies of their CNIC, a photo, addresses and an emergency contact, and keep a simple record of salary payments and any advances. These records protect both you and the worker.

## Step 10: Look after the person you hired

The hiring process does not end on the first day. Staff stay where they are respected and treated fairly. Pay on time, keep the day off, give a yearly increase, provide good food and decent quarters, and speak kindly. Ask every few months how things are going. Small problems raised early rarely become big ones.

## Questions good candidates will ask you

Interviews run both ways. Experienced workers in Islamabad have choices, and the best ones will ask you questions too. Be ready to answer clearly:

- What exactly are the hours, and how often do they run late?
- Is the day off fixed, and is it ever cancelled?
- How many people live in the house, and how often are there guests?
- For live-in roles, where is the room, and does it have a fan, heater and bathroom?
- When is salary paid, and is there a yearly increase?
- Who will give instructions?

A candidate who asks these questions is thinking seriously about the job. A family that answers them honestly attracts better staff and keeps them longer.

## Hiring two roles at once

Some families need two workers at the same time, for example a cook and a driver after moving into a new house. Hire the person who will spend most time inside the house first, usually the cook or maid, and let them settle for a week or two before the second joins. If you prefer to hire together, consider a [domestic couple](/services/couples/islamabad), who often work well because they already know and trust each other. Either way, give each worker a separate duty list so there is no confusion about who does what.

## Common mistakes when families hire domestic staff in Islamabad

- **Hiring in a rush.** Panic hiring after a sudden departure leads to poor choices. A temporary arrangement for a week is better than the wrong permanent hire.
- **Underpaying.** A low salary attracts workers who will leave as soon as a better offer appears.
- **Skipping references.** It takes ten minutes and prevents most bad hires.
- **Vague duties.** "General housework" means different things to different people.
- **No trial.** An interview cannot show everything.
- **No agreement.** Memories differ about what was promised.
- **Over-loading one worker.** One person cannot cook, clean, babysit and drive.

## How long does it take to hire domestic staff in Islamabad?

Through an agency, you can usually see a shortlist within one to three days and have someone working within a week, including the trial. On your own, it can take several weeks, depending on how many referrals you have and how quickly you can check them. Specialised roles such as live-in nurses or English speaking nannies take longer either way.

## Hiring for the first time?

Families hiring their first worker, often a young couple or someone who has just moved to Islamabad, sometimes feel awkward giving instructions or setting rules. That is normal. Remember that clear instructions are a kindness, not a burden. Workers appreciate knowing exactly what is expected and how they will be paid. Start with the essentials, be polite and firm, and adjust as you learn what works in your home. It also helps to agree a short review after the first month. Sit down together, go through the duty list and ask what is working and what is not. Many first-time employers find that a quick conversation at this point fixes small habits on both sides before they turn into a reason to part ways. Keep notes of what you agreed so you can refer back to them, and update the written agreement if hours or duties change.

Yes. Paying for trial days is fair and attracts serious candidates.
