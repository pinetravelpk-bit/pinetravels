Bahria Town Rawalpindi has grown into a genuine mixed-use community, residential phases alongside commercial plazas, small offices and clinics. That mix is exactly why we see steady demand from the area for two very different roles: household drivers and office support staff.

## Drivers for Bahria Town Rawalpindi households

Most requests are for a full-time driver covering school runs and daily commuting, though part-time arrangements are available for households with lighter needs. Every driver goes through license verification, a traffic violation history review, and a road test before being shortlisted.

## Office boys for Bahria Town businesses

Small offices, clinics and retail businesses within Bahria Town's commercial sections regularly need office boys for pantry management, courier runs and general support. We place candidates who are punctual, presentable, and trained in basic office etiquette, verified through CNIC checks, reference calls, and a personal interview before placement.

## Entry passes and phase-specific procedures

Bahria Town Rawalpindi's phases each manage their own security desks, and most require staff to be registered before a recurring entry pass is issued, a one-time visitor slip isn't workable for someone coming in daily. When we place a driver or office boy with you, we share their CNIC copy and photo so you can complete the phase's registration process without delay, and we make sure candidates understand they'll need to carry ID for gate checks.

## What a typical booking looks like

Most inquiries start with a WhatsApp message specifying the phase or commercial block, the role needed, and the schedule (full-time or part-time). We shortlist candidates who've already cleared CNIC verification, reference calls and, for drivers, a road test, usually within 48 hours. You interview the shortlisted candidates directly and confirm the one you want; we handle the paperwork handoff so the person can start with minimal delay.

## Documents and verification you can expect

For drivers, we verify the original license against the CNIC, review traffic violation history, run a road test, and call at least two previous employers about punctuality and safety record. For office boys, we confirm CNIC and current address, check references from past employers, and conduct a personal interview focused on reliability and basic office etiquette. In both cases, we share copies of the verified CNIC with you as part of the placement so you can complete Bahria Town's phase-level registration without having to chase the candidate for paperwork separately.

## Full-time vs. part-time arrangements

Full-time is the more common request for both roles here, given how spread out Bahria Town's phases and commercial blocks are, a driver on call for a few hours a day often isn't practical once you factor in school runs plus an office commute, and office boys in busier commercial blocks are needed across the full working day. That said, part-time arrangements are available for households with lighter needs or businesses only open set hours, and we'll shortlist accordingly once you tell us your actual schedule.

## Why phase and block details matter to us

Bahria Town Rawalpindi is large enough that a driver or office boy comfortable in one phase isn't necessarily familiar with the layout, gate procedures, or typical traffic patterns of another. When you reach out, we ask specifically which phase or commercial block you're in, since it affects both the registration process and which candidates already have relevant experience nearby. A driver who already knows the internal road layout and peak-hour bottlenecks of a particular phase will settle in faster than someone commuting in fresh, and the same logic applies to office boys who've worked in a specific commercial block before and already understand its rhythm.
