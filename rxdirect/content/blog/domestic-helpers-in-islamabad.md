In many Islamabad homes, the person who keeps everything moving is not the cook or the driver. It is the helper. The helper sweeps the porch before the family wakes, carries the groceries in, takes the rubbish out, cleans the car port, waters the plants, fetches bread from the market, helps the cook during a dinner and answers the gate when the guard is on a break. Domestic helpers in Islamabad do a hundred small jobs that nobody notices until they are not done.

Because the role is so broad, it is also the role most often misunderstood. Families hire a helper expecting a maid, a gardener and a part-time guard all in one, and the helper expects something much smaller. This guide clears that up. It explains what helpers typically do, how the role differs from a maid or a cleaner, what salary to expect, and how to write a job description that works for both sides.

## What does a domestic helper do?

A helper is a general support worker for the household. The exact duties vary, but in most Islamabad homes they include:

- **Outdoor cleaning:** porch, driveway, garage, roof, lawn edges, boundary walls and the area outside the gate
- **Rubbish:** collecting bins from inside the house, separating waste where needed and putting it out for collection
- **Errands on foot:** bread, milk, eggs, vegetables and small items from the local market
- **Carrying and lifting:** groceries, water bottles, gas cylinders, furniture when cleaning
- **Kitchen support:** peeling and cutting vegetables, washing large pots, helping serve when there are guests
- **Plants:** watering pots and small lawns, sweeping leaves, especially in autumn when Islamabad's trees shed heavily
- **Car washing:** in homes without a full-time driver
- **Gate and door:** receiving deliveries, couriers and meter readers when the guard is busy or there is no guard

In larger homes, a helper often works under the cook or senior maid and takes on whatever the house needs that day.

## Helper, maid or cleaner: what is the difference?

The three roles overlap, but they are not the same. Knowing which you need saves a lot of frustration.

| | Helper | Maid | Cleaner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main focus | General support, outdoor work, errands | Indoor housekeeping, laundry, rooms | Deep and regular cleaning |
| Typical gender | Usually male | Usually female | Male or female |
| Works inside bedrooms | Rarely | Yes | Yes, when cleaning |
| Laundry and ironing | Sometimes basic | Yes | Usually not |
| Errands outside | Yes | Rarely | No |
| Heavy lifting | Yes | Light only | Moderate |
| Typical arrangement | Full-time, often live-in | Full-time or part-time | Often part-time or per visit |

If your main need is indoor cleaning, laundry and keeping rooms in order, you probably want a [maid](/services/maids/islamabad). If you need thorough cleaning a few times a week, a [cleaner](/services/cleaners/islamabad) may suit better. If you need an extra pair of hands across the whole house, especially outdoors, a [helper](/services/helpers/islamabad) is the right choice.

## Which Islamabad homes need domestic helpers?

Not every home needs a helper. Apartments and small houses usually do not. Helpers become useful when:

- The house has a large porch, driveway, roof or garden that needs daily attention
- There is no full-time gardener or guard
- The family entertains often and the cook needs support
- Elderly family members need someone to fetch things and run errands
- The family lives in a sector where shops are close enough for errands on foot, as in most of the F, G and I sectors
- There are several cars that need washing every day

Big houses in E-7, F-6, F-7, F-8, Bani Gala, Chak Shahzad, Park Road and the larger plots in DHA and Bahria Town almost always benefit from a full-time helper.

## Writing a fair job description

The most common complaint from helpers is that the job keeps growing. They were hired to clean the porch and run errands, and three months later they are also guarding the gate at night and washing the neighbour's car. The most common complaint from families is that the helper refuses work. Both problems disappear with a clear job description.

Here is a sample for a family house in Islamabad:

**Hours:** 8:00 am to 6:00 pm, with a two hour break after lunch. Friday off.

**Daily duties:**
1. Sweep and wash the porch and driveway
2. Clean the garage and car port
3. Collect and put out rubbish
4. Water plants in pots and the front lawn
5. Fetch bread, milk and small items from the market when asked
6. Help the cook with cutting, washing and serving
7. Receive deliveries at the gate

**Weekly duties:**
1. Clean the roof and boundary walls
2. Wash outdoor furniture
3. Help with deep cleaning of the kitchen

**Not included:** night guard duty, driving, cleaning bedrooms, work at other houses.

Share this on the first day and keep a copy. If you need to add duties later, discuss it and adjust the salary if the load grows significantly.

## What to look for when hiring a helper

Helpers are not usually tested on complex skills, but character matters a lot because they move in and out of the house all day and often handle cash for errands. Look for:

- **Honesty with money.** Ask previous employers specifically about this.
- **Willingness.** A good helper does not argue about every small task.
- **Physical fitness.** The work involves lifting, bending and standing.
- **Basic hygiene.** Especially if they will help in the kitchen.
- **Reliability.** Turning up on time, every day.
- **Respect.** Particularly towards women and elderly family members in the house.

Young men from the villages of Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan make up a large share of domestic helpers in Islamabad. Many are hard working and honest, but some are new to city life and need a little time to learn how a household runs. Patience in the first month pays off. Many of the most dependable domestic helpers in Islamabad started exactly this way, unsure of the city in their first weeks and trusted with the keys to the house within a year.

Ask candidates simple, practical questions in the interview. Where did you work before, and what did a normal day look like? How would you handle it if the shopkeeper gave you too much change? What would you do if a stranger at the gate said he was sent by the family? The answers tell you more than any certificate.

## Live-in or live-out helpers?

Both work, depending on your home.

**Live-in helpers** suit large houses where the helper's early morning and evening work matters, homes with frequent guests, and households where a helper also provides a presence at night. You will need a decent room, bathroom and meals.

**Live-out helpers** suit homes without quarters or families who prefer privacy after working hours. Make sure the helper lives close enough to arrive on time; long commutes from Rawalpindi or the outskirts often lead to lateness and early resignation.

## Getting the best from your helper

- **Give one set of instructions.** When four family members each give the helper a different task at the same time, nothing gets done well.
- **Rotate tasks.** Repetitive outdoor work all day is tiring. Mix in some lighter tasks.
- **Provide the right equipment.** A proper broom, wiper, pipe, gloves and a bucket make a difference.
- **Protect from weather.** Islamabad summers are hot and humid; winters are cold and damp. Provide water, shade breaks in summer and warm clothing in winter.
- **Recognise good work.** A word of thanks or a small bonus after a busy wedding or Eid goes a long way.

## Helpers and other staff

In homes with several staff, the helper often sits at the bottom of the informal hierarchy. Watch that other workers do not push all their unpleasant tasks onto the helper or treat them badly. Make it clear that the helper's duties are agreed by the family, not by the cook or driver. A respected helper stays; an exploited one leaves within weeks.

## Seasonal work in Islamabad

Helpers in Islamabad have a work calendar shaped by the weather:

- **Spring (March to April):** pollen season, so dusting and washing outdoor surfaces more often. Planting in pots.
- **Summer (May to June):** watering twice a day, keeping the roof and water tank areas clean.
- **Monsoon (July to August):** clearing drains and gutters, sweeping water from porches, dealing with mosquitoes around stagnant water.
- **Autumn (October to November):** heavy leaf fall from the city's trees, so daily sweeping.
- **Winter (December to February):** carrying gas cylinders and firewood, helping set up heaters, keeping paths clear on wet days.

Planning around these seasons keeps the house in good shape all year.

## Helpers in homes with elderly parents

Many Islamabad families hire a helper mainly for an elderly father or mother. In these homes, the helper fetches medicines from the pharmacy, accompanies the parent on a slow walk around the green belt, carries a chair to the lawn on sunny winter mornings, helps them to the car for a doctor's appointment and makes sure they are never alone if the rest of the family is out. This is lighter physical work but needs patience and gentle manners. If the parent needs help with bathing, toileting or medicines, a trained [caretaker](/services/caretakers/islamabad) is the better choice, and the helper can support the caretaker rather than replace one.

## The first month with a new helper

Treat the first month as training. Walk the helper through the house on day one and show where every tool and supply is kept. Show the right way to clean marble, how much water the potted plants need, which shop to use for bread and how to count change. In the second week, add errands with cash and check the receipts together. By the end of the month, most helpers know the routine well enough to work with little supervision. Families who skip this step often decide too quickly that a helper is "not good", when the helper simply was never shown what good looked like in that house.

## Warning signs to act on early

Most helpers settle well, but act quickly if you notice change missing repeatedly from shopping, long unexplained absences during errands, strangers waiting outside the gate for the helper, rough behaviour towards children or other staff, or refusal of duties that were clearly agreed. ## Frequently asked questions

### Are domestic helpers in Islamabad the same as maids?

No. Helpers usually handle outdoor work, errands, lifting and kitchen support, while maids handle indoor housekeeping, laundry and rooms. Some households need both.

### Can a helper also guard the house at night?

Only if that is agreed and paid for. Night guard duty is a separate job, and a helper who works all day cannot also stay awake all night. Consider a [security guard](/services/security-guards/islamabad) if you need night cover.
